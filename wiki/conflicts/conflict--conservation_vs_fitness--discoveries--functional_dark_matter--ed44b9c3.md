# Which evidence should rank an unknown gene? Four disagreements inside dark-gene prioritization

The evidence-weighted framework on [[concepts/experimental-prioritization-of-functional-dark-matter]] ranks uncharacterized bacterial genes for experimental follow-up by combining several independent evidence streams — measured fitness, essentiality, conservation across pangenomes, genomic-context inference, and environmental association. The framework's premise is that these streams triangulate on the same genes. The corpus does not support that premise cleanly. Four distinct disagreements surface: (1) whether conservation-weighted and fitness-weighted rankings identify the same genes at all; (2) whether guilt-by-association context evidence (operons, neighbors, co-regulation modules) is commensurable with direct ortholog transfer and can share a confidence score; (3) whether stress conditions are or are not the characteristic setting for dark-gene phenotypes; and (4) whether pangenome openness has any environmental or metabolic correlate. Each disagreement changes what a prioritized list *is*, so none can be dissolved by averaging the ranks. This page covers all four.

## Evidence Sides

**(1) Conservation-weighted ranking — the rank is unstable under reweighting.** The framework simultaneously prioritizes genes with strong measured phenotypes and genes that are conserved or environmentally structured, but these evidence types do not guarantee the same ranking. [src: functional_dark_matter] Conservation-dominant or drop-tractability settings retained only 64% of the original fitness-active top 50, while essential-gene rankings changed substantially when tractability or neighbor context was removed. [src: functional_dark_matter] Environmental associations also support hypotheses while remaining vulnerable to sparse metadata, genus-level matching, common-genus effects, and compositional coupling. [src: functional_dark_matter]

**(1, counterpart) Conservation as a weak, condition-bounded proxy for importance.** Essential genes were enriched in the core genome (the gene set shared across a species' pangenome), but the median odds ratio — OR, the ratio of odds of essentiality inside versus outside the core — was 1.56, and essentiality was measured under a single growth condition represented by RB-TnSeq (random barcode transposon sequencing) library construction. [src: conservation_vs_fitness] Thus, the modest core enrichment does not establish that genes important under stress or other unmeasured conditions will be conserved in the same way. [src: conservation_vs_fitness] The essential-gene definition is also an upper bound because missing insertions can reflect short genes, low-complexity regions, or scaffold edges; essential genes were slightly shorter on average, consistent with insertion bias. [src: conservation_vs_fitness]

**(2) Context evidence is broad.** 6,142 dark genes belonged to ICA (independent component analysis) fitness modules — groups of genes with coordinated fitness profiles — 30,190 dark genes shared a predicted operon with an annotated gene, and 97.2% had at least one annotated neighbor within a five-gene window. [src: functional_dark_matter]

**(2, counterpart) Context evidence is not validation, and its precision is orders of magnitude below ortholog transfer.** Module and neighborhood predictions are guilt-by-association inferences rather than direct experimental validation. [src: functional_dark_matter] The report further cautions that the 97.2% neighbor rate is expected given a 75% genome-wide annotation rate. [src: functional_dark_matter] Ortholog transfer achieved 95.8% precision and 91.2% coverage for gene-level KO (KEGG Orthology functional identifier) prediction, but Module-ICA had <1% KO-level precision while capturing process-level co-regulation. [src: discoveries]

**(3) Stress dominates dark-gene phenotypes.** The overall dark-gene analysis reports stress conditions as producing the largest concentration of strong dark-gene phenotypes. [src: functional_dark_matter]

**(3, counterpart) Stress is depleted in the residual truly dark set.** The truly dark analysis reports stress proportions of 28.7% versus 43.2% for annotation-lag genes among strong-phenotype genes, with OR = 0.53 and p < 0.001, while separately reporting stress at 43.3% versus 54.7% in another comparison. [src: truly_dark_genes]

**(4) Openness has no environmental or phylogenetic signal.** One discoveries analysis found no significant relationship between pangenome openness and environment or phylogenetic effects (rho=-0.05, p=0.54 and rho=0.03, p=0.73), where rho is Spearman's rank correlation. [src: discoveries]

**(4, counterpart) Openness tracks variable metabolism strongly.** Another analysis found strong associations between variable metabolic pathways and openness (rho=0.327, p=7.2e-71, and partial rho=0.530, p=2.83e-203 after controlling for genome count). [src: discoveries]

Note on side (4): the tension text does not state how either analysis operationalizes "openness," so no definition is asserted here. Whether the two sides share one is itself part of the disagreement.

## Possible Reconciliations

These are hypotheses, not findings.

*Disagreement 1.* **Hypothesis: the two streams measure different things by construction, so instability is the correct behavior, not a defect.** Conservation is a population-genomic property; fitness is a condition-specific measurement made in one laboratory environment. Retaining 64% of the fitness-active top 50 under conservation-dominant weighting [src: functional_dark_matter] is then a measure of *how much independent information* conservation adds, not of ranking error. **Alternative hypothesis: the instability is an artifact of the essentiality call.** Because the essential-gene definition is an upper bound inflated by short genes, low-complexity regions, and scaffold edges [src: conservation_vs_fitness], part of the rank churn in essential-gene lists may be churn among false essentials rather than among real ones — which would also deflate the median OR of 1.56 toward the null. [src: conservation_vs_fitness]

*Disagreement 2.* **Hypothesis: the precision gap is a resolution mismatch, not a quality gap.** Module-ICA's <1% KO-level precision alongside genuine process-level co-regulation capture [src: discoveries] is consistent with modules being correct at a coarser grain than the KO label used to score them; scoring at pathway or COG-category grain — COG, Clusters of Orthologous Groups, a broad functional class assigned to a gene — could show both methods performing well on their own target. **Hypothesis: the 97.2% neighbor rate carries near-zero information.** If a 75% genome-wide annotation rate makes ≥1 annotated neighbor in a five-gene window nearly certain by chance [src: functional_dark_matter], then neighbor presence is not evidence at all and only neighbor *identity* or operon co-membership (30,190 genes) can be. [src: functional_dark_matter]

*Disagreement 3.* **Hypothesis: the two claims describe nested but different populations, and both are right.** Stress may be prominent among dark genes overall while being less characteristic of the residual genes that resist reannotation. The results should therefore not be collapsed. Note also that the truly dark side itself reports two different stress contrasts — 28.7% versus 43.2% and 43.3% versus 54.7% [src: truly_dark_genes] — so the denominator and condition-binning definitions differ even within one side, and the direction (dark < annotation-lag) is what replicates, not the level.

*Disagreement 4.* **Hypothesis: different predictors, different cohorts.** These results contradict one another at the level of the tested predictors, not necessarily at the level of the prioritization framework; the discrepancy should be retained until feature definitions, species coverage, genome-count control, and analysis cohorts are reconciled. Two specific candidates: the null result may lack power at its species count, and the strong result's genome-count control (partial rho=0.530 versus marginal rho=0.327) [src: discoveries] shows that conditioning materially changes the estimate — so an uncontrolled and a controlled analysis are not comparable tests.

## Resolving Work

*Disagreement 1 — conservation versus fitness as ranking evidence:*
- Recompute the top-50 retention statistic across a dense grid of evidence weights, reporting rank-correlation surfaces rather than a single 64% figure, to separate weight sensitivity from evidence disagreement. [src: functional_dark_matter]
- Recompute the core-genome essentiality OR after excluding genes flagged by the insertion-bias failure modes named in the source (short genes, low-complexity regions, scaffold edges) and report whether the median moves from 1.56. [src: conservation_vs_fitness]
- Repeat the essentiality call under conditions other than the single growth condition represented by RB-TnSeq library construction, and test whether conserved genes gain importance where stress-responsive genes do. [src: conservation_vs_fitness]
- Hold out environmentally-associated genes and test their fitness prediction against the sparse-metadata, genus-level-matching, common-genus, and compositional-coupling confounders the source names. [src: functional_dark_matter]

*Disagreement 2 — context inference versus ortholog transfer:*
- Score Module-ICA and ortholog transfer against the same held-out labels at three grains (KO, pathway, COG category) and report precision/coverage at each; the <1% versus 95.8%/91.2% comparison is only interpretable if the grain is shared. [src: discoveries]
- Compute a null expectation for the five-gene-window neighbor rate under the stated 75% genome-wide annotation rate and report the 97.2% figure as an excess over that null or not at all. [src: functional_dark_matter]
- Experimentally test a matched sample of the 30,190 operon-sharing dark genes and the 6,142 ICA-module dark genes, so that guilt-by-association predictions gain a measured hit rate rather than remaining unvalidated inference. [src: functional_dark_matter]
- Calibrate before combining: on the editorial judgement of [[concepts/experimental-prioritization-of-functional-dark-matter]] — not a claim either source states — these should remain distinct evidence products rather than being folded into one confidence score without calibration. A calibration curve per evidence stream, against a shared validated label set, is the analysis that would license combining them.

*Disagreement 3 — stress prominence:*
- Recompute both stress contrasts (28.7%/43.2% and 43.3%/54.7%) under a single shared condition ontology and a single strong-phenotype threshold, reporting which definitional choice produces the level shift. [src: truly_dark_genes]
- Recompute the overall "stress dominates" claim restricted to the annotation-lag subset alone, to test whether the overall result is carried entirely by genes that modern reannotation resolves. [src: functional_dark_matter, truly_dark_genes]
- Test whether the OR = 0.53 depletion survives stratification by organism, given that the truly dark set is not evenly distributed across organisms. [src: truly_dark_genes]
- Report stress enrichment per condition family (metals, oxidative, osmotic) separately for dark, truly dark, and annotation-lag sets rather than as one aggregated "stress" bin. [src: functional_dark_matter, truly_dark_genes]

*Disagreement 4 — pangenome openness:*
- State the openness operationalization used by each analysis side by side before any further comparison; the tension text gives neither, so the two rho values cannot currently be assumed to estimate the same quantity. [src: discoveries]
- Re-run both openness analyses on one common species cohort with one openness definition, reporting rho with and without genome-count control in both, since controlling moved rho from 0.327 to partial rho=0.530 in the positive analysis. [src: discoveries]
- Publish the feature definitions for "environment" and "variable metabolic pathways" side by side and test whether the null predictors (rho=-0.05, p=0.54; rho=0.03, p=0.73) are simply coarser encodings of the positive one. [src: discoveries]
- Run a power analysis for the null result: report the minimum detectable rho at its species count, so that "no significant relationship" can be distinguished from "underpowered." [src: discoveries]
- Test the metabolic-pathway association for phylogenetic non-independence, since the null analysis explicitly tested phylogenetic effects and the positive one reports only genome-count control. [src: discoveries]
