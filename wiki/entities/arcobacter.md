## Evidence from SSO subsurface ecology

Arcobacter was one of the genera associated with the inferred sulfur-oxidation process category across the nine-well SSO grid. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The inferred sulfur-oxidation signal peaked at well M4 at 1.9%, with Arcobacter and [[entities/thiobacillus]] identified as key genera. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Arcobacter represented 0.54% of the sediment community and 0.00% of the groundwater community in the same-well comparison. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The reported groundwater-to-sediment enrichment ratio for Arcobacter was 0×. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The sediment enrichment of Arcobacter, together with other anaerobic taxa, was interpreted as evidence that sediment-associated communities differ from groundwater communities rather than representing simple detachment into groundwater. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The sulfur-oxidation assignment is an inference from taxonomy-to-trait mapping, not a direct measurement of Arcobacter metabolism in the SSO samples. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
