## Evidence from SSO subsurface ecology

Thiobacillus was one of the genera associated with the inferred sulfur-oxidation process in the SSO subsurface community survey. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Sulfur oxidation peaked at well M4 at 1.9%, with [arcobacter](arcobacter.md) and Thiobacillus identified as the key associated genera. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The sulfur-oxidation assignment was based on taxonomy-to-trait inference from 16S amplicon sequence variant communities rather than direct measurement of Thiobacillus activity or genomic function. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The inferred sulfur-oxidation hotspot contributed to a proposed spatial redox sequence extending from oxidative processes toward denitrification, iron reduction, sulfate reduction, and fermentation; this environmental interpretation remains a hypothesis pending direct SSO geochemistry. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Genus-level functional inference covered 65 annotated genera and 21% of reads, so the reported process abundance should be treated as a lower-bound estimate rather than a complete measure of Thiobacillus-associated sulfur oxidation. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
