## Evidence from the aromatic catabolism network

Succinate was used as a non-aromatic comparison substrate in transferred Complex I fitness data from [[entities/kescience-fitnessbrowser]]. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

Complex I orthologs showed a fitness value of **-1.39** on succinate relative to the comparison conditions, representing one of the largest Complex I defects relative to background among the analyzed conditions. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

The report interprets succinate as a high-NADH-flux substrate because its oxidation through the TCA cycle generates substantial NADH, linking succinate to the [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]] distinction between aromatic dependence and dependence caused by respiratory demand. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

Succinate-associated Complex I defects support the hypothesis that Complex I dependence reflects high NADH flux rather than aromatic catabolism alone. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]
