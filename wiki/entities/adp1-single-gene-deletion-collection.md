# Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 single-gene deletion collection (de Berardinis et al. 2008)

The ADP1 single-gene deletion collection is a published complete collection of single-gene deletion mutants of [[entities/acinetobacter-baylyi-adp1]], described in de Berardinis V et al. (2008), "A complete collection of single-gene deletion mutants of Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1," *Molecular Systems Biology* 4:174 (PMID: 18319726). [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

Known aliases: "de Berardinis et al. (2008) deletion collection", "ADP1 deletion mutant collection", "ADP1 knockout collection". External identifier: PMID 18319726. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Contents and measurements

As used in the deletion-phenotype project, the complete growth matrix covers 2,034 genes with growth ratio measurements on all 8 carbon sources. Growth ratios are mutant/wild-type: values < 1.0 indicate growth defects, while values > 1.0 indicate no defect or a slight apparent growth advantage that the source attributes to experimental normalization rather than necessarily a true advantage. Conditions span a 3.3-fold range in mean growth ratio, from 0.41 for [[entities/urea]] to 1.36 for [[entities/quinate]]. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The triple-essentiality project's original analysis used the collection's deletion-mutant growth rates on 8 carbon sources, listed there as [[entities/glucose]], [[entities/acetate]], [[entities/asparagine]], [[entities/butanediol]], [[entities/glucarate]], [[entities/lactate]], pyruvate and [[entities/succinate]], taken from de Berardinis et al. (2008). [src: adp1_triple_essentiality]

## Tensions

The two projects both describe the collection's growth data as covering 8 carbon sources, but the condition lists they report differ: the deletion-phenotype project names urea and quinate as conditions in its matrix, while the triple-essentiality project's enumerated list of 8 does not include either. The supplied evidence does not establish whether the projects used different subsets of the published conditions or different condition labels; this is recorded here without resolution. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes, adp1_triple_essentiality]
