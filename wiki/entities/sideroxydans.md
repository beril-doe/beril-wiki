# Sideroxydans

## Identity

Sideroxydans is the canonical genus-level name used in this report for an iron-oxidizing taxon detected in SSO subsurface communities. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

No aliases or stable external identifier are provided in the source report. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence from SSO subsurface ecology

Sideroxydans was associated with inferred iron oxidation, which peaked at well U3 at 2.8% of the inferred process abundance. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Sideroxydans was also associated with the U3 nitrification hotspot, where nitrification peaked at 2.3%; this assignment was based on taxonomy-to-trait inference rather than direct measurement of SSO metabolism. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

In the groundwater–sediment comparison, Sideroxydans represented 0.01% of sediment communities and 0.06% of groundwater communities, corresponding to 7.0× enrichment in groundwater. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The report interprets groundwater enrichment of Sideroxydans, together with enrichment of [rhodanobacter](rhodanobacter.md) and [gallionella](gallionella.md), as consistent with a plume-associated planktonic assemblage; this environmental interpretation remains a hypothesis because direct SSO geochemistry was unavailable. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Interpretation and limitations

Sideroxydans-based process assignments were inferred from 16S amplicon taxonomy and literature-linked traits, not from direct genomic or physiological measurements of the SSO populations. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Genus-level functional annotation covered 65 of 1,038 genera and 21% of total reads, so the reported process abundances should be treated as lower-bound estimates. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Metagenomics at matching spatial resolution, loading of the 221 registered SSO geochemistry samples into CORAL, and seasonal 16S profiling are proposed to test whether Sideroxydans distribution tracks the predicted northeast-to-southwest hydrogeochemical gradient. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
