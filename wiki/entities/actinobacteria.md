# Actinobacteria

## Identity

Actinobacteria is a bacterial phylum; the document reports no additional aliases or stable external identifier for this entity. [^harvard_forest_warming]

## Evidence from Harvard Forest warming

In a 25-year +5°C soil-warming experiment at Harvard Forest Barre Woods, read-based relative abundance measured with [kraken2](kraken2.md) increased for Actinobacteria in organic soil from 0.249 in control samples to 0.315 in heated samples. [^harvard_forest_warming]

This change corresponded to a log2 fold change of +0.341 and a false-discovery-rate-adjusted q-value of 0.049. [^harvard_forest_warming]

The result supports enrichment of Actinobacteria in long-term warmed organic soil and forms part of the reported Actinobacteria-up/[acidobacteria](acidobacteria.md)-down community signal. [^harvard_forest_warming]

No phylum-level changes after FDR correction were observed in mineral soil. [^harvard_forest_warming]
