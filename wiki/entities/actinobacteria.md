# Actinobacteria

## Identity

Actinobacteria is a bacterial phylum; the document reports no additional aliases or stable external identifier for this entity. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

## Evidence from Harvard Forest warming

In a 25-year +5°C soil-warming experiment at Harvard Forest Barre Woods, read-based relative abundance measured with [[entities/kraken2]] increased for Actinobacteria in organic soil from 0.249 in control samples to 0.315 in heated samples. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

This change corresponded to a log2 fold change of +0.341 and a false-discovery-rate-adjusted q-value of 0.049. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The result supports enrichment of Actinobacteria in long-term warmed organic soil and forms part of the reported Actinobacteria-up/[[entities/acidobacteria]]-down community signal. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

No phylum-level changes after FDR correction were observed in mineral soil. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
