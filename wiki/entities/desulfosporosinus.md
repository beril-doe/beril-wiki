# Desulfosporosinus

*Desulfosporosinus* is a genus within [[entities/bacillota-b]] that recurs among the deep-clay subsurface lineages analysed in this corpus, alongside BRH-c4a and BRH-c8a; these lineages are described as dominating cultured clay isolates [src: clay_confined_subsurface, discoveries].

Aliases and identifiers: the source reports use only the genus name *Desulfosporosinus*, and no NCBI taxid or other external identifier is given in the assigned evidence [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory, clay_confined_subsurface, discoveries].

## Occurrence in deep-clay cohorts

In the bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory project, the anchor_deep_clay cohort of 10 genomes drew on Mont Terri Opalinus borehole 5 (*Desulfosporosinus*, BRH-c8a×2, BRH-c4a, +1 metagenomic *Desulfosporosinus*), Mont Terri rock-porewater MAGs (metagenome-assembled genomes) 3, and Russian Beyelii Yar borehole 2 (*Ca.* Desulforudis audaxviator and Ch130 Thermacetogeniaceae); see [[entities/mont-terri]] and [[entities/beyelii-yar]] [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory].

In the clay_confined_subsurface project, the anchor_deep genera include exactly the lineages Bagnoud (2016) identified as recurrent across 7 Mont Terri boreholes: *Desulfosporosinus* (×2), BRH-c8a (Peptococcaceae c8a in Bagnoud's nomenclature; ×2), BRH-c4a (Desulfotomaculales), *Lutibacter* and BRH-c54 (Bacteroidota), *Roseovarius* (Rhodobacterales), and *Stenotrophomonas* (the bentonite isolate) [src: clay_confined_subsurface].

The two projects report the *Desulfosporosinus* content of their deep anchor cohorts differently: clay_confined_subsurface lists *Desulfosporosinus* (×2), whereas bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory states that its anchor cohort includes 3 *Desulfosporosinus* genomes; the cohorts are defined separately in each project, and the reports do not reconcile the counts [src: clay_confined_subsurface, bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory].

## Sulfate reduction and hydrogen metabolism

Five of nine anchor_deep genomes in clay_confined_subsurface carry the dissimilatory sulfate reduction module (Sat, AprAB, DsrAB) and seven carry group 1 [NiFe]-hydrogenase markers, including direct hits on the BRH-c8a and *Desulfosporosinus* lineages; see [[entities/dissimilatory-sulfate-reduction]] and [[entities/nife-hydrogenase]] [src: clay_confined_subsurface].

The phylum-background caveat applies to [[entities/wood-ljungdahl-pathway]] and group 1 [NiFe]-hydrogenase, not to sulfate reduction: the Bacillota_B phylum, which contains *Desulfosporosinus*, BRH-c4a and BRH-c8a, carries Wood-Ljungdahl plus group 1 [NiFe]-hydrogenase at high background rates even in soil samples (Bacillota_B baseline mean toolkit 1.65). Within-Bacillota_B comparisons find Wood-Ljungdahl (5/5 vs 15/19, p=0.54) and NiFe-hydrogenase (5/5 vs 14/19, p=0.54) not enriched in deep-clay isolates beyond their phylum congeners, whereas sulfate reduction (5/5 vs 4/19, p_BH=0.04, Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted) survives this phylogenetic control [src: discoveries].

## Sporulation

The bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory project reports that its H1 sporulation-revival hits replicate Vandieken 2017 (PMID 28646634), which characterized Baltic Sea subsurface *Desulfosporosinus* species as spore-forming with broad respiratory versatility; 3 *Desulfosporosinus* genomes in the anchor cohort contributed to the sporulation-OG (orthologous group) signal. This is genomic (gene-content) evidence that agrees with the published characterization; the project does not report direct phenotypic sporulation assays of these genomes [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory].

## Caveats

The reported 5/5 Bacillota_B sulfate-reduction enrichment rests on an "n=5 vs 19" comparison that may hide further granularity: *Desulfosporosinus*, BRH-c8a and BRH-c4a may differ in which non-sulfate-reduction features they carry, so genus-level conclusions for *Desulfosporosinus* remain untested [src: clay_confined_subsurface].
