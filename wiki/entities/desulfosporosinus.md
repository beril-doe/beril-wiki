# Desulfosporosinus

Desulfosporosinus is a genus in the phylum [[entities/bacillota-b]], represented in the deep-clay anchor cohort of two reports, [[summaries/bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory__REPORT]] and [[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]]. Its description as spore-forming rests on one report's interpretation. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory, clay_confined_subsurface]

## Key facts

- The Mont Terri Opalinus borehole contributes 5 anchor genomes: Desulfosporosinus, BRH-c8a×2, BRH-c4a, and one more metagenomic Desulfosporosinus. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- Bacillota_B contains Desulfosporosinus, BRH-c4a and BRH-c8a. These lineages dominate the deep clay anchor cohort. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The Bacillota_B anchor cohort includes 3 Desulfosporosinus genomes that contributed to the sporulation-OG signal. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Indigeneity in Mont Terri Opalinus Clay

The anchor_deep genera include the lineages that Bagnoud (2016) found recurring across 7 Mont Terri boreholes. These include Desulfosporosinus (×2), BRH-c8a (×2) and BRH-c4a. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] The report reads the match of Desulfosporosinus and BRH-c8a (Peptococcaceae c8a) to the three MAGs (metagenome assembled genomes) Bagnoud detected across seven boreholes as cross-platform validation of indigeneity. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] See [[entities/mont-terri-opalinus-clay]] and [[entities/bagnoud-et-al-2016-opalinus-porewater-food-web]].

## Functional content

Seven KBase Data Lakehouse anchor_deep genomes carry group 1 [NiFe]-hydrogenase markers. These include direct hits on the BRH-c8a and Desulfosporosinus lineages. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

The report interprets its sporulation-revival hits as replicating Vandieken 2017's description of Baltic Sea subsurface Desulfosporosinus as spore-forming with broad respiratory versatility. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Caveats

Bacillota_B carries the Wood-Ljungdahl pathway (WL) and [NiFe]-hydrogenase at high background rates even in soil samples. Habitat-specific signals in these lineages are therefore confounded with phylogeny. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] See [[concepts/phylum-level-confounding-of-habitat-specific-anaerobic-gene-content]].

Desulfosporosinus, BRH-c8a and BRH-c4a may differ in the non-SR (sulfate reduction) features they carry. The report notes that a genus-level Bacillota_B pangenome analysis could surface the deep-clay-specific accessory genome. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
