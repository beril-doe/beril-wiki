# Glucarate

## Identity

Glucarate is the canonical name used for this compound in the ADP1 deletion-phenotype analysis. [^adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

Known aliases and stable external identifiers are not reported in the source document. [^adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Evidence from ADP1 deletion phenotypes

Glucarate belongs to the robust condition tier, alongside [glucose](glucose.md) and quinate, in the eight-carbon-source growth matrix for *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1. [^adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The robust-condition group containing glucarate has mean growth ratios ranging from 1.25 to 1.36, and 0.5–2.4% of genes show growth defects in this group. [^adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The analysis identifies gudD, a D-glucarate transporter, and 2,5-dioxovalerate dehydrogenase as condition-specific genes for glucarate utilization. [^adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

These glucarate-associated genes contribute to the report’s finding that carbon-source-specific fitness maps onto distinct metabolic architectures, supporting [condition-specific-fitness](../concepts/condition-specific-fitness.md). [^adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The glucarate result was obtained by integrating deletion-collection growth measurements with TnSeq essentiality classifications, functional annotations, and pangenome status, linking this entity to [multi-omics-integration](../concepts/multi-omics-integration.md). [^adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
