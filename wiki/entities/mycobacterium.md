# Mycobacterium

Mycobacterium is the genus whose single NEON MAG carries the full limonene-to-β-ketoadipate pathway, an already-cultured reference degrader. [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery] See [[summaries/neon_mag_functional_discovery__REPORT]] and [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]].

## Key facts

- The NEON cohort contains 1 FULL-class Mycobacterium MAG, with entry, step2 and late limonene-pathway steps and 5 KEGG orthologs (KOs). [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery]
- Mycobacterium is the already-cultured reference limonene degrader, cited from van Beilen 2005 (PubMed identifier, PMID, 15811996). [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery]
- It is the only NEON MAG with the full canonical limonene → β-ketoadipate pathway. Mycobacterium has been the workhorse for cultivated limonene biocatalysis for about 20 years, so the report finds no lineage novelty there. [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery]

## Accessory gene content

In the NEON H4b analysis, the one soil Mycobacterium MAG had a median MAG-KO count of 1,088. Its median field-only KO count was 71, and its median Jaccard similarity to the genus accessory pangenome was 0.54. [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery] This is a single MAG, so these values are not a genus-level estimate. [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery] For context, see [[concepts/field-only-accessory-gene-content-absent-from-cultured-pangenomes]].

## PHB production

In the phb_granule_ecology report, Mycobacterium was among the top polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB)-high genera by NMDC abundance. The others were Pseudomonas, Cupriavidus, Burkholderia and Methylobacterium, and the report calls all five well-characterized PHB producers. [src: phb_granule_ecology]
