# Open and closed pangenome framework

Tettelin et al. (2005) introduced the open/closed pangenome framework for bacterial genomes. [src: pangenome_openness] McInerney et al. (2017) argued that pangenome structure reflects a balance of selection, drift, and horizontal gene transfer (HGT). [src: pangenome_openness]

## Key facts

- Tettelin et al. (2005) introduced the framework. [src: pangenome_openness]
- McInerney et al. (2017) reviewed the gene-sharing network view of pangenomes. They argued that gene frequency distributions reflect a balance of selection, drift, and HGT. [src: fitness_effects_conservation]

## Limits of the framework

The pangenome_openness report interprets its null result as suggesting that the open/closed distinction describes gene content variability but does not predict its ecological versus phylogenetic drivers. [src: pangenome_openness] This is the report's interpretation.
