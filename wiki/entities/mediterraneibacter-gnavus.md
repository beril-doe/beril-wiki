# Mediterraneibacter gnavus

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Mediterraneibacter gnavus. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

**Known alias:** M. gnavus. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was provided in the document. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

## Evidence from ibd_phage_targeting

Mediterraneibacter gnavus was one of six actionable Tier-A targets, receiving a total prioritization score of 3.8. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

Five of six donor-2708-engraftment pathobionts passed the confound-free CD-up test, including M. gnavus with CLR-Δ = +5.13. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

M. gnavus was detected in 21 of 23 UC Davis Crohn's disease patients (91%). [^ibd_phage_targeting]

M. gnavus was part of the pathobiont module containing four to five of the six actionable targets in each E1_all, E1_CD, E3_all, and E3_CD CLR-Spearman-Louvain subnet. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

All nine E1 UC Davis patients carried the full five-species E1 pathobiont module, which included M. gnavus. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The report classified M. gnavus as temperate-only in the three-layer phage-evidence assessment, making a pure phage cocktail structurally infeasible for the complete E1 target set. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

In the proposed hybrid strategy, M. gnavus required limited or engineered targeting rather than the direct lytic-phage approach proposed for E. coli. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The report identified M. gnavus, [hungatella-hathewayi](hungatella-hathewayi.md), and [eggerthella-lenta](eggerthella-lenta.md) as a universal Tier-1 trio to retain during state-dependent treatment reassessment. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

In patient 6967, M. gnavus increased from 0.53 to 7.45 reads across two visits, corresponding to a 14.0× expansion, while the patient shifted from E1 to E3. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

A five-fold change in M. gnavus abundance was proposed as a trigger for full ecotype retesting, but this qPCR proxy and dosing rule were explicitly identified as hypotheses requiring prospective validation. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

In the two-modality canonical correlation analysis (CCA), which integrates two data modalities through correlated composite axes, all six actionable Tier-A species loaded positively on the dominant axis, CC1, whose canonical correlation was r = 0.964. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The report did not establish in-vivo efficacy for phage targeting of M. gnavus, and it recommended further external database searches and limited or engineered approaches for this coverage gap. [^ibd_phage_targeting]
