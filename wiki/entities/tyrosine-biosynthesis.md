# Tyrosine biosynthesis

This page covers the tyrosine biosynthesis pathway, the positive-correlation outlier and the only pathway without ecosystem differentiation. In the NMDC community metabolic ecology analysis it was the only amino acid pathway without significant ecosystem differentiation. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] In the plant growth promoting (PGP) pangenome analysis it failed as a negative control. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] The evidence is correlational, and the explanations offered are hypotheses.

## Key facts

- Tyrosine was the only amino acid pathway without significant ecosystem differentiation (q = 0.71). [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
- Community completeness of tyrosine biosynthesis versus ambient metabolite intensity in NMDC gave Spearman r = +0.419, p = 0.033, q (Benjamini-Hochberg, BH) = 0.117, n = 26. It was not significant after false discovery rate (FDR) correction. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
- The report called tyrosine an outlier with an anti-BQH direction. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

## Proposed explanations in NMDC

The report hypothesizes that alternative tyrosine sources, such as phenylalanine hydroxylation, complicate the biosynthesis-versus-availability relationship. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] It cautions that the outlier (r = +0.42) may reflect production of tyrosine from phenylalanine by non-biosynthetic hydroxylation. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] The tyr completeness score does not capture phenylalanine biosynthesis. Communities with high phenylalanine biosynthesis completeness may therefore still supply tyrosine, which would decouple biosynthesis from the tyrosine pool. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] See [[summaries/nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology__REPORT]], [[concepts/black-queen-dynamics-in-amino-acid-biosynthesis]] and [[concepts/ecosystem-differentiation-of-community-metabolic-potential]].

## Negative control in the PGP pangenome analysis

Tyrosine-pathway completeness served as a negative control. It also predicted ipdC presence at similar effect size (OR, odds ratio = 3.62, p = 2.3e-11). [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] The H4 tyrosine negative control therefore failed. OR = 3.62 is comparable to the trp OR of 2.81. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] The report treats this failure as mechanistically interpretable, not merely a statistical nuisance. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] See [[summaries/pgp_pangenome_ecology__REPORT]] and [[concepts/aromatic-amino-acid-biosynthesis-and-auxin-pathway-gene-coupling]].
