# Aluminum

Aluminum (Al) is a trivalent metal studied in several metal-fitness projects. It was the most independent metal in the cross-resistance consensus, and the reports link this to a distinct toxicity mechanism. [src: metal_cross_resistance]

## Key facts

- The comprehensive metal ecology report includes aluminum because it has documented bacterial toxicity and dedicated resistance systems. [src: comprehensive_metal_ecology]
- Aluminum has Fitness Browser metal-experiment coverage in 22 organisms. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]
- Aluminum, cobalt and nickel are the three United States Geological Survey (USGS) critical minerals with broad Fitness Browser coverage. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] See [[entities/cobalt]] and [[entities/nickel]].

## Cross-resistance

Aluminum was the most independent metal in the consensus analysis (mean r = 0.34), consistent with its unique trivalent toxicity mechanism. [src: metal_cross_resistance] Al-Co and Al-Ni were the weakest pairs (median r ≈ 0.30). [src: metal_cross_resistance] See universal positivity and chemistry specific magnitude of metal cross resistance. Consensus matrix values:

- Al-Zn: mean Pearson r = 0.44 across 13 organisms, 100% sign consistency. [src: metal_cross_resistance]
- Al-Cu: mean Pearson r = 0.34 across 17 organisms, 94% sign consistency. [src: metal_cross_resistance]
- Al-Ni: mean Pearson r = 0.34 across 20 organisms, 95% sign consistency. [src: metal_cross_resistance]
- Al-Co: mean Pearson r = 0.30 across 20 organisms, 95% sign consistency. [src: metal_cross_resistance]

## Conservation and specificity

In the Pan-Bacterial Metal Fitness Atlas, aluminum (toxic) has 1,381 important genes, core fraction 0.898, delta +0.107, odds ratio (OR) 2.37 and p-value 1.2e-26. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] For aluminum, 752/1,772 metal-important gene records (42.4%) are metal-specific. [src: metal_specificity] See [[concepts/metal-specific-versus-pleiotropic-stress-gene-classification]].

## Salt and counter-ion effects

In the counter-ion analysis, the aluminum core fraction changed from 0.897 to 0.866 against baseline 0.799. The delta weakened from +0.099 to +0.068. [src: counter_ion_effects] The report describes this as a modest decrease after shared-stress correction. [src: counter_ion_effects] Aluminum (chloride) had mean sodium chloride (NaCl) overlap of 46.1% across 12 organisms, with mean maximum Cl⁻ of 8 mM. [src: counter_ion_effects] In DvH, the whole-genome fitness correlation between aluminum and NaCl was r=0.420. [src: counter_ion_effects] See [[concepts/counter-ion-confounding-in-metal-fitness-assays]].

## Niche breadth

In the bacterial global MGnify niche-breadth analysis of metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs), the Al-specific set of 34 KOs has a significant negative association (β = −0.023, Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate (BH-FDR) p = 8.8×10⁻⁶). [src: comprehensive_metal_ecology]
