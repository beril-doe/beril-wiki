# Urease Complex

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Urease Complex  
**Known aliases:** Urease; urease enzyme complex [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]  
**Stable external identifier:** None reported in the source document. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The urease complex is the pathway-level set of genes required for urea utilization in *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Key facts

Urea was the most demanding of the 8 tested carbon sources, with a mean growth ratio of 0.41 and 97.9% of genes showing severe defects at a growth ratio below 0.5. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The urea condition selected ureA, ureB, ureC, ureD, ureE, ureF, and ureG as top condition-specific genes. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

These genes correspond to the urease complex and indicate that urea-dependent growth is concentrated around urease-associated functions in the deletion phenotype data. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The urea condition belongs to the demanding tier, alongside acetate and butanediol, with 95–100% of genes defective at a growth ratio below 0.8. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

In principal component analysis (PCA), a method that summarizes correlated variation into orthogonal components, urea was separated from the other conditions by PC2, which explained 12.7% of the variance and had a urea loading of +0.75. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Interpretation

The urea-associated phenotype supports [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]] by showing that pathway requirements can be highly dependent on the tested growth condition. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

It also refines [[concepts/gene-essentiality]] because the urease genes are highlighted by condition-specific phenotyping rather than treated only as universally essential or non-essential genes. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The complete growth matrix contained 2,034 genes measured across 8 carbon sources, but excluded 499 essential genes and 316 genes with incomplete data, so these urease-associated observations are based on the analyzed deletion-collection subset. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
