# Methylobacterium extorquens

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Methylobacterium extorquens. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Known alias:** *M. extorquens*. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported for this organism in the source document. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Key facts from the lanthanide methylotrophy atlas

*Methylobacterium extorquens* was the dominant carrier of [lanmodulin](lanmodulin.md) among the organisms identified with Bakta-validated Lanmodulin products. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The atlas identified 22 *Methylobacterium extorquens* genomes carrying lanmodulin, with 1 lanmodulin copy in each genome. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The association between *Methylobacterium extorquens*, [lanmodulin](lanmodulin.md), and [xoxf](xoxf.md) contributes to the source report's analysis of clade-restricted lanthanide-dependent methanol oxidation. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The plant microbiome analysis lists *Methylobacterium extorquens* among 47 of 65 plant-associated species with at least 20 genomes that lacked phylogenetic-tree coverage; this **refines** the atlas's genome-marker interpretation by identifying a current limitation on testing within-species plant-association subclades, not a contradiction of its lanmodulin association. [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

These observations are based on genome-marker annotations and do not establish enzyme activity or methylotrophic physiology in every *Methylobacterium extorquens* genome. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
