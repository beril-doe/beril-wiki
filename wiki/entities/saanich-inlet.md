# Saanich Inlet

Saanich Inlet (also written Saanich Inlet, BC) is an oceanographic oxygen-minimum-zone site in British Columbia. In the AlphaEarth genome geolocation data it is a legitimate field site with 1,529 genomes, not an institutional-address artifact. [src: discoveries, env_embedding_explorer]

## Key facts

- Location: 48.36, -123.30, described as an oceanographic O2-minimum zone. [src: env_embedding_explorer]
- Genome count: 1,529 AlphaEarth genomes. [src: discoveries, env_embedding_explorer]
- Status: the discoveries digest lists it as an oceanographic time series among flagged locations that are legitimate research sites. [src: discoveries]

## Why it was flagged

Some AlphaEarth locations were flagged as potential institutional addresses. Several of them turned out to be legitimate field research sites. [src: env_embedding_explorer] Saanich Inlet is one of these. The digest names Rifle, CO (1,883 genomes) and Siberian soda lakes (812) as other examples.
