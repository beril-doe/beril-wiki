# DerSimonian-Laird random-effects meta-analysis

DerSimonian-Laird random-effects meta-analysis is the random-effects meta-analytic estimator used to pool per-organism AMR knockout fitness shifts. In the amr_fitness_cost project it gave a positive pooled shift across 25 organisms. [src: amr_fitness_cost] See [[concepts/fitness-cost-of-antimicrobial-resistance-genes]] for the wider question and [[summaries/amr_fitness_cost__REPORT]] for the project.

## Key facts

- The analysis pooled 25 organisms and gave a pooled effect of +0.086 [95% confidence interval (CI): +0.074, +0.098], z = 14.3, p ~ 0. [src: amr_fitness_cost]
- The central digest describes the same result as covering 801 AMR genes across 25 organisms, with all 25/25 organisms positive. [src: discoveries]
- Heterogeneity was I² = 54.3%. [src: amr_fitness_cost]
- Cochran's Q was 52.54, p = 0.0007. [src: amr_fitness_cost]

## Interpretation

The pooled interval excludes zero, and the heterogeneity statistics show that organisms differ in effect size beyond sampling error. [src: amr_fitness_cost] The positive sign was seen in every organism in the digest's account. [src: discoveries] The digest also reports this result in [[summaries/discoveries]]. [src: discoveries]
