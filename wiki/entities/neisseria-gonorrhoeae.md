# Neisseria gonorrhoeae

*Neisseria gonorrhoeae* (abbreviated *N. gonorrhoeae*) is the organism on which the [[summaries/cog_analysis__REPORT]] project ran its initial analysis. That project studies [[entities/cog-functional-categories]], the Clusters of Orthologous Groups functional classes. [src: cog_analysis]

## Role in the corpus

The project reports that all 8 predictions from the initial *N. gonorrhoeae* analysis were confirmed across 32 species. [src: cog_analysis] The central digest [[summaries/discoveries]] records the same result as a hypothesis validation: all 8 predictions from the initial *N. gonorrhoeae* analysis were confirmed across 32 species. [src: discoveries]

The assigned evidence does not list the 8 predictions or name the 32 species. The finding here is therefore limited to the reported confirmation. It does not establish what each prediction says about *N. gonorrhoeae* biology. [src: cog_analysis, discoveries]
