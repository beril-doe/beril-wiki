# KEScience PubMed

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** KEScience PubMed. [^berdl_data_atlas]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were identified in the source document. [^berdl_data_atlas]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was provided in the source document. [^berdl_data_atlas]

KEScience PubMed is a literature dataset inventoried in the [BERDL Data Atlas](../summaries/berdl_data_atlas__REPORT.md). [^berdl_data_atlas]

## Key facts

The BERDL inventory contains 39,994,988 PubMed article records in the literature reference layer. [^berdl_data_atlas]

The atlas identifies KEScience PubMed as a literature resource for research workflows alongside [KEScience PaperBLAST](kescience-paperblast.md). [^berdl_data_atlas]

The dataset is part of an inventory spanning 1,740 deduplicated tables across 119 databases and 17 tenants. [^berdl_data_atlas]

## Related integration

The atlas lists literature among the cross-tenant linkage domains represented by its 29 canonical join keys. [^berdl_data_atlas]

KEScience PubMed therefore contributes to the BERDL literature layer that can be used alongside genome, protein, annotation, and functional resources such as [UniProt](uniprot.md) and [KBase KE Pangenome](kbase-ke-pangenome.md). [^berdl_data_atlas]
