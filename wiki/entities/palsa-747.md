# PALSA-747 sp.

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** PALSA-747 sp. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Known aliases:** Palsa-747 sp.; PALSA-747. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the document. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

PALSA-747 sp. is an organism included in the metabolic capability–dependency study and its metabolic ecotype analysis. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

## Key facts

PALSA-747 sp. was represented by 51 genomes and analyzed with two metabolic clusters. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

Its metabolic clustering silhouette score was 0.349, the lowest among the 10 target species analyzed, while still exceeding the study's inclusion threshold of 0.2. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The study used at least 50 genomes and at least 15 variable pathways as inclusion criteria for target-species metabolic clustering; PALSA-747 sp. met the genome criterion with 51 genomes. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The document does not report a statistically significant association between PALSA-747 sp. metabolic clusters and isolation environment. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The ecotype analysis was observational, and the study cautioned that environment–cluster relationships could be confounded by phylogenetic structure because explicit phylogenetic correction was not performed. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]
