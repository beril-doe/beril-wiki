# Spirochaeta

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Spirochaeta. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was provided in the source. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Spirochaeta is a bacterial genus included among the 65 annotated genera used for metabolic-guild and biogeochemical-process inference in the SSO subsurface community analysis. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence from SSO subsurface communities

- Fermentation was inferred to peak at well L9 at **5.3%**, with Spirochaeta and [[entities/paenisporosarcina]] identified as the key associated genera. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- The report associated the L9 fermentation hotspot with the lower end of an inferred redox sequence, described as O₂ → NO₃⁻ → Fe(III) → SO₄²⁻ → fermentation. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Spirochaeta was analyzed as part of a genus-level inference dataset covering **65 of 1,038 genera**, or **21%** of genera; the report therefore treats process-abundance estimates as lower bounds. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- The reported fermentation association is a taxonomy-to-trait inference rather than a direct measurement of Spirochaeta metabolism in the SSO samples. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- The functional assignment is also limited by incomplete taxonomy: genus-level classification covered **44%** of sediment reads, species-level classification was approximately **0%**, and **56%** of reads remained outside genus-level inference. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

These observations contribute to [[concepts/subsurface-bacillota-specialization]] through the report's broader interpretation of depth-associated microbial functions along a subsurface redox gradient, and to [[concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content]] through the association of community composition and inferred function with hydrogeological position. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
