# Pleurotus

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Pleurotus. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source document. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Pleurotus is a fungal genus detected in the lignin-enrichment community experiment. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Evidence from lignin community enrichment

Pleurotus reached 50.0% relative abundance in the Round-2 LC-LC condition, in which communities previously exposed to lignin plus labile carbon were passaged again with lignin plus labile carbon. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The Pleurotus emergence in LC-LC was notable but requires confirmation because fungal replicate consistency was poor and the fungal memory effect was not statistically detectable. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The LC-LC fungal group contained 7 ± 2 observed OTUs and had Shannon diversity of 0.49 ± 0.32. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The broader ITS dataset used 893 initial OTUs, with prevalence and abundance filtering retaining 440 ITS OTUs; genus-level assignment was 81.2%. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Interpretation and limitations

Pleurotus provides a candidate taxon for testing whether repeated lignin-plus-labile-carbon exposure selects a reproducible fungal assemblage, but the current observation is preliminary rather than an established ecological-memory finding. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The source attributed the functional interpretation of lignin degradation to taxonomic associations and literature context rather than direct measurement of gene-level pathway enrichment. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Confirmation would require larger replication, deeper ITS sequencing, improved fungal taxonomy, and intermediate passage time points. [^lignin_community_enrichment]
