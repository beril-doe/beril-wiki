Pleurotus is a fungal genus detected in the lignin-enrichment community experiment. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

## Evidence from lignin community enrichment

Pleurotus reached 50.0% relative abundance in the Round-2 LC-LC condition, in which communities previously exposed to lignin plus labile carbon were passaged again with lignin plus labile carbon. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The Pleurotus emergence in LC-LC was notable but requires confirmation because fungal replicate consistency was poor and the fungal memory effect was not statistically detectable. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The LC-LC fungal group contained 7 ± 2 observed OTUs and had Shannon diversity of 0.49 ± 0.32. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The broader ITS dataset used 893 initial OTUs, with prevalence and abundance filtering retaining 440 ITS OTUs; genus-level assignment was 81.2%. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

## Interpretation and limitations

Pleurotus provides a candidate taxon for testing whether repeated lignin-plus-labile-carbon exposure selects a reproducible fungal assemblage, but the current observation is preliminary rather than an established ecological-memory finding. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The source attributed the functional interpretation of lignin degradation to taxonomic associations and literature context rather than direct measurement of gene-level pathway enrichment. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

Confirmation would require larger replication, deeper ITS sequencing, improved fungal taxonomy, and intermediate passage time points. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]
