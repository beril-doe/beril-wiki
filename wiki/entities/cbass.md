# CBASS

## Identity

CBASS is the canonical name used for a bacterial anti-phage defense-system family in this report. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

- Known aliases: none reported in this source. [^phage_defense_arsenal]
- Stable external identifier: none reported in this source. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

## Evidence from the Pan-Bacterial Defense Arsenal

CBASS markers were included among seven anti-phage defense-system families surveyed across the BERDL pangenome. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

CBASS was detected in 7.2% of species-level pangenomes, the lowest prevalence among the seven surveyed defense-system families. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

CBASS prevalence remained below 20% in every reported phylum except Bacteroidota, where it was approximately 10–15%. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

CBASS and [crispr-cas](crispr-cas.md) had the largest prevalence gap among the defense-system pairs, with CBASS at 7% and CRISPR-Cas at 96% in the report’s rounded comparison. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

CRISPR-Cas × CBASS was the only one of 28 defense-system pairs that did not show significant positive co-occurrence after 1,000 phylum-stratified column-permutation tests, with z = 0.21 and empirical p = 0.98. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

CBASS gene clusters had 11.8% core and 61.1% singleton representation in the pangenome analysis. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

The CBASS singleton fraction was enriched relative to the 37.9% singleton background, consistent with accessory acquisition of defense loci through mobile genetic elements. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

## Related Research

This evidence extends [cofitness-network-architecture](../concepts/cofitness-network-architecture.md) by showing that CBASS participates in a broad defense-system co-occurrence framework while lacking significant co-occurrence with CRISPR-Cas. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

The accessory and singleton enrichment of CBASS contributes to [gene-function-acquisition-depth](../concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth.md) by providing evidence that anti-phage defense loci are frequently distributed outside the conserved pangenome. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

CBASS is part of the defense repertoire relevant to the proposed environmental comparisons in [environmental-resistome](../concepts/environmental-resistome.md). [^phage_defense_arsenal]
