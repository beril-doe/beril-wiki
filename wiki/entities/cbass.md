# CBASS

## Identity

CBASS is the canonical name used for a bacterial anti-phage defense-system family in this report. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

- Known aliases: none reported in this source. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]
- Stable external identifier: none reported in this source. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

## Evidence from the Pan-Bacterial Defense Arsenal

CBASS markers were included among seven anti-phage defense-system families surveyed across the BERDL pangenome. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

CBASS was detected in 7.2% of species-level pangenomes, the lowest prevalence among the seven surveyed defense-system families. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

CBASS prevalence remained below 20% in every reported phylum except Bacteroidota, where it was approximately 10–15%. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

CBASS and [[entities/crispr-cas]] had the largest prevalence gap among the defense-system pairs, with CBASS at 7% and CRISPR-Cas at 96% in the report’s rounded comparison. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

CRISPR-Cas × CBASS was the only one of 28 defense-system pairs that did not show significant positive co-occurrence after 1,000 phylum-stratified column-permutation tests, with z = 0.21 and empirical p = 0.98. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

CBASS gene clusters had 11.8% core and 61.1% singleton representation in the pangenome analysis. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The CBASS singleton fraction was enriched relative to the 37.9% singleton background, consistent with accessory acquisition of defense loci through mobile genetic elements. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

This evidence extends [[concepts/cofitness-network-architecture]] by showing that CBASS participates in a broad defense-system co-occurrence framework while lacking significant co-occurrence with CRISPR-Cas. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]
