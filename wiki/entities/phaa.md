# phaA

## Identity

**Canonical name:** phaA. **Known aliases:** none reported in the source document. **Stable external identifier:** none reported in the source document. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

phaA is an upstream gene in the complete polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB) pathway, which the report defines using phaC together with phaA and phaB. [src: phb_granule_ecology] The pathway context is summarized in [[concepts/phb-granule-ecology]] and [[entities/polyhydroxybutyrate-biosynthesis]].

## Evidence from phb_granule_ecology

The pangenome census identified **86,318 phaA gene clusters across 17,969 species**. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

At the species level, **12,869 species** were classified as having precursor genes without the complete PHB pathway, while **6,005 species** were classified as complete and **8,199 species** as absent. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

The report interprets the precursor-only category cautiously because phaA and phaB also participate in general fatty-acid and thiolase metabolism, so the **46.5%** precursor-only proportion likely overestimates partial PHB capability. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

The report links phaA prevalence analysis to pangenome annotations and pathway completeness, with broader integration described in [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] and the source summary [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]]. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Interpretation and limitations

The presence of phaA alone should not be treated as definitive evidence of PHB production because phaA has metabolic roles beyond PHB biosynthesis and the precursor-only category may therefore include species lacking a complete PHB pathway. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

The source reports that the pangenome contains both complete genomes and metagenome-assembled genomes with variable genome quality and gene-detection rates, which may affect phaA detection and pathway classification. [src: phb_granule_ecology]
