# Trehalose

## What this entity is

Trehalose is a compound; the canonical name is **trehalose**. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

No aliases or stable external identifier for trehalose were reported in this document. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

## Evidence from fw300_metabolic_consistency

*Pseudomonas* FW300-N2E3 increased trehalose in its Web of Microbes exometabolome. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

Trehalose had a complete pathway prediction from [[entities/gapmind]], and the organism showed growth on trehalose in [[entities/kescience-fitnessbrowser]] experiments. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

In [[entities/bacdive]], 1 of 6 *Pseudomonas fluorescens* strains utilized trehalose, while 5 did not; this result was assigned moderate confidence. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The report interprets trehalose production combined with low species-level catabolism as potentially reflecting an osmoprotectant role rather than a carbon-source role. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The trehalose result contributes to the report's broader cross-database integration of exometabolomics, gene-fitness measurements, species-level utilization phenotypes, and pathway predictions in [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]]. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The comparison also illustrates the distinction between metabolite production and utilization addressed in [[concepts/metabolic-model-gapfilling]]: production in exometabolomics does not by itself establish growth through trehalose catabolism. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
