# Trehalose

## What this entity is

Trehalose is a compound; the canonical name is **trehalose**. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

No aliases or stable external identifier for trehalose were reported in this document. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

## Evidence from fw300_metabolic_consistency

*Pseudomonas* FW300-N2E3 increased trehalose in its Web of Microbes exometabolome. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

Trehalose had a complete pathway prediction from [gapmind](gapmind.md), and the organism showed growth on trehalose in [kescience-fitnessbrowser](kescience-fitnessbrowser.md) experiments. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

In [bacdive](bacdive.md), 1 of 6 *Pseudomonas fluorescens* strains utilized trehalose, while 5 did not; this result was assigned moderate confidence. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The report interprets trehalose production combined with low species-level catabolism as potentially reflecting an osmoprotectant role rather than a carbon-source role. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The trehalose result contributes to the report's broader cross-database integration of exometabolomics, gene-fitness measurements, species-level utilization phenotypes, and pathway predictions in [multi-omics-integration](../concepts/multi-omics-integration.md). [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The comparison also illustrates the distinction between metabolite production and utilization addressed in [metabolic-model-gapfilling](../concepts/metabolic-model-gapfilling.md): production in exometabolomics does not by itself establish growth through trehalose catabolism. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]
