# ITS amplicon sequencing for fungal-community profiling

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** ITS amplicon sequencing for fungal-community profiling. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Known aliases:** ITS amplicon sequencing; ITS2 amplicon sequencing; fungal-community profiling by ITS sequencing. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Type:** Method. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Stable external identifier:** None was reported in the source document. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

ITS amplicon sequencing profiles fungal communities by sequencing the internal transcribed spacer 2 (ITS2) region; in this study, it was paired with bacterial 16S V3–V4 amplicon sequencing as part of a cross-marker community analysis linked to [multi-omics-integration](../concepts/multi-omics-integration.md). [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Use in the lignin-enrichment study

The study analyzed ITS2 data from 21 samples across 7 groups, with 3 replicates per group, to evaluate fungal-community responses to lignin enrichment, labile-carbon co-supplementation, and sequential passaging. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The 42 paired-end libraries had Q30 values greater than 0.94 for all libraries. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The ITS workflow retained 98.5% of reads after primer trimming, 99.8% after quality filtering, and 99.3% after paired-end merging, for approximately 98% total retention. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The final ITS dataset contained 893 OTUs, and prevalence and abundance filtering retained 440 ITS OTUs. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Per-sample ITS read counts ranged from 6,953 to 200,902 before rarefaction, and genus-level assignment reached 81.2%. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The analysis used 97% OTU clustering, Bray–Curtis distances, PCoA, and PERMANOVA, a permutation-based test of community-composition differences. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Findings generated by the method

The base fungal community contained 253 observed OTUs and had Shannon diversity of 3.50, with Fusarium representing 26.5% of the community. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Lignin-only enrichment reduced the fungal community to 8 observed OTUs and Shannon diversity of 1.37; Fusarium represented 43.4% and Fusicolla 30.3% of that community. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Lignin plus labile carbon yielded 6 observed OTUs and Shannon diversity of 0.62, with Chrysosporium at 61.4% and Aspergillus at 27.2%. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

In Round 2, Malassezia reached 63.4% in L-L and 50.0% in LC-L, while Pleurotus reached 50.0% in LC-LC. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Across all 7 groups, ITS community separation had F=3.02, R²=0.582, and p=0.001. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The Base-versus-Round-1 ITS model had F=6.92, R²=0.698, and p=0.003, while the Round-2 factorial model had F=1.72, R²=0.424, and p=0.011. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Reproducibility and limitations

Fungal replicate consistency was substantially worse than bacterial consistency: within-group Bray–Curtis distances reached 0.99–1.00 for several Round-2 groups, compared with 0.09 for 16S. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The Round-2 ITS history effect was not statistically detectable, with R²=0.142 and p=0.090. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

PERMDISP was significant for ITS at p=0.0001, indicating that differences in dispersion contributed to the PERMANOVA signal. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The ITS analysis used NCBI ITS_RefSeq_Fungi with 19,375 reference sequences rather than UNITE, so environmental taxa may have been missed and genus-level assignments were considered more reliable than finer assignments. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

ITS findings were described as preliminary because replicate consistency was poor, sequencing depth varied from 6,953 to 200,902 reads per sample before rarefaction, some samples had very low diversity, and sample LL_1 was excluded after retaining 74 reads, reducing L-L to n=2. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

A planned Procrustes comparison of 16S and ITS ordinations was not completed because the extreme ITS variability would have made the rotation uninformative. [^lignin_community_enrichment]
