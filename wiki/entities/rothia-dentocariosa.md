# Rothia dentocariosa

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** *Rothia dentocariosa*. [^cf_formulation_design]

**Known aliases:** No aliases are reported in this source. [^cf_formulation_design]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in this source. [^cf_formulation_design]

*Rothia dentocariosa* is a lung-adapted commensal candidate prioritized for competitive exclusion of [pseudomonas-aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) in cystic-fibrosis airways. [^cf_formulation_design]

## Evidence from the formulation study

Among species detected in patient metagenomes, *R. dentocariosa* had an engraftability score of **0.422**, defined as prevalence × log(activity ratio), placing it above the median among inhibition-tested species. [^cf_formulation_design]

In the core-species assessment, *R. dentocariosa* showed **79%** best inhibition, **29** pangenome genomes, **14/18** conserved amino-acid pathways, and **10** lung genomes, corresponding to **38%** of the species' available lung genomes. [^cf_formulation_design]

The recommended two-species clinical candidate combines *R. dentocariosa* with [neisseria-mucosa](neisseria-mucosa.md). This formulation provided **18%** niche coverage, **84%** inhibition, and combined engraftability of **0.820**. [^cf_formulation_design]

The report recommends this two-species formulation as the primary clinical candidate because both organisms are lung-adapted, while the alternative three-species formulation had engraftability of **0.140**. [^cf_formulation_design]

GapMind, a pathway-completeness method, found that *R. dentocariosa* conserved **14/18** amino-acid pathways and **39/41** carbon pathways across its analyzed genomes at the stated conservation threshold of **>95%**. [^cf_formulation_design]

The proposed prebiotic strategy identifies myoinositol as a substrate predicted to support *R. dentocariosa*, but this prediction requires experimental validation before use. [^cf_formulation_design]

## Interpretation and limitations

The evidence supports *R. dentocariosa* as a promising formulation member because its patient prevalence and transcriptional activity indicate comparatively strong inferred engraftability, while its pathway conservation supports species-level robustness. [^cf_formulation_design]

Engraftability was inferred from patient prevalence and transcriptional activity rather than measured after administration, so the clinical performance of *R. dentocariosa* remains to be established in vivo. [^cf_formulation_design]

The formulation evidence was generated primarily with planktonic inhibition assays against PA14, whereas cystic-fibrosis-lung populations of [pseudomonas-aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) primarily occupy structured biofilms. [^cf_formulation_design]
