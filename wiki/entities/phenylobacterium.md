# Phenylobacterium

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Phenylobacterium. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Known alias:** Phenylobacterium sp., the label used for this organism group in the source analysis. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

## Findings in metabolic capability–dependency analysis

The analysis included 51 Phenylobacterium genomes and assigned them to 2 metabolic clusters. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The Phenylobacterium clusters had a silhouette score of 0.544, indicating that the metabolic profiles formed separable clusters under the study's clustering analysis. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

Metabolic cluster membership was significantly associated with isolation environment in Phenylobacterium, with χ² = 12.2, df = 1, and p = 0.0005. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

This environment–cluster association is observational and does not establish that isolation environment caused the metabolic differentiation. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The association may be confounded by phylogenetic structure because the analysis did not apply explicit phylogenetic correction. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The broader study found that the strongest pathway heterogeneity across analyzed metabolic clusters occurred in valine/leucine biosynthesis (0.60), tryptophan biosynthesis (0.51), and lysine/threonine biosynthesis (0.49); the source did not report Phenylobacterium-specific heterogeneity values for these pathways. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]
