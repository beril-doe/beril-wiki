# Oak Ridge Reservation

This page covers the Oak Ridge Reservation, the Oak Ridge metal-contaminated field site used for the lab-to-field comparison. Lab metal tolerance and genus abundance there were correlated in the predicted direction, but the result was suggestive and nonsignificant (Spearman rho=0.503, p=0.095). [src: lab_field_ecology]

## Key facts

- The lab_field_ecology analysis correlated a lab-derived metal tolerance score (negative mean fitness under stress, higher = more tolerant) with the high-uranium/low-uranium field abundance ratio. The result was suggestive but not significant (Spearman rho=0.503, p=0.095, n=12 genera). [src: lab_field_ecology]
- The bacdive_metal_validation report cites the same project as rho=0.50, p=0.095, n=11 genera, using Fitness Browser (FB) lab tolerance. [src: bacdive_metal_validation] The two reports disagree on n (11 versus 12); this page states both and does not reconcile them. [src: bacdive_metal_validation, lab_field_ecology]
- The trend is in the predicted direction: genera with higher lab tolerance tend to have higher abundance at contaminated sites. Power is limited by the small number of genera. [src: lab_field_ecology]
- Only 12 FB genera had enough data for the metal tolerance correlation, which limits statistical power. [src: lab_field_ecology]
- The 108 overlapping samples may not capture the full range of Oak Ridge geochemistry. [src: lab_field_ecology]

## Relation to the BacDive analysis

The bacdive_metal_validation report presents its BacDive analysis as stronger evidence for the same hypothesis at much larger scale (n=10 for heavy metal, n=176 for all contamination, d=+0.43-1.00). [src: bacdive_metal_validation] It attributes the difference in significance to the power gained from BacDive's larger sample size compared with the Oak Ridge site-specific analysis. [src: bacdive_metal_validation] See [[concepts/validating-metal-tolerance-predictions-against-culture-collection-metadata]] and [[concepts/laboratory-fitness-as-a-predictor-of-field-abundance]].

## Proposed follow-up

The bacdive_metal_validation report proposes cross-referencing ENIGMA CORAL community data at the Oak Ridge metal-contaminated site for a complementary field validation. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
