# Photosystem II

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Photosystem II. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

**Known alias:** PSII. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported for this entity in the source document. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Photosystem II is analyzed as a focal function associated with [[entities/cyanobacteriia]] in the Gene Function Ecological Agora atlas. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

## Key findings

The Cyanobacteriia × PSII hypothesis was supported at class rank, with producer Cohen’s d = +1.50 and consumer Cohen’s d = +0.70; the producer p-value was 2×10⁻⁵ and the consumer p-value was 2×10⁻⁴. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The PSII result was rank-specific: genus, family, and order tests were STABLE, with producer d values of +0.08, +0.20, and +0.19, respectively. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The class-level sample contained n = 21 PSII KOs, while the phylum consumer statistic was unavailable because of insufficient reference data. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

PSII gains showed 2.05% ancient events, compared with 14.90% across atlas-wide gains, a pattern consistent with a class-level donor-origin signature but not sufficient to establish donor identity. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The mean leaf_consistency for the Cyanobacteriia × PSII hypothesis was 0.88, compared with an atlas reference of 0.20; leaf_consistency is the fraction of species in a recipient clade carrying a KO. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The PSII-focused gene-neighborhood analysis examined 27,148 focal features and 218,321 neighbor pairs. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

A total of 10.91% of PSII focal features had at least one mobile-genetic-element neighbor within ±5 kb, compared with a Poisson baseline expectation of 10.6%, and the mean mobile-genetic-element-neighbor fraction was 1.65%. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The PSII hypothesis set had an MGE-machinery rate of 0.00%, against an atlas-wide baseline of 1.37% of KO-bearing gene clusters; this indicates that the focal PSII KOs were not themselves predominantly phage, transposase, integrase, plasmid, insertion-sequence, or recombinase products. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The architecture census found a median of 1 architecture per PSII KO, compared with medians of 5 for mycolic-acid KOs, 15 for two-component-system histidine-kinase KOs, and 46 for mixed-category KOs. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The source interpreted the focused architecture census as consistent with an exploratory association between architectural diversity and cross-clade exchange, but it did not establish causation. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

## Environmental and phenotype context

[[entities/cyanobacteriia]] showed 2.77× photic-aquatic enrichment with p < 10⁻⁵², providing environmental consistency for the class-level PSII interpretation rather than evidence that environment causally produced the gene-function pattern. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Among 90 Cyanobacteriia species with AlphaEarth embeddings, 35.6% were assigned to a marine+sponge-dominant cluster in a k = 10 environmental clustering analysis. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Cyanobacteriia phenotype coverage in BacDive was too thin for interpretation, with n = 4. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

## Limitations

The PSII finding should not be generalized across all taxonomic ranks because it was supported at class rank while genus, family, and order results were STABLE. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The analysis did not identify donors at deep ranks because per-CDS sequence data were unavailable in queryable BERDL schemas, and the exploratory tree-based donor inference was not compositionally confirmed or fully reconciled with DTL methods. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The environmental result establishes association or consistency with expected environments, not a causal effect of environment on PSII innovation. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]
