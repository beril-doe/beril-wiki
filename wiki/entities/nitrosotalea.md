# Nitrosotalea

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Nitrosotalea. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

**Known alias:** “Ca. Nitrosotalea” (candidate Nitrosotalea). [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

**Stable external identifier:** Not reported in the source document. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Nitrosotalea was identified as the key genus associated with inferred nitrification at the SSO subsurface site. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence from SSO subsurface communities

Nitrification-associated inference peaked at well U3, where the estimated process contribution was **2.3%**, with Nitrosotalea identified as the key genus. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The report places U3 near the oxidative end of an inferred environmental sequence, but this placement is a hypothesis based on taxonomy-to-trait inference rather than a direct measurement of Nitrosotalea activity or local geochemistry. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Genus-level functional inference covered **65 annotated genera**, representing **21% of total reads**, so the Nitrosotalea-associated nitrification estimate should be treated as a lower-coverage inference. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The source reports that trait scores were consensus estimates based on literature-linked taxonomy rather than empirical measurements of the specific SSO populations. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence gaps

Direct SSO geochemistry was unavailable, so the report could not directly test whether the inferred U3 nitrification hotspot corresponds to measured nitrate, oxygen, pH, or metal gradients. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The report recommends loading the **221** registered SSO geochemistry samples into CORAL and applying metagenomics at matching spatial resolution to test the inferred functional assignments. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
