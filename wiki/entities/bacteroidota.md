# Bacteroidota

## Identity

**Bacteroidota** is the canonical name of a bacterial phylum treated as a focal clade in the Gene Function Ecological Agora atlas. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

- **Known aliases:** None reported in the source. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]
- **Stable external identifier:** None reported in the source. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

## Key facts

The Bacteroidota × polysaccharide-utilization-locus (PUL) carbohydrate-active-enzyme analysis did not satisfy the original absolute-zero Innovator-Exchange criterion at UniRef50 resolution. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

Sankoff-parsimony diagnostics recovered a small relative horizontal-gene-transfer signal for Bacteroidota × PUL carbohydrate-active enzymes, with Cohen’s d = 0.15, so the final synthesis classified the result as a qualified pass or reframed finding rather than support for the original strong criterion. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

The Bacteroidota focal clade showed 1.40× gut/rumen environmental enrichment with p < 10⁻³⁵. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

Among 577 Bacteroidota species in the BacDive phenotype integration, the profile was saccharolytic and glycoside-hydrolase-rich, and anaerobic phenotypes occurred in 33.2% of records compared with 22.0% across the atlas. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

AlphaEarth environmental embeddings with k = 10 clustering placed 729 Bacteroidota species across marine- and gut-associated clusters. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

The Bacteroidota × PUL gene-neighborhood scans were deferred because large-scale Spark and pandas joins exceeded available memory. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

The Bacteroidota PUL hypothesis set had an MGE-machinery rate of 0.00%, indicating that the focal KOs were not identified as predominantly phage, transposase, integrase, plasmid, insertion-sequence, or recombinase products in that analysis. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

The synthesis-stage leaf_consistency value for Bacteroidota × PUL was 0.41, where leaf_consistency is the fraction of species in a recipient clade carrying a KO. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

The lanthanide methylotrophy atlas **refines** this functional profile: Bacteroidota has calls for [xoxf](xoxf.md) (the rare-earth-element-dependent methanol dehydrogenase marker) but zero annotations for [mxaf](mxaf.md), the calcium-dependent methanol dehydrogenase marker. These annotation-based calls do not establish enzyme activity or methylotrophic physiology. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The Bacteroidota result contributes to [ecotype-environment-gene-content](../concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content.md) by linking clade-specific gene-function patterns with environmental enrichment and phenotype consistency. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

It also contributes to [environment-embedding-geography](../concepts/environment-embedding-geography.md) through the AlphaEarth clustering result and to [pangenome-integration](../concepts/pangenome-integration.md) through integration of KO presence, GTDB phylogeny, environmental metadata, and gene-context measurements. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

The new atlas evidence **supports** the pangenome-integration connection by adding GTDB-r214-stratified, cross-genome marker distributions to the existing KO and environmental integration, while **refining** interpretation of gene-function evidence through marker-source calibration and annotation-gap caveats. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
