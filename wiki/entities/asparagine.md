## Key facts from ADP1 deletion phenotyping

Asparagine was one of 8 carbon sources used to measure growth phenotypes in the *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 deletion collection. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

Asparagine belonged to the report's moderate-condition tier, together with lactate; these conditions had mean growth ratios in the range 0.80–0.82 and 37–45% of genes showing defects. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The analysis identified aspartate ammonia-lyase and L-asparaginase among the genes selected as condition-specific for asparagine. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

Growth ratios were defined as mutant-to-wild-type values, with values below 1.0 indicating growth defects and values above 1.0 indicating no defect or a slight growth advantage from experimental normalization. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

These findings connect Asparagine to [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]], which synthesizes how gene fitness depends on the tested growth condition. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
