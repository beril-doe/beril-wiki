## Evidence from metal_specificity

UCP030820 was classified as metal-specific in 2 of 3 organisms across 7 metals, corresponding to 67% specificity. [^metal_specificity]

Its mean sick rate across the analyzed non-metal experiments was 0.021. [^metal_specificity]

The report prioritized UCP030820 as one of the three strongest novel candidate families because it combined 50–67% metal-specificity with lower pleiotropic effects across multiple species. [^metal_specificity]

The report proposes further AlphaFold-based examination of UCP030820 for possible metal-binding sites, but this analysis had not yet been performed. [^metal_specificity]
