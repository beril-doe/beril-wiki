## Key facts

Urobilin was one of 52 differentially abundant metabolites identified from 579 testable named metabolites in HMP2 metabolomics data. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

Urobilin was CD-down, with Cliff's delta = −0.38 and FDR = 0.09. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The urobilin/porphyrin theme showed 100 % sign concordance in strict cross-cohort bridging between HMP2 and FRANZOSA data. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

Urobilin loaded negatively on the dominant taxonomy–metabolomics canonical correlation axis, CC1, which had canonical correlation r = 0.964. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

These findings connect urobilin to the cross-cohort metabolomics integration described in [multi-omics-integration](../concepts/multi-omics-integration.md) and to the source report [ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT](../summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT.md). [^ibd_phage_targeting]
