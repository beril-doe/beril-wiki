# Resistance island

This page covers resistance islands, also called resistance islands in the plural. The amr_strain_variation report detected 1,517 of them across 705 species using phi coefficients and hierarchical clustering. [src: amr_strain_variation] The evidence is one report's survey, repeated in the central discoveries digest. See [[concepts/resistance-islands-and-co-occurrence-of-amr-genes]] for the wider argument and [[summaries/amr_strain_variation__REPORT]] for the source report.

## Key facts

- The islands span 705 species, which is 54% of those analyzed. [src: amr_strain_variation]
- The discoveries digest gives the same count as 705/1,305 species. [src: discoveries]
- Mean island size is 6.2 genes (median 4, max 43). [src: amr_strain_variation]
- The mean phi coefficient is 0.827, which the report calls very tight co-occurrence within each island. [src: amr_strain_variation, discoveries]
- Of the islands, 88% (1,343/1,517) contain genes from multiple resistance mechanisms. [src: amr_strain_variation]
- Efflux pumps (954 islands) and enzymatic inactivation (698) are the most common components. [src: amr_strain_variation]

## Interpretation

The report suggests that the high phi coefficients (mean 0.827) mean these elements are inherited as intact units rather than randomly assembled. This is the report's interpretation, not a direct test. [src: amr_strain_variation]
