## Relevance to the study

*Neisseria meningitidis* was one of four species compared with *Caulobacter crescentus*, *Acinetobacter baumannii*, and *Moraxella catarrhalis* to assess species-specific routes for lipid A loss.[^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The comparison identified 9 capsule-biosynthesis PaperBLAST hits in *N. meningitidis*, representing an alternative route considered in the cross-species analysis.[^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

*N. meningitidis* also had 3 late acyltransferase *lpxX/lpxL* hits in the comparison.[^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The report concluded that *N. meningitidis* lacks the *Caulobacter*-restricted sphingolipid substitute pathway and ChvG–ChvI regulatory circuit among the tested species, while possessing species-specific alternatives associated with lipid A loss.[^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Evidence limitations

The comparative PaperBLAST analysis was vulnerable to naming-convention false negatives, so the reported hit counts should be interpreted as results of that analysis rather than exhaustive pathway inventories.[^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The source recommends deeper Pfam HMM searches against named RefSeq proteomes to detect unannotated paralogs and strengthen cross-species absence claims.[^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
