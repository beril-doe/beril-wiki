# xoxJ

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** xoxJ. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Known aliases:** xoxj. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Stable external identifier:** The atlas operationalizes xoxJ with eggNOG KO K02030, but reports that this KO is non-specific rather than a definitive xoxJ identifier. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

xoxJ is a functional marker evaluated in the [lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas__REPORT](../summaries/lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas__REPORT.md) atlas of lanthanide-dependent methanol oxidation. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Evidence from the lanthanide methylotrophy atlas

In a 134,578-row hit-bearing marker matrix, xoxJ occurred in 41 genomes in both eggNOG and Bakta, 46,369 genomes in eggNOG only, and 20 genomes in Bakta only. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Because eggNOG KO K02030 is non-specific, the atlas preferred Bakta for xoxJ interpretation rather than treating all eggNOG-only calls as definitive xoxJ detections. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The source disagreement for xoxJ illustrates the broader annotation-calibration problem discussed in [gene-function-acquisition-depth](../concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth.md): marker presence depends strongly on the annotation source and marker-specific validation. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The xoxJ results were generated as part of a 293,059-genome pangenome atlas stratified with GTDB-r214 taxonomy and therefore contribute to the cross-genome functional-marker integration described in [pangenome-integration](../concepts/pangenome-integration.md). [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Interpretation and limitations

xoxJ calls should not be interpreted alone as proof of lanthanide-dependent methanol oxidation because the eggNOG K02030 assignment is non-specific and the study did not perform sequence-level screening for pseudogenes, truncated open reading frames, or assembly fragmentation. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The atlas recommends using Bakta product evidence preferentially for xoxJ in future BERDL analyses. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
