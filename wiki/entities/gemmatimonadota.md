# Gemmatimonadota

## What this entity is

Gemmatimonadota is a bacterial phylum represented in the lanthanide-dependent methanol oxidation atlas. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

No aliases or stable external identifier were specified in the source report. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Key facts from the atlas

Gemmatimonadota contained 386 genomes in the analyzed collection. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Among these genomes, 98 carried xoxF and 1 carried mxaF. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The resulting xoxF rate was 25.4%, and the xoxF:mxaF ratio was 98:1. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The xoxF-versus-mxaF directional test remained significant after correction, with p_BH = 1.5 × 10⁻²⁷. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

These findings place Gemmatimonadota among the high-rate carriers of xoxF in the atlas, extending the observed distribution of this marker beyond canonical methylotroph lineages; marker detection alone does not establish enzyme activity or methylotrophic physiology. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
