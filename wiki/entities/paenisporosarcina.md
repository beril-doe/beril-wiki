Paenisporosarcina is a genus included in the SSO subsurface 16S amplicon community analysis. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence from SSO subsurface communities

Paenisporosarcina was identified with [[entities/spirochaeta]] as a key genus associated with the inferred fermentation guild at well L9, where fermentation reached 5.3%. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The fermentation assignment was inferred from taxonomy rather than directly measured from genomes, transcripts, metabolites, or geochemistry. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The report assigned 65 annotated genera across 11 metabolic guilds, but genus-level functional annotation covered only 21% of total reads; therefore, the Paenisporosarcina-associated fermentation estimate should be treated as an inference with incomplete read-level coverage. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

This observation contributes to the report's hypothesis that spatially varying hydrogeological and redox conditions structure SSO microbial communities, including a possible fermentation-enriched zone toward L9. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
