# Siberian soda lakes

Siberian soda lakes are extremophile sampling locations in Siberia. [src: discoveries, env_embedding_explorer] In the AlphaEarth genome geolocation data they are a legitimate field site. [src: discoveries] The evidence is two brief mentions, so it is thin and descriptive.

## Key facts

- The lakes are listed at coordinates 52.11, 79.17, with 812 AlphaEarth genomes from extremophile sampling campaigns. [src: env_embedding_explorer]
- The cross-project digest names them among flagged locations that are legitimate research field sites, with 812 genomes from extremophile campaigns. [src: discoveries]

## Relevance

The digest flags some locations as possible artifacts and treats the soda lakes as a genuine site. Rifle, CO (1,883 genomes) and Saanich Inlet (1,529) are the other legitimate sites it names. [src: discoveries] For the artifact problem, see [[concepts/coordinate-quality-and-institutional-address-artifacts-in-genome-geolocation]].
