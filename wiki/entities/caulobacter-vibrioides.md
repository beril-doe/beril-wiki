# Caulobacter vibrioides

## Identity

**Canonical name:** *Caulobacter vibrioides* [^essential_metabolome]

**Known alias:** Caulo [^essential_metabolome]

**Stable external identifier reported:** genome accession GCF_000022005.1 [^essential_metabolome]

## Evidence from Essential Metabolome

*Caulobacter vibrioides* was one of 7 organisms successfully mapped for the [essential_metabolome__REPORT](../summaries/essential_metabolome__REPORT.md) pilot analysis using [gapmind](gapmind.md), a computational pathway-completeness method. [^essential_metabolome]

It had complete predictions for 18 of 18 amino-acid biosynthesis pathways, corresponding to 100% pathway completeness in the analysis. [^essential_metabolome]

The 18 amino-acid biosynthesis pathways assessed were arg, asn, chorismate, cys, gln, gly, his, ile, leu, lys, met, phe, pro, thr, trp, tyr, and val. [^essential_metabolome]

The mapped *C. vibrioides* genome had 694 GapMind predictions. [^essential_metabolome]

The pilot reported fumarate, succinate, acetate, propionate, L-lactate, all amino acids, deoxyribose, deoxyribonate, and putrescine as conserved carbon sources across all 7 analyzed organisms, including *C. vibrioides*. [^essential_metabolome]

## Interpretation and Limitations

The result contributes to the report's observation that 17 of 18 amino-acid biosynthesis pathways were complete in all 7 analyzed organisms, while 6 of 7 organisms had all 18 pathways. [^essential_metabolome]

This observation is limited to a 7-organism pilot and cannot be generalized to bacteria as a whole because the sample had limited phylogenetic diversity and consisted mostly of Proteobacteria plus one Deltaproteobacterium. [^essential_metabolome]

GapMind pathway calls are computational and may miss non-canonical pathways, divergent enzymes below homology thresholds, or genes absent from genome annotations. [^essential_metabolome]
