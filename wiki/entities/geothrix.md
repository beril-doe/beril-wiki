# Geothrix

Geothrix is a bacterial genus of iron reducers. In this corpus it appears with [[entities/geobacter]] as a dominant member of rock-attached communities in Opalinus clay, as the cited literature describes [src: discoveries, clay_confined_subsurface]. The assigned evidence gives no alias or external identifier (such as an NCBI taxid), so none is recorded here.

## Mont Terri Opalinus clay

At the [[entities/mont-terri]] Opalinus clay site, the Bagnoud (2016) porewater paradigm and the Mitzscherling (2023) rock-attached paradigm describe qualitatively different microbial communities at the same site. Sulfate reducers dominate the porewater, whereas Geobacter/Geothrix iron reducers dominate rock surfaces. This contrast is recorded as a discovery that rests on the two cited literature studies, not on a new measurement made in this corpus [src: discoveries].

The cited Mitzscherling et al. (2023) rock-attached community profile reports sulfate-reducing bacteria (SRB) at <0.2% and iron-reducing bacteria (IRB) at 4.3–10.2%, dominated by *Geobacter* and *Geothrix*. The clay-confined subsurface project uses this profile as a literature comparator. It is not a corrected iron-marker result for that project's cultured cohort [src: clay_confined_subsurface].

## Representation in the cultured cohort

In the clay-confined subsurface project, rock-attached *Geobacter* / *Geothrix* lineages are essentially absent from the analysed cohort, as are CPR / DPANN episymbionts (Bell 2022). The report therefore says that work augmented with MAGs (metagenome-assembled genomes) is needed to test whether the genuine clay-confined community matches the literature predictions. Until that work is done, the claim that Geothrix is prominent in clay-confined communities rests on the cited literature, because the lineage is essentially absent from this project's analysed cohort [src: clay_confined_subsurface].
