# Malate synthase gene (aceB/glcB)

## What this entity is

The canonical name is the malate synthase gene, also known as **aceB** or **glcB**, and it is associated with the glyoxylate cycle. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The stable functional identifier reported for this entity is KEGG Orthology **K01638**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

## Evidence from Harvard Forest warming

In the 25-year +5°C Harvard Forest soil-warming experiment, aceB/glcB (K01638) increased in heated mineral-soil RNA, with a log2 fold change of **+0.268** and **p=0.037**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

This signal was observed alongside an increase in the related isocitrate lyase gene [[entities/acea-icl]] (aceA/icl; K01637), which had a log2 fold change of **+0.460** and **p=0.037** in heated mineral-soil RNA. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The aceB/glcB result was a nominal gene-level RNA signal and was not reported as surviving false-discovery-rate correction across 14K KOs. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The study used contig-annotation counts for metatranscriptome KOs rather than TPM-quantified expression, so the result represents transcript-pool composition and may be affected by assembly quality. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
