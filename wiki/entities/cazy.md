# CAZy

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** CAZy. In this report, CAZy denotes the classification of carbohydrate-active enzyme families used to analyze gene neighborhoods and horizontal gene transfer (HGT). [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

**Known aliases:** No aliases are reported in this source. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in this source. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Key facts from the source

A total of 92 CAZy families showed elevated co-occurrence with type IV secretion system (T4SS) loci at distances of ≤10 kb, although threshold validation remains pending. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

[[entities/gt2-glycosyltransferases]] was the top CAZy hit, occurring in 767 genomes with an average gene length of 5,041 bp. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

T4SS–CAZy associations were enriched in marine sediment (OR=5.5, q<10⁻⁹⁸), barley rhizosphere (OR=10.4), and maize rhizosphere (OR=4.1). [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

The GT2 gene tree contained 77 detected HGT events, including 32 normalized high-confidence cross-phylum events; the strongest event, Node_4915, spanned 8 phyla with a maximum divergence of 4.843. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

In 376 genomes with parsed GT2 neighborhood regions, T4SS occurred in 503 neighborhood entries and GT2 occurred in 495 entries, confirming contig-level syntenic co-localization. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

[[entities/gh23]], a murein lytic transglycosylase family, was the second most common CAZy family in GT2 neighborhoods, with 106 occurrences. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

GT2-neighborhood MAGs had a mean of 0.045 metal-resistance types, compared with 0.004 for non-GT2 MAGs (n=260,276; Mann–Whitney p=8.6e-27), and genomes with GT2 in T4SS-proximal neighborhoods carried 11× more metal-resistance genes. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

CAZy genes were not detected on plasmids by ICEfinder, while 12 integrative mobilizable elements occurred among the top 100 accumulators. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

The report interprets T4SS co-localization with GT2 cassettes and GT2 phylogenetic incongruence as positive evidence for cross-phylum HGT, but emphasizes that the associations are observational and require experimental validation for mechanistic confirmation. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
