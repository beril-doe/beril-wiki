# Mesorhizobium

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Mesorhizobium. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in this document. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in this document. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

Mesorhizobium is a rhizobial genus associated with plant roots and linked to the [[concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content|ecotype–environment–gene-content relationship]]. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

## Evidence from plant microbiome ecotypes

Mesorhizobium was among the genera dominating root-associated communities, alongside Rhizobium and Bradyrhizobium. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

The plant-associated genome census identified 292 root-associated species and 2,446 root-associated genomes across the dataset. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

Within the within-species phylogenetic analysis, Mesorhizobium sp002294985 was one of 5 of 17 testable plant-associated species that passed the Bonferroni-corrected Fisher test, with p = 1.1×10⁻⁴. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

The broader analysis found significant plant-association segregation in 5 of 17 testable species, but only 18 of 65 candidate species had any phylogenetic-tree distance data; therefore, the prevalence of host-linked subclade structure across Mesorhizobium and other plant-associated species remains uncertain. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

Mesorhizobium ciceri and Mesorhizobium medicae were among the 47 of 65 candidate species lacking phylogenetic-tree coverage, limiting comparative inference for these species. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

These findings support linking Mesorhizobium to [[concepts/environment-embedding-geography|environmental embedding and geography]], [[concepts/pangenome-integration|pangenome integration]], and [[concepts/multi-omics-integration|multi-omics integration]]. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]
