# Mont Terri Opalinus Clay

This page covers Mont Terri Opalinus Clay. Mont Terri boreholes and rock porewater supplied genomes to the deep-clay anchor cohort of [[summaries/bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory__REPORT]] [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory], and Opalinus boreholes supplied 8 of the 9 genomes in the clay-confined cohort of [[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]]. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Key facts

- The deep-clay [[entities/bacillota-b]] anchor cohort has 10 genomes. They come from the Mont Terri Opalinus borehole, Mont Terri rock-porewater MAGs (metagenome assembled genomes) and the Russian Beyelii Yar borehole. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The Opalinus borehole contributes 5 of those anchor genomes: Desulfosporosinus, BRH-c8a×2, BRH-c4a and one metagenomic Desulfosporosinus. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] See [[entities/desulfosporosinus]].
- Mont Terri rock-porewater MAGs contribute 3 genomes to the same cohort. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Clay-confined cohort

A separate KBase Data Lakehouse clay-confined deep-subsurface cohort has 9 genomes. Eight come from Mont Terri Opalinus boreholes and 1 from a bentonite formation. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] The cohort is described in [[entities/clay-confined-cultured-genome-cohort]].

The anchor_deep cohort in the clay-confined report went from 9 to 6 genomes after quality filtering. Before filtering, its sources were the Mont Terri Opalinus borehole (BRC-3 and BIC-A1) with 8 genomes and the bentonite formation with 1. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Borehole origin and cultivation bias

All 8 Opalinus genomes in the Data Lakehouse come from the BRC-3 or BIC-A1 boreholes. The Desulfosporosinus and BRH-c8a (Peptococcaceae c8a) lineages match three MAGs that Bagnoud detected across seven boreholes. The report presents this as cross-platform validation of indigeneity. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

The report reads this borehole origin as a direct, quantitative diagnostic of cultivation bias. In its words, the Data Lakehouse captures the porewater fraction, not the rock-attached fraction. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] This is the report's interpretation. See [[concepts/cultivation-bias-toward-porewater-versus-rock-attached-subsurface-communities]] and [[concepts/porewater-bias-as-a-general-signature-of-cultivation-based-subsurface-collections]].

## Proposed work

The report proposes an exploratory comparison within Mont Terri, BRC-3 porewater versus BIC-A1 borehole, with 5 + 3 genomes per borehole. It would test whether the Bagnoud-paradigm sulfate-reduction-rich pattern is borehole-specific or site-wide. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] With so few genomes, see [[concepts/small-cohort-and-phylogenetic-confounding-in-lineage-level-comparisons]].
