# Streptococcus pseudopneumoniae

## What this entity is

**Streptococcus pseudopneumoniae** is the canonical organism name used in the report; no aliases or stable external identifier were reported. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Key facts from the document

- *Streptococcus pseudopneumoniae* was one of 12 species with valid gene-content ecotypes in the study’s 15-species sample. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The dataset contained 127 genomes for this species, of which 118 were assigned to ecotypes. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- Clustering identified 4 ecotypes for *Streptococcus pseudopneumoniae*. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- Its mean silhouette score was 0.131, among the weakest separation signals reported across the sampled species. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- Ecotypes were identified using principal component analysis (PCA), a dimensionality-reduction method, followed by KMeans clustering; KMeans searches for a selected number of groups based on cluster separation. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The study evaluated whether ecotype gene-content differences were associated with functional differences using COG (Clusters of Orthologous Groups) profiles. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- Across the 12 species with valid clusters, all 12 had at least one significantly differentiated COG category after Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction at q < 0.05. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The report does not provide a species-specific list of significantly differentiated COG categories for *Streptococcus pseudopneumoniae*. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
