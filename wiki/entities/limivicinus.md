## Key facts

*Limivicinus* sp. was one of 10 target species examined for within-species metabolic ecotypes, with each target required to have at least 50 genomes and at least 15 variable pathways. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The analysis included 50 *Limivicinus* genomes and identified 2 metabolic clusters, with a silhouette score of 0.528. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The silhouette score indicates a positive degree of separation among the metabolic clusters under the study's clustering analysis, although the source does not establish that the clusters represent causal ecological adaptations. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

These results contribute to the cross-project topic of metabolic profiles, environment, and gene content in [[concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content]]. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]
