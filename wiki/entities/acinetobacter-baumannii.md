## Key facts from caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss

* *A. baumannii* was used as a comparator species in an analysis of alternative routes associated with lipid A loss in [[summaries/caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss__REPORT]]. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
* NCBI annotation classified *spt* and *cerR* as Caulobacter-unique across *C. crescentus*, *A. baumannii*, *N. meningitidis*, and *M. catarrhalis*, with pattern 1000; therefore, the comparator lacked the Caulobacter sphingolipid-substitute genes tested in this analysis. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
* ChvG and ChvI also showed pattern 1000 across the tested species, supporting the absence of the Caulobacter ChvG–ChvI regulatory circuit in *A. baumannii*. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
* The comparative analysis identified PBP1A and Ld-transpeptidases as alternative lipid A-loss-associated routes in *A. baumannii*, in contrast to the sphingolipid substitution route associated with Caulobacter and [[entities/lpxc]] loss. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
* *A. baumannii* had 8 late-acyltransferase *lpxX/lpxL* hits in the comparative analysis. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
* The report treats these species-specific alternatives as comparative annotation findings rather than direct experimental demonstrations that each route rescues lipid A loss in *A. baumannii*. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Evidence limitations

* The comparative PaperBLAST analysis was vulnerable to naming-convention false negatives, so the cross-species absence and presence claims were strengthened by NCBI annotation and literature rather than by PaperBLAST alone. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
* The report recommends a deeper Pfam HMM search against named RefSeq proteomes to detect unannotated paralogs in the comparative species. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
