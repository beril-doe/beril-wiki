# Pseudomonas stutzeri RCH2

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** *Pseudomonas stutzeri* RCH2. [^costly_dispensable_genes]

**Known alias:** psRCH2. [^costly_dispensable_genes]

No stable external identifier for this strain is provided in the source document. [^costly_dispensable_genes]

## Key facts

*Pseudomonas stutzeri* RCH2 is an outlier in the cross-organism analysis of genes that are both costly in laboratory fitness measurements and dispensable in the pangenome. [^costly_dispensable_genes] It contributes **21.5%** of its genes to the costly+dispensable category, compared with **14.0%** for the next organism, [bacteroides-thetaiotaomicron](bacteroides-thetaiotaomicron.md). [^costly_dispensable_genes]

The report interprets this unusually high proportion as potentially reflecting a recent mobile-element invasion or strain-specific genomic expansion, but the cause remains unresolved. [^costly_dispensable_genes] This strain-level outlier contributes to the broader [pangenome-integration](../concepts/pangenome-integration.md) finding that laboratory fitness burden and pangenome conservation can identify evolutionarily unstable gene sets. [^costly_dispensable_genes] It also informs [gene-essentiality](../concepts/gene-essentiality.md) by illustrating that costly genes need not be conserved across the pangenome. [^costly_dispensable_genes]

The new functional-dark-matter analysis **refines** this outlier context by including *P. stutzeri* among organisms with **45** GapMind-gapped pathways and by selecting a *P. stutzeri* RCH2 stress experiment in its ten-experiment campaign. [^functional_dark_matter] These results support further condition-specific investigation but do not establish that the strain’s costly+dispensable genes cause the pathway gaps. [^functional_dark_matter]

The pathway-capability analysis further **supports** condition-specific investigation by listing *P. stutzeri* RCH2 among the seven Tier 1 organisms used to compare GapMind pathway completeness with Fitness Browser fitness data; only 7 of 48 Fitness Browser organisms had matching GapMind genome data. [^pathway_capability_dependency] This inclusion establishes comparative data coverage for the strain, but does not attribute any particular Active Dependency, Latent Capability, Incomplete but Important, or Missing classification to RCH2 in the report. [^pathway_capability_dependency]

The source cautions that the **21.5%** proportion may reflect strain-specific genomic features rather than a general pattern. [^costly_dispensable_genes]
