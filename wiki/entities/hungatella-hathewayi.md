# Hungatella hathewayi

## Identity

Hungatella hathewayi is the canonical organism name used in this report. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

No aliases or stable external identifier were reported for this organism in the source document. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

## Evidence from ibd_phage_targeting

Hungatella hathewayi was one of six actionable Tier-A pathobiont targets, with a total prioritization score of 4.0. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

In the donor-2708 engraftment analysis, Hungatella hathewayi passed the confound-free Crohn's disease-up test with CLR-Δ = +0.92. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

Species-level pathway analysis associated Hungatella hathewayi with purine/pyrimidine recycling (OR = 4.86, FDR = 0.048) and TMA/choline metabolism (OR = 9.33, FDR = 0.048). [^ibd_phage_targeting]

Hungatella hathewayi was detected in 19 of 23 UC Davis Crohn's disease patients (83 %). [^ibd_phage_targeting]

All 9 patients assigned to the E1 ecotype carried the full five-species E1 pathobiont module, which included Hungatella hathewayi. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The report identified Hungatella hathewayi as a phage-coverage gap, so direct phage targeting was not supported by the available evidence. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The proposed hybrid-cocktail framework therefore places Hungatella hathewayi among targets requiring a non-phage alternative or an external phage-database search, alongside the phage evidence summarized in [phagefoundry](phagefoundry.md). [^ibd_phage_targeting]

In the longitudinal profile of patient 6967, Hungatella hathewayi increased 1.3-fold between visits and was retained in the cocktail profile at both visits. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The report proposes retaining Hungatella hathewayi in the universal Tier-1 target trio during state-dependent reassessment, but this workflow requires prospective validation. [^ibd_phage_targeting]
