# NMDS

## What it is

NMDS (non-metric multidimensional scaling) is an ordination method used to represent ecological community dissimilarities in a lower-dimensional space. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

- **Canonical name:** Non-metric multidimensional scaling
- **Known alias:** NMDS

## Use in SSO subsurface community ecology

NMDS was applied to the SSO sediment community data, which comprised 23,458 amplicon sequence variants across 37 sediment core samples aggregated per well from 9 wells. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The NMDS ordination had stress = 0.067. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The analysis was used alongside Bray–Curtis dissimilarity, Mantel testing, and Procrustes correspondence to evaluate spatial structure across the approximately 6 m SSO well grid. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The NMDS results contributed to the interpretation that SSO microbial communities exhibit meter-scale spatial structure associated with hydrogeological organization and a hypothesized northeast-to-southwest contamination-plume corridor, although the plume explanation was not directly validated by geochemistry. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
