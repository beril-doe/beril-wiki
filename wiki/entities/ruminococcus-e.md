# Ruminococcus E sp.

## What this entity is

**Ruminococcus E sp.** is the canonical organism name used in the report for a Ruminococcus species-level group included in metabolic capability and ecotype analysis. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Known alias:** *Ruminococcus* E sp. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported for this organism in the document. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

## Key facts from the document

Ruminococcus E sp. was one of 10 target species analyzed for metabolic clustering, with selection requiring at least 50 genomes and at least 15 variable pathways. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The analysis included 53 Ruminococcus E genomes, assigned 2 metabolic clusters, and obtained a silhouette score of 0.609. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The silhouette score indicates that the metabolic clustering result exceeded the study's inclusion criterion of 0.2 for target species. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The report did not identify a statistically significant or non-significant association between Ruminococcus E metabolic clusters and isolation environment. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The Ruminococcus E result contributes to the report's broader observation that all 10 analyzed target species formed metabolic clusters, while environment-cluster associations were detected only for *Salmonella enterica* and *Phenylobacterium* sp. among the species for which those associations were tested. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

## Interpretation and limitations

The Ruminococcus E clustering result is observational and does not establish that environmental conditions caused the metabolic grouping. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The report notes that metabolic ecotype interpretations may be confounded by phylogenetic structure because explicit phylogenetic correction was not performed. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]
