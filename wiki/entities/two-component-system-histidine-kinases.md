# Two-component-system histidine kinases

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Two-component-system histidine kinases. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

**Known aliases:** TCS-HK; two-component system histidine kinases. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was supplied in the source document. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Two-component-system histidine kinases are treated in the Gene Function Ecological Agora atlas as a function class for comparing phylogenetic acquisition depth, producer–participation behavior, and protein-architecture diversity across clades. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

## Evidence from Gene Function Ecological Agora

TCS histidine kinases showed 45.1% recent and 4.4% ancient Sankoff-parsimony gains, producing a recent-to-ancient ratio of 10.3×. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The atlas assigned 17,073,194 Sankoff gain events to recipient-rank depth bins, and TCS histidine kinases were one of the function classes used to compare recent-to-ancient acquisition signatures. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The Alm 2006 architectural analysis retained qualitative support for a relationship between consumer-side KO-to-architecture concordance and function, with a concordance of r = 0.673; TCS histidine kinases were included among the function classes informing this architecture-oriented analysis. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The architecture census reported a median of 15 architectures per TCS histidine-kinase KO. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The project’s exploratory interpretation associates greater architectural diversity with cross-clade exchange, but the focused architecture census does not establish causation. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

## Interpretation and limitations

The 10.3× recent-to-ancient ratio is an acquisition-depth signature, not proof that all TCS histidine-kinase gains were horizontally transferred or that a particular donor lineage was identified. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Deep-rank donors were not identified because per-CDS sequence data were unavailable in queryable BERDL schemas, and the project’s tree-based donor inference was exploratory rather than a definitive donor reconstruction. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The acquisition-depth result contributes to [[concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth]], while the architecture result relates to [[concepts/cofitness-network-architecture]] and the broader cross-source synthesis in [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]]. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]
