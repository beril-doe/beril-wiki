# Candidatus Desulforudis audaxviator

Candidatus Desulforudis audaxviator (also written Desulforudis audaxviator) is a subsurface sulfate-reducing bacterium in [[entities/bacillota-b]]. Its genome, GB_GCA_020725505.1, is in the deep-clay anchor cohort of the Bacillota_B subsurface report. Literature cited by two reports treats it as the exemplar of a self-sufficient deep-subsurface genome. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory, clay_confined_subsurface]

## Key facts

- Genome GB_GCA_020725505.1 belongs to the deep-clay Bacillota_B anchor cohort. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The Russian Beyelii Yar borehole contributes 2 anchor genomes: Ca. Desulforudis audaxviator and a Ch130 Thermacetogeniaceae genome. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Self-sufficiency

Becraft et al. (2021) described the genome as suggesting complete self-sufficiency. It contains all genes necessary for carbon and nitrogen fixation and encodes all necessary amino acid biosynthesis pathways. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] The report reads this as a genome that keeps full N-fixation, amino-acid biosynthesis and C-fixation without streamlining. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] The report finds Bacillota_B genome expansion consistent with Beaver & Neufeld (2024)'s self-sufficiency hypothesis. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] Beaver & Neufeld's synthesis proposes biosynthetic self-sufficiency as the canonical adaptation of deep-subsurface life, with this organism as the example. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] See [[concepts/biosynthetic-self-sufficiency-as-a-deep-subsurface-adaptation]] and [[concepts/genome-streamlining-and-genome-size-in-ecological-specialists]].

## Limits for cultured cohorts

The clay-confined report did not find self-sufficiency in its own data. It interprets this as an absence of extreme self-sufficient lineages from the KBase Data Lakehouse cultured cohort. Such lineages are characteristically uncultivated, recovered as metagenome assembled genomes (MAGs) or single-cell genomes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] The report concludes that the archetype is real for uncultivated MAG/SAG-recovered lineages like this one but does not propagate to the cultivable cohort the Lakehouse captures. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] This is the report's interpretation. See [[summaries/bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory__REPORT]] and [[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]].
