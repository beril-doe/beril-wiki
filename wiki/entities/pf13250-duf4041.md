# PF13250/DUF4041

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** PF13250/DUF4041, the SNIPE-associated domain marker. [src: snipe_defense_system]

**Known aliases:** DUF4041; T5orf172; SNIPE-associated domain. [src: snipe_defense_system]

**Stable external identifiers:** Pfam PF13250; InterPro IPR025280. [src: snipe_defense_system]

PF13250/DUF4041 is a protein-domain marker associated with the [[entities/snipe-defense-system]] phage-defense system and is distinct from the SNIPE nuclease-family marker [[entities/pf13455-mug113]]. [src: snipe_defense_system]

## Key facts

- The corrected domain architecture places PF13250 at positions 232–333 in the 558-amino-acid *Escherichia coli* SNIPE protein A0A0A1A5Z2, while PF13455 occurs at positions 443–520. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- PF13250/DUF4041 is the correct Pfam assignment for DUF4041, also called the SNIPE-associated domain and associated with InterPro IPR025280. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- A survey of the BERDL pangenome identified 4,572 DUF4041-containing gene clusters across 1,696 species and 33 bacterial and archaeal phyla. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- The largest species counts were 556 in Pseudomonadota, 334 in Actinomycetota, 275 in Bacillota_A, 206 in Bacillota, 114 in Bacteroidota, 45 in Nitrospirota, 28 in Cyanobacteriota, and 22 in Planctomycetota. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- Of the 4,572 DUF4041-containing clusters, 13.3% were core, 30.7% were accessory, and 56.1% were singleton, giving a combined accessory-plus-singleton fraction of 86.7%. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- The accessory-plus-singleton distribution supports the interpretation that SNIPE-associated domains are commonly gained or lost and are consistent with mobile defense-island carriage rather than stable core inheritance. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- An estimated 80.4% of DUF4041-containing clusters, or 3,675/4,572, were represented by the descriptions T5orf172 or DUF4041 and formed a high-confidence SNIPE-family set. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- The remaining approximately 20%, or 897 clusters, included primary annotations such as histidine kinase in 319 clusters and seryl-tRNA aminoacylation in 238 clusters, potentially reflecting secondary-domain detection or annotation noise. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- Zero surveyed gene clusters contained both DUF4041/PF13250 and canonical GIY-YIG PF01541. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- Only 54 of the 4,572 DUF4041 clusters carried the description “Meiotically up-regulated gene 113,” consistent with full-length SNIPE proteins that also contain the Mug113 nuclease annotation. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- The report recommends searching PF13250, DUF4041, or T5orf172 together with PF13455 rather than using canonical GIY-YIG PF01541 as the SNIPE nuclease marker. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- In the Fitness Browser, PF13455 occurred in 7 genes across 6 organisms, but only one full two-domain SNIPE protein, from [[entities/methanococcus-maripaludis]] JJ at locus MMJJ_RS01635, contained PF13250 together with PF13455. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- [[entities/phagefoundry]] detected DUF4041 in [[entities/klebsiella]] but not in [[entities/acinetobacter-baumannii]], [[entities/pseudomonas-aeruginosa]], or *Pseudomonas viridiflava* in the four-species comparison. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- The three detected *Klebsiella* DUF4041 proteins—JJW41_17025, KFB13_RS08150, and KFB31_RS01490—were each 558 amino acids long. [src: snipe_defense_system]
- DUF4041 is strongly associated with SNIPE, but it may also occur in non-SNIPE proteins, and divergent SNIPE homologues may be missed by eggNOG Pfam annotations. [src: snipe_defense_system]
