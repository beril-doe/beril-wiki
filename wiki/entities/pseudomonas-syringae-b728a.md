# Pseudomonas syringae B728a

Pseudomonas syringae B728a (also written P. syringae) is a plant pathogen. Its dark genes with in planta or stress phenotypes are enriched in plant-associated genomes, which is consistent with their lab phenotypes. [src: functional_dark_matter] The evidence comes from two reports: [[summaries/functional_dark_matter__REPORT]] and [[summaries/cofitness_coinheritance__REPORT]].

## Key facts

- The strongest within-species environmental signals in the functional dark matter analysis are in Pseudomonas and P. syringae. [src: functional_dark_matter]
- Psyr_0167 has an in planta phenotype with |fit| 4.6. Its carrier environment is plant_assoc, its odds ratio is 11.9, its FDR (false discovery rate) is 0.005, and its breadth is universal. [src: functional_dark_matter]
- Psyr_2830 has a stress phenotype with |fit| 3.4. Its carrier environment is plant_assoc, its odds ratio is 10.9, its FDR is 0.031, and its breadth is universal. [src: functional_dark_matter]

For the wider question, see [[concepts/concordance-between-laboratory-fitness-phenotypes-and-field-environments-of-gene-carriers]].

## Co-fitness and co-inheritance

The extracted SyringaeB728a dataset contained 126 genomes, 4,999 clusters and 371,004 cofit pairs, with a phylogenetic tree. [src: cofitness_coinheritance] In this dataset, 129,385 cofit pairs had mean phi 0.049, against 0.043 for random pairs. The delta was +0.006 and the two-sided p-value was 2.9e-4. [src: cofitness_coinheritance] See [[concepts/co-fitness-as-a-predictor-of-gene-co-inheritance-in-pangenomes]].
