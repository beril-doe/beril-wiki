# Methylomirabilota

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Methylomirabilota. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the document. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Stable external identifier:** None was provided in the document. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Methylomirabilota is a GTDB-r214 taxonomic phylum represented in the lanthanide-dependent methanol-oxidation atlas. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Evidence from the lanthanide methylotrophy atlas

Among 80 Methylomirabilota genomes, 23 carried xoxF, the marker for a rare-earth-element-dependent methanol dehydrogenase, and 7 carried mxaF, the marker for a calcium-dependent methanol dehydrogenase. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The phylum had a 28.7 % xoxF rate and a 3.3 : 1 xoxF:mxaF ratio. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The directional test for xoxF dominance in Methylomirabilota had a Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted p-value of 0.011. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

These results place Methylomirabilota among the phyla with high reported xoxF rates, while also showing a higher relative contribution of mxaF than Acidobacteriota and Gemmatimonadota in this atlas. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The marker calls are annotation-based and do not by themselves establish methanol-oxidation activity or methylotrophic physiology in Methylomirabilota genomes. [^lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
