## Key facts from PaperBLAST

* *Drosophila melanogaster* is one of the five organisms accounting for **72.8%** of all gene-paper records in PaperBLAST, together with *Homo sapiens*, *Mus musculus*, *Rattus norvegicus*, and *Arabidopsis thaliana*. [^paperblast_explorer]
* The PaperBLAST organism-level analysis covers **20,723** organisms with at least one literature record; the top **1,000** organisms capture **94.2%** of the literature, leaving **19,723** organisms with **5.8%**. [^paperblast_explorer]
* The concentration of literature around *Drosophila melanogaster* is part of the report’s broader evidence for highly unequal research attention across organisms. [^paperblast_explorer]
