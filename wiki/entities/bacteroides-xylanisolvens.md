# Bacteroides xylanisolvens

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Bacteroides xylanisolvens. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

**Known aliases:** None reported in the source document. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

**Stable external identifier:** None reported in the source document. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

Bacteroides xylanisolvens is an organism analyzed in the [ecotype_functional_differentiation__REPORT](../summaries/ecotype_functional_differentiation__REPORT.md) study of within-species gene-content ecotypes. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Ecotype differentiation

The analysis included 207 Bacteroides xylanisolvens genomes and identified 6 gene-content ecotypes, with all 207 genomes assigned to an ecotype. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

Bacteroides xylanisolvens showed the second-clearest ecotype separation among the sampled species, with a mean silhouette score of 0.366. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The ecotypes were identified by principal component analysis followed by KMeans clustering, searching k = 2–6 and selecting the result with the best silhouette score. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Related research

This organism provides multi-species evidence for [ecotype-environment-gene-content](../concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content.md), which examines how within-species gene-content variation relates to ecological and functional differentiation. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

Its analysis also contributes to [pangenome-integration](../concepts/pangenome-integration.md) by linking pangenome gene-content clustering with COG functional profiles. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]
