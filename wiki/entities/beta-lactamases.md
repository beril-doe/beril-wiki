# Beta-lactamases

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** beta-lactamases. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

**Known alias:** β-lactamases. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

**Stable external identifier:** Not specified in the source report. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

## Key facts

The Gene Function Ecological Agora project used beta-lactamases as a function class in its Sankoff-parsimony acquisition-depth analysis. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Beta-lactamase gains were distributed as 44.2% recent and 4.9% ancient events, giving a recent-to-ancient ratio of 9.0×. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

This acquisition-depth signature was more recent-skewed than the clean tRNA-synthetase controls, which had 24.7% recent and 10.7% ancient gains and a 2.3× ratio. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The beta-lactamase result contributes evidence to [[concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth]] about function-class differences in phylogenetically attributed acquisition timing. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

It also feeds the [[concepts/environmental-resistome]] synthesis because beta-lactamases were analyzed alongside CRISPR-Cas and two-component-system histidine kinases as acquisition-depth signatures relevant to resistance and defense functions. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]
