# Beta-lactamases

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** beta-lactamases. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

**Known alias:** β-lactamases. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

**Stable external identifier:** Not specified in the source report. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

## Key facts

The Gene Function Ecological Agora project used beta-lactamases as a function class in its Sankoff-parsimony acquisition-depth analysis. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

Beta-lactamase gains were distributed as 44.2% recent and 4.9% ancient events, giving a recent-to-ancient ratio of 9.0×. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

This acquisition-depth signature was more recent-skewed than the clean tRNA-synthetase controls, which had 24.7% recent and 10.7% ancient gains and a 2.3× ratio. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

The beta-lactamase result contributes evidence to [gene-function-acquisition-depth](../concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth.md) about function-class differences in phylogenetically attributed acquisition timing. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]

It also feeds the [environmental-resistome](../concepts/environmental-resistome.md) synthesis because beta-lactamases were analyzed alongside CRISPR-Cas and two-component-system histidine kinases as acquisition-depth signatures relevant to resistance and defense functions. [^gene_function_ecological_agora]
