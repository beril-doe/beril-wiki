# Azospirillum brasilense

Azospirillum brasilense (short name azobra) has all 18 amino acid pathways complete in the GapMind analysis. [src: essential_metabolome] The evidence comes from three project reports that list it as one organism among several.

## Key facts

- GapMind analysis in [[summaries/essential_metabolome__REPORT]]: 18/18 amino acid pathways are complete (100%). [src: essential_metabolome]
- The same report lists genome GCF_000011365.1 with 1,786 GapMind predictions, and the organism is present in the collection. [src: essential_metabolome]
- Annotation gaps in [[summaries/annotation_gap_discovery__REPORT]]: 21 total gaps, of which 13 were resolved (61.9%). [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

## Fitness Browser membership

A. brasilense is one of the Fitness Browser organisms studied in [[summaries/pathway_capability_dependency__REPORT]]. The others are Desulfovibrio vulgaris Hildenborough, Shewanella oneidensis MR-1, Pseudomonas putida, Pseudomonas stutzeri, Caulobacter crescentus and Sinorhizobium meliloti.
