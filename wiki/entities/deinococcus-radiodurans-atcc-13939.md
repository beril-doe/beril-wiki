# Deinococcus radiodurans ATCC 13939

Deinococcus radiodurans ATCC 13939 is listed as a Deinococcota organism with the GramNeg template. [src: thermodynamic_impact] The thermodynamic impact analysis modeled it, and the plant-microbiome marker analysis misclassified it. [src: thermodynamic_impact, plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

## Key facts

- The model has 712 genes, 1,070 reactions and growth 0.5000. [src: thermodynamic_impact]

## Thermodynamic impact (TMFA)

TMFA is thermodynamics-based metabolic flux analysis. For D. radiodurans the impact table reports 1,070 reactions, of which 757 were constrained. [src: thermodynamic_impact] Five reactions changed flux (percentage column: 0.47). [src: thermodynamic_impact] None were newly blocked, 4 were newly active, and 1 was reversed. [src: thermodynamic_impact] There were 0 Marvin-versus-OPAM2 differences. [src: thermodynamic_impact] See [[summaries/thermodynamic_impact__REPORT]] and [[concepts/effect-of-pka-source-on-flux-predictions-across-diverse-organisms]].

## Plant-microbiome marker cohorts

The refined plant-microbiome marker cohorts misclassified neutral D. radiodurans as beneficial. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] It was one of 4 neutral controls, and all 4 were misclassified. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] See [[summaries/plant_microbiome_ecotypes__REPORT]] and [[concepts/dual-nature-plant-associated-bacteria-and-the-validity-of-marker-based-cohorts]].
