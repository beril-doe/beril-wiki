## Key facts from the document

The study analyzed *Stutzerimonas stutzeri* as one of 10 target species in a metabolic ecotype analysis. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The analysis included 149 genomes, identified 2 metabolic clusters, and produced a silhouette score of 0.780. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The silhouette score greater than 0.2 indicates that the metabolic clustering met the study's criterion for detectable cluster structure. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The association between metabolic clusters and isolation environment was not significant for *Stutzerimonas* in the reported comparison. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The study grouped *Stutzerimonas* with marine organisms for which environment-cluster associations were not significant. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The authors suggested that non-significant marine associations could reflect insufficient isolation-source metadata, depth- or nutrient-zone structure, or pathway annotations that do not resolve relevant gene-content differences; these interpretations are observational and do not establish causality.
