# ACIAD3137 and ACIAD2176

ACIAD3137 and ACIAD2176 are two uncharacterized proteins in [[entities/acinetobacter-baylyi-adp1]]. ACIAD3137 is a UPF0234 protein (also called YitK) and ACIAD2176 is a DUF2280 protein. Both co-fit with [[entities/respiratory-complex-i-nadh-ubiquinone-oxidoreductase]] genes at r > 0.98, so they are candidate accessory factors. The evidence is correlational only. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network, discoveries]

## Key facts

- ACIAD3137 is UPF0234 and ACIAD2176 is DUF2280. Both show r > 0.98 co-fitness with Complex I. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]
- In the cross-project digest, ACIAD3137 (UPF0234/YitK) and ACIAD2176 (DUF2280) correlate at r > 0.98 with Complex I genes across 8 growth conditions. [src: discoveries]
- The aromatic catabolism report counts them among 16 previously uncharacterized genes assigned to specific subsystems. It calls them candidate Complex I accessory factors. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]
- Both lack flux balance analysis (FBA) reaction mappings and have no assigned metabolic function. [src: discoveries]

## Interpretation

The near-perfect co-fitness with the nuo operon suggests a physical or regulatory association with Complex I. [src: discoveries] This is a hypothesis, not an established role.

## Caveats

The 11 Complex I co-fitness assignments beyond the core nuo operon rest on phenotypic correlation only. Some may be indirect connections rather than physical association with Complex I. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network] The report proposes protein-protein interaction studies or co-purification with Complex I. These would test whether the proteins are physical accessory factors or indirect regulatory connections. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]
