# pqqD

## Identity

**Canonical name:** pqqD. Known aliases and stable external identifiers were not provided in the report. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

pqqD is a gene associated with the plant-growth-promoting gene set analyzed across the BERDL pangenome. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] The analysis relates pqqD to [[entities/pqqc]] and the functional pqqB–pqqC unit, although pqqD can sometimes occur as a standalone gene. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Evidence from PGP pangenome analysis

pqqD had a core fraction of **55.5%**, an auxiliary fraction of **17.0%**, and a singleton fraction of **27.5%**. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

Among the highlighted PGP genes, pqqD had the lowest reported core fraction and the highest reported singleton fraction. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

The core-fraction difference for pqqD relative to the genome-wide baseline was statistically significant, with Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate q = **2.2e-86**. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

The report identifies pqqD as an outlier to the broader pattern in which PGP genes were predominantly core, suggesting the hypothesis that pqqD can sometimes spread as a standalone gene. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Interpretation and limitations

The pqqD result concerns gene-cluster annotations derived from Bakta annotations and was not functionally validated; truncations, frameshifts, and pseudogenization were not filtered. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

The pqqD finding therefore constrains, but does not by itself establish, the mechanism of PGP gene inheritance or transfer. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

This evidence feeds the broader question of genome organization and inheritance in [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] and [[concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth]]. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
