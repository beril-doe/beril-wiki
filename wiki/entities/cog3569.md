# COG3569

COG3569 (the COG3569 ortholog group) is the top eggNOG ortholog-group (OG) hit separating plant-associated species from other species. Its odds ratio (OR) is partly attenuated under phylogenetic control, but the signal stays strong. [src: discoveries, plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

## Key facts

- The top hit is COG3569, with OR=6.01 phylo-controlled. [src: discoveries]
- The plant-association test gave Fisher OR=8.92, phylo-controlled OR=6.01 and q=7.7e-242. The OG occurred in 54% of plant-associated species and 12% of non-plant species. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]
- In the separate summary of the top 10 novel OGs, controlled odds ratios ranged from 4.4 (COG5516) to 6.6 (COG1845, cytochrome oxidase ctaE). That summary gives q-values as small as 10⁻⁹⁶ and attributes the smallest to COG3569. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] The report gives q=7.7e-242 for COG3569 in the plant-association test above. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] The two q-values differ, and the quotes do not say why.
- The NB03 analysis also listed COG3569 among the top enriched OGs, with OR=8.92. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

## Phylogenetic attenuation

After control for 28 phyla as fixed effects, the odds ratio fell from 8.92 to 6.01. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] The report reads this drop as partial phylogenetic confounding, because plant-associated species cluster taxonomically. It also states that a substantial ecological signal persists after correction. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] See [[concepts/genome-sampling-depth-genome-size-and-phylogeny-as-confounders-of-gene-content-and-pangenome-associations]] for the general problem.
