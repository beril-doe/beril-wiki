# Streptococcus salivarius

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Streptococcus salivarius. [^cf_formulation_design]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source document. [^cf_formulation_design]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [^cf_formulation_design]

Streptococcus salivarius is a commensal organism evaluated as a candidate for competitive exclusion of [pseudomonas-aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) in cystic-fibrosis airways. [^cf_formulation_design]

## Evidence from cf_formulation_design

Streptococcus salivarius had a best inhibition value of 98%, an engraftability score of 0.172, 153 pangenome genomes, 18/18 conserved amino-acid pathways, and 5 lung genomes, representing 4% of its analyzed genomes. [^cf_formulation_design]

The organism was one of three isolates identified as a candidate dual-mechanism inhibitor after metabolic-model residual analysis; Streptococcus salivarius ASMA-737 had a positive residual of +74.1%. [^cf_formulation_design]

Its engraftability score of 0.172 was above the median among inhibition-tested species. [^cf_formulation_design]

Streptococcus salivarius was included in the strict-safe five-species core with [neisseria-mucosa](neisseria-mucosa.md), [micrococcus-luteus](micrococcus-luteus.md), [rothia-dentocariosa](rothia-dentocariosa.md), and [gemella-sanguinis](gemella-sanguinis.md). [^cf_formulation_design]

The k=3 formulation containing Micrococcus luteus, Neisseria mucosa, and Streptococcus salivarius achieved 100% niche coverage, 75% inhibition, and engraftability 0.140. [^cf_formulation_design]

Xylitol was proposed as a candidate prebiotic to support Streptococcus salivarius, but this prediction requires experimental validation before use. [^cf_formulation_design]

## Interpretation and limitations

The organism's strong inhibition and complete conservation of the 18/18 analyzed amino-acid pathways support its consideration as a formulation component, but the source does not establish clinical efficacy or in vivo engraftment. [^cf_formulation_design]

The inhibition measurements were planktonic assays against PA14 rather than structured biofilm assays or a broad panel of clinical strains. [^cf_formulation_design]

Pairwise interaction data were limited to 8 comparisons across 5 unique pairs, and the complete 10-pair interaction matrix for the five-species core was not measured. [^cf_formulation_design]
