# YebC

## Identity

**Canonical name:** YebC. [^metal_specificity]

**Known aliases:** yebc; OG01383. [^metal_specificity]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [^metal_specificity]

YebC is a candidate gene family associated with metal-specific fitness defects. [^metal_specificity]

## Evidence from metal-specificity analysis

YebC was metal-specific in 7 of 12 organisms across 6 metals, corresponding to 58% specificity, with a mean sick rate of 0.056 across the analyzed non-metal experiments. [^metal_specificity]

The analysis prioritized YebC as one of its three strongest novel candidates because it combined 50–67% metal-specificity across multiple species with lower pleiotropic effects. [^metal_specificity]

The YebC result supports the distinction between metal-specific determinants and broadly general stress genes described in [metal-cross-resistance](../concepts/metal-cross-resistance.md). [^metal_specificity]

## Proposed mechanism

The report states that YebC functions as a translation factor for proline-rich proteins. [^metal_specificity]

The hypothesis that metal-induced demand for transporters containing proline-rich regions creates a translation bottleneck that YebC resolves is explicitly proposed, rather than established, by the report. [^metal_specificity]

## Related analyses

YebC was evaluated alongside [ucp030820](ucp030820.md) and [duf1043-yhcb](duf1043-yhcb.md) as a candidate metal-specific family. [^metal_specificity]
