# Anaeromyxobacter

## What this entity is

Anaeromyxobacter is an organism associated with iron reduction in the SSO subsurface community analysis. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

- **Canonical name:** Anaeromyxobacter. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- **Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source document. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- **Stable external identifier:** Not provided in the source document. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence from SSO subsurface communities

Anaeromyxobacter was the key genus associated with the iron-reduction hotspot at well U1, where inferred iron reduction peaked at 2.3%. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

In the groundwater–sediment comparison, Anaeromyxobacter represented 1.24% of sediment communities and 0.03% of groundwater communities, corresponding to a reported groundwater-to-sediment ratio of 0.02×. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The report interprets Anaeromyxobacter enrichment in sediment, together with other anaerobic taxa, as evidence that sediment-associated communities differ from groundwater communities rather than representing simple detachment from sediment. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

These functional and environmental assignments are inferred from taxonomy-to-trait associations and community composition, not from direct measurement of Anaeromyxobacter metabolism or SSO geochemistry. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
