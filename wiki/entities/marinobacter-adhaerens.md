# Marinobacter adhaerens

Marinobacter adhaerens is a Fitness Browser organism with strong essential-core enrichment. [src: conservation_vs_fitness] Its metal ranking comes from a genus-level result. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]

## Key facts

- In the conservation analysis, M. adhaerens had one of the strongest essential-core enrichment signals, with an odds ratio (OR) of 3.08. [src: conservation_vs_fitness]
- The same report lists *Methanococcus maripaludis* S2 (OR=5.21) and *Ralstonia syzygii* PSI07 (OR=3.41) as stronger signals. [src: conservation_vs_fitness]
- In the metal atlas, the genus Marinobacter ranks at the 75th percentile after genome-size normalization (metal clusters / total KEGG-annotated clusters). [src: metal_fitness_atlas]
- In that normalization, *Leptospirillum* ranks at the 91st percentile, *Acidithiobacillus* at the 77th and *Sulfobacillus* at the 71st. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]
