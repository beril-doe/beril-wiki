# Oak Ridge Field Research Center

## What this entity is

**Oak Ridge Field Research Center** is the canonical name for the groundwater research site associated with the isolation of *Pseudomonas* FW300-N2E3. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

Known aliases: **Oak Ridge Field Research Center**, **Oak Ridge Field Research Centre**, and **ORFRC**; the report does not provide a stable external identifier. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The lab–field ecology study **supports** the site's role as a testbed for connecting laboratory fitness with environmental community structure: it compared Fitness Browser measurements with 16S amplicon profiles and geochemistry across 108 Oak Ridge groundwater sites. [^lab_field_ecology]

## Key facts

*Pseudomonas* FW300-N2E3 was isolated from groundwater at the Oak Ridge Field Research Center in the context of an ENIGMA SFA project. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The report proposes that secretion by a prototrophic organism unable to re-assimilate tryptophan could supply that amino acid to auxotrophic community members in the Oak Ridge groundwater community, although the pathway-level distinction between biosynthetic, catabolic, and regulatory fitness genes remains unresolved because the planned NB04 analysis was deferred. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

FW300-N2E3 produced or increased 58 metabolites in Web of Microbes exometabolomics data, including increased tryptophan; the report identifies this production-plus-growth-but-no-species-level-catabolism pattern as a hypothesis consistent with cross-feeding or signaling. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The report proposes community metabolic modeling of the Oak Ridge groundwater community to test whether tryptophan cross-feeding could explain the ecological role of FW300-N2E3 secretion. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The new field study **refines** this cross-feeding and fitness context by showing that field abundance is not a simple readout of aggregate laboratory metal tolerance: five of 11 tested genera had uranium associations after FDR correction, with both positive and negative directions, while the aggregate tolerance–abundance relationship was only suggestive (Spearman rho=0.503, p=0.095, n=12 genera). [^lab_field_ecology]
