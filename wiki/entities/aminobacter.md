# Aminobacter

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Aminobacter. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source document. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

Aminobacter is a bacterial genus whose abundance increased in Round-2 communities following lignin enrichment. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

## Evidence from lignin enrichment

Aminobacter was absent from the base community and from the Round-1 groups. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

In Round 2, Aminobacter represented 7.6% of reads in the L-L group and 6.4% in the LC-L group. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

These observations support a conditional association between Aminobacter enrichment and communities carrying lignin-related passage histories, rather than evidence that Aminobacter was abundant in the starting community. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The finding contributes to [[concepts/ecological-memory]] because Aminobacter emerged in communities whose composition depended on Round-1 history. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

It also informs [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]], where taxon abundance changes are interpreted as condition-specific community selection. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The source study did not directly measure lignin-degradation genes or pathways in Aminobacter, so a functional role in lignin degradation remains a hypothesis. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]
