# Type I restriction-modification system

## What this entity is

The canonical name is **Type I restriction-modification system**. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

Known aliases are **R-M Type I** and **restriction-modification Type I**. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

No stable external identifier is reported for this entity in the source document. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

## Key facts

Type I restriction-modification markers were analyzed as one of seven anti-phage defense-system families in the BERDL pangenome. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

Type I restriction-modification systems occurred in **76.8%** of species-level pangenomes in the analysis. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

Their prevalence exceeded **80%** in both Pseudomonadota and Bacillota. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

Type I restriction-modification systems were significantly enriched in the auxiliary and singleton pangenome relative to a background of **46.8% core** and **37.9% singleton** gene clusters. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

Type I restriction-modification markers were **9.8% core** and **65.3% singleton** gene clusters. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The singleton fraction of Type I restriction-modification markers was **1.5–1.8×** the **37.9%** background singleton fraction, consistent with horizontal transfer of defense loci through mobile genetic elements. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

Type I restriction-modification systems participated in the broader defense-system syndrome pattern: **27 of 28** defense-system pairs showed significant positive co-occurrence after 1,000 phylum-stratified column-permutation null permutations and Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The Type I restriction-modification × [[entities/disarm]] pair had **4,970** observed co-occurrences versus a null mean of **4,673**, with **z = 27.5** and **odds ratio (OR) = 8.4**. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The Type I restriction-modification × [[entities/brex]] pair had **5,963** observed co-occurrences versus a null mean of **5,732**, with **z = 26.9** and **OR = 8.7**. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The Type I restriction-modification × [[entities/restriction-modification-type-ii]] pair had **3,948** observed co-occurrences versus a null mean of **3,665**, with **z = 22.5** and **OR = 7.6**. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

These co-occurrence results support integration of Type I restriction-modification systems into the network-level defense architecture described in [[concepts/cofitness-network-architecture]]. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

## Interpretation and limitations

The accessory and singleton enrichment of Type I restriction-modification markers provides evidence consistent with mobile acquisition of defense loci, but the source does not establish the transfer mechanism for individual loci. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The defense-system syndrome results were calculated from species-level presence or absence across the quality-filtered analysis set of **7,323** species, which represented **26%** of the **27,690** pangenome species. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The analysis controlled for phylum categorically but did not use a phylogenetically corrected regression, so the co-occurrence results do not formally establish phylogenetically independent associations. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]
