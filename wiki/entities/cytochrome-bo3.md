# Cytochrome bo3

## Identity

**Canonical name:** cytochrome bo3. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

**Known alias:** cytochrome bo3 ubiquinol oxidase. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

**Stable external identifier:** not reported in this document. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

Cytochrome bo3 is a respiratory-chain component of [[entities/acinetobacter-baylyi-adp1]] whose requirement changes with the carbon source. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

## Evidence from Condition-Specific Respiratory Chain Wiring

ADP1 has 4 genes assigned to the cytochrome bo3 subsystem within a respiratory-chain inventory of 62 genes across 8 subsystems. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

Cytochrome bo3 is required on acetate, where the respiratory chain also requires Complex I, ACIAD3522, and additional components. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

Cytochrome bo3 is specifically required on lactate, while Complex I is mildly important and cytochrome bd is dispensable under that condition. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

Cytochrome bo3 is dispensable on quinate, whereas Complex I is required. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

Glucose has no specifically required respiratory component, and all listed components show full redundancy, including cytochrome bo3. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

Urea is generally demanding across the respiratory chain and requires everything in the reported respiratory-chain profile, including cytochrome bo3. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

These results support [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]] by showing that cytochrome bo3 fitness depends qualitatively on the growth substrate rather than following a simple uniform respiratory-chain requirement. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

The report interprets these condition-specific requirements together with gene-phenotype data, flux-balance analysis (FBA), theoretical stoichiometry, cross-species Fitness Browser data, and proteomics. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]
