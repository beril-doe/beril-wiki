# Homo sapiens

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Homo sapiens. [^paperblast_explorer]

**Known aliases:** human; *H. sapiens*. [^paperblast_explorer]

**Stable external identifier:** Not reported in the source document. [^paperblast_explorer]

## Key facts in PaperBLAST

Homo sapiens accounts for **46.7%** of all gene-paper records in the PaperBLAST collection. [^paperblast_explorer]

Homo sapiens is the leading organism by literature coverage in PaperBLAST; the top five organisms—Homo sapiens, Mus musculus, Rattus norvegicus, Arabidopsis thaliana, and Drosophila melanogaster—account for **72.8%** of all gene-paper records. [^paperblast_explorer]

PaperBLAST contains literature links for **20,723** organisms, and the top **1,000** organisms capture **94.2%** of the literature, leaving **19,723** organisms with **5.8%**. [^paperblast_explorer]

The **50** most-referenced genes in PaperBLAST are all human genes, illustrating the concentration of gene-level literature coverage within Homo sapiens. [^paperblast_explorer]

The PaperBLAST collection contains **12.4 million** rows across **14** tables linking sequences and genes to literature, annotations, structures, and full-text snippets. [^paperblast_explorer]

The report cautions that PaperBLAST literature coverage is based on text-mined PubMed Central full-text articles, so papers behind paywalls and genes mentioned only outside the available full text may be missed. [^paperblast_explorer]
