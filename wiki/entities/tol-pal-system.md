# Tol–Pal system

## What this entity is

The canonical name is **Tol–Pal system**. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

Known aliases are **Tol-Pal** and **Pal-Tol**. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The source reports no stable external identifier for this system. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The Tol–Pal system is implicated in retrograde outer-membrane phospholipid transport and lipid-homeostasis remodeling after loss of outer-leaflet lipid A. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Evidence from the Caulobacter lipid A-loss study

In *Caulobacter crescentus*, Pal/CCNA_00784 was induced at both the transcript and protein levels, with transcript logFC **+2.08** and protein logFC **+2.84**. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

Pal/CCNA_00784 was also included in the late-consequence cohort of the published ChvI-induced regulons. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The report interprets Pal-Tol upregulation as consistent with increased lipid-remodeling demand after loss of outer-leaflet lipid A. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

Direct Pal–peptidoglycan contacts and a Caulobacter division-associated role are treated as additional context rather than as direct measurements in this project. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The Pal result contributes to the study’s multi-omics interpretation, which combines transcript, single-replicate outer-membrane proteome, fitness, regulon, and comparative-annotation evidence. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Limitations and resolving work

The outer-membrane proteome had a single replicate per strain, so the Pal protein increase had no per-protein statistical test. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

Replicated outer-membrane proteomics, targeted Pal assays, lipidomics, and Tol-Pal phospholipid-transport assays are needed to test whether the observed induction represents functional remodeling after lipid A loss. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
