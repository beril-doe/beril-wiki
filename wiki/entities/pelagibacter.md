# Pelagibacter

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Pelagibacter. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Known alias:** Pelagibacter sp. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Stable external identifier:** Not reported in the source document. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

## Key facts from the document

Pelagibacter was one of 10 target species analyzed for within-species metabolic ecotypes. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The analysis included 79 Pelagibacter genomes, identified 2 metabolic clusters, and reported a silhouette score of 0.430. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

Pelagibacter therefore showed metabolic clustering above the study’s inclusion threshold of 0.2. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

Metabolic clusters were not significantly associated with isolation environment in Pelagibacter. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The source interprets the lack of a significant marine environment association as potentially reflecting insufficient isolation-source metadata, depth- or nutrient-zone structure, or pathway annotations that do not resolve relevant gene-content differences; this is an observational interpretation and does not establish causality. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

These findings contribute to [ecotype-environment-gene-content](../concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content.md), which compares metabolic clustering with environmental metadata, and are documented in [metabolic_capability_dependency__REPORT](../summaries/metabolic_capability_dependency__REPORT.md). [^metabolic_capability_dependency]
