## Key facts from PaperBLAST

PaperBLAST lists *Mus musculus* among the five organisms accounting for the largest share of gene-paper records, alongside [homo-sapiens](homo-sapiens.md), [rattus-norvegicus](rattus-norvegicus.md), [arabidopsis-thaliana](arabidopsis-thaliana.md), and [drosophila-melanogaster](drosophila-melanogaster.md). [^paperblast_explorer]

The five leading organisms collectively account for **72.8%** of all gene-paper records in PaperBLAST. [^paperblast_explorer]

PaperBLAST contains literature for **20,723** organisms; the top **1,000** organisms capture **94.2%** of the literature, leaving **19,723** organisms with **5.8%**. [^paperblast_explorer]

This organism-level concentration is part of the broader literature-coverage inequality analyzed in [paperblast_explorer__REPORT](../summaries/paperblast_explorer__REPORT.md) and contributes to the uneven acquisition of functional knowledge discussed in [gene-function-acquisition-depth](../concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth.md). [^paperblast_explorer]
