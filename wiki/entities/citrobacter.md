# Citrobacter

This page covers Citrobacter, an enteric genus with the highest candidate metal-type diversity among shortlisted genera. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology] One Citrobacter OTU is ranked at priority 3 for microcosm experiments [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology], but the genus was not detected in the field check in PRJNA1084851. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology] See [[summaries/microbeatlas_metal_ecology__REPORT]] and [[summaries/amr_pangenome_atlas__REPORT]].

## Key facts

- The report says Citrobacter tops metal-type diversity, at 3.7 types. It links the genus to silP/A, arsC/D, chrA, and merA/P. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]
- The ENIGMA candidate table gives Citrobacter a groundwater prevalence (%) of 0.89, a 2.8× fold enrichment versus non-groundwater, and 3.7 metal types. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]
- The report calls the 2.8× enrichment, shared with Enterococcus, consistent with its ENIGMA experimental predictions. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]
- In the bacterial atlas, Citrobacter has 134 antimicrobial resistance (AMR) clusters per species. [src: amr_pangenome_atlas]

## Microcosm priority

The report ranks Citrobacter operational taxonomic unit (OTU) `97_14443` at priority 3 for microcosm experiments. Its Levins' B is 0.854, its metal types are 3.7, and the recommended stress is As or Hg. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]

## Field validation limit

Citrobacter and Thermodesulfovibrio were not detected in PRJNA1084851. The report says this may mean they were below detection, or that their genus names did not match SILVA nomenclature. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology] See [[concepts/field-validation-of-metal-diversity-genera-in-contaminated-groundwater]] and [[concepts/genus-level-taxonomy-bridging-to-pangenome-clades-and-its-coverage-limits]].
