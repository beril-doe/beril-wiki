# Protocatechuate

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Protocatechuate. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

**Known aliases:** None reported in the source document. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

**Stable external identifier:** None reported in the source document. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

Protocatechuate is an aromatic intermediate in *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 quinate catabolism. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

## Role in ADP1 Quinate Catabolism

Protocatechuate is processed by the [[entities/beta-ketoadipate-pathway]], which converts aromatic substrates through β-ketoadipate to succinyl-CoA and acetyl-CoA for entry into the TCA cycle. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

The [[entities/quinate-degradation-pathway]] includes protocatechuate 3,4-dioxygenase, encoded by pcaGH, which cleaves the protocatechuate aromatic ring. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

Protocatechuate 3,4-dioxygenase is a non-heme Fe²⁺-dependent intradiol ring-cleavage enzyme, linking protocatechuate catabolism to iron acquisition in the reported 51-gene support network. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

The pca/qui region forms a 12-gene operon spanning pcaIJFBDCHG-quiABC and transport genes. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

## Relation to the Support Network

Protocatechuate is part of the aromatic-pathway core surrounded by the 51-gene quinate-specific support network. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

The network assigns 44/51 genes (86%) to four functional subsystems: 8 aromatic-pathway genes, 21 Complex I genes, 7 iron-acquisition genes, and 2 PQQ-biosynthesis genes. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

The source links protocatechuate cleavage to the network’s iron-acquisition subsystem because pcaGH requires non-heme Fe²⁺. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]
