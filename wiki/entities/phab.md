# phaB

## Identity

**Canonical name:** phaB. [^phb_granule_ecology]

**Known aliases:** No alternative alias is reported in the source document. [^phb_granule_ecology]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is provided in the source document. [^phb_granule_ecology]

phaB is analyzed as a PHB-related biosynthetic gene in the [phb-granule-ecology](../concepts/phb-granule-ecology.md) study. [^phb_granule_ecology]

## Evidence from phb_granule_ecology

The survey identified 9,617 phaB gene clusters across 6,977 species in a 27,690-species GTDB analysis using [eggnog](eggnog.md) annotations. [^phb_granule_ecology]

The report defined a complete PHB pathway as phaC plus phaA and phaB, and found that 21.7% of the 27,690 species had this complete pathway. [^phb_granule_ecology]

The gene-level census classified 6,005 species as having a complete pathway, 12,869 as precursors-only, 555 as accessory-only, and 8,199 as absent. [^phb_granule_ecology]

The 12,869-species precursors-only category represented 46.5% of the surveyed species and likely overestimates partial PHB capability because phaB, like phaA, participates in general metabolism beyond PHB biosynthesis. [^phb_granule_ecology]

The report’s interpretation of phaB therefore depends on pathway context, particularly the presence of [phac](phac.md) and the other PHB biosynthetic components, rather than on phaB detection alone. [^phb_granule_ecology]
