## Evidence from FW300-N2E3

- Glycine increased in the Web of Microbes exometabolome of *Pseudomonas* FW300-N2E3. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- Glycine was one of 13 metabolites mapped to GapMind, which predicted a complete pathway for FW300-N2E3. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- Glycine also showed growth in Fitness Browser experiments, contributing to the report's 13/13 agreement between GapMind pathway predictions and Fitness Browser measurements. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- BacDive recorded glycine utilization in 0 of 1 strain, with 1 negative result and low evidentiary confidence because the sample size was n=1. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- The report classifies glycine among four metabolites showing Web of Microbes production but BacDive non-utilization; because only one BacDive measurement was available, it considers this result insufficient for a conclusion. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- The glycine comparison illustrates the distinction between metabolite production measured by exometabolomics and utilization measured by growth assays, which are not intrinsically contradictory. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

## Community metabolic ecology

Community-scale NMDC–GTDB analysis **refines** the strain-level GapMind evidence above: glycine biosynthesis showed strong ecosystem-associated differentiation, with a Kruskal–Wallis statistic of H = 92.98 and Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate-adjusted q = 1.2×10⁻¹⁹. It was the strongest listed amino-acid pathway difference, among 17 of 18 pathways significantly differentiated across ecosystem types. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

This community result **supports** the interpretation that glycine-related metabolic potential varies with ecological context, while not establishing whether the difference reflects habitat physicochemistry, taxonomic composition, or both. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
