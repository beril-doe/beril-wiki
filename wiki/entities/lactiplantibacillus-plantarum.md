*Lactiplantibacillus plantarum* enters this corpus from two directions: as a "plant-associated and gut-associated Firmicute with well-developed genetic tools" proposed as an experimental host for testing uncharacterized genes [src: functional_dark_matter], and as a commonly proposed probiotic whose safety in a cystic fibrosis context is questioned [src: cf_formulation_design].

## Names used in this corpus

- *Lactobacillus plantarum* — the name used in the functional-dark-matter candidate list [src: functional_dark_matter]
- *Lactobacillus plantarum* WCFS1 — the specific strain named as a candidate organism [src: functional_dark_matter]
- *Lactiplantibacillus plantarum* — the name used in the cystic fibrosis formulation-design report [src: cf_formulation_design]

## Probiotic-safety caution in cystic fibrosis formulation design

The cystic fibrosis formulation-design project records that *Lactiplantibacillus plantarum* — a commonly proposed probiotic — actually **enhanced** [[entities/pseudomonas-aeruginosa]] (PA) pathogenicity in a CF sputum–epithelial cell model, per Drost & Fuhrmann (2025); the project carries this as a caveat against generic probiotic-safety assumptions when designing live-organism formulations [src: cf_formulation_design].

Evidence grading for that caution: it is a bibliographic caveat, not a measurement made in this corpus. The enhanced-pathogenicity statement is the finding of an externally cited paper — Drost M, Fuhrmann G (2025), "Pseudomonas aeruginosa and Its Unsuspected Ally Lactiplantibacillus plantarum: Enhanced Pathogenicity in A Combined Cystic Fibrosis Sputum–Epithelial Cell Model," *ACS Infect Dis*, DOI: 10.1021/acsinfecdis.5c00759 — so the effect size, model details and conditions must be read from the primary publication before the caution is used as a hard design constraint [src: cf_formulation_design].

## Candidate host for dark-gene experiments

In the functional-dark-matter candidate list, *Lactobacillus plantarum* WCFS1 appears as candidate 6, a Firmicute (Bacilli) selected because it would complement [[entities/bacillus-subtilis]] with a different ecological niche and would enable testing carbon-source dark genes in a fermentative bacterium; the stated purpose is to broaden Firmicutes ecological coverage [src: functional_dark_matter]. This is a prioritization proposal, not an executed experiment; see [[concepts/experimental-prioritization-of-functional-dark-matter]] for the selection logic it belongs to [src: functional_dark_matter].
