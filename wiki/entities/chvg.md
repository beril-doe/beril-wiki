# ChvG

**Canonical name:** ChvG. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the document. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

ChvG is the sensor-regulator partner of the Caulobacter-restricted ChvG–[[entities/chvi]] circuit. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Evidence from the document

NCBI annotation produced pattern 1000 for ChvG and ChvI across *Caulobacter crescentus*, *Acinetobacter baumannii*, *Neisseria meningitidis*, and *Moraxella catarrhalis*, supporting restriction of the ChvG–ChvI circuit to *C. crescentus* among the tested species. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The comparative analysis therefore indicates that the three tested comparator species lack the Caulobacter ChvG–ChvI regulatory circuit. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The document places ChvG–ChvI within the regulatory architecture associated with the Δ*fur* Δ*sspB* background and Δ*lpxc* viability, while noting that the detailed mechanism connecting this circuit to lipid A loss remains unresolved. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
