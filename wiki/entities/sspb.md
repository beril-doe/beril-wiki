# SspB

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** SspB. **Known aliases:** none reported in this document. **Stable external identifier:** not provided in this document. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

SspB is implicated in buffering a micro-aerobic respiratory program during the *Caulobacter crescentus* lipid A-loss rescue state, but the mechanistic importance of that buffering remains a hypothesis. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Evidence from the document

The 4584-vs-4580 contrast, comparing Δ*fur* Δ*sspB* Δ*rsaA* with Δ*rsaA*, correlated with Leaden 2018’s Δ*fur* signal across 93 differentially expressed genes, with Spearman ρ = 0.315, p = 2.08e-03, and 71% sign concordance. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

Of those 93 genes, 53 were buffered in the present data, showing logFC approximately 0 despite Leaden values of -5 to -9. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The buffered genes were dominated by the cbb3 / cyd / *fix*-NOPQ micro-aerobic respiratory operon, including CCNA_01466-01476, *ccoNOPQ*, *cydCDA*, and *fixG/H/I*. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The Caulobacter RB-TnSeq compendium covered 198 experiments, including 22 envelope-stress experiments and zero iron-limitation experiments. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

For the 26-gene SspB-buffered set used in the enrichment test, 9 genes were phenotype-bearing, or 34.6%, corresponding to 1.04× enrichment over the genome-wide envelope-stress background and hypergeometric p = 0.515. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The genome-wide envelope-stress background contained 1311 phenotype-bearing genes among 3943 genes, or 33.25%, so the SspB-buffered set was indistinguishable from background in this analysis. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

Consequently, the report does not establish that SspB-buffered respiratory genes are required for envelope remodeling or Δ*lpxc* rescue; it identifies that mechanism as a working hypothesis requiring additional tests. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
