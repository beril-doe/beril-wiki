# ICEfinder

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** ICEfinder. **Known aliases:** none reported in this document. **Stable external identifier:** none reported in this document. [^t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

ICEfinder is a computational method used to identify integrative conjugative elements (ICEs) and related mobile genetic elements in genome sequences. [^t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Key facts from the source

In the analysis of type IV secretion system (T4SS) and carbohydrate-active enzyme neighborhoods, ICEfinder detected no CAZy genes on plasmids. [^t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

Among the top 100 mobile-element accumulators, 12 integrative mobilizable elements (IMEs) were identified. [^t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

The ICEfinder result, together with the observation that T4SS-positive genomes had 10× higher mobile genetic element density than other genomes, was consistent with chromosomal or integrative transfer rather than plasmid mobilization. [^t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

These observations support, but do not establish, the hypothesis that T4SS-associated [gt2-glycosyltransferases](gt2-glycosyltransferases.md) diversity can disseminate through chromosomal or integrative mechanisms involving IMEs or integrative conjugative elements. [^t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
