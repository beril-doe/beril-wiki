# Particulate methane monooxygenase subunit B gene

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Particulate methane monooxygenase subunit B gene. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

**Known aliases:** pmoB; K10945. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

**Stable external identifier:** KEGG Orthology K10945. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

Particulate methane monooxygenase subunit B is represented in this study by the pmoB gene and KO K10945. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

## Evidence from Harvard Forest warming

In the 25-year +5°C Harvard Forest soil-warming experiment, pmoB (K10945) showed a positive RNA log2 fold change of **+0.669** in organic soil, with **p=0.012**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

In mineral soil, pmoB (K10945) showed a positive RNA log2 fold change of **+0.880**, with **p=0.054**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The pmoB signals were nominal and did not survive false-discovery-rate correction across 14K KOs. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

These results provide directional transcript-pool evidence for increased particulate methane monooxygenase subunit B representation under warming in both horizons, but they do not establish a statistically significant FDR-controlled response. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
