# R2A medium

R2A medium is a rich medium in which 10 ENIGMA groundwater isolates were profiled, giving 1,050 observations in the Web of Microbes snapshot. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

## Key facts

The fraction of metabolite changes that are de novo production ("emerged", E) rather than amplification of existing metabolites ("increased", I) varies 2-fold across these isolates in R2A. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer] The report calls the 15–32% range of de novo production a new metabolic novelty rate measure, enabled by the E/I distinction. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

## Novelty rates by isolate

## Interpretation and caveats

The report hypothesizes that FW300-N2E3 trehalose secretion during growth on R2A may reflect constitutive stress protection rather than metabolic overflow. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

R2A is a rich medium, whereas Fitness Browser (FB) fitness was measured on minimal medium with single C/N sources. Some WoM metabolites may therefore appear only on rich media, which limits comparison between the two datasets. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
