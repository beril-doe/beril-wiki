# Methionine

## What this entity is

Methionine is an amino acid assessed through its biosynthesis pathway in a community-scale comparison of pathway completeness and ambient metabolite intensity. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

- **Canonical name:** Methionine. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
- **Known aliases:** None reported in the source document. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
- **Stable external identifier:** None reported in the source document. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

## Evidence from the NMDC community-metabolic-ecology analysis

Methionine biosynthesis had the largest negative correlation among the tested amino-acid pathways, with Spearman r = −0.496, n = 18, and q = 0.117. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

The negative association indicates that samples with greater community methionine-pathway completeness tended to have lower ambient methionine metabolite intensity, a direction consistent with the community-scale Black Queen Hypothesis interpretation tested in the report. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

Despite its largest effect size, the association was not significant after Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction because q = 0.117 exceeded 0.05. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

The report therefore treats methionine as suggestive but underpowered evidence rather than as a statistically supported pathway-level finding. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

The limited sample size of n = 18 reduced power, and the report identifies larger metabolomics datasets as necessary to evaluate the methionine association more reliably. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
