## Evidence from lignin enrichment

Aeromonas increased from 0.1% in lignin-only communities to 20.2% when labile carbon was co-supplemented with lignin. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

This enrichment was part of a broader shift toward a distinct, fast-growing copiotrophic assemblage: under labile-carbon supplementation, [[entities/acinetobacter-baylyi-adp1|Acinetobacter]] increased from 25.2% to 41.7%, [[entities/pseudomonas-aeruginosa|Pseudomonas]] declined from 39.3% to 23.0%, and Aeromonas increased to 20.2%. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The result supports [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness|condition-specific fitness]] as an interpretation of Aeromonas enrichment, because its abundance changed substantially with the carbon regime rather than simply tracking lignin enrichment alone. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

In the L-versus-LC comparison, Aeromonas had a CLR effect of +6.4 in the labile-carbon condition. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The reported Aeromonas enrichment is based on 16S amplicon community composition, not direct measurement of Aeromonas lignin-degradation genes or pathway activity. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

## Follow-up relevance
