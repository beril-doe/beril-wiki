## Evidence from lignin enrichment

Aeromonas increased from 0.1% in lignin-only communities to 20.2% when labile carbon was co-supplemented with lignin. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

This enrichment was part of a broader shift toward a distinct, fast-growing copiotrophic assemblage: under labile-carbon supplementation, [Acinetobacter](acinetobacter-baylyi-adp1.md) increased from 25.2% to 41.7%, [Pseudomonas](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) declined from 39.3% to 23.0%, and Aeromonas increased to 20.2%. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The result supports [condition-specific fitness](../concepts/condition-specific-fitness.md) as an interpretation of Aeromonas enrichment, because its abundance changed substantially with the carbon regime rather than simply tracking lignin enrichment alone. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

In the L-versus-LC comparison, Aeromonas had a CLR effect of +6.4 in the labile-carbon condition. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The reported Aeromonas enrichment is based on 16S amplicon community composition, not direct measurement of Aeromonas lignin-degradation genes or pathway activity. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Follow-up relevance

The document proposes cross-referencing lignin-enriched genera with annotations in [KBase KE pangenome](kbase-ke-pangenome.md) to connect taxonomic enrichment with pathway content; this could test whether Aeromonas carries genomic features associated with the observed labile-carbon response. [^lignin_community_enrichment]
