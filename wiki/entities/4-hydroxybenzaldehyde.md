# 4-Hydroxybenzaldehyde

## Identity

**Canonical name:** 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source document. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

**Stable external identifier:** The compound was structure-resolved with an InChIKey through [pubchem](pubchem.md), but the exact InChIKey was not reported in the source document. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Key Facts

4-Hydroxybenzaldehyde was one of the 8 compounds with an ENIGMA-isolate utilizer call in the ENIGMA Carbon Census. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

It belonged to the Shikimates/Phenylpropanoids chemical class, which contained 5 callable compounds among 25 census compounds. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The census identified 125 ENIGMA-isolate utilizer prediction rows for 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

Across the 8 ENIGMA-isolate-callable compounds, 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde was among the capacities whose distribution was evaluated for co-occurrence and specialization. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The strongest reported cross-block association involving this compound was with Phenylethylamine, with an odds ratio of 2.84, a 95% confidence interval of 1.49–5.40, q=0.035, and n11=11. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The reported 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde–Phenylethylamine association was accompanied by a Jaccard index of approximately 0.04, indicating limited genome-level overlap despite the odds-ratio signal. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

Among the implicated utilizer genera, Novosphingobium and Sphingobium showed approximately 92–100% 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde capacity. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

Comamonas showed 85% capacity for 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

GTDB placement included 47 Sphingomonadales strains associated with 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde utilization. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The project treated the callable phenotype as specialist-dominated: 675 genomes carried exactly 1 of the 8 aromatic/alkaloid capacities, whereas 18 carried at least 3, with a maximum of 4 capacities. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The broader chemical-class coverage hypothesis was not formally supported: the chi-square test across the 8 ENIGMA-isolate-callable compounds gave χ²=5.07, p=0.53, df=6. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]
