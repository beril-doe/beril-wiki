# Pedobacter

## What it is

**Pedobacter** is the canonical genus name for this organism entity. [^lab_field_ecology]

- **Known aliases:** No aliases are specified in the source document. [^lab_field_ecology]
- **Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in the source document. [^lab_field_ecology]

## Evidence from lab_field_ecology

*Pedobacter* was one of 14 genera represented in the [kescience-fitnessbrowser](kescience-fitnessbrowser.md) that were detected in [enigma-coral](enigma-coral.md) groundwater communities across 108 [oak-ridge-field-research-center](oak-ridge-field-research-center.md) sites. [^lab_field_ecology]

*Pedobacter* was among the 11 detected genera with prevalence of at least 10 sites that were tested for association with uranium concentration. [^lab_field_ecology]

Its abundance decreased with uranium concentration, with Spearman rho=-0.266, p=0.005, and Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate (BH-FDR) q=0.013. [^lab_field_ecology]

This association passed the report's FDR significance threshold of q<0.05 and was one of five significant genus-level associations, which occurred in both positive and negative directions. [^lab_field_ecology]

The association was measured using community profiles generated by [16s-amplicon-sequencing](16s-amplicon-sequencing.md), a marker-gene method that resolved the field observations at genus level rather than species or strain level. [^lab_field_ecology]

The finding contributes to the report's conclusion that field community composition varies along the uranium gradient and reflects multiple geochemical and historical dimensions rather than a simple increase in laboratory metal-tolerant organisms. [^lab_field_ecology]
