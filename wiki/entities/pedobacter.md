# Pedobacter

## What it is

**Pedobacter** is the canonical genus name for this organism entity. [src: lab_field_ecology]

- **Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in the source document. [src: lab_field_ecology]

## Evidence from lab_field_ecology

*Pedobacter* was one of 14 genera represented in the [[entities/kescience-fitnessbrowser]] that were detected in [[entities/enigma-coral]] groundwater communities across 108 [[entities/oak-ridge-field-research-center]] sites. [src: lab_field_ecology]

*Pedobacter* was among the 11 detected genera with prevalence of at least 10 sites that were tested for association with uranium concentration. [src: lab_field_ecology]

Its abundance decreased with uranium concentration, with Spearman rho=-0.266, p=0.005, and Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate (BH-FDR) q=0.013. [src: lab_field_ecology]

This association passed the report's FDR significance threshold of q<0.05 and was one of five significant genus-level associations, which occurred in both positive and negative directions. [src: lab_field_ecology]

The association was measured using community profiles generated by [[entities/16s-amplicon-sequencing]], a marker-gene method that resolved the field observations at genus level rather than species or strain level. [src: lab_field_ecology]

The finding contributes to the report's conclusion that field community composition varies along the uranium gradient and reflects multiple geochemical and historical dimensions rather than a simple increase in laboratory metal-tolerant organisms. [src: lab_field_ecology]
