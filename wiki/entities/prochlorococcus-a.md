# Prochlorococcus A sp.

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Prochlorococcus A sp. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Known alias:** Prochlorococcus A. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

Prochlorococcus A sp. is an organism analyzed for metabolic capability, pathway variation, and metabolic ecotype structure in the [metabolic capability versus dependency study](../summaries/metabolic_capability_dependency__REPORT.md). [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

## Findings in metabolic capability and dependency analysis

Prochlorococcus A sp. was one of 10 target species analyzed for metabolic clustering, with a minimum of 50 genomes and at least 15 variable pathways required for inclusion. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The analysis included 74 Prochlorococcus A genomes, assigned them to 2 metabolic clusters, and reported a silhouette score of 0.429. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

A silhouette score measures how well observations fit their assigned cluster relative to neighboring clusters; the reported score indicates a positive but moderate metabolic-clustering structure for Prochlorococcus A sp. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The Prochlorococcus A sp. clusters were not significantly associated with isolation environment. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The study reported that marine-organism non-associations, including the result for Prochlorococcus A sp., may reflect insufficient isolation-source metadata, depth- or nutrient-zone structure, or pathway annotations that lack resolution for relevant gene-content differences. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

This environment-cluster result is observational and does not establish that environment causes the metabolic clustering. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]
