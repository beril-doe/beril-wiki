# Burkholderiales

Burkholderiales is a betaproteobacterial order whose genera dominate predicted aromatic-catabolism versatility and periphyton abundance. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1] Most of the evidence comes from [[summaries/enigma_carbon_census_1__REPORT]]. One family-level PHB result comes from [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]]. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Key facts

- Callable catabolic capacity in the census is phylogenetically concentrated in Burkholderiales and is a specialist trait. The census has 675 single-capacity genomes and only 18 generalists with ≥3 capacities. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- Predicted isolate utilizers concentrate in Pseudomonadota/Burkholderiales. Pseudomonadales and Sphingomonadales add compound-specific contributions, for example 47 Sphingomonadales strains for 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- The report supports phylogenetic concentration. Burkholderiales dominate utilizer counts, and the high-versatility genera Polaromonas, Alcaligenes, Burkholderia, Paraburkholderia, Comamonas and Hydrogenophaga cluster in that order. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Versatility as a clade trait

Generalist genome chassis concentrate in Paraburkholderia (5), Burkholderia (4), Hydrogenophaga (2) and scattered Burkholderiaceae. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1] This is the same clade that dominates the versatility ranking. The report reads this as confirming that versatility is a clade trait, not a widely portable module. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The report says the literature supports Burkholderiales/Comamonadaceae dominance best for phthalate/terephthalate catabolism, which matches its versatility ranking. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1] See [[concepts/taxonomic-distribution-of-aromatic-compound-catabolism]] and [[concepts/co-occurrence-and-modularity-of-catabolic-capacities-across-genomes]].

## Periphyton abundance

Separating a periphyton (freshwater-biofilm) ENVO/GOLD class (ENVO = Environment Ontology) from bulk freshwater reveals a Comamonadaceae/Burkholderiales reservoir at ~97% prevalence. The report says this is relevant to siting SSO-relevant enrichments. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1] The periphyton class comprises epilithon, epipsammon and epiphyton biofilms. It is a strong reservoir for Rhizobacter, Variovorax, Polaromonas, Methylibium, Sphingomonas and Hydrogenophaga, at ~96–97% prevalence and mean relative abundance ~0.005–0.009. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1] See [[concepts/biome-abundance-atlases-as-a-proxy-for-catabolic-potential]].

Caveat: this enrichment rests mainly on the project's own NB07b abundance data (~97% prevalence in periphyton samples), not on a comparative-microbiology citation. The report says to read it as the project's own observation pending a targeted literature anchor. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## PHB-enriched families

In the PHB granule ecology analysis, enriched Pseudomonadota families include Burkholderiaceae and Burkholderiaceae_B, alongside Rhodocyclaceae, Caulobacteraceae, Sphingomonadaceae, Xanthobacteraceae, Rhodanobacteraceae and Legionellaceae. [src: phb_granule_ecology] See [[concepts/phylogenetic-distribution-of-phb-synthesis-across-bacteria-and-archaea]].
