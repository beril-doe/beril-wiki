# Malassezia

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Malassezia. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Known aliases:** None reported in the source. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Stable external identifier:** None reported in the source. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Malassezia is a fungal genus detected in the Round-2 communities analyzed by the study. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Evidence from lignin-enriched communities

Malassezia reached 63.4% relative abundance in the L-L group, which received lignin in Round 1 and lignin again in Round 2. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Malassezia reached 50.0% relative abundance in the LC-L group, which received lignin plus labile carbon in Round 1 and lignin in Round 2. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The difference between 63.4% in L-L and 50.0% in LC-L indicates that Malassezia was dominant but variable across Round-2 histories under the same current lignin condition. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Fungal Round-2 replicate consistency was poor, with within-group Bray–Curtis distances reaching 0.99–1.00 for several groups, so the reported Malassezia dominance should be treated as preliminary rather than a robust reproducible response. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The fungal Round-2 history effect was not statistically detectable (R²=0.142, p=0.090), limiting attribution of Malassezia abundance differences specifically to Round-1 history. [^lignin_community_enrichment]
