# Pectobacterium carotovorum

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** *Pectobacterium carotovorum*. **Known aliases:** not reported in the source document. **Stable external identifier:** not reported in the source document. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Evidence from the document

*Pectobacterium carotovorum* was one of 12 species in which valid gene-content ecotypes were detected during the study. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The analysis included 57 genomes of *Pectobacterium carotovorum*, identified 2 ecotypes, and assigned all 57 genomes to an ecotype. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The ecotypes were identified by applying principal component analysis (PCA) to genome gene-content profiles and then using KMeans clustering, with candidate cluster counts from 2 through 6 selected by silhouette score. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The study compared ecotype profiles across COG (Clusters of Orthologous Groups) functional categories using chi-square or Fisher’s exact tests with Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction at q < 0.05. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

These findings support the broader conclusion that within-species gene-content variation can be functionally differentiated, although the report states that the analysis cannot distinguish ecological adaptation from phylogenetic or demographic substructure without within-species phylogenetic controls. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]
