# Rare earth elements

Rare earth elements (REE; including cerium and lanthanum) are the lanthanide group of metals, treated here as a class. The [[entities/fitness-browser]] holds no experiments on them. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]

## Key facts

- The Fitness Browser contains no rare earth element experiments. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]
- The metal_fitness_atlas report describes REEs as the most strategically important critical minerals, and calls their bioleaching/biorecovery a rapidly growing field. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]
- Cerium is the most abundant REE. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]

## Environmental association with the xoxF cassette

In the lanthanide methylotrophy atlas, 37 REE-impacted genomes had an xoxF rate of 10.81 %. The odds ratio was 3.51 against generic_environmental, with BH-FDR (Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate) p_BH 0.082. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas] The report describes this as a 3.5-fold descriptive elevation (10.8 % vs 3.7 % baseline). It says n=37 is too small to clear the false discovery rate (FDR) threshold. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas] The enrichment is therefore descriptive only. A larger collection of explicitly REE-impacted metagenomes would test whether REE supply per se selects for the cassette. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas] See [[concepts/environmental-association-of-lanthanide-dependent-methanol-oxidation-genes]] and [[summaries/lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas__REPORT]].

## Proposed experiments

These are proposals, not results. See [[concepts/metal-coverage-gaps-and-proposed-validation-experiments]] and [[summaries/metal_fitness_atlas__REPORT]].

- metal_fitness_atlas proposes lanthanum chloride RB-TnSeq (random barcode transposon sequencing) at 0.1-1 mM in Putida, Cup4G11, Keio and Marino. The aim is the first genome-wide REE tolerance gene set. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]
- It also proposes cerium chloride at 0.1-1 mM on the same panel. Comparing La and Ce fitness profiles would show whether tolerance uses a shared or an element-specific gene set. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]
- The lanthanide methylotrophy atlas proposes targeted RB-TnSeq with lanthanum / cerium chloride in the Fitness Browser organism panel, following the metal_fitness_atlas Priority 2 wet-lab proposal. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- It expects genome-wide fitness under La/Ce stress to give the first transposon-validated REE-tolerance gene set. The target organisms are xoxF-carrying Pseudomonas putida, Cupriavidus, Sphingomonas and similar soil organisms. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
