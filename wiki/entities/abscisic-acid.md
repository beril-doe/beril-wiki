Abscisic acid is a compound classified in the Terpenoids chemical class in the ENIGMA Carbon Census. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Key facts from the ENIGMA Carbon Census

Abscisic acid was one of 8 compounds with an ENIGMA-isolate utilizer prediction, alongside salicylic acid, 3-hydroxybenzoic acid, 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde, phthalic acid, terephthalic acid, Phenylethylamine, and xanthine. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The census reported 2 ENIGMA-isolate utilizer prediction rows for Abscisic acid, out of 569 rows across the 8 ENIGMA-isolate-callable compounds. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

Abscisic acid belonged to the Terpenoids group, which contributed 1 of the 8 ENIGMA-isolate-callable compounds. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The document reported that the chemical-class coverage hypothesis was not formally supported: the chi-square test across the 8 ENIGMA-isolate-callable compounds gave χ²=5.07, p=0.53, and df=6. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The project’s callable-compound analysis was underpowered because only 9 callable compounds were available before the xanthine carbon-catabolism correction, and the reported physicochemical comparisons were descriptive rather than calibrated inference. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]
