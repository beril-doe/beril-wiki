# Methanoperedens

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Methanoperedens. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

**Known alias:** “Ca. Methanoperedens,” the notation used in the report. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in the source document. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Methanoperedens was one of the taxa used in genus-level biogeochemical functional inference from SSO 16S amplicon sequence variant communities. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence from SSO subsurface communities

Methanotrophy peaked at M4 at 1.9%, with *Ca. Methanoperedens* identified as a key genus. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

In the groundwater–sediment comparison, *Ca. Methanoperedens* represented 0.42% of sediment communities and 0.00% of groundwater communities, and was reported as 0× enriched in groundwater relative to sediment. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The report interprets the sediment enrichment of *Ca. Methanoperedens*, together with enrichment of other anaerobic taxa, as evidence that sediment-associated and groundwater communities are distinct rather than representing simple detachment. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

These assignments are based on taxonomy-to-trait inference rather than direct genomic evidence from the specific SSO populations. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Genus-level functional annotation covered 65 of 1,038 genera and 21% of total reads, so the reported process abundance estimates are treated as lower bounds. [^enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
