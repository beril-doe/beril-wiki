# Myoinositol

## Identity

**Canonical name:** myoinositol. [^cf_formulation_design]

**Known alias:** myo-inositol. [^cf_formulation_design]

**Stable external identifier:** Not provided in the source report. [^cf_formulation_design]

Myoinositol is a compound proposed as a selective prebiotic substrate for [rothia-dentocariosa](rothia-dentocariosa.md) in formulations intended to suppress [pseudomonas-aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) in cystic-fibrosis airways. [^cf_formulation_design]

## Evidence from cf_formulation_design

Genomic pathway comparison identified myoinositol as one of six pathways complete in at least one core commensal species but absent or nearly absent in PA14. [^cf_formulation_design]

PA pathway completeness for myoinositol was 0%, corresponding to a selectivity value of 1.00. [^cf_formulation_design]

The report predicts that myoinositol can support [rothia-dentocariosa](rothia-dentocariosa.md), shifting prebiotic design away from amino-acid competition toward sugar alcohols and pentoses. [^cf_formulation_design]

This prediction requires experimental validation before use. [^cf_formulation_design]
