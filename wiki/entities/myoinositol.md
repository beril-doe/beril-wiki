# Myoinositol

## Identity

**Canonical name:** myoinositol. [src: cf_formulation_design]

**Known alias:** myo-inositol. [src: cf_formulation_design]

**Stable external identifier:** Not provided in the source report. [src: cf_formulation_design]

Myoinositol is a compound proposed as a selective prebiotic substrate for [[entities/rothia-dentocariosa]] in formulations intended to suppress [[entities/pseudomonas-aeruginosa]] in cystic-fibrosis airways. [src: cf_formulation_design]

## Evidence from cf_formulation_design

Genomic pathway comparison identified myoinositol as one of six pathways complete in at least one core commensal species but absent or nearly absent in PA14. [src: cf_formulation_design]

PA pathway completeness for myoinositol was 0%, corresponding to a selectivity value of 1.00. [src: cf_formulation_design]

The report predicts that myoinositol can support [[entities/rothia-dentocariosa]], shifting prebiotic design away from amino-acid competition toward sugar alcohols and pentoses. [src: cf_formulation_design]

This prediction requires experimental validation before use. [src: cf_formulation_design]

## Related research

The compound is discussed in the source summary [[summaries/cf_formulation_design__REPORT]] and relates to [[concepts/metabolic-model-gapfilling]], where pathway completeness comparisons identify metabolic gaps between PA14 and candidate commensals. [src: cf_formulation_design]

It also contributes to [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]], because its proposed value depends on substrate-specific growth in the cystic-fibrosis airway environment. [src: cf_formulation_design]
