# Eggerthella lenta

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Eggerthella lenta. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in this document. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in this document. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

Eggerthella lenta is a bacterial species prioritized as a Tier-A pathobiont target in the report’s Crohn’s disease and inflammatory bowel disease analysis. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

## Evidence from ibd_phage_targeting

Eggerthella lenta received a total Tier-A prioritization score of **3.3**. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

In the donor-2708-engraftment pathobiont analysis, E. lenta was CD-up with a CLR-Δ of **+2.30** in the confound-free test. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

E. lenta was one of three species identified as an active network member in the bile-acid 7α-dehydroxylation narrative, together with [flavonifractor-plautii](flavonifractor-plautii.md) and [enterocloster-bolteae](enterocloster-bolteae.md). [^ibd_phage_targeting]

In **468 paired HMP2 samples**, E. lenta correlated with ketodeoxycholate at **ρ = −0.14**. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The report interpreted these bile-acid associations as supporting a bile-acid coupling cost for targeting E. lenta, although the cost was assigned as lower than the highest cost assigned to F. plautii. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

E. lenta was detected in **16 of 23 UC Davis Crohn’s disease patients (70 %)**. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The proposed state-dependent workflow retained E. lenta in the universal Tier-1 trio with [mediterraneibacter-gnavus](mediterraneibacter-gnavus.md) and [hungatella-hathewayi](hungatella-hathewayi.md). [^ibd_phage_targeting]

In the longitudinal trajectory of patient 6967, E. lenta increased **3.1×** between visits. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

## Phage relevance

E. lenta was classified as having lytic-literature phage evidence in the report’s three-layer phage evidence stack. [^ibd_phage_targeting]

The report recommended monitored lytic-phage targeting for E. lenta rather than treating available evidence as proof of in-vivo efficacy. [^ibd_phage_targeting]
