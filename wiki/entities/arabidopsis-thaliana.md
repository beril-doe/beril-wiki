## PaperBLAST coverage

*Arabidopsis thaliana* is one of the five organisms with the greatest representation in PaperBLAST gene–paper records, alongside *Homo sapiens*, *Mus musculus*, *Rattus norvegicus*, and *Drosophila melanogaster*. [^paperblast_explorer]

Together, these five organisms account for **72.8%** of all gene–paper records in the collection. [^paperblast_explorer]

PaperBLAST contains literature for **20,723** organisms, with the top **1,000** organisms capturing **94.2%** of the literature and the remaining **19,723** organisms accounting for **5.8%**. [^paperblast_explorer]

The organism-level literature distribution has a Gini coefficient of **0.967**, indicating extreme concentration of coverage among a small number of organisms. [^paperblast_explorer]
