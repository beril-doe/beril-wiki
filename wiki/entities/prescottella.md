# Prescottella

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Prescottella [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

**Known aliases:** No aliases are reported in the source document [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in the source document [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

Prescottella is an organismal genus prioritized for active-learning experiments because of its low observed growth across tested conditions [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

## Evidence from genotype-to-phenotype modeling

Prescottella showed growth across 16% of the conditions tested in the integrated growth-phenotype corpus [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

The study selected Prescottella as an especially informative genus for follow-up because its low growth rate across tested conditions could expose model failures and improve discrimination among poorly characterized genotype–condition combinations [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

The active-learning framework ranked conditions using error rate, model uncertainty, and field-relevance weight, then proposed 50 next experiments [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

The proposed active-learning experiments corresponded to 7,844 current prediction failures in the genus-blocked holdout audit [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

The report states that formal retrospective comparison of active-learning selection with random selection remains outstanding [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

## Related research context

Prescottella is relevant to [condition-specific-fitness](../concepts/condition-specific-fitness.md), which distinguishes predictable binary growth capability from poorly predictable continuous growth kinetics [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

Its prioritization also contributes to [subsurface-bacillota-specialization](../concepts/subsurface-bacillota-specialization.md), because the proposed experiments include acidic, nitrogen-related, and organic-acid conditions relevant to contaminated subsurface physiology [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

The source report integrates growth curves, genome content, environmental data, and active-learning analysis as part of [multi-omics-integration](../concepts/multi-omics-integration.md) [^genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]
