# Prescottella

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Prescottella [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

**Known aliases:** No aliases are reported in the source document [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in the source document [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

Prescottella is an organismal genus prioritized for active-learning experiments because of its low observed growth across tested conditions [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

## Evidence from genotype-to-phenotype modeling

Prescottella showed growth across 16% of the conditions tested in the integrated growth-phenotype corpus [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

The study selected Prescottella as an especially informative genus for follow-up because its low growth rate across tested conditions could expose model failures and improve discrimination among poorly characterized genotype–condition combinations [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

The active-learning framework ranked conditions using error rate, model uncertainty, and field-relevance weight, then proposed 50 next experiments [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

The proposed active-learning experiments corresponded to 7,844 current prediction failures in the genus-blocked holdout audit [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]

The report states that formal retrospective comparison of active-learning selection with random selection remains outstanding [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]
