# Rattus norvegicus

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Rattus norvegicus. [^paperblast_explorer]

**Known alias:** R. norvegicus. [^paperblast_explorer]

**Stable external identifier:** Not specified in the paperblast_explorer report. [^paperblast_explorer]

## Key facts

Rattus norvegicus is one of the five organisms accounting together for 72.8% of all gene-paper records in the PaperBLAST collection. [^paperblast_explorer]

The other organisms in this top-five group are [homo-sapiens](homo-sapiens.md), Mus musculus, Arabidopsis thaliana, and [drosophila-melanogaster](drosophila-melanogaster.md). [^paperblast_explorer]

The report does not provide a separate gene-paper count or percentage for Rattus norvegicus. [^paperblast_explorer]

Its inclusion in the top-five group illustrates the organism-level research concentration analyzed in [gene-function-acquisition-depth](../concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth.md), where PaperBLAST coverage is used to identify uneven acquisition of functional knowledge. [^paperblast_explorer]

The organism-level analysis is part of the provenance and coverage characterization summarized in [provenance-aware-resource-discovery](../concepts/provenance-aware-resource-discovery.md) and documented in [paperblast_explorer__REPORT](../summaries/paperblast_explorer__REPORT.md).
