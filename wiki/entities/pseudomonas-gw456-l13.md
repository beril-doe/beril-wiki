# Pseudomonas sp. GW456-L13

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** *Pseudomonas* sp. GW456-L13. [^webofmicrobes_explorer]

**Known aliases:** GW456-L13; `pseudo13_GW456_L13`. [^webofmicrobes_explorer]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in this document. [^webofmicrobes_explorer]

This organism is an ENIGMA groundwater isolate with a direct match to the [Fitness Browser](kescience-fitnessbrowser.md) strain `pseudo13_GW456_L13`. [^webofmicrobes_explorer]

## Key facts

The direct Fitness Browser match contains 5,243 genes and 106 experiments. [^webofmicrobes_explorer]

The isolate was included among ENIGMA isolates grown in R2A medium in the 2018 [Web of Microbes data-explorer analysis](../summaries/webofmicrobes_explorer__REPORT.md). [^webofmicrobes_explorer]

Its Web of Microbes record contains 49 increased metabolites (`I`) and 34 emerged metabolites (`E`). [^webofmicrobes_explorer]

The fraction of emerged metabolites, calculated as `E/(E+I)`, was reported as 32.4%, representing the highest metabolic novelty rate among the five ENIGMA isolates listed in that comparison. [^webofmicrobes_explorer]

The organism's direct overlap with Fitness Browser provides a basis for connecting its metabolite-production observations to gene-fitness experiments, although the report does not establish a specific metabolite-to-gene result for this strain. [^webofmicrobes_explorer]
