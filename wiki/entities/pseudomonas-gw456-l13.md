# Pseudomonas sp. GW456-L13

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** *Pseudomonas* sp. GW456-L13. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

**Known aliases:** GW456-L13; `pseudo13_GW456_L13`. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in this document. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

This organism is an ENIGMA groundwater isolate with a direct match to the [[entities/kescience-fitnessbrowser|Fitness Browser]] strain `pseudo13_GW456_L13`. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

## Key facts

The direct Fitness Browser match contains 5,243 genes and 106 experiments. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

The isolate was included among ENIGMA isolates grown in R2A medium in the 2018 [[summaries/webofmicrobes_explorer__REPORT|Web of Microbes data-explorer analysis]]. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

Its Web of Microbes record contains 49 increased metabolites (`I`) and 34 emerged metabolites (`E`). [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

The fraction of emerged metabolites, calculated as `E/(E+I)`, was reported as 32.4%, representing the highest metabolic novelty rate among the five ENIGMA isolates listed in that comparison. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

The organism's direct overlap with Fitness Browser provides a basis for connecting its metabolite-production observations to gene-fitness experiments, although the report does not establish a specific metabolite-to-gene result for this strain. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]
