# 2,3-Butanediol

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** 2,3-butanediol. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

**Known aliases:** butanediol, as used as a carbon-source label in the report. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is provided in the source report. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Key facts

2,3-Butanediol was one of the three demanding carbon-source conditions in the ADP1 deletion-collection analysis, alongside urea and acetate. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

Across the demanding conditions, mean growth ratios ranged from 0.41 to 0.65, and 95–100% of genes showed defects at a growth ratio below 0.8. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

The report identifies 2,3-butanediol dehydrogenase and E2 acetyltransferase among the top condition-specific genes associated with growth on 2,3-butanediol. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

These findings support the interpretation that 2,3-butanediol exposes condition-dependent gene requirements rather than only binary essential/non-essential behavior. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
