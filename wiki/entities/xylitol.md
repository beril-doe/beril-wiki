# Xylitol

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Xylitol. [^cf_formulation_design]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source. [^cf_formulation_design]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source. [^cf_formulation_design]

Xylitol is a sugar alcohol proposed as a selective prebiotic substrate in formulations designed to support commensals against [pseudomonas-aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md). [^cf_formulation_design]

## Evidence from cf_formulation_design

Genomic pathway comparison identified xylitol as one of six pathways complete in at least one core commensal species but absent or nearly absent in PA14. [^cf_formulation_design]

PA pathway completeness for xylitol was 0%, and the corresponding selectivity value was 1.00. [^cf_formulation_design]

The report predicted that xylitol could support [streptococcus-salivarius](streptococcus-salivarius.md) growth preferentially over PA14. [^cf_formulation_design]

This prediction supports the [metabolic-model-gapfilling](../concepts/metabolic-model-gapfilling.md) concept because pathway-completeness comparisons identified a metabolic gap between PA14 and candidate commensals. [^cf_formulation_design]

The proposed xylitol strategy shifts prebiotic design away from amino-acid competition toward sugar alcohols and pentoses, because no tested amino acid or simple sugar provided a clear commensal growth advantage over PA14. [^cf_formulation_design]

The xylitol prediction requires experimental validation before use. [^cf_formulation_design]
