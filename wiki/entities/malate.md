# Malate

Malate is a metabolite with four-way database concordance for FW300-N2E3. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency] The evidence comes from a cross-database consistency comparison and a Web of Microbes (WoM) explorer report. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency, webofmicrobes_explorer]

## Key facts

- Three metabolites in the FW300-N2E3 comparison achieved concordance across all four databases, and malate is one of them. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- WoM data show malate as Increased for FW300-N2E3. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- Fitness Browser supported growth on malate, with 92 genes. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- In BacDive, malate was utilized in 49 of 49 cases (100%). [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- The GapMind pathway for malate was complete. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- The report treats malate, arginine and valine as the gold standard of cross-database consistency. The organism produces the metabolite and grows on it, the species utilizes it, and the pathway is computationally complete. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- The WoM explorer report links WoM malate with action I to the Fitness Browser condition L-Malic acid as carbon source for Pseudomonas FW300-N2E3. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]
