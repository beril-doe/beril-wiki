## Evidence from fw300_metabolic_consistency

Malate increased in the Web of Microbes exometabolome of *Pseudomonas* FW300-N2E3. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

Growth on malate in Fitness Browser involved 92 significant genes. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

Malate was utilized by 49/49 *Pseudomonas fluorescens* strains in BacDive, corresponding to 100% utilization. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

GapMind predicted a complete malate pathway in FW300-N2E3. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

Malate is one of three metabolites in the report with four-way consistency across WoM production, Fitness Browser growth, BacDive utilization, and GapMind pathway prediction. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]

The malate result supports the report's conclusion that some metabolite observations can be concordant across exometabolomics, gene-fitness, species-level phenotype, and pathway-prediction datasets. [^fw300_metabolic_consistency]
