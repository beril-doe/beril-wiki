# Alistipes onderdonkii

Alistipes onderdonkii is a gut commensal with substantial intraspecific metabolic diversity. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] It is one of the two species with the most metabolic ecotypes, 8. [src: discoveries, pathway_capability_dependency]

## Key facts

- Alistipes onderdonkii was a top ecotyped species, with 8 metabolic ecotypes. [src: discoveries]
- Barnesiella intestinihominis also had 8 ecotypes. [src: discoveries, pathway_capability_dependency]

## Relevance

With 8 ecotypes, the species is a leading example for [[concepts/within-species-metabolic-ecotypes-from-pathway-variation]]. [src: discoveries, pathway_capability_dependency] See also [[summaries/pathway_capability_dependency__REPORT]] and [[summaries/discoveries]].
