# COG0468

COG0468 is a functionally dark ortholog group (OG), meaning a gene family with no assigned function. The central discoveries digest names it first among the top-ranked unknowns, alongside [[entities/cog0443]] and cog0491 [src: discoveries].

## Rank among dark gene families

The functional dark matter project has a table of top knowledge gaps. COG0468 is rank 1 in that table. It is a kingdom-level true knowledge gap found in 27,427 species and 142 phyla, with an importance score of 23.9. The table describes it as "Pan-bacterial, present in virtually all sequenced genomes" [src: functional_dark_matter].

The project ranks all 11,774 dark gene OGs with an importance score of `conservation × ignorance`. The score takes a tier-adjusted conservation score plus a log₂-scaled species fraction and multiplies the result by an ignorance multiplier. Kingdom-level true knowledge gaps score highest and species-specific strong hypotheses score lowest. The reasoning is that experimental work on the most broadly conserved genes would yield the most new biological insight. The project's text on top-ranked OGs lists COG0468 (27,427 species, 142 phyla, true knowledge gap), [[entities/cog0443]] (27,279 species, true knowledge gap) and COG0172 (27,431 species, weak lead). The project calls these some of the most universally conserved genes in bacteria that are still functionally uncharacterized [src: functional_dark_matter].

## Pangenome-wide context

The discoveries digest reports a full pangenome analysis of 27,690 species (notebook NB11b). That analysis finds that over half of functionally dark gene OGs are pan-bacterial, present in thousands of species across multiple phyla. The number of species per dark OG ranges from 1 to 27,482, with a median of 135. According to the digest, COG0468, [[entities/cog0443]] and cog0491 are present in virtually all sequenced bacterial genomes yet have zero functional evidence. The digest concludes that functional dark matter is not a minor annotation gap. Instead, it is a fundamental limitation in understanding broadly conserved biology [src: discoveries].

## Caveats

The two lists of top OGs that come with COG0468 have different scopes. They do not disagree about its rank. The project's top-knowledge-gaps table is limited to true knowledge gaps. It lists COG0468, COG0443 and COG0491, the same three OGs the discoveries digest names [src: functional_dark_matter, discoveries]. The project's broader text on top-ranked OGs also includes COG0172, which it explicitly classes as a weak lead rather than a true knowledge gap [src: functional_dark_matter]. The phrase "virtually all sequenced genomes" is a description. The sources give a species count, not the fraction of genomes that carry the OG [src: discoveries, functional_dark_matter].
