# Flavobacterium

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Flavobacterium. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were specified in the source document. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was provided in the source document. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Flavobacterium is a bacterial genus profiled by 16S amplicon sequencing in the [lignin_community_enrichment__REPORT](../summaries/lignin_community_enrichment__REPORT.md) study. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Evidence from lignin community enrichment

Flavobacterium represented 0.1% of the base bacterial community. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

After one round of lignin-only enrichment, Flavobacterium increased to 14.1% of the bacterial community. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

In the Base-versus-Lignin comparison, Flavobacterium had a centered-log-ratio (CLR) effect of +11.2. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

When labile carbon was co-supplemented with lignin, Flavobacterium declined to 1.4%. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

This pattern supports a **condition-specific fitness** interpretation: Flavobacterium was strongly enriched under lignin-only conditions but was not similarly favored when labile carbon was added. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The source interpreted the response as conditional enrichment associated with the carbon regime, rather than as evidence that Flavobacterium was universally dominant in lignin-containing communities. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The study used 16S V3–V4 amplicon data from 21 samples across 7 groups, with 3 replicates per group, and assigned 86.5% of reads at the genus level. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The reported taxonomic response does not directly demonstrate lignin-degradation genes or pathway activity, because gene-level pathway enrichment was not measured. [^lignin_community_enrichment]
