# Terephthalic acid

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Terephthalic acid. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source document. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

**Stable external identifier:** The report states that all census compounds, including terephthalic acid, were structure-resolved with InChIKeys through [[entities/pubchem]], but it does not provide a compound-specific identifier. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Key facts

Terephthalic acid was one of 83 compounds included in the ENIGMA Carbon Census and was sourced from necromass. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

It was one of the 8 compounds with an ENIGMA-isolate utilizer call. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The census recorded 34 ENIGMA-isolate utilizer prediction rows for terephthalic acid. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

Terephthalic acid was represented among 494 strain records placed on [[entities/gtdb]] taxonomy, including 359 distinct strains across the full callable set. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

All 34 of 34 terephthalic-acid placement records were classified as high-certainty records. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The report cautions that the 34/34 high-certainty result was a single-reaction-signature artifact rather than strong evidence of pathway completeness, because certainty was calculated as the raw number of carried signature reactions divided by required reactions and is not directly comparable across compounds with different signature lengths. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

Terephthalic acid was concentrated in the necromass-sourced portion of the callable set, which contained only 2 of the 8 ENIGMA-isolate-callable compounds; both necromass-callable compounds, terephthalic acid and [[entities/phthalic-acid]], were phthalate-class aromatics with Actinomycetota-heavy utilizers. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The source-tracking hypothesis comparing groundwater and necromass was untestable because only 2 of the 8 ENIGMA-isolate-callable compounds were necromass-sourced, and both belonged to the phthalate class. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

Terephthalic acid contributes to the [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] workflow by linking compound-level pathway evidence to genome-scale utilizer predictions and [[entities/gtdb]] strain placement. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

It also contributes to [[concepts/cross-tenant-data-bridging]] because the census connected compound identity, genome annotations, ENIGMA-isolate predictions, and taxonomy across multiple resources. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
