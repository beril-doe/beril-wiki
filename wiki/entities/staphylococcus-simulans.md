# Staphylococcus simulans

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** *Staphylococcus simulans*. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

**Stable external identifier:** None was reported in the source. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Key facts from the document

*Staphylococcus simulans* was one of the 12 species with valid gene-content ecotypes in a stratified 15-species analysis of the BERDL pangenome database. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The analysis included 78 *Staphylococcus simulans* genomes, identified 3 ecotypes, and assigned 63 genomes to ecotypes. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

Its ecotype clustering had a silhouette score of 0.118, among the weakest separation signals reported in the study. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The ecotypes were identified using PCA, or principal component analysis, followed by KMeans clustering. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The species-level comparison tested COG, or Clusters of Orthologous Groups, functional profiles across ecotypes using chi-square or Fisher’s exact tests with BH-FDR, the Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

All 12 species with valid clusters had at least one significantly differentiated COG category, so *Staphylococcus simulans* contributed to the study’s conclusion that ecotype gene-content variation was not functionally random. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The study cautioned that the *Staphylococcus simulans* ecotype boundaries may overlap because the overall clustering used KMeans, which assumes spherical clusters, and because the reported analysis lacked within-species phylogenetic controls. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]
