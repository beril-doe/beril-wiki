# Particulate methane monooxygenase subunit A gene

## What this entity is

The canonical name is particulate methane monooxygenase subunit A gene, with the alias **pmoA** and KEGG Orthology identifier **K10944**. [^harvard_forest_warming]

## Evidence from Harvard Forest warming

- In the Harvard Forest long-term +5°C soil-warming experiment, pmoA RNA had a log2 fold change of **+0.730** in organic soil, with **p=0.009**, in heated relative to control samples. [^harvard_forest_warming]
- In mineral soil, pmoA RNA had a log2 fold change of **+0.743**, with **p=0.029**, in heated relative to control samples. [^harvard_forest_warming]
- The pmoA signals were directionally positive across both organic and mineral horizons but were nominal signals that did not survive false-discovery-rate correction across approximately **14K KOs**. [^harvard_forest_warming]
- The report describes the pmoA result as a directional gene-level RNA signal rather than an established quantitative increase in methane oxidation activity. [^harvard_forest_warming]

These findings connect pmoA to [multi-omics-integration](../concepts/multi-omics-integration.md) through the comparison of RNA functional-pool responses with other molecular layers, and to [ecotype-environment-gene-content](../concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content.md) through horizon-specific responses to long-term warming. [^harvard_forest_warming]
