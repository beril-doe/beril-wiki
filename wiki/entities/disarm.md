# DISARM

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** DISARM (defense systems associated with restriction-modification).

**Known aliases:** No additional aliases were reported in the source document. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

DISARM is one of seven anti-phage defense-system families analyzed across the BERDL pangenome. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

## Key facts

DISARM was detected in 58.4% of species-level pangenomes under the strict call. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The quality-filtered analysis included 7,323 species-level pangenomes with at least five genomes per species. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

DISARM showed significant positive co-occurrence with most other defense-system families in the phylum-stratified permutation analysis, including BREX × DISARM with 4,968 observed co-occurrences versus a null mean of 4,629, z = 31.1, and odds ratio (OR) = 8.2. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

Additional significant pairings involving DISARM included DISARM × Retron with 4,280 observed co-occurrences versus a null mean of 3,844, z = 28.4, and OR = 5.2; R-M Type I × DISARM with 4,970 versus 4,673, z = 27.5, and OR = 8.4; and R-M Type II × DISARM with 3,424 versus 2,981, z = 24.4, and OR = 4.1. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

DISARM was near the pangenome background in core and singleton composition, with 40.6% core gene clusters and 42.3% singleton gene clusters compared with background fractions of 46.8% and 37.9%, respectively. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The report treats DISARM as an unreliable result for accessory-enrichment analysis because the DrmB SNF2 helicase Pfam anchor, PF00176, is a widespread housekeeping-helicase domain. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

The source states that this DISARM classification problem does not affect its arms-race and syndrome results because those analyses use species-level presence or absence rather than per-cluster classification. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]

A PADLOC MacSyFinder-style refinement using profile-HMM and gene-order or context rules is recommended to distinguish DISARM loci from non-DISARM SNF2 helicases. [src: phage_defense_arsenal]
