# Planet Microbe

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Planet Microbe. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

**Known aliases:** No additional aliases were reported in the source. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

**Stable external identifier:** Not reported in the source. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

Planet Microbe is a marine metagenomic data source used in the ENIGMA Carbon Census environmental abundance atlas. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Role in the ENIGMA Carbon Census

The census analyzed 302 Planet Microbe marine runs to assess the environmental occurrence and abundance of genera implicated as compound utilizers. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The Planet Microbe analysis covered 68 listed genera, and all 68/68 showed positive abundance across the 302 runs. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The marine arm was primarily used as a negative biome contrast because terrestrial and freshwater genera generally occurred at approximately 1e-3 to 1e-4 abundance in open ocean samples. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

Alteromonas was identified as a genuine marine member, occurring at abundance 0.048 in 240/302 runs, with prevalence 0.79. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

Pseudomonas and Sphingomonas had abundances of 0.011 and 0.0063, respectively, in the Planet Microbe analysis. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Interpretation and limitations

Planet Microbe measurements represented organismal abundance or occurrence rather than catabolic activity because the environmental datasets did not measure the census compounds. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The marine arm was small and gene-blind, with 302 runs providing presence and abundance data only. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The Planet Microbe data were integrated with compound identity, pathway and genome annotations, measured fitness, taxonomy, and other environmental datasets in the tiered workflow described in [[summaries/enigma_carbon_census_1__REPORT]]. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

This dataset therefore contributes an environmental-occupancy comparison to [[concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content]] and a cross-resource integration example for [[concepts/cross-tenant-data-bridging]] and [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]]. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
