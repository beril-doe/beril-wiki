## Evidence from the report

Enterococcus D gallinarum was one of the 12 species in which valid gene-content ecotypes were detected. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The analysis included **95 genomes**, identified **3 ecotypes**, and assigned **86 genomes** to ecotypes for Enterococcus D gallinarum. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The ecotypes were identified by principal component analysis followed by KMeans clustering, where KMeans partitions observations into a selected number of clusters. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The species-level ecotype result contributes to the report’s conclusion that within-species gene-content variation is functionally differentiated across the sampled species, although the report does not provide a species-specific COG result for Enterococcus D gallinarum in the supplied summary.
