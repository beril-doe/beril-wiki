# Rhizobium

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Rhizobium [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

**Known aliases:** None reported in this document [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

**Stable external identifier:** None reported in this document [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

Rhizobium is a bacterial genus identified as one of the dominant genera in the plant root-associated genome census. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

## Key facts from the document

The census classified 292 species and 2,446 genomes as root-associated, and root communities were dominated by Rhizobium, Mesorhizobium, and Bradyrhizobium. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

Across all plant contexts, the study identified 1,136 plant-associated species and 7,995 plant-associated genomes from a total of 293,059 GTDB r214 genomes. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

The report did not provide a Rhizobium-specific species or genome count within the root-associated census. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]
