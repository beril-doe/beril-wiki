# Acetatifactor intestinalis

## Identity

**Canonical name:** *Acetatifactor intestinalis*. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Known aliases:** None reported in the supplied document. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

**Stable external identifier:** None reported in the supplied document. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

## Key facts

*Acetatifactor intestinalis* was one of the 10 target species analyzed for within-species metabolic ecotype structure. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The analysis included 59 genomes of *Acetatifactor intestinalis*, identified 6 metabolic clusters, and obtained a silhouette score of 0.657. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

Its silhouette score exceeded the study’s 0.2 criterion for metabolic clustering, supporting separation of genomes into metabolic-profile clusters in this dataset. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The study reported metabolic clustering for all 10 target species, but did not report a statistically significant isolation-environment association specifically for *Acetatifactor intestinalis*. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

The ecotype analysis was observational, and the study did not apply explicit phylogenetic correction; therefore, the reported clustering should not be interpreted as establishing an environmental cause. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]
