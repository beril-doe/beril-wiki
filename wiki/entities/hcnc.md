# hcnC

## What it is

**Canonical name:** hcnC  
**Known aliases:** None reported in this document.  
**Stable external identifier:** None reported in this document.  

hcnC is a gene marker analyzed as part of plant-growth-promoting (PGP) gene distribution across the BERDL pangenome. [^pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Key facts

hcnC showed significant positive co-occurrence with pqqC, which had an odds ratio (OR) of 1.91 in the focal-gene pair analysis. [^pgp_pangenome_ecology]

hcnC was also part of the putative pqqC–acdS–hcnC rhizosphere-effectiveness module identified from co-occurrence patterns. [^pgp_pangenome_ecology]

Among 1,039 soil/rhizosphere species and 10,233 species from other environments, hcnC prevalence was 11.3% in soil/rhizosphere species versus 6.4% in other species, corresponding to OR = 1.85 and Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate q = 6.1e-08. [^pgp_pangenome_ecology]

The report therefore identifies hcnC as significantly enriched in soil/rhizosphere species, alongside acdS and pqqC. [^pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Interpretation and limitations

The hcnC enrichment supports the broader environment–gene-content relationship described in [ecotype-environment-gene-content](../concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content.md), while its co-occurrence with pqqC contributes to the proposed non-diazotrophic rhizosphere PGP module. [^pgp_pangenome_ecology]

The co-occurrence result does not establish physical linkage between hcnC and pqqC, because the analysis did not determine whether the genes occupy the same genomic island, operon, or separate loci. [^pgp_pangenome_ecology]

The report proposes focused hcnA–hcnC phylogeny as a next analysis. [^pgp_pangenome_ecology]
