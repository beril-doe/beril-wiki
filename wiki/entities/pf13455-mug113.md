# PF13455/Mug113

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** PF13455/Mug113 SNIPE-associated nuclease family. [src: snipe_defense_system]

**Known aliases:** PF13455; Mug113; SNIPE nuclease family. [src: snipe_defense_system]

**Stable external identifiers:** Pfam PF13455; GIY-YIG clan CL0418. [src: snipe_defense_system]

PF13455 is the nuclease-family component associated with the [[entities/snipe-defense-system]] architecture, and it belongs to GIY-YIG clan CL0418 while remaining distinct from canonical GIY-YIG PF01541. [src: snipe_defense_system]

## Key facts

The *E. coli* SNIPE protein A0A0A1A5Z2 is 558 aa long and contains PF13250 at positions 232–333 and PF13455 at positions 443–520. [src: snipe_defense_system]

Among the surveyed KBase Data Lakehouse annotations, zero gene clusters contained both DUF4041/PF13250 and canonical GIY-YIG PF01541. [src: snipe_defense_system]

Of 4,572 DUF4041-containing gene clusters, 54 carried the description “Meiotically up-regulated gene 113,” consistent with full-length SNIPE proteins containing the Mug113 nuclease annotation. [src: snipe_defense_system]

The report recommends searching PF13250, DUF4041, or T5orf172 together with PF13455 rather than using PF01541 as the SNIPE nuclease marker. [src: snipe_defense_system]

PF13455 occurred in 7 genes across 6 Fitness Browser organisms: [[entities/azospirillum-brasilense]], [[entities/bacteroides-thetaiotaomicron]], Cupriavidus, [[entities/desulfovibrio-vulgaris-hildenborough]], Paraburkholderia kururiensis, and [[entities/methanococcus-maripaludis]]. [src: snipe_defense_system]

Only [[entities/methanococcus-maripaludis]] had the complete PF13250-plus-PF13455 architecture in the Fitness Browser survey. [src: snipe_defense_system]

The complete two-domain SNIPE protein in *Methanococcus maripaludis* JJ occurred at locus MMJJ_RS01635, carried PF13250, PF13455, and PF10544, and had 129 experiments of fitness data. [src: snipe_defense_system]

That *M. maripaludis* protein had minimum fitness of -1.16 on formate/acetate and was dispensable under most conditions. [src: snipe_defense_system]

## Context and related pages

PF13455 is part of the corrected domain architecture described in the [[summaries/snipe_defense_system__REPORT]], which links SNIPE nuclease activity to phage-defense action at the ManYZ transporter pore. [src: snipe_defense_system]

The family therefore supports the SNIPE architecture discussed in [[concepts/phage-defense-syndromes-and-arms-race]] and the pangenome-distribution analysis discussed in [[concepts/pangenome-integration]]. [src: snipe_defense_system]
