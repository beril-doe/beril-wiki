# Franzosa

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Franzosa. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

**Known aliases:** No aliases are reported in the source document. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in the source document. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

Franzosa is a microbiome/metabolomics dataset used alongside [[entities/hmp2]] for cross-cohort validation in the [[summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT]]. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Key facts from ibd_phage_targeting

- Cross-cohort HMP2-to-Franzosa metabolomics bridging produced 9 strict replications. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- Theme-level sign concordance between HMP2 and Franzosa was 100 % for urobilin/porphyrin, 80 % for acyl-carnitines, and 75 % for long-chain polyunsaturated fatty acids. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- Polyamines had no mass-to-charge-ratio bridge between HMP2 and Franzosa. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- Pooled HMP2 and Franzosa metabolomics clustering separated the cohorts, indicating an uncorrected batch-effect limitation. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- The first principal component explained 79 % of variance in the pooled metabolomics data. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- Cross-cohort leave-one-substudy-out adjusted Rand index was 0.000, compared with a taxonomic baseline of 0.113. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
