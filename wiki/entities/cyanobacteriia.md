## Key facts

The Gene Function Ecological Agora atlas associated Cyanobacteriia with photosystem II (PSII), a photosynthetic system, as a focal class–function relationship. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

At class rank, the Cyanobacteriia × PSII result was supported, with producer d = +1.50 and consumer d = +0.70; producer p = 2×10⁻⁵ and consumer p = 2×10⁻⁴. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The class-level PSII sample contained n = 21 PSII KOs. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The PSII result was rank-specific: genus, family, and order tests were STABLE, with producer d values of +0.08, +0.20, and +0.19, respectively. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Cyanobacteria PSII gains contained 2.05% ancient events, compared with 14.90% across all PSII gains in the atlas; this pattern was consistent with a class-level donor-origin signature but did not establish donor identity. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The leaf_consistency metric, defined as the fraction of species in a recipient clade carrying a KO, was 0.88 for Cyanobacteriia × PSII, compared with an atlas reference of 0.20. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Cyanobacteriia showed 2.77× photic-aquatic enrichment with p < 10⁻⁵². [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

AlphaEarth environmental embeddings with k = 10 clustering included 90 Cyanobacteriia species, of which 35.6% were in a marine+sponge-dominant cluster. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

BacDive phenotype coverage for Cyanobacteriia was too thin for interpretation, with n = 4. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The focal PSII hypothesis set had an MGE-machinery rate of 0.00%; the atlas-wide baseline was 1.37% of KO-bearing gene clusters. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

PSII gene-neighborhood analysis examined 27,148 focal features and 218,321 neighbor pairs. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Among PSII focal features, 10.91% had at least one MGE neighbor within ±5 kb, compared with a Poisson baseline expectation of 10.6%, and the mean MGE-neighbor fraction was 1.65%. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The source cautions that the class-level PSII interpretation should not be generalized to all taxonomic ranks because genus, family, and order results were STABLE and the phylum consumer statistic was unavailable because of insufficient reference data. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The targeted Cyanobacteriia pangenome-openness test was underpowered, with n = 83 genera. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]
