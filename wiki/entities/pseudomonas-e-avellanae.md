## Key facts

Pseudomonas_E avellanae was one of 17 plant-associated species that met the testability criteria for within-species subclade analysis. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

It was one of 5 of those 17 species that passed the Bonferroni-corrected Fisher test at α = 0.0029, with p = 7.3×10⁻⁴. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

Pseudomonas_E avellanae was also one of three of the five significant species that satisfied Cochran’s rule for chi-square validity. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

The report treats this result as weak support for within-species plant-association segregation because only 18 of 65 candidate species had phylogenetic-tree distance data. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]
