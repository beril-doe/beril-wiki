# Pseudomonas_E avellanae

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Pseudomonas_E avellanae. [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in the source document. [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

Pseudomonas_E avellanae is a plant-associated species evaluated for within-species phylogenetic subclade structure. [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

## Key facts

Pseudomonas_E avellanae was one of 17 plant-associated species that met the testability criteria for within-species subclade analysis. [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

It was one of 5 of those 17 species that passed the Bonferroni-corrected Fisher test at α = 0.0029, with p = 7.3×10⁻⁴. [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

Pseudomonas_E avellanae was also one of three of the five significant species that satisfied Cochran’s rule for chi-square validity. [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

The report treats this result as weak support for within-species plant-association segregation because only 18 of 65 candidate species had phylogenetic-tree distance data. [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

The source report and its broader interpretation are available at [plant_microbiome_ecotypes__REPORT](../summaries/plant_microbiome_ecotypes__REPORT.md). [^plant_microbiome_ecotypes]
