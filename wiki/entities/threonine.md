# Threonine

## Identity

**Canonical name:** threonine. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

**Known aliases:** None reported in this source. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

**Stable external identifier:** None reported in this source. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

Threonine is an amino acid whose biosynthesis was evaluated as one of the GapMind pathways in the study. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

## Key facts

All-gene GapMind completeness for threonine biosynthesis was 0.803, while core-only completeness was 0.663, producing an exact core-versus-all gap of 0.140. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

This gap indicates that threonine biosynthetic pathway completeness depends partly on genes outside the universally conserved core. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

The accessory-dependent pattern for threonine biosynthesis was interpreted as consistent with Black Queen dynamics and as a potential basis for community-level sharing of biosynthetic capacity, although the analysis did not demonstrate metabolite exchange. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]
