Salicylic acid is a compound in the Shikimates/Phenylpropanoids group used in the ENIGMA Carbon Census. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Evidence from the ENIGMA Carbon Census

Salicylic acid was one of 8 compounds with an ENIGMA-isolate utilizer call in the census of 83 enrichment compounds. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The census identified 129 ENIGMA-isolate utilizer prediction rows for salicylic acid. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

Salicylic acid was part of the aromatic-funnel subset that dominated the ENIGMA-isolate-callable compounds; the other compounds in this subset were [[entities/3-hydroxybenzoic-acid]], [[entities/4-hydroxybenzaldehyde]], phthalic acid, and terephthalic acid. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The callable-compound results were interpreted as evidence of specialized, phylogenetically concentrated utilization rather than a broadly portable module set: across the 8 ENIGMA-isolate-callable capacities, 675 genomes carried exactly 1 capacity, 18 carried at least 3, and the maximum was 4 capacities per genome. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

The broader census reported that only 9 of 83 compounds were callable under its project definition, consisting of 8 ENIGMA-isolate utilizer calls and 1 Tier-1 measured RB-TnSeq carbon-source fitness experiment. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
