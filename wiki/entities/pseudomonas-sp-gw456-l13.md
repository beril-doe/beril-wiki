# Pseudomonas sp. GW456-L13

Pseudomonas sp. GW456-L13 (also GW456-L13, Fitness Browser organism pseudo13_GW456_L13) is an ENIGMA groundwater isolate. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer] It is an exact strain match between [[entities/web-of-microbes]] and the [[entities/fitness-browser]] (FB). [src: discoveries, webofmicrobes_explorer] It also has the highest reported metabolic novelty rate among the reported ENIGMA isolate profiles in R2A medium. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]

## Key facts

- Web of Microbes (WoM) lists Pseudomonas sp. GW456-L13 as an exact FB strain match to pseudo13_GW456_L13, with 5,243 genes and 106 experiments. [src: discoveries, webofmicrobes_explorer]
- It is one of two directly matched WoM Pseudomonas strains. Both are ENIGMA groundwater isolates with rich FB data. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer] The other is [[entities/pseudomonas-fluorescens-fw300-n2e3]]. [src: discoveries]

## Metabolic profile in R2A

In WoM R2A medium profiles, GW456-L13 has 49 increased (I) metabolites and 34 emerged (E) metabolites. Its reported novelty rate is 32.4%. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer] This is the highest among the reported ENIGMA isolate profiles. FW507-8R2A has the lowest, at 15.2%. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer] See also [[entities/r2a-medium]].

## Proposed use

The webofmicrobes_explorer report proposes a gene fitness and metabolite production analysis for pseudo3_N2E3 and pseudo13_GW456_L13. It would identify fitness-important genes on carbon sources that the same organism produces. This would test whether metabolic output predicts gene essentiality. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer] The idea is a proposal, not a result. For related context, see [[concepts/matching-exometabolite-profiles-to-gene-fitness-experiments]].
