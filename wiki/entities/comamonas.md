# Comamonas

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Comamonas. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Known aliases:** No aliases were reported in this document. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in this document. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Comamonas is a bacterial genus detected in the lignin-enrichment community experiment. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Evidence from lignin passage

Comamonas increased from 0.1% in the base bacterial community to 3.6–5.1% in Round-2 groups, corresponding to a reported 17–51× enrichment. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Its enrichment during sequential passage supports [condition-specific-fitness](../concepts/condition-specific-fitness.md) by showing that the abundance of a bacterial genus depended on the lignin and labile-carbon history of the community. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Comamonas was also conditionally responsive in the L-versus-LC comparison, where it was enriched in lignin-only communities with a CLR effect of -6.8. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The reported abundance and CLR effects are taxonomic associations rather than direct measurements of lignin-degradation genes or pathway activity. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

## Relation to ecological memory

The persistence of Comamonas enrichment into Round-2 communities contributes to the study’s broader evidence for history-dependent community restructuring, which is synthesized under [ecological-memory](../concepts/ecological-memory.md). [^lignin_community_enrichment]
