# Bacteroides

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Bacteroides. [^lab_field_ecology]

**Known aliases:** None specified in the source. [^lab_field_ecology]

**Stable external identifier:** None specified in the source. [^lab_field_ecology]

Bacteroides is a genus represented in the [kescience-fitnessbrowser](kescience-fitnessbrowser.md) and detected in [enigma-coral](enigma-coral.md) groundwater communities at the [oak-ridge-field-research-center](oak-ridge-field-research-center.md). [^lab_field_ecology]

## Evidence from Lab–Field Ecology

Bacteroides was one of 26 unique genera represented in the [kescience-fitnessbrowser](kescience-fitnessbrowser.md), and 14 of those genera were detected in Oak Ridge groundwater communities characterized using [16s-amplicon-sequencing](16s-amplicon-sequencing.md). [^lab_field_ecology]

Bacteroides had a statistically significant positive association with uranium concentration: Spearman rho=+0.264, p=0.006, and Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate q=0.013. [^lab_field_ecology]

This association was one of five significant genus-level uranium associations among 11 tested genera, with significant relationships observed in both positive and negative directions. [^lab_field_ecology]

The study interpreted genus-level field associations as evidence of ecological sorting across the uranium gradient, while cautioning that community composition also varies with redox conditions and carbon and energy sources. [^lab_field_ecology]

## Interpretation and Limitations

The Bacteroides result supports the study’s partially supported hypothesis that genus-level abundance is associated with uranium concentration, but it does not establish that Bacteroides abundance is driven by uranium tolerance alone. [^lab_field_ecology]

The study could not match 16S amplicon observations to Fitness Browser organisms at species or strain level, and therefore the Bacteroides association should not be interpreted as a strain-specific fitness result. [^lab_field_ecology]

Proposed follow-up work includes species- or strain-level matching against ENIGMA CORAL metagenomic genome or assembly tables and multivariate CCA or RDA analysis controlling for pH, redox, and carbon sources. [^lab_field_ecology]
