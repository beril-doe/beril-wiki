# mxaF

## What it is

**Canonical name:** mxaF. It is the marker for the Ca-dependent methanol dehydrogenase, associated with eggNOG K14028 and EC 1.1.2.7. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Known aliases:** mxaF. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Stable external identifiers:** eggNOG K14028; EC 1.1.2.7. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Evidence from the lanthanide methylotrophy atlas

The atlas detected mxaF in 195 genomes, compared with 3,690 genomes containing [[entities/xoxf]], producing a global xoxF:mxaF ratio of 18.92:1. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

mxaF represented the minority of joint xoxF plus mxaF calls: xoxF accounted for 0.9498 of the joint calls, with a 95% confidence interval of [0.9425, 0.9558]. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Among phyla with methanol dehydrogenase calls and adequate sample size, the directional test favoring xoxF over mxaF remained supported after Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction across 29 testable phyla. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The phylogeny-aware analyses also favored xoxF: equal weighting across 271 methanol-dehydrogenase-informative GTDB families gave an xoxF fraction of 0.960 with a 95% confidence interval of [0.941, 0.976], while a Bayesian binomial generalized linear mixed model gave 0.993 with a 95% credible interval of [0.992, 0.994]. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Distribution across lineages

Acidobacteriota contained 3 mxaF genomes and 285 xoxF genomes among 1,006 genomes, corresponding to a 95:1 xoxF:mxaF ratio. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Gemmatimonadota contained 1 mxaF genome and 98 xoxF genomes among 386 genomes, corresponding to a 98:1 xoxF:mxaF ratio. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Methylomirabilota contained 7 mxaF genomes and 23 xoxF genomes among 80 genomes, corresponding to a 3.3:1 xoxF:mxaF ratio. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Pseudomonadota contained 171 mxaF genomes and 2,988 xoxF genomes among 117,619 genomes, corresponding to a 17.5:1 xoxF:mxaF ratio. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Verrucomicrobiota contained 5 mxaF genomes and 19 xoxF genomes among 2,440 genomes, corresponding to a 3.8:1 xoxF:mxaF ratio. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Bacteroidota, Cyanobacteriota, Chloroflexota, Planctomycetota, Campylobacterota, Actinomycetota, Halobacteriota, and Thermoproteota had xoxF calls but zero mxaF annotations in this atlas. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

These observations support a broad predominance of xoxF over mxaF in the analyzed pangenome, but mxaF annotation counts alone do not establish enzyme activity or methylotrophic physiology. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Interpretation and limitations

The atlas used eggNOG K14028 as the primary source for mxaF calls because it recorded 4 calls in both eggNOG and Bakta, 191 eggNOG-only calls, and 8 Bakta-only calls. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The mxaF comparison is therefore sensitive to marker-detection methodology, and the report did not screen sequence-level evidence for pseudogenes, truncated open reading frames, or assembly fragmentation. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The mxaF findings are part of the cross-genome functional-marker analysis described in [[summaries/lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas__REPORT]] and connect to [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] and [[concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth]]. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
