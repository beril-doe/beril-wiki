# Harvard Forest Barre Woods warming-experiment site

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Harvard Forest Barre Woods warming-experiment site. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

**Known aliases:** Harvard Forest; Harvard Forest Barre Woods. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

**Stable external identifier:** NMDC `nmdc:sty-11-8ws97026`. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The site is the location of a long-term soil-warming experiment at Harvard Forest's Barre Woods. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

## Key facts

- The experiment applied **+5°C soil warming** for more than 25 years. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- The study included **42 biosamples** spanning warming treatment, organic versus mineral soil horizons, and direct versus laboratory-incubated conditions. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- The analysis used the `nmdc_metadata` and `nmdc_results` tables and did not use `nmdc_arkin`. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- In 14 control and 14 heated direct samples, Actinobacteria increased in organic soil from **0.249 to 0.315** with log2 fold change **+0.341** and q-value **0.049**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- Acidobacteria decreased in organic soil from **0.035 to 0.024** with log2 fold change **−0.549** and q-value **0.049**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- Mineral soil showed no phylum-level changes after FDR correction. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- PERMANOVA on Bray–Curtis genus distances attributed **7.6%** of variance to treatment (**p=0.069**), **30.6%** to horizon (**p=0.0002**), and **41%** to the treatment × horizon four-cell factor (**p=0.0002**). [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- In direct-sample sensitivity analyses, treatment effects explained **12.7%** of DNA variation in mineral soil (**n=14, F=1.75, p=0.081**), **11.4%** of RNA variation in mineral soil (**n=11, F=1.15, p=0.254**), and **10.0%** of RNA variation in organic soil (**n=14, F=1.33, p=0.190**). [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- A curated 62-KO carbon-cycling list was enriched among heated-up DNA KOs in organic soil, with **5 of 57** carbon-cycling hits versus **428 of 12,806** other hits, Fisher odds ratio **2.78** and one-sided **p=0.042**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- RNA signals for particulate methane monooxygenase were directionally positive in both horizons: pmoA (K10944) had log2 fold changes of **+0.730** in organic soil (**p=0.009**) and **+0.743** in mineral soil (**p=0.029**). [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- Heated mineral samples had **155 ± 4** ChEBI identifications per sample versus **167 ± 9** in control mineral samples, with Mann–Whitney **p=0.012**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- The samples were collected on a single date, **2017-05-24**, so the study could not assess seasonal effects. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
