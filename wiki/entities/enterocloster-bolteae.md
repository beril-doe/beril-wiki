# Enterocloster bolteae

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Enterocloster bolteae. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

**Known alias:** E. bolteae. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

**Stable external identifier:** Not reported in this document. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

Enterocloster bolteae is a bacterial pathobiont prioritized for state-dependent intervention in the Crohn’s disease and inflammatory bowel disease framework described in [[summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT]]. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Evidence from ibd_phage_targeting

E. bolteae was one of six actionable Tier-A targets, receiving a total prioritization score of 2.8. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

In donor-2708 engraftment analysis, E. bolteae passed the confound-free CD-up test with CLR-Δ = +1.09. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

CLR-Spearman-Louvain networks placed 4–5 of the 6 actionable targets, including E. bolteae, in one pathobiont module in each of the E1_all, E1_CD, E3_all, and E3_CD subnetworks. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

In 468 paired HMP2 samples, E. bolteae correlated with deoxycholate at ρ = +0.17 and lithocholate at ρ = +0.18. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

These bile-acid associations contributed to the report’s assessment of a bile-acid coupling cost for targeting the relevant species. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

Paired metabolomics found that E. bolteae had an opposite lactate association to Acetobacterium caccae, with E. bolteae at ρ = −0.20; the analysis identified only 7 strict cross-feeding triangles and did not support the proposed cross-feeding interpretation. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

Among 23 UC Davis Crohn’s disease patients, E. bolteae was present in 19 patients (83%). [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

The phage-evidence stack classified E. bolteae as having lytic-literature evidence, supporting monitored lytic-phage targeting rather than the direct clinical-trial-stage evidence available for Escherichia coli. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

In the state-dependent patient framework, E. bolteae was among the targets shared between visits for patient 6967, while it was present only at visit 1 in the reported longitudinal comparison. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
