# ChvI

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** ChvI. **Known alias:** CCNA_00237. **Stable external identifier:** no additional stable external identifier is provided in this report. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

ChvI is the *Caulobacter crescentus* response regulator associated with early and late transcriptional phases during envelope remodeling. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Key facts from caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss

Published ChvI-induced regulons from Stein 2021 and Quintero-Yanes 2022 contained 488 genes in the tested universe. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

These regulons partitioned into 20 unique-to-early genes, 10 genes induced in both phases, and 49 late-consequence genes, for a disjoint total of 79 ChvI-induced genes. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

ChvI itself was present in the both-phases group and had logFC +1.45 in the 4584-vs-4580 contrast, supporting ChvI autoregulation. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The unique-to-early group included the lasso peptide cyclase CCNA_02794 at +8.6, the ApbE iron-sulfur cluster repair protein at +3.8, and *imuB* at +1.5. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The both-phases group also included SIMPL-family CCNA_02378, which changed from +1.9 to +3.9, and amelogenin/CpxP-related CCNA_03997. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The late-consequence group included LolA-family CCNA_03820 at +2.89, Pal-like CCNA_00784 at +2.08, a *zot*-like membrane perturber, *osrP*, and multiple TBDTs. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The phase-structure threshold of at least 10 genes per cohort passed. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The late cohort's envelope/transport/regulator enrichment was 24.5%, below the relaxed 50% criterion, and the late-versus-early Fisher test was p = 0.243. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The report therefore supports a ChvI-associated phase structure but does not establish that the late cohort is coherently enriched under the relaxed criterion. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

Caulobacter SigU, CCNA_02977, is uncharacterized in the published literature, and a PaperBLAST scout returned zero substantive snippets, so SigU as the driver of the late cohort remains only partially supported. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
