## Key facts from the document

*Limosilactobacillus fermentum* was one of 12 species with valid gene-content ecotypes in the study’s 15-species sample. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The analysis included 118 genomes for this species, identified 4 ecotypes, and assigned 107 genomes to ecotypes. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

Genome gene-content profiles were clustered using [principal-component-analysis](principal-component-analysis.md) followed by [kmeans-clustering](kmeans-clustering.md), with candidate cluster counts from 2 through 6 and selection by silhouette score. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The study compared ecotype profiles across [cog-functional-categories](cog-functional-categories.md); all 12 species with valid clusters had at least one significantly differentiated COG category after Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

These findings contribute to [ecotype-environment-gene-content](../concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content.md), which evaluates links between within-species gene-content variation, function, and environment. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The result also contributes to [pangenome-integration](../concepts/pangenome-integration.md) by connecting pangenome gene-content clustering with functional profiles. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]
