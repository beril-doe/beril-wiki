## Key facts from the document

*Limosilactobacillus fermentum* was one of 12 species with valid gene-content ecotypes in the study’s 15-species sample. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The analysis included 118 genomes for this species, identified 4 ecotypes, and assigned 107 genomes to ecotypes. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

Genome gene-content profiles were clustered using [[entities/principal-component-analysis]] followed by [[entities/kmeans-clustering]], with candidate cluster counts from 2 through 6 and selection by silhouette score. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The study compared ecotype profiles across [[entities/cog-functional-categories]]; all 12 species with valid clusters had at least one significantly differentiated COG category after Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

These findings contribute to [[concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content]], which evaluates links between within-species gene-content variation, function, and environment. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The result also contributes to [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] by connecting pangenome gene-content clustering with functional profiles. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
