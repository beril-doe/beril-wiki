# Aromatic Amino Acid Biosynthesis

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Aromatic Amino Acid Biosynthesis. The document does not report a known alias or stable external identifier for this pathway. [^acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]

This entity denotes the aromatic amino acid biosynthetic pathway context examined in engineered *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 strains. [^acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]

## Key facts

The database documents 7 engineered ADP1 strains, including wild-type ADP1 and 6 derivatives with aromatic amino acid pathway modifications involving ΔaroF, ΔaroG, or dgoA variants. [^acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]

Proteomics data are available for all 7 engineered strains, with protein abundance measured for 2,383 genes. [^acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]

Cross-strain proteomics correlation was high, indicating targeted rather than global effects of the engineered aromatic amino acid pathway modifications. [^acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]

The pathway modifications are part of a multi-omics database that integrates genome features, TnSeq essentiality, flux balance analysis (FBA), mutant growth fitness, proteomics, pangenome classification, functional annotations, metabolic reactions, and growth phenotypes. [^acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]
