# Streptomyces

This page covers the genus Streptomyces. It appears in two projects here. In the ENIGMA contamination bridge it maps to 378 clades. [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential] In the plant microbiome work it is a dual-nature plant-associated genus. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] The evidence comes from table listings and genus-level tallies, not from tests on Streptomyces itself.

## Key facts

- The ENIGMA bridge lists streptomyces with 378 clades. It is among the top ambiguous genera, after pseudomonas (433) and ahead of prevotella (358). [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential]
- This ambiguity is the taxonomy-bridging problem described in [[entities/genus-to-species-clade-taxonomy-bridge]] and [[concepts/genus-level-taxonomy-bridging-to-pangenome-clades-and-its-coverage-limits]]. See also [[summaries/enigma_contamination_functional_potential__REPORT]]. [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential]

## Plant association

Streptomyces is described as a key dual-nature genus with 261 species. Its plant growth promoting (PGP) markers are phenazine and phosphate solubilization. Its pathogenic markers are cell wall degrading enzymes (CWDE) and the type III secretion system (T3SS). [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

In the plant-associated genus dossier, Streptomyces had 35 plant-associated species and a dual-nature assignment. Its PGP ratio was 0.44. Its cluster-robust markers were cellulase, pectinase, effector, phenazine and phosphate-sol, and it had 593 MGnify soil biosynthetic gene clusters (BGCs). No H7-significant species was listed, and no H6-significant species–host pair was listed. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

The dossier gives 35 plant-associated species, while the other description gives 261 species. The claims do not say how the two counts relate. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

In the MGnify integration, Streptomyces was a top producer of NRP/siderophore BGCs, with 113. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

## Caveats

Streptomyces scabiei is a major plant-associated taxon missing from the reported phylogenetic-tree coverage.
