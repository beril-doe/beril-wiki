# Streptococcus pneumoniae

Streptococcus pneumoniae has a small AMR repertoire that is almost entirely accessory, and it is found almost only in clinical settings. [src: amr_environmental_resistome] It also appeared as an AlphaEarth-only hit in a GC-ecology analysis. [src: gc_ecotype_ecology] Each finding comes from a single report.

## Key facts

- The AMR survey counts 7,944 genomes, 59 AMR clusters, 1 core cluster and 58 accessory clusters (98%). Its dominant environment is Clinical (99.6%). [src: amr_environmental_resistome] See [[summaries/amr_environmental_resistome__REPORT]], [[concepts/environmental-and-clinical-distribution-of-antimicrobial-resistance]] and [[concepts/within-species-variation-of-antimicrobial-resistance-gene-content]].
- In the GC-ecotype analysis, S. pneumoniae was an AlphaEarth-only hit, with r = -0.34 over 527 genomes. [src: gc_ecotype_ecology] The report presents this and other AlphaEarth-only hits as suggesting that the within-species GC × ecology signal is not specific to a bacterial subset. [src: gc_ecotype_ecology] See [[summaries/gc_ecotype_ecology__REPORT]] and [[concepts/independent-continuous-environment-embeddings-versus-categorical-isolation-source]].
