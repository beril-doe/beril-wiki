# DUF1043/YhcB

## Identity

**Canonical name:** DUF1043/YhcB. [^metal_specificity]

**Known aliases:** DUF1043; YhcB. [^metal_specificity]

**Reported identifier:** OG03264. [^metal_specificity]

No additional stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [^metal_specificity]

## Evidence from metal_specificity

DUF1043/YhcB was metal-specific in 3 of 6 organisms across 5 metals, corresponding to 50% specificity, with a mean sick rate of 0.054. [^metal_specificity]

The report prioritized DUF1043/YhcB as one of three strongest novel candidate families because its 50% metal-specificity was observed across multiple species alongside comparatively lower pleiotropic effects. [^metal_specificity]

This prioritization places DUF1043/YhcB alongside [ucp030820](ucp030820.md) and [yebc](yebc.md) as a candidate for further investigation of metal-tolerance mechanisms. [^metal_specificity]

The report proposed using AlphaFold predictions to examine possible metal-binding sites in DUF1043/YhcB, but this validation was identified as future work rather than a completed analysis. [^metal_specificity]

## Related context

The DUF1043/YhcB result contributes to the distinction between metal-specific fitness determinants and broadly stress-responsive genes discussed in [metal-cross-resistance](../concepts/metal-cross-resistance.md). [^metal_specificity]

Its classification was based on comparing fitness defects in metal experiments with sick rates across non-metal experiments, linking it to the cross-condition framework in [condition-specific-fitness](../concepts/condition-specific-fitness.md). [^metal_specificity]
