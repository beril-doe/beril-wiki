## Evidence from metal_specificity

DUF1043/YhcB was metal-specific in 3 of 6 organisms across 5 metals, corresponding to 50% specificity, with a mean sick rate of 0.054. [src: metal_specificity]

The report prioritized DUF1043/YhcB as one of three strongest novel candidate families because its 50% metal-specificity was observed across multiple species alongside comparatively lower pleiotropic effects. [src: metal_specificity]

This prioritization places DUF1043/YhcB alongside [[entities/ucp030820]] and [[entities/yebc]] as a candidate for further investigation of metal-tolerance mechanisms. [src: metal_specificity]

The report proposed using AlphaFold predictions to examine possible metal-binding sites in DUF1043/YhcB, but this validation was identified as future work rather than a completed analysis. [src: metal_specificity]
