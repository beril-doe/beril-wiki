# Atacama Desert

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Atacama Desert. [^metal_resistance_global_biogeography]

**Known alias or study label:** Atacama/Andean region of Chile. [^metal_resistance_global_biogeography]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [^metal_resistance_global_biogeography]

## Evidence from the global biogeography study

The Atacama/Andean region of Chile was the top spatial hotspot for environmental bacterial metal resistance in the study's 5° grid analysis. [^metal_resistance_global_biogeography]

The hotspot was centered at **lat=-25°, lon=-70°**, had **21.8% prevalence**, an **odds ratio (OR) of 9.83**, **q=7.6e-12** after Benjamini–Hochberg false discovery rate correction, and included **n=101 MAGs**. [^metal_resistance_global_biogeography]

The result was based on metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs) with usable geographic coordinates; across the analysis, **22,356 MAGs** had coordinates, and **2.8%** carried at least one metal-resistance type. [^metal_resistance_global_biogeography]

The report treats this regional signal as preliminary because hotspot interpretation requires checking distinct `sample_accession` prefixes and testing whether the signal persists after sampling-effort normalisation by **log(n_MAGs)**. [^metal_resistance_global_biogeography]
