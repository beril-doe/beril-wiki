# Gallionella

## What this entity is

Gallionella is a genus-level taxon detected in the SSO subsurface 16S amplicon dataset and associated with iron oxidation in the report’s taxonomy-based functional inference. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The canonical name used here is **Gallionella**. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Known aliases were not reported in the source document. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

No stable external identifier was reported in the source document. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence from SSO subsurface communities

Gallionella was enriched in groundwater relative to sediment at the same wells, with a relative abundance of 0.14% in groundwater versus 0.01% in sediment, corresponding to a reported 8.9× groundwater enrichment. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Iron oxidation peaked at well U3 at 2.8% in the report’s genus-level functional inference, with Gallionella identified as the key genus. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The report interprets groundwater enrichment in iron oxidizers, including Gallionella, as consistent with a plume-associated planktonic assemblage, but this interpretation is a hypothesis because direct SSO geochemistry was unavailable. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

Gallionella was detected in a dataset whose genus-level functional annotation covered 65 annotated genera and 21% of total reads, so the process-abundance estimates were treated as lower bounds. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

The report further states that the taxonomic trait assignments are based on literature-linked taxonomy rather than direct genomic evidence from the specific SSO populations. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
