## Findings in metabolic capability and dependency

*Alteromonas macleodii* was one of 10 target species in the metabolic ecotype analysis and had 56 genomes available for analysis. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

Its metabolic profiles formed 2 clusters, with a silhouette score of 0.738. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The study found no significant association between metabolic cluster membership and isolation environment for the marine organisms *Stutzerimonas*, *Alteromonas*, *Pelagibacter*, and *Prochlorococcus*. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

This non-significant result may reflect insufficient isolation-source metadata, ecological structure related to depth or nutrient zones, or pathway annotations that do not resolve the relevant gene-content differences; these explanations are hypotheses rather than established causal findings.
