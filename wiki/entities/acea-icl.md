# Isocitrate lyase gene of the glyoxylate cycle

## What this entity is

The canonical name is **isocitrate lyase gene of the glyoxylate cycle**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

Known aliases are **aceA** and **icl**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The identifier reported in this document is **K01637**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

Isocitrate lyase is part of the [[entities/glyoxylate-shunt]], and its paired glyoxylate-cycle gene is [[entities/aceb-glcb]]. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

## Evidence from Harvard Forest warming

In heated mineral soil RNA, aceA/icl (K01637) increased with a log2 fold change of **+0.460** and **p=0.037**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The aceA/icl result was a directional gene-level RNA signal, and individual RNA signals did not survive FDR correction across **14K KOs**. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

This result contributes to the comparison of DNA and RNA functional-pool responses in [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]] and is documented in [[summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT]]. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
