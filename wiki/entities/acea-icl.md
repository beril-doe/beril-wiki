# Isocitrate lyase gene of the glyoxylate cycle

## What this entity is

The canonical name is **isocitrate lyase gene of the glyoxylate cycle**. [^harvard_forest_warming]

Known aliases are **aceA** and **icl**. [^harvard_forest_warming]

The identifier reported in this document is **K01637**. [^harvard_forest_warming]

Isocitrate lyase is part of the [glyoxylate-shunt](glyoxylate-shunt.md), and its paired glyoxylate-cycle gene is [aceb-glcb](aceb-glcb.md). [^harvard_forest_warming]

## Evidence from Harvard Forest warming

In heated mineral soil RNA, aceA/icl (K01637) increased with a log2 fold change of **+0.460** and **p=0.037**. [^harvard_forest_warming]

The aceA/icl result was a directional gene-level RNA signal, and individual RNA signals did not survive FDR correction across **14K KOs**. [^harvard_forest_warming]

This result contributes to the comparison of DNA and RNA functional-pool responses in [multi-omics-integration](../concepts/multi-omics-integration.md) and is documented in [harvard_forest_warming__REPORT](../summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT.md). [^harvard_forest_warming]
