# Beyelii Yar borehole sampling site

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** Beyelii Yar borehole sampling site.  
**Known alias:** Russian Beyelii Yar borehole. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

Beyelii Yar is a Russian borehole sampling location represented in the deep-clay [[concepts/subsurface-bacillota-specialization]] comparison of Bacillota_B genomes. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

No stable external identifier for the sampling site is provided in the source report. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Key facts

- The anchor_deep_clay cohort included two Russian Beyelii Yar borehole genomes. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The broader anchor cohort was described as comprising 10 genomes from Mont Terri Opalinus borehole and rock-porewater samples plus two Russian Beyelii Yar borehole genomes. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The comparison used the anchor cohort against 62 phylum-matched soil-baseline Bacillota_B genomes. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The anchor cohort was borehole- and porewater-dominated by construction, reflecting cultivation bias toward porewater isolates. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- Because the anchor cohort was borehole- and porewater-dominated, the study could not test whether rock-attached Bacillota_B differ in gene content. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

These sampling-context limitations matter when interpreting the reported deep-clay specialization signal and its [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] comparison. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
