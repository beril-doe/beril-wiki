# Mont Terri Opalinus borehole and rock-porewater sampling site

## What this entity is

The canonical name is **Mont Terri Opalinus borehole and rock-porewater sampling site**. [^bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

Known aliases are **Mont Terri Opalinus borehole** and **Mont Terri rock-porewater sampling site**. [^bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

No stable external identifier was reported for this site in the source documents. [^bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory][^clay_confined_subsurface]

## Key facts

The site contributed genomes to the deep-clay Bacillota_B anchor cohort, which compared subsurface lineages with soil-baseline Bacillota_B genomes. [^bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

That earlier anchor cohort comprised 10 genomes from Mont Terri Opalinus borehole and rock-porewater samples plus two Russian Beyelii Yar borehole genomes. [^bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

The source characterizes this anchor cohort as borehole- and porewater-dominated, reflecting cultivation bias toward porewater isolates. [^bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

The newer clay-confined analysis **refines** the sampling context: its deep cohort contained 8 Opalinus genomes from BRC-3 or BIC-A1 borehole isolation sources, alongside 1 bentonite-formation genome. [^clay_confined_subsurface]

That analysis **supports** the porewater-dominated interpretation: 5/9 deep-cohort genomes carried dissimilatory sulfate-reduction markers, with 0.018 expected under the rock-attached null among 9 genomes and binomial p = 4.0×10⁻¹², whereas the iron-reduction comparison was not significant. [^clay_confined_subsurface]

Because rock-attached Bacillota_B were not represented in the earlier comparison, it could not test whether rock-attached lineages differ in gene content from porewater-associated lineages. [^bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

The newer study further **supports** this cultivation-bias limitation: the cultured cohort applies to porewater-cultured deep-clay isolates, not the full Mont Terri community, and recommends MAG-augmented sampling to assess rock-attached lineages. [^clay_confined_subsurface]

The site is therefore part of the sampling context for [subsurface-bacillota-specialization](../concepts/subsurface-bacillota-specialization.md), including enrichment of anaerobic-respiration, sporulation-revival, mineral-attachment, regulatory, and osmoadaptation functions in deep-clay Bacillota_B. [^bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

It is also relevant to cohort construction and lineage-comparison issues in [pangenome-integration](../concepts/pangenome-integration.md). [^bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
