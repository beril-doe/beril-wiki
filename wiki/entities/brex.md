# BREX

## Identity

BREX is the canonical name used in this report for a bacterial anti-phage defense-system family. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

- **Known aliases:** No additional aliases are reported in this document. [^phage_defense_arsenal]
- **Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is provided in this document. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

## Evidence from phage_defense_arsenal

BREX was detected as one of seven anti-phage defense-system families in the BERDL pangenome analysis. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

BREX occurred in 80.1% of species-level pangenomes in the prevalence analysis. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

BREX showed significant positive co-occurrence with DISARM in 4,968 species-level pangenomes, compared with a null mean of 4,629, giving z = 31.1 and an odds ratio of 8.2 after 1,000 phylum-stratified column-permutation tests and Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

BREX also co-occurred positively with Retron in 5,021 species-level pangenomes, compared with a null mean of 4,681, giving z = 29.7 and an odds ratio of 8.6. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

Additional significant BREX pairings were observed with R-M Type I, with 5,963 observed co-occurrences versus 5,732 expected, z = 26.9, and odds ratio = 8.7; R-M Type II, with 3,929 versus 3,637, z = 23.0, and odds ratio = 6.4; and DISARM, with 4,968 versus 4,629, z = 31.1, and odds ratio = 8.2. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

BREX markers were distributed across 20.7% of core gene clusters and 55.8% of singleton gene clusters, compared with a background of 46.8% core and 37.9% singleton clusters. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

The report identifies BREX as one of six systems significantly enriched in the auxiliary and singleton pangenome, with χ² p ≈ 0 for all six systems. [^phage_defense_arsenal]

The accessory and singleton enrichment of BREX is consistent with possible horizontal transfer of defense loci through mobile genetic elements, although the pangenome association does not by itself establish the transfer mechanism. [^phage_defense_arsenal]
