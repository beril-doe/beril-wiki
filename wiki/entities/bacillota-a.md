# Bacillota_A

Bacillota_A (also written Firmicutes_A) is a GTDB phylum. In the cazyme_pangenome_ecology report, it is the phylum where carbohydrate-active enzyme (CAZyme) density correlates most strongly with pangenome openness. The evidence is a within-phylum rank correlation; the report calls the overall relationship weak. [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology]

## Key facts

- In the antimicrobial resistance (AMR) atlas, Bacillota_A has 2,254 species with AMR, 8,776 total AMR, a mean of 3.9 AMR per species, and 57.5% of species with AMR. [src: amr_pangenome_atlas] See [[summaries/amr_pangenome_atlas__REPORT]].
- Within Bacillota_A, the CAZyme–pangenome openness correlation across 939 species was Spearman ρ = 0.288, BH-adjusted p = 2.2 × 10^-18, and it was significant. [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology] See [[entities/spearman-rank-correlation]].
- Under the name Firmicutes_A, the same correlation across 1,625 species was ρ = 0.105, BH-adjusted p = 7.8 × 10^-5, and it was also significant. [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology] The two rows differ in species number and ρ, and the claims do not explain why. [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology]

## Interpretation

The report describes the correlation between CAZyme density and pangenome openness as weak but real. It suggests that CAZymes are part of the accessory gene pool in specific lineages, particularly Bacillota_A and [[entities/bacteroidota]], both known carbohydrate specialists. [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology] This is an interpretation, not a direct test. See [[summaries/cazyme_pangenome_ecology__REPORT]] and [[concepts/pangenome-openness-and-its-correlates]].

In the phb_granule_ecology report, a high accessory fraction of phaC in SAR324, Bacillota_A and Eremiobacterota suggests that these lineages are active recipients of phaC through horizontal gene transfer (HGT). [src: phb_granule_ecology] This is an inference from core/accessory status. See [[concepts/horizontal-transfer-of-storage-pathway-genes-inferred-from-pangenome-core-accessory-status]] and [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]].
