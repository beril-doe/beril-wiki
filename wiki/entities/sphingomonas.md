# Sphingomonas

This page covers the genus Sphingomonas. It was detected at most Oak Ridge sites [src: lab_field_ecology] and was less abundant at high-uranium sites. [src: lab_field_ecology]

## Key facts

- Sphingomonas was detected at 93% of 108 Oak Ridge sites. [src: lab_field_ecology]
- Its abundance fell with uranium: Spearman rho was -0.382, p-value 4.5e-5, and FDR (false discovery rate) q was 2.5e-4. The report describes it as less abundant at high-U sites. [src: lab_field_ecology]
- The report interprets Caulobacter and Sphingomonas as sensitive indicators of uranium contamination. It proposes Herbaspirillum and Bacteroides as possible tolerant colonizers. [src: lab_field_ecology]
- Related pages: [[entities/uranium]], [[entities/caulobacter-crescentus]] and [[summaries/lab_field_ecology__REPORT]].

## Lignin enrichment

Sphingomonas was higher in the base community (1.74%) than in the enriched conditions. The report suggests that lignin enrichment does not select for it, even though the genus is known for aromatic catabolism. [src: lignin_community_enrichment] See selective filtering of communities by lignin enrichment and [[summaries/lignin_community_enrichment__REPORT]].
