## Key facts from the Gene Function Ecological Agora

The [[summaries/gene_function_ecological_agora__REPORT]] project supported the [[entities/mycobacteriaceae]] × mycolic-acid hypothesis at family rank, with producer Cohen’s d = +0.31 and consumer Cohen’s d = −0.19. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The same result was supported at order rank, with producer d = +0.288 and consumer d = −0.285. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

At family rank, the Innovator-Isolated subset contained 67 of 582 mycolic-acid-related tuples, or 11.51%, compared with 5.85% across the atlas. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Among 53,916 mycolic-acid gains assigned by Sankoff parsimony, 79.87% were recent, 16.72% older-recent, 3.41% mid, 0.00% older, and 0.00% ancient. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The corresponding distribution across all mycolic-acid gains was 48.79% recent, 31.81% older-recent, 9.60% mid, 6.12% older, and 3.68% ancient. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The low mycolic-acid leaf_consistency value of 0.15, where leaf_consistency is the fraction of species in a recipient clade carrying a KO, showed that the family-level result is a mixture of within-family sub-clades rather than a uniform family property. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

A follow-up sub-clade analysis found producer d = +0.394 for the mycolic-positive sub-clade comprising 10 of 13 genera, compared with family-rank d = +0.309 and mycolic-low sub-clade d = +0.211. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Consumer d was +0.006 for the mycolic-positive sub-clade, indicating that the original consumer-side Innovator-Isolated signal is rank-dependent and strongest at family aggregation. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

Mycolic-acid gene clusters had an MGE-machinery rate of 0.57%, compared with an atlas-wide baseline of 1.37% of KO-bearing gene clusters. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The project deferred large-scale mycolic-acid gene-neighborhood scans because Spark and pandas joins exceeded memory limits, so the non-phage-borne interpretation relies on per-cluster MGE-machinery rates and literature context rather than complete cargo-neighborhood scans. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The focused architecture census reported a median of 5 architectures per mycolic-acid KO, compared with 1 for PSII KOs, 15 for TCS histidine-kinase KOs, and 46 for mixed-category KOs. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

This architecture comparison supports an exploratory hypothesis that architectural diversity is associated with cross-clade exchange, but it does not establish causation. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]
