## Evidence from lignin community enrichment

Chrysosporium was the dominant reported fungal genus under lignin plus labile-carbon enrichment, reaching 61.4% relative abundance. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The lignin-plus-labile-carbon community contained 6 observed fungal OTUs and had Shannon diversity of 0.62. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

Chrysosporium was observed alongside Aspergillus, which reached 27.2% under the same enrichment condition. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The source did not report a gene-level measurement of lignin degradation, so the association between Chrysosporium and lignin processing remains taxonomic rather than a directly measured pathway result. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The Chrysosporium observation contributes to [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]] by showing that fungal community composition differed under a specific lignin and labile-carbon regime. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

Its measurement is part of the paired bacterial 16S and fungal ITS profiling summarized in [[summaries/lignin_community_enrichment__REPORT]], while the broader fungal comparison is relevant to [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]]. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]
