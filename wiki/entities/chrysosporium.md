## Evidence from lignin community enrichment

Chrysosporium was the dominant reported fungal genus under lignin plus labile-carbon enrichment, reaching 61.4% relative abundance. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The lignin-plus-labile-carbon community contained 6 observed fungal OTUs and had Shannon diversity of 0.62. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Chrysosporium was observed alongside Aspergillus, which reached 27.2% under the same enrichment condition. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The source did not report a gene-level measurement of lignin degradation, so the association between Chrysosporium and lignin processing remains taxonomic rather than a directly measured pathway result. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

The Chrysosporium observation contributes to [condition-specific-fitness](../concepts/condition-specific-fitness.md) by showing that fungal community composition differed under a specific lignin and labile-carbon regime. [^lignin_community_enrichment]

Its measurement is part of the paired bacterial 16S and fungal ITS profiling summarized in [lignin_community_enrichment__REPORT](../summaries/lignin_community_enrichment__REPORT.md), while the broader fungal comparison is relevant to [multi-omics-integration](../concepts/multi-omics-integration.md).
