# ACIAD2176

## What it is

**Canonical name:** ACIAD2176. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

**Known alias:** DUF2280. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

**Stable external identifier:** None is reported in the source document. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

ACIAD2176 is a DUF-associated candidate accessory factor linked to [complex-i](complex-i.md) in [acinetobacter-baylyi-adp1](acinetobacter-baylyi-adp1.md). [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

## Evidence from the aromatic catabolism network

ACIAD2176 was one of 23 genes initially categorized as Other or Unknown in the ADP1 quinate-catabolism analysis. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

Co-fitness analysis assigned 16 of those 23 initially uncharacterized genes to support subsystems with medium or high confidence, including ACIAD2176. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

ACIAD2176 correlated with Complex I genes at **r > 0.98**, making it a candidate uncharacterized Complex I accessory factor. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

This assignment contributes to the 51-gene support network surrounding the [beta-ketoadipate-pathway](beta-ketoadipate-pathway.md) and [quinate-degradation-pathway](quinate-degradation-pathway.md). [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

## Interpretation and limitations

The ACIAD2176–Complex I association is based on co-fitness correlation rather than direct evidence of physical interaction. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

The co-fitness matrix used only 8 conditions and 8-dimensional growth vectors, limiting the resolution of gene-gene correlations. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

The report therefore presents ACIAD2176 as a candidate accessory factor rather than an established Complex I subunit. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

## Validation directions

Protein-protein interaction or co-purification experiments could test whether ACIAD2176 physically associates with Complex I. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]

Expanding the condition panel beyond the 8-condition matrix could determine whether the ACIAD2176 correlation remains specific to Complex I across additional aromatic substrates, iron limitation, and respiratory inhibitors. [^aromatic_catabolism_network]
