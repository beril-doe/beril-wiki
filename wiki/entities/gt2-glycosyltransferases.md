# GT2 Glycosyltransferases

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** GT2 glycosyltransferases. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

**Known aliases:** GT2; GT2 glycosyltransferase family. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in this document. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

GT2 glycosyltransferases are carbohydrate-active enzyme genes analyzed in relation to [[entities/type-iv-secretion-system]], environmental horizontal gene transfer, and [[entities/cazy]] gene neighborhoods. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Key facts

GT2 glycosyltransferases were the top CAZy co-occurrence hit near T4SS loci, among 92 CAZy families with elevated co-occurrence at distances of ≤10 kb; GT2 occurred in 767 genomes, with an average gene length of 5,041 bp. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

GT2–T4SS associations were enriched in marine sediment, barley rhizosphere, and maize rhizosphere, with reported odds ratios of 5.5, 10.4, and 4.1, respectively. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

The GT2 gene tree contained 77 detected horizontal gene transfer events, including 32 normalized high-confidence cross-phylum events. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

Node_4915 was the strongest reported GT2 transfer event, spanning 8 phyla at a maximum divergence of 4.843. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

Node_4915 contained 35 genes, 82.9% of which were syntenic, and included WOR-3, Desulfobacterota, Patescibacteria, Bacteroidota, Firmicutes_A, Methanobacteriota, Bdellovibrionota, and Acidobacteriota. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

Across the detected events, 65 spanned 2 phyla and 12 spanned ≥3 phyla. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

Maximum divergence and synteny were negatively correlated, with Spearman ρ = −0.615 and p<0.001. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

In 376 genomes with parsed GT2 neighborhood regions, T4SS occurred in 503 neighborhood entries and GT2 occurred in 495 entries, confirming contig-level syntenic co-localization in the report's analysis. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

[[entities/gh23]] was the second most common CAZy family in GT2 neighborhoods, with 106 occurrences. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

GT2-neighborhood MAGs (metagenome-assembled genomes) had a mean of 0.045 metal-resistance types, compared with 0.004 for non-GT2 MAGs; the difference was significant by Mann–Whitney test with p=8.6e-27. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

Genomes with GT2 in T4SS-proximal neighborhoods carried 11× more metal-resistance genes, an association relevant to [[concepts/environmental-resistome]] and [[concepts/metal-cross-resistance]]. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

The report found no CAZy genes on plasmids in its ICEfinder analysis; 12 integrative mobilizable elements occurred among the top 100 accumulators. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

T4SS-positive genomes had 10× higher mobile genetic element density than other genomes, with p<0.001. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

These observations support the hypothesis that T4SS machinery contributes to environmental dissemination of GT2 and other carbohydrate-active enzyme diversity through chromosomal or integrative mechanisms, but they do not establish a transfer mechanism experimentally. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
