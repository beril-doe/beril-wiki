## Key facts

GH23 was the second most common CAZy family in GT2 neighborhoods, with **106 occurrences** among the parsed GT2-neighborhood entries. [^t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

Its occurrence in GT2–T4SS syntenic loci suggests that cell-wall-remodeling genes cluster with these loci; this is an observational interpretation rather than a demonstrated mechanism. [^t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
