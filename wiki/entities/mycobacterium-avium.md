# Mycobacterium avium

## What this entity is

**Mycobacterium avium** is the canonical organism name used in this report; no aliases or stable external identifier are reported in the source. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Key facts from the document

Mycobacterium avium was one of the 12 species with valid gene-content ecotypes in the analysis of 15 sampled species. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The analysis included **249 genomes** of Mycobacterium avium, identified **6 ecotypes**, and assigned **249 genomes** to those ecotypes. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The report assessed ecotype differentiation using COG (Clusters of Orthologous Groups) functional profiles after PCA (principal component analysis) and KMeans clustering. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

Because all 12 species with valid clusters had at least one significantly differentiated COG category after BH-FDR (Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate) correction, Mycobacterium avium was included in a species set showing at least one such category. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]

The report cautions that the ecotype interpretation for Mycobacterium avium, like that for the other analyzed species, is not separated from possible within-species phylogenetic or demographic structure because core-genome phylogenetic controls were not applied. [^ecotype_functional_differentiation]
