# Lead

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** lead. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]

**Known alias:** Pb. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]

**Stable external identifier:** Not reported in the source document. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]

Lead is one of the nine soil metals analyzed for associations with microbial functional gene content. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]

## Key facts from the source

- The analysis covered 51,748 soil samples and included lead alongside copper, cobalt, chromium, nickel, zinc, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- Across the nine metals, the study identified 2,355 significant COG–metal associations at FDR < 0.05; COG denotes a Cluster of Orthologous Groups functional category and FDR denotes false discovery rate. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- Lead and chromium produced the strongest signals among the nine metals. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- Transporter functions, including ABC and RND systems, and biosynthesis genes dominated the top COG–metal hits. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- Lead commonly co-varied with chromium, copper, and zinc in industrial soils, making it unresolved whether individual lead-associated COGs represent lead-specific responses or a generic response to multi-metal contamination. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- The report identifies partial-correlation modeling of COG ~ Cr | Cu + Zn + Pb as a planned analysis to help distinguish metal-specific associations from effects of co-contamination. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- The source characterizes all reported associations as observational, and effect sizes had not been systematically reported across all 2,355 associations. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]
