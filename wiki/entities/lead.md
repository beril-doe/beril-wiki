# Lead

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** lead. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]

**Known alias:** Pb. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]

**Stable external identifier:** Not reported in the source document. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]

Lead is one of the nine soil metals analyzed for associations with microbial functional gene content. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]

## Key facts from the source

- The analysis covered 51,748 soil samples and included lead alongside copper, cobalt, chromium, nickel, zinc, arsenic, cadmium, and mercury. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- Across the nine metals, the study identified 2,355 significant COG–metal associations at FDR < 0.05; COG denotes a Cluster of Orthologous Groups functional category and FDR denotes false discovery rate. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- Lead and chromium produced the strongest signals among the nine metals. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- Transporter functions, including ABC and RND systems, and biosynthesis genes dominated the top COG–metal hits. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- Lead commonly co-varied with chromium, copper, and zinc in industrial soils, making it unresolved whether individual lead-associated COGs represent lead-specific responses or a generic response to multi-metal contamination. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- The report identifies partial-correlation modeling of COG ~ Cr | Cu + Zn + Pb as a planned analysis to help distinguish metal-specific associations from effects of co-contamination. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]
- The source characterizes all reported associations as observational, and effect sizes had not been systematically reported across all 2,355 associations. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]

## Related pages

Lead-related findings contribute to the synthesis on [environmental-resistome](../concepts/environmental-resistome.md), where metal-associated transporter and biosynthesis shifts are considered as evidence about environmental resistance functions. [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]

The co-variation of lead with chromium, copper, and zinc directly informs [metal-cross-resistance](../concepts/metal-cross-resistance.md). [^soil_metal_functional_genomics]
