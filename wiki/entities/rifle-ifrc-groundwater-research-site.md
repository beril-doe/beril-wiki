# Rifle IFRC groundwater research site

The Rifle IFRC groundwater research site, also called Rifle, CO, is a DOE field research site in Colorado. [src: env_embedding_explorer] It appears among AlphaEarth locations flagged as possible institutional addresses, but the reports treat it as a legitimate field site. [src: discoveries, env_embedding_explorer]

## Key facts

- Location: Rifle, CO, at 39.54, -107.78. [src: env_embedding_explorer]
- It is a DOE IFRC groundwater research site. [src: env_embedding_explorer]
- It holds 1,883 AlphaEarth genomes. [src: discoveries, env_embedding_explorer]

## Role in coordinate-quality checks

Several AlphaEarth locations flagged as potential institutional addresses turned out to be legitimate field research sites. [src: env_embedding_explorer] Rifle is one of them. [src: discoveries, env_embedding_explorer] See [[concepts/coordinate-quality-and-institutional-address-artifacts-in-genome-geolocation]] for the wider issue. The same discussion names [[entities/saanich-inlet]] and Siberian soda lakes as other legitimate flagged sites. [src: discoveries]
