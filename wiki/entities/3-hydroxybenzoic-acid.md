# 3-Hydroxybenzoic acid

## What it is

**Canonical name:** 3-hydroxybenzoic acid. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

**Known aliases:** No additional alias was reported in the source document. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

**Stable external identifier:** The compound was structure-resolved with an InChIKey through [pubchem](pubchem.md), but the specific InChIKey value was not included in the source document. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

3-Hydroxybenzoic acid was classified in the Shikimates/Phenylpropanoids chemical class used by the census. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Evidence from the ENIGMA Carbon Census

3-Hydroxybenzoic acid was one of 8 compounds callable through ENIGMA-isolate utilizer predictions. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The census produced 127 ENIGMA-isolate utilizer prediction rows for 3-hydroxybenzoic acid. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

It was among the aromatic-funnel compounds that dominated the isolate-callable set, which contained 5 Shikimates/Phenylpropanoids compounds, 2 Alkaloids compounds, and 1 Terpenoids compound. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The broader hypothesis that callable coverage differed formally by chemical class was not supported: the chi-square test across the 8 ENIGMA-isolate-callable compounds gave χ²=5.07, p=0.53, df=6. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

Among field-detected implicated utilizer genera, 3-hydroxybenzoic-acid utilizers reached approximately 0.90 prevalence in the SSO field atlas. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

Within the called set, Castellaniella and Hylemonella showed approximately 100% 3-hydroxybenzoic-acid capacity, while Comamonas showed 92% capacity. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]

The environmental atlas measured organismal abundance or occurrence rather than 3-hydroxybenzoic-acid catabolic activity. [^enigma_carbon_census_1]
