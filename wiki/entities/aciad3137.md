# ACIAD3137

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** ACIAD3137. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

**Known alias:** UPF0234. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is provided in the source report. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

ACIAD3137 is a DUF-associated candidate accessory factor linked to [[entities/complex-i]] in *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

## Evidence from aromatic catabolism

ACIAD3137 was one of 23 genes initially categorized as Other or Unknown, and co-fitness analysis assigned it to a support subsystem with medium or high confidence. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

Its growth-fitness profile correlates with Complex I genes at **r > 0.98**, making it a candidate uncharacterized Complex I accessory factor. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

The candidate assignment comes from co-fitness analysis of an 8-condition matrix with 8-dimensional growth vectors, so the apparent association may reflect an indirect functional connection rather than a physical interaction. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]

The report proposes testing ACIAD3137 through protein-protein interaction or co-purification studies. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]
