# REEB76

## What this entity is

**Canonical name:** REEB76 clade. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Known aliases:** f__REEB76; g__REEB76. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

**Stable external identifier:** No stable external identifier is reported in the source. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

REEB76 is a previously uncharacterised family/genus-level clade identified among metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs) from rare-earth-element acid-mine-drainage contaminated river water. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Key facts

The REE-AMD dataset contains 37 MAGs and includes the f__REEB76 / g__REEB76 clade among its taxonomically diverse lineages. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The report describes the REE-AMD MAG collection as being led by acidophilic and metal-tolerant lineages, including Acidocella, Acidiphilium, Thiomonas, Metallibacterium, Burkholderiaceae_A/_B genera, Chitinophagaceae, Acidimicrobiia, Chloroflexota, Cyanobacteriota, and REEB76. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

Across all 37 REE-AMD MAGs, 4/37 carried xoxF, while 0/37 carried Bakta-validated lanmodulin or xoxJ; the report does not provide a separate marker count for REEB76. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The REE-AMD collection showed strong representation of stress-associated products: RecN occurred in 33 MAGs, RadA in 32, RecO in 31, RecA in 28, RadC in 24, FtsH zinc metalloprotease in 31, proton-translocating NAD(P)+ transhydrogenase in 27, MerR-family heavy-metal-responsive regulators in 30, thioredoxin reductase in 25, and glutathione peroxidase in 24. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

These observations support interpreting the REE-AMD MAG set primarily as a stress-biology context rather than as evidence that REEB76 performs lanthanide-dependent methanol oxidation. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

The source recommends characterizing REEB76 as a future direction because its biology remains uncharacterised. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
