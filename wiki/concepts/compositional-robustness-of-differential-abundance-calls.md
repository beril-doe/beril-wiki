Differential abundance (DA) — testing which taxa differ in abundance between sample groups — returns answers that depend on two choices made before any test is run: how counts are normalized, and how the contrast is designed. Both choices can reverse the direction of a call, not merely its significance. The methodological grounding is explicit in the corpus: cited work identifies microbiome datasets as compositional, i.e. constrained by a sum-to-constant [src: ibd_phage_targeting]; a cited review sets out the fundamentals, tools and challenges of compositional data analysis of the microbiome [src: ibd_phage_targeting]; and a cited method paper supplies analysis of compositions of microbiomes with bias correction [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. The direct evidence below is method-vs-method and design-vs-design comparison on the same data, which is strong for the comparison itself, but it comes from one human gut project plus the cross-project digest, so the magnitude of the effect elsewhere is untested.

## Normalization can reverse the sign of a call

On a curated battery of protective and pathobiont species, a raw [[entities/mann-whitney-u-test]] on relative abundance mis-directs 4+ of 8 protective species, and centered log-ratio (CLR) correction — rescaling each taxon against the sample's geometric mean before testing — recovers them [src: ibd_phage_targeting].

The case that motivated the re-analysis is [[entities/clostridium-scindens]], the named species central to the apparent Crohn's-disease (CD) enrichment paradox: a preliminary pooled Mann–Whitney differential-abundance analysis called it CD-enriched at log₂FC +2.67, contradicting its established role as a bile-acid-producing protective species at ~79 % prevalence in healthy individuals, and three explanations were left open — compositional artifact, strain heterogeneity, or ecotype mixing in the pooled analysis [src: ibd_phage_targeting].

Compositional correction recovers a depletion signal that the raw test misses: *F. prausnitzii*, *A. muciniphila*, *R. hominis*, *L. eligens* and *A. rectalis* are reported as flipping from "n.s." under the raw test to "CD↓" under CLR [src: ibd_phage_targeting].

*Roseburia intestinalis* shows an outright sign flip, raw CD↑ → CLR CD↓, reproducing the *C. scindens* artifact pattern on a second species [src: ibd_phage_targeting].

The reversal is not universal: the top reported pathobionts agree across methods, with [[entities/mediterraneibacter-gnavus]] (R. gnavus), [[entities/enterocloster-bolteae]], [[entities/eggerthella-lenta]] and [[entities/hungatella-hathewayi]] all CD-enriched under both raw and CLR analyses [src: ibd_phage_targeting].

A null result bounds the fix: *C. scindens* remains CD-enriched under both methods (raw log₂FC +5.66; CLR Δ +1.25), so compositional correction alone is insufficient, and the strain-heterogeneity and ecotype-mixing explanations remained live at that stage [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. The two raw log₂FC values reported for this species in the project (+2.67 in the preliminary pooled analysis and +5.66 in the curated-battery comparison) come from different analyses and are recorded here as reported, not reconciled [src: ibd_phage_targeting].

This refines rather than vindicates the normalization fix. Pooled Mann–Whitney on relative abundance is described as systematically under-sensitive for protective-species depletion, an artifact of compositional bias plus pooled-cohort heterogeneity; *C. scindens* was not resolvable at that level, and under a later within-IBD-substudy CD-vs-nonIBD design that eliminates study confounding the species is still CD↑ — read in that section not as a paradox but as the expected behavior of a species more prevalent in the IBD-cohort source studies than in the healthy-cohort source studies of the curated resource [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. The revised confound-free analysis reports the within-substudy result directly: across the four IBD sub-studies (242 CD / 369 nonIBD pooled) *C. scindens* has pooled CLR-Δ = +1.18 at false discovery rate (FDR, the expected share of false positives among calls) 1e-8 with 4/4 sign concordance, and is described as genuinely CD↑ in this design [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. See [[concepts/study-batch-confounding-of-environmental-associations]] and [[concepts/cross-cohort-microbiome-portability]] for the same confound seen from the design side.

## Second-method concordance

The project reports reproducing the compositional-bias result directly on a curated battery and using [[entities/linda]] — linear models for differential abundance analysis of microbiome compositional data, i.e. linear CLR regression with bias correction — as a second-method concordance check [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. LinDA was used as the second compositional DA method alongside CLR plus Mann–Whitney in NB04c §4 and implemented in pure NumPy for the project [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. Second-method agreement does not by itself rescue a confounded design: the revised report states that both compositional-bias-aware methods tried on the within-ecotype subsets (CLR-MW and LinDA) share the bias, so evidence counts from within-ecotype methods alone do not resolve the direction reversals [src: ibd_phage_targeting].

The comparison is published as a figure: `NB00_protective_species_paradox.png` plots raw log₂FC against CLR Δ on 14 curated protective + pathobiont species [src: ibd_phage_targeting].

## Contrast design changes the target list

Stratifying the contrast prunes candidates as hard as changing the normalization does. In the cross-project digest's account, within ecotype E3 — one data-driven stratum of gut microbiome samples, see [[concepts/gut-microbiome-ecotypes-as-patient-strata]] — 3 of 15 candidates passed all three filters: [[entities/mediterraneibacter-gnavus]] (within-substudy CD↑ +5.13, LinDA E3 +1.64), [[entities/flavonifractor-plautii]] (within-substudy +1.89, LinDA +2.91) and blautia wexlerae (within-substudy +0.91, LinDA +2.00); these were described there as the trustworthy Tier-A, the tier of candidates cleared for downstream targeting [src: discoveries].

The scope consequence stated at that stage was that per-ecotype Tier-A reduces from "33 candidates across both E1 and E3" to "3 candidates, E3 only", that E1 needs a reformulated analytical approach, and that the current within-ecotype DA is not a usable target list; the candidate fixes listed were to rebuild ecotypes on a functional (pathway / KEGG) feature matrix to break taxonomic-feature leakage, to run within-IBD-substudy CD-vs-nonIBD stratified by ecotype as the primary analysis — which requires substudy × ecotype × diagnosis cells with ≥ 10 samples each and, at the time of the digest, still needed checking — or to broaden to UC-vs-HC + CD-vs-HC combined as IBD-vs-control for power [src: discoveries]. The downstream consequence for intervention design is carried in [[concepts/ecological-cost-of-microbiome-target-depletion]].

The revised project report supersedes that target list, which makes the digest's counts historical rather than current. Under a within-ecotype × within-substudy meta-analysis on the four IBD sub-studies carrying both CD and nonIBD (HallAB_2017, LiJ_2014, IjazUZ_2017, NielsenHB_2014), E1 Tier-A = 51 candidates meta-analysed across 2 sub-studies (HallAB_2017 + NielsenHB_2014, 82 CD / 280 nonIBD), all 100 % sign-concordant, and E3 Tier-A = 40 candidates from single-study HallAB_2017 (22 CD / 31 nonIBD), flagged as provisional and needing replication [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. The cell-eligibility question raised by the digest has therefore been answered for these strata — E1 is eligible in two sub-studies while E3 is eligible only in HallAB_2017 — and the E3 list is to be treated as provisional until a second cMD-IBD sub-study populates E3 with ≥ 10 CD and ≥ 10 nonIBD samples [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. Within the stricter E3 × HallAB_2017 test, *F. plautii* replicates at +2.26 (FDR 0.02) while *Blautia wexlerae* does not replicate (+0.25, FDR 0.80) and is removed from the rock-solid set, the earlier triad having rested on cohort-level rather than E3-restricted within-substudy evidence [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. This **contradicts** the digest's "3 candidates, E3 only" framing in both direction and size: tightening the design did not shrink E1 to nothing but produced a classical-pathobiont E1 list, and it removed one member of the three-species set the digest called trustworthy.

## Tensions

**Does compositional correction fix direction errors, or only some of them?** One side: raw Mann–Whitney mis-directs 4+ of 8 protective species and CLR recovers them, with *Roseburia intestinalis* flipping outright from raw CD↑ to CLR CD↓ [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. The other side: *C. scindens* stays CD-enriched under both methods (raw log₂FC +5.66; CLR Δ +1.25), so compositional correction alone is insufficient there and strain heterogeneity and ecotype mixing remained live explanations [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. The project's §4 interprets the surviving call as cohort-composition behavior — a species more prevalent in the IBD-cohort source studies than the healthy-cohort source studies [src: ibd_phage_targeting] — but that interpretation is not established by the revised analysis, which finds the enrichment persisting *within* sub-studies after the study confound is removed (pooled CLR-Δ +1.18, FDR 1e-8, 4/4 sign concordance) and calls the species genuinely CD↑ [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. Between-study prevalence alone therefore cannot be the explanation, and which transformation to trust in general remains unsettled.

**Which stratification is the target list?** The digest's within-ecotype DA yielded 33 candidates across both E1 and E3, and its stricter filter requiring within-substudy and LinDA agreement left 3, E3 only, declaring the within-ecotype list not usable [src: discoveries]. The revised within-ecotype × within-substudy meta-analysis instead reports 51 E1 candidates across two eligible sub-studies and 40 provisional E3 candidates from one [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. The sides are not averaged: the later, design-controlled count replaces the earlier one, with E3 explicitly provisional pending a second eligible sub-study [src: ibd_phage_targeting], which also bears on whether the ecotype partition itself is stable — see [[concepts/ecotype-clustering-validity]].

## Open Directions

- Obtain a second cMD-IBD sub-study that populates E3 with ≥ 10 CD and ≥ 10 nonIBD samples and re-run the E3 × within-substudy test, to convert the 40 provisional E3 candidates into replicated calls or retire them [src: ibd_phage_targeting].
- Re-test the former E3 triad under the stricter design on new data, since *F. plautii* replicates at +2.26 (FDR 0.02) while *B. wexlerae* does not (+0.25, FDR 0.80), to establish which of the digest's three candidates survive at all [src: ibd_phage_targeting].
- Rebuild ecotypes on a functional (pathway / KEGG) feature matrix to break taxonomic-feature leakage and re-run within-ecotype DA, to test whether the E1 and E3 candidate lists are stable to the feature space used for clustering [src: discoveries].
- Run the combined UC-vs-HC + CD-vs-HC IBD-vs-control contrast proposed for power, and compare its call list against the design-controlled E1 and E3 Tier-A lists to see whether broader contrasts recover candidates or only add noise [src: discoveries].
- Extend the raw-vs-CLR-vs-LinDA comparison beyond the 14-species curated battery of `NB00_protective_species_paradox.png` to the full species table, to measure how often sign flips occur outside hand-picked examples [src: ibd_phage_targeting].
- Separate strain heterogeneity from cohort composition for *C. scindens* with strain-level profiling inside single substudies, the explanation that compositional correction could not exclude and that the persistent within-substudy enrichment leaves open [src: ibd_phage_targeting].
