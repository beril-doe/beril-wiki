# Cross-cohort portability and batch dominance in microbiome and metabolomic stratification

Taxonomic ecotypes were portable across cohorts, while raw m/z-bridge metabolite clusters were not. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] The page stops before translation to specific therapies. See also [[concepts/substudy-nesting-and-feature-leakage-in-pooled-cohort-microbiome-comparisons]] and [[concepts/gut-microbiome-ecotype-stability-and-cross-study-reproducibility]].

## Pooled analyses and leakage

Pooled DA and ecotype-conditioned DA both gave calls that later analyses overturned. The v2 preliminary report called *C. scindens* CD-enriched (log₂FC = +2.67) from pooled Mann-Whitney DA, and the report identifies this as a false call. [src: discoveries] It proposed three mechanisms. Ruminococcaceae loss may inflate the relative abundance of surviving *C. scindens*. [src: discoveries] Not all strains carry the `bai` operon. [src: discoveries] Pooling may mix ecotypes. [src: discoveries] The proposed fix was compositional-aware DA (ANCOM-BC, MaAsLin2 or LinDA) within ecotypes. [src: discoveries] See [[entities/clostridium-scindens]].

The later confound-free analysis changed this picture. Within substudies, *C. scindens* was CD↑ (pooled CLR-Δ +1.18, FDR 1e-8, 4/4 sign concordance), which directly contradicted the H2c paradox resolution. [src: discoveries] Leave-one-out refitting showed CD↑ in both E1 and E3 once the test species was excluded from ecotype definition. That identified NB04's nonsignificant call as a leakage self-selection artifact. [src: discoveries] NB04 had reported 33 within-ecotype Tier-A candidates (18 E1, 15 E3) and marked the paradox "RESOLVED by stratification". [src: discoveries]

Ecotype divergence itself survived. The permutation-null p was 0.000, with observed Jaccard 0.104 against a null mean of 0.785 ± 0.054. [src: discoveries] The report concludes that the divergence is real but NB04's effect-size framing used the wrong statistic. [src: discoveries] The ecotype framework remains usable for patient stratification (NB02 H1b stands). Only within-ecotype DA is invalidated for target selection. [src: discoveries]

The repair used three filters on every NB04 candidate [src: discoveries]:
- A bootstrap CI lower bound > 0.3 on within-ecotype CLR-Δ. [src: discoveries]
- LinDA CD↑ with FDR < 0.10 in the same ecotype. [src: discoveries]
- A within-substudy CD-vs-nonIBD meta-analysis across 4 sub-studies, with CD↑ and ≥ 66 % sign concordance. [src: discoveries]

Only the third was independent of the ecotype definition that generated NB04. [src: discoveries] None of the 18 E1 candidates passed all three filters. [src: discoveries] The discoveries digest attributes the apparent CD↑ of E1 candidates in within-ecotype DA to compounded feature leakage and compositional bias. [src: discoveries] All 14 E1 candidates that passed filters 1 and 2 had negative effects under the confound-free contrast, so they are ecotype-markers rather than CD drivers. [src: discoveries] In E3, 3 of 15 candidates passed all three filters and were designated trustworthy Tier-A: *Mediterraneibacter gnavus*, *Flavonifractor plautii* and *Blautia wexlerae*. [src: discoveries] Per-ecotype Tier-A fell from 33 candidates across E1 and E3 to 3 candidates, E3 only. [src: discoveries] E1 needs a reformulated approach before NB05. [src: discoveries] The repair applies to the next three Pillar 2 deliverables. [src: discoveries] Pre-computed ref_* tables are treated as starting points to verify, not ground truth. [src: discoveries]

The report proposes three candidate approaches for E1. The first rebuilds ecotypes on a functional (pathway or KEGG) feature matrix to break taxonomic-feature leakage. [src: discoveries] The second runs within-IBD-substudy CD-vs-nonIBD analysis stratified by ecotype. It requires substudy × ecotype × diagnosis cells with ≥ 10 samples each, and their availability needs checking. [src: discoveries] The third combines UC-vs-HC and CD-vs-HC as IBD-vs-control for power. [src: discoveries]

## Structural confounding in curatedMetagenomicData

The pitfalls digest shows why pooled contrasts cannot be adjusted. No curatedMetagenomicData sub-study in the reported CMD_HEALTHY versus CMD_IBD comparison contains both CD and HC samples. [src: pitfalls] A pooled log_abundance ~ diagnosis + (1 | substudy) model is therefore structurally unidentifiable, because substudy perfectly predicts diagnosis. [src: pitfalls] statsmodels.mixedlm with lbfgs returns without error but gives no usable fixed-effect estimate on this design. [src: pitfalls] Within ecotype-assigned CrohnsPhage samples, 45 sub-studies have ≥ 10 HC samples and 5 have ≥ 10 CD samples, but 0 have both. [src: pitfalls] The report cites Vujkovic-Cvijin 2020 on this kind of confounding, but says a standard random-effects model cannot adjust it on the pooled contrast. [src: pitfalls] If a pooled CD-vs-HC effect must be reported, the report says to quote it as-is with a caveat that study, cohort, sequencing center and sample prep are all collinear with diagnosis. [src: pitfalls] Four IBD sub-studies do have ≥ 10 CD and nonIBD samples each (HallAB_2017, LiJ_2014, IjazUZ_2017, NielsenHB_2014; aggregate 242 CD / 369 nonIBD). [src: pitfalls]

Naming also breaks cross-cohort pivots. Pivoting on taxon_name_original or another string key can silently split one species into non-overlapping rows when MetaPhlAn3 database vintages or processing stages differ. [src: pitfalls] In the CrohnsPhage mart, a naive pivot gave log₂FC ≈ 28 (≈ 2.68 × 10⁸ fold) for every renamed species, because one cohort's mean abundance collapsed to pseudocount. [src: pitfalls]

## Classifier transfer

A good training AUC did not predict patient-level usefulness. A classifier using is_ibd, sex and age to predict K=4 ecotypes reached macro one-versus-rest (OvR) AUC 0.80 in 5-fold CV, above the 0.70 threshold. [src: discoveries] On UC Davis patients, all IBD with is_ibd = 1 constant, it agreed with the NB02 metagenomic projection only 41 % of the time. [src: discoveries] It predicted E1 for 19/22 patients, following a learned rule of "IBD → E1", the marginal mode among IBD training samples. [src: discoveries] The AUC counted healthy-versus-IBD separability. The UC Davis test needed the harder separation of transitional E1 from severe E3 within IBD. [src: discoveries] Training-cohort AUC is a weak proxy whenever a strong cohort-axis variable is constant in the held-out patients. [src: discoveries] The recommended check is to apply the classifier to an independent cohort with the cohort-axis variable held fixed and report agreement there. [src: discoveries]

## Cross-classifier projection

The held-out cohort used Kaiju NCBI-NR read classification. The reference embedding used MetaPhlAn3 marker-gene abundance. [src: discoveries] LDA on pseudo-counts was robust, because non-detection does not force a projection coordinate. [src: discoveries] Kuehl samples mapped to E0, E1 and E3 in proportions 27 / 42 / 31 %, described as biologically plausible. [src: discoveries] GMM on CLR + PCA was fragile. Kuehl detects only 54 % of training species [src: discoveries], and 70 % of training species have no Kuehl detection. [src: discoveries] CLR imputes zeros with a small pseudocount, and PCA then places the sparse vectors in one dominant Gaussian cluster regardless of biology. [src: discoveries] All 26 Kuehl samples projected to E3 at GMM confidence > 0.97, which the report calls an artifact. [src: discoveries] The report recommends LDA or MMvec as primary and CLR-based methods as advisory at best. [src: discoveries] See [[entities/uc-davis-kuehl-wgs-crohn-s-disease-cohort]].

## Choice of K

LDA training perplexity and GMM BIC on CLR + PCA-20 were monotone with K and always preferred the largest K. [src: discoveries] Five-fold held-out perplexity was also monotone at ~8.5K samples, so it could not separate overfitting from real structure. [src: discoveries] Cross-method ARI between LDA and GMM did show peaks and valleys. The report argues that the methods agree only on K values reflecting real partitions. [src: discoveries] ARI peaked at K=7 (0.140) and K=4 (0.131). [src: discoveries] A parsimony rule (smallest K within 0.02 of the peak) selected K=4. [src: discoveries] The report recommends picking the K where two model families agree most, not each model's own preference. [src: discoveries] K=4 on 8,489 curatedMetagenomicData MetaPhlAn3 samples (5,333 HC + 3,156 IBD/other) gave ecotypes aligned with published enterotype work. [src: discoveries] CD and UC patients spread across E1 and E3. [src: discoveries] See [[entities/curatedmetagenomicdata]].

A K-choice caveat also applies to the metabolomics clustering below. Its K=4 was imposed by the plan to match the taxonomic ecotypes, and the natural count might be K=2, separating HMP2 from Franzosa. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Batch dominance in metabolomics

Pooling HMP2 and Franzosa metabolomics on an m/z-bridge panel, with PCA and K-means K=4, gave clusters that separated completely by cohort and not by diagnosis. [src: discoveries, ibd_phage_targeting] One claim describes the panel as 122 metabolites. [src: discoveries] Another describes the clustering as 326 subjects × 111 m/z-bridge features [src: ibd_phage_targeting], and a third as a 111-feature panel. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] The claims do not state how these counts relate. Separately, the NB09b bridge (±0.005 Da) mapped 122 of 592 HMP2 named metabolites to Franzosa peaks, with 118 testable. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] PC1 explained 79 % of total variance and was essentially the cohort batch effect. [src: discoveries, ibd_phage_targeting] It separated HMP2 (PC1 ≈ +12) from Franzosa (PC1 ≈ −5) cleanly. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] Cross-cohort LOSO ARI was 0.000, against a 0.113 taxonomic-ecotype LOSO baseline. [src: discoveries, ibd_phage_targeting]

The within-pooled bootstrap (80 % × 30) gave ARI 0.937, against 0.16 for the taxonomic NB04b comparator. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] The report calls this misleading, because it measures how reproducibly subsampled clustering recovers cohort-batch structure, not biological information. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] Within-pooled bootstrap stability measures batch reproducibility when batch dominates. [src: discoveries, ibd_phage_targeting] The hypothesis H3d-clust, that metabolite clustering would be more cross-cohort stable than taxonomy, was not supported. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] The report concludes that unaddressed batch effects dominate m/z-bridge cluster structure. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

The report attributes taxonomic portability to unitless, sum-to-1 MetaPhlAn3 abundances, which normalize naturally across cohorts. [src: discoveries, ibd_phage_targeting] Metabolite clustering instead inherits absolute-intensity differences between LC-MS runs, such as instruments, ionization tuning and solvent batches. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] It needs explicit batch correction before PCA and K-means. [src: discoveries] Candidates are ComBat, SVA, RUV and within-method quantile normalization. [src: discoveries, ibd_phage_targeting] NB09d applied no correction, and the report expects correction would likely improve LOSO ARI substantially, so its verdict covers raw m/z-bridge clustering only. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] Refitting metabolite ecotypes is not a drop-in replacement for taxonomic ecotypes within this project's data scope, and would need external batch-correction infrastructure. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

The report generalizes in three points:
- Compositional spaces (taxonomy, pathway-fraction, MAG-fraction) are usually portable as-is. [src: discoveries]
- Absolute-intensity spaces (mass spectrometry, RNA-seq counts, protein abundance) are not portable without correction, and batch will dominate. [src: discoveries]
- Within-pooled bootstrap stability measures batch reproducibility when batch dominates. [src: discoveries]

The failure is specific to cross-cohort portability. Within-cohort metabolite ecotypes may still be useful inside a single laboratory's pipeline. [src: discoveries]

### What did replicate across cohorts

Metabolite-level replication was partial. NB09b matched 122 of 592 named HMP2 metabolites to a Franzosa peak, and 118 of 122 passed Franzosa CD-vs-Control DA filters. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] Overall sign concordance was 64 % (76 of 118). [src: ibd_phage_targeting] Three themes replicated at ≥ 75 % sign concordance (urobilin, acyl-carnitines, long-chain PUFA). [src: ibd_phage_targeting] Urobilin/porphyrin was the strongest, at 100 % (n=3) with median Franzosa cliff −0.747. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] There were 9 strict cross-cohort replications, the strongest cross-cohort signal in the project's metabolomics. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] Primary bile acids were 57 % concordant and secondary 40 %, weaker than the within-HMP2 paired sample-level evidence in NB09c. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] The report treats the paired within-HMP2 species–metabolite correlations as the more robust mechanism evidence, because cohort-aggregate DA is dominated by batch effects. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

Polyamines could not be tested. The m/z bridge returned 0 polyamine matches. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] This reflects a low-mass HILIC-pos gap. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] None of the 9 polyamines behind the HMP2 OR=14.6 enrichment were recovered. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] That enrichment stays a single-cohort finding until replication with external Franzosa tables carrying compound names, which is outside the project's scope. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] In HMP2 CD versus nonIBD, the polyamine theme had 5 CD-up of 9 members (OR 14.6, FDR 0.008). [src: ibd_phage_targeting] Long-chain PUFA had 6 CD-up of 15 (OR 7.9, FDR 0.009). [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Tensions

- Pathway-level DA found `06_polyamine_urea` CD-DOWN (OR=0.42). [src: discoveries, ibd_phage_targeting] Metabolomics found polyamines CD-UP at OR=14.6, the largest theme-level metabolite effect in the project. [src: discoveries, ibd_phage_targeting] The report says this is not necessarily a contradiction. Pathway DA measures production capacity and untargeted metabolomics measures steady-state pool size. [src: discoveries] Pools reflect production minus consumption plus dietary and host inputs, so pool and flux measurements are not interchangeable. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] The report proposes that pool accumulation can come from more dietary-protein or mucin catabolism plus reduced microbial clearance, without elevated biosynthesis. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- Pooled DA called *C. scindens* CD-enriched, and the report calls that a false call. [src: discoveries] Confound-free within-substudy analysis nonetheless found it CD↑. [src: discoveries] The two disagree on the evidence behind the label, and the report treats the within-substudy result as current. [src: discoveries]

## Open Directions

- Rebuild ecotypes on functional pathway or KEGG feature matrices to break taxonomic-feature leakage. This would test whether E1 yields a usable target list.
- Run within-substudy CD-vs-nonIBD analysis stratified by ecotype, first checking that substudy × ecotype × diagnosis cells have ≥ 10 samples.
- Combine UC-vs-HC and CD-vs-HC as IBD-vs-control for power.
- Rerun the HMP2 and Franzosa pooled clustering after ComBat or quantile normalization, and compare LOSO ARI to 0.000 and 0.113. Also test K=2 against K=4. This would show whether the m/z bridge can be rescued.
- Replicate the polyamine enrichment in Franzosa using name-annotated supplementary tables, since the m/z bridge cannot reach it.
- Repeat the K-selection by cross-method ARI in a second cohort. This would test whether K=4 generalizes.
