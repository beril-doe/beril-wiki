# Outer-membrane lipid homeostasis after lipid A loss

Outer-membrane lipid homeostasis after lipid A loss describes coordinated lipid replacement, transport, envelope remodeling, and temporally distinct regulatory responses that maintain bacterial envelope integrity when lipid A is absent. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] In the Δ*fur* Δ*sspB* background of *Caulobacter crescentus*, viability of Δ*lpxc* was evaluated using transcriptomics, published ChvI regulons, single-replicate outer-membrane proteomics, RB-TnSeq fitness data, and comparative annotation. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The combined evidence supports a phased, component-specific rescue architecture rather than a single-gene phenotype or a uniform transcriptional response, while leaving causal order unresolved. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Caulobacter rescue architecture and regulatory timing

Comparative evidence most directly supports a Caulobacter-specific lipid-substitution route: *spt* and *cerR* were Caulobacter-unique across *C. crescentus*, *A. baumannii*, *N. meningitidis*, and *M. catarrhalis*, with patterns 1000, while ChvG and ChvI also showed pattern 1000. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] This supports the interpretation that Caulobacter uses a sphingolipid-based substitute pathway and a Caulobacter-restricted ChvG–ChvI regulatory circuit, whereas the tested comparator species lack both features. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The [sphingolipid-biosynthesis](../entities/sphingolipid-biosynthesis.md) pathway was constitutive rather than induced in the tested comparison: 0/6 biosynthesis genes were UP, *spt* was DOWN by -0.64 with FDR 0.002, and *sphk* was DOWN by -0.40 with FDR 0.02. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] This **refines** an acute-induction model: the measured pathway showed no biosynthesis-gene induction under the tested condition, shifting attention toward transport, retention, and envelope remodeling. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The phased regulatory evidence comes from comparing published ChvI-induced regulons with the 4584-vs-4580 transcript contrast in the Δ*fur* Δ*sspB* Δ*rsaA* and Δ*rsaA* backgrounds. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The published ChvI-induced regulons from Stein 2021 and Quintero-Yanes 2022 contained 488 genes in the tested universe. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] These genes partitioned into 20 unique-to-early genes, 10 genes induced in both phases, and 49 late-consequence genes, producing a disjoint total of 79 ChvI-induced genes; the phase-structure threshold of at least 10 genes per cohort was therefore passed. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The early-only cohort included the lasso peptide cyclase CCNA_02794 at +8.6 logFC, the ApbE iron-sulfur cluster repair protein at +3.8 logFC, and *imuB* at +1.5 logFC. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] These observations support an early response involving specialized envelope-associated products, iron-sulfur cluster maintenance, and stress-associated functions. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The both-phases cohort included ChvI itself, CCNA_00237, at +1.45 logFC in the 4584-vs-4580 contrast, supporting ChvI autoregulation. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] SIMPL-family CCNA_02378 changed from +1.9 to +3.9 logFC across the phase comparisons, and amelogenin/CpxP-related CCNA_03997 was also present in this cohort. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The late-consequence cohort included the LolA-family protein CCNA_03820 at +2.89 logFC, Pal-like CCNA_00784 at +2.08 logFC, a *zot*-like membrane perturber, *osrP*, and multiple TBDTs. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] TBDTs, or TonB-dependent transporters, are therefore prominent among the later transport-associated consequences reported in this response. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] This **supports** connecting regulatory timing to lipid transport and envelope organization, but the late cohort's envelope, transport, and regulator enrichment was 24.5%, below the relaxed 50% criterion, and the late-versus-early Fisher test gave p = 0.243. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Thus, separable early, shared, and late cohorts are supported, whereas the stronger claim that the late cohort is statistically more enriched for envelope functions than the early cohort is not established. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The pattern is consistent with an early ChvI-associated response followed by a later program involving transport, outer-envelope organization, and regulatory consequences. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Because the late-cohort enrichment and late-versus-early comparison did not meet their relaxed criteria, this interpretation is a supported phase model rather than proof of a fully resolved causal sequence. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Caulobacter SigU, CCNA_02977, is uncharacterized in the published literature, and a PaperBLAST scout returned zero substantive snippets. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] SigU may contribute to the late program, but its role as the driver of that cohort remains only partially supported. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Component-specific transcript–protein responses

Transcript–protein discordance—differences in the direction or magnitude of RNA and protein changes—indicates regulation between transcription and protein accumulation rather than a simple transcript-level response. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] In this rescue context, the strongest evidence is concentrated in lipid-transport and outer-membrane components, although the single-replicate proteome limits mechanistic inference. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The measurements are summarized in [caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss__REPORT](../summaries/caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss__REPORT.md) and connect to [multi-omics-integration](multi-omics-integration.md). [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

At the transcript level, no canonical Lpt component was DOWN; the MsbA-like CCNA_00307 was UP by +0.89 with FDR 0.01, and LptC-related CCNA_03716 was UP by +0.56 with FDR 0.005. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] At the protein level, detected canonical components moved oppositely: LptD changed by log2(4672/4659) = -0.47 and by log2(4672/4580) = -0.62, while LptE changed by -0.78 and -0.68, respectively. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] CCNA_00307 was detected only in the rescued strain, at abundance 300 in 4672 and NaN in the other strains, so its direction relative to the wild-type baseline was uncomputable. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] This **contradicts** a model in which transcript increases uniformly propagate into increased Lpt protein abundance and instead supports regulation through translation, protein stability, complex assembly, or turnover; the responsible process remains unidentified. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The sphingolipid transporter CCNA_01226, *lptC2*, showed transcript -0.60 with FDR 0.034 but protein log2(4672/4659) = +1.08. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Because its protein level was already DOWN by -0.42 log2 in 4659/4580, its net protein change versus wild-type baseline was +0.66 log2, reported as approximately 1.58×. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] CCNA_01217 protein changed by +0.77 in 4672 versus 4659, or +0.74 versus wild type. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] These observations are **consistent with** post-transcriptional stabilization or retention of sphingolipid-transport machinery, but the mechanism is not statistically established. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The transporter-level protein increases **refine** the pathway result from biosynthetic induction to possible maintenance or stabilization of transport capacity during lipid A loss. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Tol-Pal, peptidoglycan, and envelope remodeling

Pal/CCNA_00784 was induced at both transcript and protein levels, with +2.08 transcript and +2.84 protein, and it was among the late-cohort ChvI genes. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The report interprets [tol-pal-system](../entities/tol-pal-system.md) upregulation primarily through retrograde phospholipid transport and outer-membrane lipid homeostasis; direct Pal–peptidoglycan contacts and a Caulobacter division-associated role remain context rather than direct measurements in this project. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Pal-Tol induction is therefore **consistent with** increased lipid-remodeling demand and with the late regulatory program, while its causal role in rescue remains unresolved. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The broader peptidoglycan-remodeling set contained 53 loci, of which 28 unique loci met the H4 threshold: 25 were transcript-significant in 4599-vs-4584 at FDR<0.05 and 6 had outer-membrane-proteome |log2|>1. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The response was predominantly downregulatory, with 20 DOWN loci and specific inductions comprising 5 UP transcript hits plus 3 UP protein hits. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Induced activities included SdpA/CCNA_01252 at +4.8 log2 protein, Pal/CCNA_00784 at +2.08 transcript and +2.84 protein, PleA, PbpX, and CCNA_01754. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Basal division and elongation machinery was broadly reduced, including FtsI, PbpZ at -1.08 transcript, PbpC at -1.15 protein, a D,D-transpeptidase at -1.27 protein, *murD*, *mviN/murJ*, multiple LdpD/E/F M23-family endopeptidases, *amiC*, *ripA*, and membrane-bound transglycosylase A at -2.47 log2 protein. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

Pal shows concordant RNA and protein induction, whereas LptD and LptE decline despite transcript-level increases elsewhere in the apparatus. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] This **supports** a component-specific rather than globally uniform post-transcriptional response. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Together, the Pal-Tol and peptidoglycan findings **support** coordinated outer-membrane lipid transport and envelope-structure remodeling, and **refine** the phased model by linking late-cohort genes to measured remodeling responses, but they do not establish causal order. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Fur, ChvI, and fitness context

The phased ChvI response occurs within a broader Δ*fur* derepression signal: the 4584-vs-4580 contrast correlated with Leaden 2018's Δ*fur* signal across 93 differentially expressed genes, with Spearman ρ = 0.315, p = 2.08e-03, and 71% sign concordance. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Of those 93 DEGs, 53 were buffered in the present data, with logFC approximately 0 despite Leaden values of -5 to -9; the buffered set was dominated by the cbb3 / cyd / *fix*-NOPQ micro-aerobic respiratory operon, including CCNA_01466-01476, *ccoNOPQ*, *cydCDA*, and *fixG/H/I*. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Fur derepression is statistically supported, but the mechanistic importance of the Δ*sspB*-buffered respiratory program for lipid A-loss rescue remains a hypothesis. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The ChvI-induced regulons contained 488 genes in the tested universe and partitioned into 20 unique-to-early genes, 10 genes induced in both phases, and 49 late-consequence genes, for a disjoint total of 79 ChvI-induced genes. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The late cohort included LolA-family CCNA_03820 at +2.89, Pal-like CCNA_00784 at +2.08, a *zot*-like membrane perturber, *osrP*, and multiple TBDTs. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The phase structure is supported, but envelope/transport/regulator enrichment was 24.5%, below the relaxed 50% criterion, and the late-versus-early Fisher test was p = 0.243. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Thus, ChvI-associated envelope remodeling is plausible, whereas SigU as the driver of the late cohort remains only partially supported. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The Caulobacter RB-TnSeq compendium covered 198 experiments, including 22 envelope-stress experiments but zero iron-limitation experiments. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The genome-wide envelope-stress background contained 1311 phenotype-bearing genes among 3943 genes, or 33.25%. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The 26-gene Δ*sspB*-buffered set contained 9 phenotype-bearing genes, or 34.6%, corresponding to 1.04× enrichment with p = 0.515, and was indistinguishable from background. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] This **weakens** the interpretation that the buffered respiratory program is required for envelope remodeling, while the 32-gene concordant-strong Fur signature showed 17 phenotype-bearing genes, or 53.1%, corresponding to 1.60× enrichment with hypergeometric p = 0.016. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

ChvT, CCNA_03108, had envelope-stress |*t*| = 43.7, while other Fur-derepressed TBDTs included CCNA_02910, CCNA_00210, CCNA_02048, and CCNA_00028 with |*t*| values of 9-28. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The preregistered threshold of at least 10% phenotype-bearing genes was below the 33.25% genome background and was not informative without background correction. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] These results support using background-calibrated fitness tests to distinguish transcriptional participation from condition-specific necessity, consistent with [gene-essentiality](gene-essentiality.md). ## Tensions

The central tension is between transcript-level activation of selected Lpt-related genes and protein-level decline of LptD and LptE. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The data support an RNA-level regulatory response but do not establish increased abundance or function of the canonical Lpt apparatus. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] A related tension is that *lptC2* and CCNA_01217 protein increases, and Pal induction, are compatible with stabilization or increased remodeling capacity, whereas LptD and LptE decline; the single-replicate design cannot distinguish translation, stability, assembly, turnover, or downstream stress effects. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Pal induction is likewise compatible with increased remodeling demand, but cannot be shown causal for rescue. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

The phase model is supported by cohort structure, but the proposed functional distinction between early and late responses is weakened by the late-cohort enrichment of 24.5%, the late-versus-early Fisher test p = 0.243, and the unresolved role of SigU. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The transcript-level respiratory signature is clear, but its fitness enrichment is only 1.04× with p = 0.515, so transcriptional prominence should not be treated as evidence of causal necessity. [^caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
