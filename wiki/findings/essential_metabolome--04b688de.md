# Desulfovibrio vulgaris alone lacks a complete serine biosynthesis pathway

Desulfovibrio vulgaris (DvH) is the only organism among the 7 that lacks a complete serine biosynthesis pathway according to GapMind (score_category: complete or likely_complete). [src: essential_metabolome]

## Evidence

> ***Desulfovibrio vulgaris*** (DvH) is the **only organism** among the 7 that lacks a complete serine biosynthesis pathway according to GapMind (score_category: complete or likely_complete). [src: essential_metabolome]

From the report [[summaries/essential_metabolome__REPORT]].

## Caveat

DvH may use a non-canonical serine biosynthesis pathway not in GapMind's reference database. [src: essential_metabolome]

> DvH may use a **non-canonical serine biosynthesis pathway** not in GapMind's reference database [src: essential_metabolome]

## Other caveats in this report

- Escherichia coli genomes are completely absent from GapMind in the KBase pangenome collection. [src: essential_metabolome]
- The report's GapMind coverage limitation restricts the analysis to a pilot study with 7 organisms rather than the intended 45 Fitness Browser organisms with essential gene data. [src: essential_metabolome]
- The serine biosynthesis pathway in Desulfovibrio vulgaris may use divergent enzymes below GapMind's homology threshold. [src: essential_metabolome]
- Serine biosynthesis genes in Desulfovibrio vulgaris may be present but unannotated in the genome assembly. [src: essential_metabolome]
- The analyzed sample has limited phylogenetic diversity, consisting mostly of Proteobacteria plus 1 Deltaproteobacterium. [src: essential_metabolome]
