- The report interprets the moderate mean silhouette score of 0.215 as overlapping ecotype boundaries rather than discrete populations. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The identified bacterial ecotypes represent statistical gene-content tendencies rather than sharply defined lineages, with ongoing auxiliary gene acquisition/loss. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The ecotype analysis retained 12 species from 15 sampled (80%), out of 456 eligible, and its pangenome-size-stratified sample may not capture phylogenetic or ecological breadth. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
