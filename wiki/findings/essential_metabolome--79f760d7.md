## Caveat

The original hypothesis of a universally complete metabolic core across bacteria was only partially supported with significant caveats. [src: essential_metabolome]

## Other caveats in this report

- The ecological rationale for DvH serine auxotrophy is speculative: external serine availability and dependency were not tested. [src: essential_metabolome]
- Shared central catabolic capacity is an interpretation of the predictions, not direct metabolic measurement. [src: essential_metabolome]
- The report attributes E. coli absence to GTDB pangenome construction excluding species with too many genomes. [src: essential_metabolome]
- GapMind coverage constrained this pilot to 7 organisms instead of the intended 45 Fitness Browser organisms with essential-gene data. [src: essential_metabolome]
- Ancestral amino-acid prototrophy is a hypothesis inferred from only 6 of 7 organisms (85.7%), not an established pan-bacterial finding. [src: essential_metabolome]
