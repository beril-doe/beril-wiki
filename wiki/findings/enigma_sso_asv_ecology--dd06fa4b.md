# Well identity is not a significant PERMANOVA factor in SSO sediment

In PERMANOVA of 37 SSO sediment core segments, well identity explains 19.2% of community variance but is not significant (F = 0.80, p = 0.979). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Key figures

- **19.2%**: variance explained by well identity (denominator community variance; scope 37 SSO sediment core segments) [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- **p = 0.979**: PERMANOVA significance [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence

> PERMANOVA on sample-level communities (37 sediment core segments) reveals: - **Hydrogeological zone** (VZ/VSZ/SZ1/SZ2) explains **27.5%** of community variance (F = 4.05, p = 0.0001) - **Well identity** explains **19.2%** but is **not significant** (F = 0.80, p = 0.979) [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

From the report [[summaries/enigma_sso_asv_ecology__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Sediment cores were collected once per well (Feb-Mar 2023), with no within-well temporal replication for sediment. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

> Sediment cores were collected once per well (Feb-Mar 2023). No within-well temporal replication exists for sediment. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- The 18-month offset between SSO sediment sampling in 2023 and groundwater sampling in 2024 confounds material type with time and limits cross-material temporal comparisons. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- SSO geochemistry, comprising 221 sample tubes registered in CORAL, has not been loaded into the KBase Data Lakehouse, so environmental inferences rely entirely on community composition and the plume model awaits geochemical confirmation. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- SSO sediment cores (Feb-Mar 2023) and groundwater (Sep 2024) were collected 18 months apart, allowing seasonal or plume dynamics to confound the comparison. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Only 5 of 9 SSO wells have groundwater ASV data (L7, L9, M4, M6, U2), excluding critical wells M5 (denitrification hotspot) and U3 (plume entry). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- SSO denitrification and iron oxidation hotspot interpretations are based on sediment, and the report says groundwater validation at these wells would substantially strengthen the plume model. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
