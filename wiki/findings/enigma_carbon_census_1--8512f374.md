# Freshwater biofilm samples show high prevalence of Burkholderiales/Comamonadaceae genera

The newly surfaced periphyton class (freshwater biofilms) is described as a strong reservoir, with Burkholderiales/Comamonadaceae genera (Rhizobacter, Variovorax, Polaromonas, Methylibium, Sphingomonas, Hydrogenophaga) at ~96–97% prevalence and mean relative abundance ~0.005–0.009. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Key figures

- **~96–97%**: prevalence of the listed genera in periphyton samples (scope periphyton class in the NMDC atlas) [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- **~0.005–0.009 relative abundance**: mean relative abundance of the listed genera (scope periphyton) [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Evidence

> The newly surfaced **periphyton** class (freshwater biofilms: epilithon/epipsammon/epiphyton) is a strong reservoir — Burkholderiales/Comamonadaceae (Rhizobacter, Variovorax, Polaromonas, Methylibium, Sphingomonas, Hydrogenophaga) at ~96–97% prevalence and mean relative abundance ~0.005–0.009. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Caveat

Environmental genus occurrence does not validate the compound→pathway→organism inference and cannot show that any genus degrades a census compound in any sampled environment, since the atlas reports biome occupancy rather than catabolic flux. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

> it does *not* validate the upstream compound→pathway→organism inference, and it cannot claim that any genus degrades a census compound in any sampled environment. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Other caveats in this report

- Lauric acid's measured-fitness organism is a reference bacterium, not an ENIGMA isolate. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- Resource darkness is not evidence of biological absence; a shallow PubMed title screen yielded zero literature rescues. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- Xanthine's R02107 reaction reflects purine nitrogen acquisition, not carbon catabolism; the effective carbon-callable count is 8 rather than 9. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- The class-gradient analysis is underpowered and confounded by uneven annotation coverage. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- Because rare capacities compress the φ coefficient, the reanalysis used Haldane-corrected odds ratios with 95% confidence intervals and Jaccard indices from saved 2×2 counts. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
