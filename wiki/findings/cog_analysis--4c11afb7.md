# Translation genes are the most core-enriched category

The report identifies core-gene enrichment in COG J (Translation), reporting -4.65% enrichment and 97% consistency as the strongest depletion in its novel-versus-core comparison, meaning translation genes are over-represented in core genes. [src: cog_analysis]

- **-4.65%**: enrichment of COG J in the novel/singleton comparison, strongest depletion [src: cog_analysis]
- **97%**: consistency [src: cog_analysis]

- The report notes that eggNOG v6 annotations may differ from original COG assignments. [src: cog_analysis]
