# Callable capacity mostly single-capacity, Burkholderiales-concentrated, suggesting a clade trait

Among genomes carrying at least 1 of the 8 aromatic/alkaloid capacities, 675 are specialists (exactly 1 capacity) and only 18 are generalists (3 or more; max 4), with generalist chassis concentrated in Paraburkholderia (5), Burkholderia (4) and Hydrogenophaga (2); the report concludes from this that versatility is a clade trait, not a widely portable module. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Key figures

- **675 genomes**: specialist genomes with exactly 1 capacity (denominator genomes carrying ≥1 of the 8 capacities) [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- **18 genomes**: generalist genomes with ≥3 capacities (max 4) (denominator genomes carrying ≥1 of the 8 capacities) [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- **5 genomes**: generalist genomes in Paraburkholderia (scope Paraburkholderia (5), Burkholderia (4), Hydrogenophaga (2)) [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Evidence

> Of genomes carrying ≥1 of the 8 aromatic/alkaloid capacities, **675 are specialists** (exactly 1 capacity) and only **18 are generalists** (≥3; max 4). Generalist chassis concentrate in **Paraburkholderia (5), Burkholderia (4), Hydrogenophaga (2)** and scattered Burkholderiaceae — the same clade that dominates the versatility ranking, confirming versatility is a *clade trait*, not a widely portable module. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Caveat

Clade conservation fractions are denominated over depot genomes that already carry some catabolic call, not a census of all genomes of each genus, so they describe within-called-set specialization rather than absolute prevalence. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Other caveats in this report

- Lauric acid's measured-fitness organism is a reference bacterium, not an ENIGMA isolate. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- Resource darkness is not evidence of biological absence; a shallow PubMed title screen yielded zero literature rescues. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- Xanthine's R02107 reaction reflects purine nitrogen acquisition, not carbon catabolism; the effective carbon-callable count is 8 rather than 9. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- The class-gradient analysis is underpowered and confounded by uneven annotation coverage. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- Because rare capacities compress the φ coefficient, the reanalysis used Haldane-corrected odds ratios with 95% confidence intervals and Jaccard indices from saved 2×2 counts. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
