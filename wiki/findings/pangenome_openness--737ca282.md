# Pangenome openness does not correlate with phylogeny effect

Across species with pangenome statistics and ecotype analysis results, pangenome openness was not significantly correlated with the phylogeny effect (Spearman rho 0.03, p-value 0.73). [src: pangenome_openness]

## Key figures

- **0.03**: Spearman rho of openness vs phylogeny effect (scope species with pangenome statistics and ecotype analysis results) [src: pangenome_openness]
- **0.73**: p-value of the correlation [src: pangenome_openness]

## Evidence

> | Openness vs Phylogeny effect | 0.03 | 0.73 | [src: pangenome_openness]

From the report [[summaries/pangenome_openness__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Environment and phylogeny effects are derived from partial correlations that may not fully disentangle confounded variables. [src: pangenome_openness]

> Environment and phylogeny effects are derived from partial correlations that may not fully disentangle confounded variables [src: pangenome_openness]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report suggests that core/accessory classification may not capture functional adaptation because variable genes may not be those responding to environment. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The pangenome openness analysis is limited to species with both pangenome statistics and ecotype analysis results. [src: pangenome_openness]
- Pangenome openness is a single summary metric that may not capture the full complexity of pangenome structure. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The upstream ecotype analysis may have limited statistical power for species with few genomes. [src: pangenome_openness]
