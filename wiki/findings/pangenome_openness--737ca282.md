# Pangenome openness is not associated with the phylogeny effect

Pangenome openness versus the phylogeny effect gave a correlation of 0.03 with a p-value of 0.73, so no significant relationship was found. [src: pangenome_openness]

- The report raises, but does not test, the possibility that horizontal gene transfer is opportunistic rather than niche-specific. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The report raises the possibility that core/accessory classification misses environmentally responsive functional adaptation. [src: pangenome_openness]
