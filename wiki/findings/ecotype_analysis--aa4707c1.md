# No significant difference in environment effects between free-living and host-associated bacteria

Testing whether environmental (free-living, where lat/lon is meaningful) bacteria show stronger environment effects than host-associated bacteria found no significant difference (p=0.66). [src: ecotype_analysis]

## Key figures

- **p=0.66**: significance of the difference in environment effect between free-living and host-associated bacteria (scope free-living versus host-associated comparison) [src: ecotype_analysis]

## Evidence

> Tested whether "environmental" bacteria (free-living, where lat/lon is meaningful) show stronger environment effects than host-associated bacteria. **No significant difference found** (p=0.66). [src: ecotype_analysis]

From the report [[summaries/ecotype_analysis__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Host-associated organisms have less meaningful lat/lon data, as the reported coordinates reflect collection site rather than the organism's actual microenvironment. [src: ecotype_analysis]
