# Corrected iron-reduction comparison shows no significant cohort differences

After the iron-reduction marker correction, the original "shallow >> deep IR" pattern reverses (deep is slightly higher than shallow) and none of the cohort comparisons are statistically significant (all Fisher p ≥ 0.46). [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Caveat

The original iron-reduction comparison of anchor_deep (1/9 IR-positive) versus anchor_shallow (15/30 IR-positive) was driven by misidentified iron-reduction markers and is withdrawn. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

> The H3 (porewater-bias) IR-side analysis in this report — specifically the comparison of "anchor_deep" (1/9 IR-positive) vs "anchor_shallow" (15/30 IR-positive) used to argue that shallow clay shows a "Mitzscherling rock-attached IR-rich pattern" — was driven by **misidentified iron-reduction markers**. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Other caveats in this report

- KEGG has no canonical KO for the Geobacter omcS or Shewanella mtr operon multi-heme outer-surface cytochromes, and the report's original analysis substituted unrelated genes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The correction withdraws the narrative that the KBase Data Lakehouse clay cohort diverges from the Mitzscherling rock-attached community because shallow clay is iron-reduction-rich. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report's corrected porewater-bias headline is supported by sulfate reduction alone, leaving the rock-attached-versus-porewater dichotomy unsupported on the iron-reduction side. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report cites sister-project NB04 as independently flagging unreliable KEGG KO assignment for niche genes without canonical KOs, including omcS, mtrCAB, and MGE-borne resistance cassettes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- Before correction, the report claimed 3.7-fold iron-reduction-marker enrichment in shallow clay versus the broader soil baseline (p=0.0025), a result invalidated by the marker correction. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
