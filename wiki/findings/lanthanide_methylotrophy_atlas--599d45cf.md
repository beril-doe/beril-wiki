# High xoxF per-genome rates occur in phyla rarely cited for methylotrophy

The report identifies Acidobacteriota (28 %) and Gemmatimonadota (25 %) as high per-genome xoxF carriers, phyla rarely cited in the methylotrophy literature; its phylum table also shows Methylomirabilota at a comparable rate, so the report's own ranking of the top carrier is not settled. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Key figures

- **28 %**: xoxF per-genome rate in Acidobacteriota [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- **25 %**: xoxF per-genome rate in Gemmatimonadota [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Evidence

> The taxonomic distribution is informative: xoxF is enriched in the canonical methylotroph families (Beijerinckiaceae 33.7 % rate, Hyphomicrobiaceae 58.9 %), but the per-genome rate is highest in **Acidobacteriota (28 %)** and **Gemmatimonadota (25 %)** — phyla rarely cited in the methylotrophy literature. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Caveat

xoxF and PQQ presence/absence is assessed at the gene-call level, without screening for pseudogenes, truncated ORFs, or assembly fragmentation. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

> **Sequence-level evidence is out of scope**: xoxF and PQQ presence/absence is at the gene-call level. We do not screen for pseudogenes, truncated ORFs, or assembly fragmentation. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report acknowledges that its per-phylum binomial framework does not formally control phylogenetic non-independence. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- The report suggests that the 13/62 lanmodulin-without-xoxF genomes may reflect annotation incompleteness or genuine alternative lanthanide-handling pathways, since lanmodulin can bind REEs without co-location with a lanthanide-MDH operon. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- The report says REE-impacted samples show a 3.5-fold descriptive xoxF elevation (10.8 % vs 3.7 % baseline), but n=37 is too small to clear the FDR threshold (p_BH = 0.082). [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- The 897 genomes (24 % of all xoxF carriers) lacking PQQ evidence from either source are candidates for assembly incompleteness, pseudogenization or community-acquired PQQ dependence, not established cases of any one explanation. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- Discriminating assembly incompleteness, pseudogenization and community-acquired PQQ dependence requires sequence-level inspection outside the report's scope. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
