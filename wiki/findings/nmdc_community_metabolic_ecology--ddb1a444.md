# Most amino acid pathways show negative completeness-metabolite correlations, as Black Queen predicts

Across 13 testable amino acid biosynthesis pathways, 11 of 13 (85%) showed negative Spearman correlations between community pathway completeness and ambient amino acid metabolite intensity, the direction predicted by the Black Queen Hypothesis (BQH). [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

## Key figures

- **11 of 13**: pathways with negative Spearman correlation (scope testable amino acid biosynthesis pathways) [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
- **85%**: share of pathways with negative correlation [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

## Evidence

> Across 13 testable amino acid biosynthesis pathways, **11 of 13 (85%) showed negative Spearman correlations** between community pathway completeness and ambient amino acid metabolite intensity — the direction predicted by the Black Queen Hypothesis (BQH). [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

## Caveat

All 33 Freshwater samples lacked paired metabolomics in NMDC, so H1 is effectively a soil-only test and freshwater BQH dynamics are untested. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

> All 33 Freshwater samples lacked paired metabolomics in NMDC, so H1 is effectively a soil-only test. Whether BQH dynamics operate in freshwater communities at the same scale is untested. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- Methionine biosynthesis had the largest reported correlation effect (r = −0.496), but sample size limited significance (n = 18, q = 0.117). [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
- All 33 Freshwater samples lacked paired NMDC metabolomics and were excluded from H1 testing but retained for H2 PCA using pathway completeness alone. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
- Glutamine was skipped in the NMDC H1 pathway correlation analysis with n = 4. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
- Proline was skipped in the NMDC H1 pathway correlation analysis with n = 9. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
- In the NMDC H1 dataset, 95% of samples (125/131) originated from study nmdc:sty-11-r2h77870, with the remaining 6 from nmdc:sty-11-547rwq94. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
