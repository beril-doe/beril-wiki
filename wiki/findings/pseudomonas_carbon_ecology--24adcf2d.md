# Higher rhamnose completeness in P. aeruginosa did not survive FDR correction

Rhamnose pathway completeness is higher in P. aeruginosa (66.8%) than in the P. fluorescens group (41.3%), though this difference was not statistically significant after FDR correction. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

## Key figures

- **66.8%**: rhamnose pathway completeness in P. aeruginosa (scope P. aeruginosa) [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
- **41.3%**: rhamnose pathway completeness in P. fluorescens group (scope P. fluorescens group) [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

## Evidence

> Notably, rhamnose and fucose are more complete in *P. aeruginosa* (66.8%) than *P. fluorescens* group (41.3% and 45.1% respectively), though this difference was not statistically significant after FDR correction. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

## Caveat

P. aeruginosa comprises 53% of all genomes (6,760/12,732) due to clinical importance, while many environmental species have <10 sequenced genomes, which affects the power of species-level comparisons. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

> 1. **Sampling bias**: *P. aeruginosa* comprises 53% of all genomes (6,760/12,732) due to clinical importance, while many environmental species have <10 sequenced genomes. This imbalance affects the power of species-level comparisons. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report describes Pseudomonas carbon pathway profiles as not strongly predictive of isolation environment despite carrying ecological signal. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
- Classifiable isolation source metadata was available for 64.2% of the Pseudomonas genomes (8,171/12,732). [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
- The report attributes moderate ecology-classification accuracy in Pseudomonas to small class sizes, overlapping environment types such as soil and rhizosphere, and the coarse resolution of 62 GapMind pathways. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
- Pseudomonas carbon profiles carry ecological signal but are insufficient alone to discriminate fine-grained environment types among free-living species. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
- GapMind's 62 carbon pathways (Price et al. 2022) may be too coarse to capture strain-level niche-specific metabolic differences within environmental Pseudomonas species. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
