# A ferredoxin oxidoreductase OG marks deep-clay Bacillota_B

OG 1VFGN (4Fe-4S dicluster, EC 1.2.7.3 = 2-oxoglutarate:ferredoxin oxidoreductase) occurred in 8/10 deep-clay Bacillota_B anchor genomes versus 4/62 soil-baseline genomes. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Key figures

- **8/10**: anchor genomes positive [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- **4/62**: baseline genomes positive [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report states that its keyword scanner missed a substantial fraction of anaerobic-niche signals in enriched Bacillota_B OGs because these were encoded by gene-family or domain names rather than pre-registered keywords. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The report contrasts Bacillota_B gene-content expansion with Tian et al. (2020)'s streamlining finding, noting that Tian's claim was specific to Patescibacteria/CPR with ≤1 Mbp genomes and an episymbiotic lifestyle. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The report states that clay_confined_subsurface's iron-reduction analysis used mismatched markers: K07811, K17324, and K17323 encode TMAO reductase, glycerol ABC transport, and glycerol permease respectively. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The report concludes that the clay project's porewater-bias headline remains supported via SR alone, while the Mitzscherling rock-attached versus Bagnoud porewater framing is unsupported on the corrected IR side. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome contains 334 Bacillota_B genomes, much smaller than the v1.1 plan's 6,700 estimate. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
