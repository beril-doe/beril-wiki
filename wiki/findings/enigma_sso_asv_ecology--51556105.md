# Groundwater and sediment host distinct taxa within the same wells

Reported sediment/groundwater genus percentages show Rhodanobacter 1.23/3.62 (2.9× groundwater), Gallionella 0.01/0.14 (8.9×) and Sideroxydans 0.01/0.06 (7.0×), while Anaeromyxobacter 1.24/0.03 (0.02×), Arcobacter 0.54/0.00 and Ca. Methanoperedens 0.42/0.00 are depleted in groundwater. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Caveat

Sediment cores were sampled once per well in Feb–Mar 2023, and the 18-month offset to groundwater sampling in 2024 confounds material type with time. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

> **Note on sediment temporal resolution**: Sediment cores were collected once per well (Feb-Mar 2023). No within-well temporal replication exists for sediment. The 18-month offset between sediment (2023) and groundwater (2024) confounds material type with time, and cross-material temporal comparisons should be interpreted with this caveat. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- NMDS stress is 0.067, but Procrustes correspondence with the physical grid is marginal (m² = 0.379, p = 0.080). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Biogeochemical processes are inferred from taxonomy rather than directly measured; class-level annotation covers 22 classes and 78%, while genus-level annotation covers 65 genera and 21%. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Guild analysis assigns 65 annotated genera to 11 guilds and tests co-occurrence across nine wells; these correlations do not demonstrate interactions. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- A northeast-to-southwest saturated-zone plume is the report's explanatory model, not an independently verified flow-path measurement. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- The report compares its 16S amplicon evidence with separate ORR work on 77 sediment and 33 groundwater metagenomes; these are distinct data. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
