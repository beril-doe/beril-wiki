# Community turnover does not follow the hillslope

Uphill–downhill structuring is not supported (Mantel rho = −0.049, p = 0.580); the east–west association is suggestive but not conventionally significant (rho = 0.227, p = 0.092). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Key figures

- **−0.049 rho**: uphill-downhill Mantel rho [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- **0.580**: p-value for uphill-downhill Mantel [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- **0.227 rho**: east-west column Mantel rho [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence

> Critically, community turnover is **not** aligned with the hillslope (uphill-downhill Mantel ρ = −0.049, p = 0.580) but instead with the **east-west axis** (column Mantel ρ = 0.227, p = 0.092). This contradicts the naive expectation of topography-driven hydrology and points to a lateral structuring force. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Caveat

Procrustes correspondence between community ordination and the physical grid is only marginal (m² = 0.379, p = 0.080). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

> Sediment-associated microbial communities across the 9 SSO wells (3×3 grid, ~6 m span) show significant distance-decay of similarity (Mantel test: Spearman ρ = 0.323, p = 0.029, 9,999 permutations). Mean Bray-Curtis dissimilarity = 0.747, ranging from 0.558 (U3–M6, most similar) to 0.872 (U3–L9, most dissimilar). NMDS ordination achieves low stress (0.067), and Procrustes analysis shows marginal correspondence between community ordination and physical grid (m² = 0.379, p = 0.080). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- Biogeochemical processes are inferred from taxonomy rather than directly measured; class-level annotation covers 22 classes and 78%, while genus-level annotation covers 65 genera and 21%. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Guild analysis assigns 65 annotated genera to 11 guilds and tests co-occurrence across nine wells; these correlations do not demonstrate interactions. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Sediment cores were sampled once per well in Feb–Mar 2023 with no within-well temporal replication; the 18-month offset to groundwater sampling in 2024 confounds material and time. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- A northeast-to-southwest saturated-zone plume is the report's explanatory model, not an independently verified flow-path measurement. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- The report compares its 16S amplicon evidence with separate ORR work on 77 sediment and 33 groundwater metagenomes; these are distinct data. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
