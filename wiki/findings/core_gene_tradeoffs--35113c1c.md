# Costly yet conserved genes suggest purifying selection despite metabolic cost

The report identifies the 28,017 costly-but-conserved genes as its strongest evidence for purifying selection maintaining genes despite their metabolic cost, suggesting they are essential in natural environments missing from lab experiments. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]

- **28,017 genes**: costly, conserved core genes [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]
