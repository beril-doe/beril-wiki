# Meter-scale distance-decay of sediment community similarity across the SSO grid

Sediment-associated microbial communities across the 9 SSO wells (3×3 grid, ~6 m span) show significant distance-decay of similarity (Mantel test: Spearman ρ = 0.323, p = 0.029, 9,999 permutations). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Key figures

- **ρ = 0.323**: Mantel Spearman correlation of distance-decay (scope 9 SSO wells, ~6 m span) [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- **p = 0.029**: Mantel significance (denominator 9,999 permutations) [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence

> Sediment-associated microbial communities across the 9 SSO wells (3×3 grid, ~6 m span) show significant distance-decay of similarity (Mantel test: Spearman ρ = 0.323, p = 0.029, 9,999 permutations). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Caveat

SSO geochemistry (221 sample tubes registered in CORAL) has not been loaded into the KBase Data Lakehouse, so environmental inferences rely entirely on community composition and the plume model awaits geochemical confirmation. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

> - **No direct geochemistry**: SSO geochemistry (221 sample tubes registered in CORAL) has not been loaded into BERDL. All environmental inferences are from community composition alone. The contamination plume model generates testable predictions that await geochemical confirmation. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- SSO sediment cores were collected once per well in Feb-Mar 2023, with no within-well temporal replication. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- The 18-month offset between SSO sediment sampling in 2023 and groundwater sampling in 2024 confounds material type with time and limits cross-material temporal comparisons. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- SSO sediment cores (Feb-Mar 2023) and groundwater (Sep 2024) were collected 18 months apart, allowing seasonal or plume dynamics to confound the comparison. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Only 5 of 9 SSO wells have groundwater ASV data (L7, L9, M4, M6, U2), excluding critical wells M5 (denitrification hotspot) and U3 (plume entry). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- SSO denitrification and iron oxidation hotspot interpretations are based on sediment, and the report says groundwater validation at these wells would substantially strengthen the plume model. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
