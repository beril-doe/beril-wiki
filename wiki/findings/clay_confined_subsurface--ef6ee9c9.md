# Only sulfate reduction survives phylogenetic control within Bacillota_B

Only sulfate reduction (dsrAB-aprAB-sat) survives the phylogenetic control: 5/5 deep Bacillota_B isolates carry it versus 4/19 soil-baseline Bacillota_B isolates (OR=∞, p=0.003, p_BH=0.04). [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Key figures

- **5/5**: deep Bacillota_B isolates carrying sulfate reduction [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- **4/19**: soil-baseline Bacillota_B isolates carrying sulfate reduction [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- **0.04 p_BH**: BH-adjusted p-value [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Evidence

> **Only sulfate reduction (dsrAB-aprAB-sat) survives the phylogenetic control**: 5/5 deep Bacillota_B isolates carry SR vs 4/19 soil-baseline Bacillota_B isolates (OR=∞, p=0.003, p_BH=0.04). [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Caveat

The 5/5 Bacillota_B sulfate-reduction enrichment rests on an n=5 vs 19 structure that may hide genus-level differences among Desulfosporosinus, BRH-c8a and BRH-c4a. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

> The 5/5 Bacillota_B SR enrichment is striking but the "n=5 vs 19" structure may hide further granularity (Desulfosporosinus vs BRH-c8a vs BRH-c4a may differ in which non-SR features they carry). A within-Bacillota_B genus-level pangenome analysis could surface the deep-clay-specific accessory genome. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report's correction notice states that its original clay-cohort iron-reduction analysis used misidentified markers and is withdrawn. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- KEGG has no canonical KO for the Geobacter omcS or Shewanella mtr operon multi-heme outer-surface cytochromes, and the report's original analysis substituted unrelated genes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The correction withdraws the narrative that the KBase Data Lakehouse clay cohort diverges from the Mitzscherling rock-attached community because shallow clay is iron-reduction-rich. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report's corrected porewater-bias headline is supported by sulfate reduction alone, leaving the rock-attached-versus-porewater dichotomy unsupported on the iron-reduction side. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report cites sister-project NB04 as independently flagging unreliable KEGG KO assignment for niche genes without canonical KOs, including omcS, mtrCAB, and MGE-borne resistance cassettes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
