# E. coli is rarer in this Crohn's cohort than AIEC literature implies

Only 8 of 23 UC Davis CD patients carried detectable E. coli (35 %), in contrast with the canonical CD literature framing of AIEC as the prototypical pathobiont. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Key figures

- **8 patients**: UC Davis CD patients with detectable E. coli (denominator 23; scope UC Davis CD cohort) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- **35 %**: E. coli carriage (denominator 23 patients; scope UC Davis CD cohort) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Evidence

> **Principle 3 — E. coli AIEC is over-emphasized in the literature relative to UC Davis cohort prevalence.** Only 8 of 23 UC Davis patients carry detectable E. coli (35 %), versus the canonical CD literature framing where AIEC is the prototypical CD pathobiont (Darfeuille-Michaud 2004). [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Caveat

The 23-patient cohort is small, so results are exemplars or templates, not statistically robust per-patient validation. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

> - 23-patient cohort is small — results are exemplars/templates, not statistically robust per-patient validation. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Other caveats in this report

- The two phage-gap species score 4.0 and 3.3; INPHARED and IMG/VR searches remain a priority rather than completed evidence. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- No E1 patient has a feasible pure-phage cocktail in the proposed design, which instead combines direct phages, alternative therapies, and limited or engineered interventions. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- F. plautii occurs in 78 % of patients, including all nine E1 patients; its bile-acid-coupling cost and phage gap motivate deprioritization. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- E. coli is present in only 35 % of UC Davis patients, limiting direct applicability of the five-phage cocktail to ~8/23 patients. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- The proposed dosing rules call for ecotype reassessment every 3-6 months, ecotype-conditional target inclusion, and full retesting after a 5-fold M. gnavus qPCR change; these are proposed clinical rules, not validated outcomes. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
