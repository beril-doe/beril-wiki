# Confound-free analysis reverses NB04 within-ecotype pathobiont conclusions

Under the confound-free design, the classical engraftment pathobionts from donor 2708 → P1 → P2 are unambiguously CD-enriched, the opposite of what NB04's within-ecotype analysis suggested. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Evidence

> Under the confound-free design, the classical engraftment pathobionts from donor 2708 → P1 → P2 are *unambiguously* CD-enriched — the opposite of what NB04's within-ecotype analysis suggested: [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

From the report [[summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT]].

## Caveat

NB04's original within-ecotype Tier-A cannot be trusted directly because the feature-leakage criterion fails in both ecotypes. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
