# Sulfate-reduction signal in deep clay survives while iron-reduction side fails

The earlier clay project's sulfate-reduction H3 finding (deep cohort 5/9 SR-positive vs Mitzscherling rock-attached null 0.2%, binomial p=4×10⁻¹²) remains robust, since the SR markers K11180, K11181, K00394, K00395 and K00958 were correctly identified, whereas the iron-reduction side is unsupported after marker correction. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Key figures

- **5/9**: deep cohort SR-positive genomes (denominator 9 deep genomes; scope clay project) [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- **4×10⁻¹²**: binomial p versus 0.2% rock-attached null [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Evidence

> The clay project's SR-side H3 finding (deep cohort 5/9 SR-positive vs Mitzscherling rock-attached null 0.2%, binomial p=4×10⁻¹²) **remains robust** — the SR markers (K11180, K11181, K00394, K00395, K00958) were correctly identified. The clay project's "porewater bias" headline still holds via SR alone, but the IR-side comparison loses its narrative force; the "Mitzscherling rock-attached vs Bagnoud porewater" framing is half-supported (SR side) and half-unsupported (IR side, after marker correction). [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Caveat

The correction does not settle whether the original clay H3 narrative is fully recovered or partially undercut: the SR side is robust but the IR-side narrative loses force. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

> - **Phase 1 IR correction** is robust for the multi-heme cytochrome signal but doesn't address whether the original clay project's H3 narrative ("BERDL clay cohort matches Bagnoud porewater pattern") is fully recovered or partially undercut. The SR-side is robust; the IR-side narrative loses force. A revised clay-project REPORT.md amendment will note this. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Other caveats in this report

- The keyword screen placed 462 enriched OGs in an other/unannotated category that includes missed anaerobic-niche signals. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- Gene-family and domain names caused the preregistered keyword scan to miss anaerobic-niche signals; manual review of the top 15 enriched OGs found additional anaerobic-respiration/electron-transfer hits. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The report estimates that manual reclassification could raise the H1-relevant count from the keyword count of 42 to approximately 80–100 of 547; this is not a finalized count. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- Bacillota_B gene-content expansion contrasts with Patescibacteria/CPR streamlining (≤1 Mbp, episymbiotic), but the cited Tian result concerns another superphylum. The report connects expansion to a self-sufficiency hypothesis and identifies *Ca.* Desulforudis audaxviator genome GB_GCA_020725505.1 in the anchor. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- Only ~6 clusters across four multi-heme-cytochrome PFAMs were available in Bacillota_B; sequence-level CXXCH motif counting supplied most of the corrected iron-reduction signal. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
