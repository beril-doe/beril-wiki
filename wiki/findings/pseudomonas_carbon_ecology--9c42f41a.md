# P. aeruginosa group has lost most plant-sugar catabolism relative to Pseudomonas_E

Pseudomonas sensu stricto (the P. aeruginosa group) shows near-complete loss of plant-derived sugar catabolism compared to Pseudomonas_E (the P. fluorescens/putida group), with 43 of the 62 GapMind carbon pathways tested differing significantly between the two subgenera (Mann-Whitney U, BH-FDR < 0.05). [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

## Key figures

- **43 pathways**: GapMind carbon pathways differing significantly between subgenera (denominator 62 GapMind carbon pathways tested; scope Mann-Whitney U, BH-FDR < 0.05, Pseudomonas s.s. vs Pseudomonas_E) [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

## Evidence

> *Pseudomonas* sensu stricto (the *P. aeruginosa* group) shows near-complete loss of plant-derived sugar catabolism compared to *Pseudomonas_E* (the *P. fluorescens/putida* group). Of the 62 GapMind carbon pathways tested, **43 differ significantly** between the two subgenera (Mann-Whitney U, BH-FDR < 0.05). [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

## Caveat

The dominant PCA signal separates subgenera rather than lifestyles, and the analyses do not explicitly control for phylogenetic non-independence among species. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

> 4. **Phylogenetic confounding**: The dominant signal in PCA separates subgenera rather than lifestyles. Within *Pseudomonas_E*, lifestyle-associated differences are subtle, and the analyses do not explicitly control for phylogenetic non-independence among species. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- The principal pathway-profile axis separates subgenera, while lifestyle categories overlap within Pseudomonas_E; ecological interpretation is therefore limited by phylogenetic separation. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
- Only 64.2% of genomes (8,171/12,732) had classifiable isolation sources; categories were assigned by keywords. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
- Small classes, overlapping environments and coarse pathway resolution may explain the classifier's moderate accuracy. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
- The report contrasts within-infection metabolic loss with a hypothesis of ancestral lineage-level streamlining; the timing remains an interpretation. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
- The report notes strain-level hydrocarbon-gene variation and hypothesizes that 62 GapMind pathways may be too coarse for fine-grained environment prediction. [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
