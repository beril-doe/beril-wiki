# Accessory genes are not systematically burdensome

Contrary to the streamlining hypothesis, accessory genes are less costly than core genes, not more. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Evidence

> **Accessory genes are NOT systematically burdensome**: Contrary to the "streamlining" hypothesis, accessory genes are LESS costly than core genes, not more. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

From the report [[summaries/conservation_fitness_synthesis__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Condition-specific doesn't mean niche-specific: core genes simply have more detectable effects. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

> **Condition-specific doesn't mean niche-specific**: Genes with strong condition-specific fitness effects are MORE likely core, not more accessory. Core genes simply have more detectable effects. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report describes the positive relationship between gene fitness importance and conservation as modest. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The report states that laboratory conditions are an impoverished proxy for natural environments. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- A gene whose deletion gives positive fitness in rich media may nevertheless be essential in soil, biofilm, host tissue, or other natural conditions. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The report attributes the absence of a module-family breadth gradient to an already high baseline core fraction. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The identities of the 5,526 costly and dispensable genes, including whether they are mobile elements or recently acquired genes being lost, remain open questions. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
