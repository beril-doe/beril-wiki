# Core and accessory AMR genes show identical fitness

Core (intrinsic, N=638) and accessory (acquired, N=163) AMR genes show virtually identical fitness distributions: mean −0.024 vs −0.024, Cohen's d = 0.002, MWU p = 0.33. [src: amr_fitness_cost]

## Key figures

- **0.002**: Cohen's d between core and accessory AMR genes (scope N=638 core, N=163 accessory) [src: amr_fitness_cost]
- **0.33**: Mann-Whitney U p-value [src: amr_fitness_cost]

## Evidence

> Core (intrinsic, N=638) and accessory (acquired, N=163) AMR genes show virtually identical fitness distributions: mean −0.024 vs −0.024, Cohen's d = 0.002, MWU p = 0.33. [src: amr_fitness_cost]

## Caveat

Most FB species have few genomes in GTDB (median 9, range 2–399), so the ≥95% core designation is imprecise and a gene present in all 9 could be accessory at larger sampling depth. [src: amr_fitness_cost]

> An important caveat: most FB species have few genomes in GTDB (median 9, range 2–399), which makes the ≥95% prevalence threshold for "core" designation imprecise. With only 2–9 genomes, a gene present in all 9 is labeled "core" but could be accessory at larger sampling depth. [src: amr_fitness_cost]
