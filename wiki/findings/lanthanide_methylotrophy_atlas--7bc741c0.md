# Lanmodulin–xoxF co-occurrence falls just below the pre-registered threshold

Lanmodulin and xoxF co-occurrence from any source is 49 / 62 = 79.0 %, just under the pre-registered 80 % threshold, with one-sided binomial p = 0.65, so H3b is not formally supported. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Key figures

- **49 / 62 = 79.0 %**: lanmodulin genomes also carrying xoxF [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- **p = 0.65**: one-sided binomial p [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Evidence

> xoxF co-occurrence (any source) is **49 / 62 = 79.0 %**, just under the pre-registered 80 % threshold (one-sided binomial p = 0.65, **H3b not formally supported**). [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Caveat

The 13/62 lanmodulin-without-xoxF genomes may reflect annotation incompleteness or genuine alternative lanthanide-handling pathways. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

> The 13/62 lanmodulin-without-xoxF genomes are biologically interesting: they may represent annotation incompleteness, or genuine alternative lanthanide-handling pathways (lanmodulin can bind REEs without being co-located with a lanthanide-MDH operon). [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report acknowledges that its per-phylum binomial framework does not formally control phylogenetic non-independence. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- The report says REE-impacted samples show a 3.5-fold descriptive xoxF elevation (10.8 % vs 3.7 % baseline), but n=37 is too small to clear the FDR threshold (p_BH = 0.082). [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- The 897 genomes (24 % of all xoxF carriers) lacking PQQ evidence from either source are candidates for assembly incompleteness, pseudogenization or community-acquired PQQ dependence, not established cases of any one explanation. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- Discriminating assembly incompleteness, pseudogenization and community-acquired PQQ dependence requires sequence-level inspection outside the report's scope. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- REE-AMD enrichment is descriptive-only at n=37, OR=3.51, p_BH=0.082, and a larger explicitly REE-impacted metagenome collection would test whether REE supply per se selects for the cassette. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
