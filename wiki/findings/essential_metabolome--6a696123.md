# Nearly all amino-acid biosynthesis pathways predicted complete across seven organisms

GapMind predicts that 17 of 18 amino acid biosynthesis pathways are present in all 7 organisms analyzed (100% within this sample). [src: essential_metabolome]

## Key figures

- **17 of 18 pathways**: amino acid biosynthesis pathways present in all analyzed organisms (denominator 18 pathways; scope GapMind predictions, 7 organisms) [src: essential_metabolome]
- **7 organisms**: organisms analyzed (scope pilot sample) [src: essential_metabolome]
- **100%**: within-sample presence of the 17 pathways (denominator 7 organisms; scope this sample only) [src: essential_metabolome]

## Evidence

> **17 of 18** amino acid biosynthesis pathways are present in **all 7 organisms** analyzed (100% within this sample): - Complete pathways: arg, asn, chorismate, cys, gln, gly, his, ile, leu, lys, met, phe, pro, thr, trp, tyr, val [src: essential_metabolome]

From the report [[summaries/essential_metabolome__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The pilot covers 7 organisms (not 45 as originally planned) with limited phylogenetic diversity (mostly Proteobacteria plus 1 Deltaproteobacterium), so it cannot be generalized to a pan-bacterial scale. [src: essential_metabolome]

> - **7 organisms** analyzed (not 45 as originally planned) - Limited phylogenetic diversity (mostly Proteobacteria + 1 Deltaproteobacterium) - Cannot generalize to "pan-bacterial" scale [src: essential_metabolome]

## Other caveats in this report

- The ecological rationale for DvH serine auxotrophy is speculative: external serine availability and dependency were not tested. [src: essential_metabolome]
- Shared central catabolic capacity is an interpretation of the predictions, not direct metabolic measurement. [src: essential_metabolome]
- The report attributes E. coli absence to GTDB pangenome construction excluding species with too many genomes. [src: essential_metabolome]
- GapMind coverage constrained this pilot to 7 organisms instead of the intended 45 Fitness Browser organisms with essential-gene data. [src: essential_metabolome]
- Ancestral amino-acid prototrophy is a hypothesis inferred from only 6 of 7 organisms (85.7%), not an established pan-bacterial finding. [src: essential_metabolome]
