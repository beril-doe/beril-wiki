# NDH-2 presence does not predict Complex I dispensability on aromatics

In the cross-species analysis, organisms with NDH-2 show larger Complex I aromatic deficits (mean = -0.297) than those without (mean = -0.156), the opposite of the compensation prediction, and the difference is not significant (p = 0.52). [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

## Key figures

- **-0.297**: mean Complex I aromatic deficit in organisms with NDH-2 [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]
- **-0.156**: mean Complex I aromatic deficit in organisms without NDH-2 [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]
- **0.52**: p-value of the difference [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

## Evidence

> Organisms WITH NDH-2 show LARGER Complex I aromatic deficits (mean = -0.297) than those WITHOUT (mean = -0.156), the opposite of the compensation prediction (p = 0.52, not significant). [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

## Caveat

Cross-species comparison has only 4 organisms without NDH-2, insufficient for statistical significance. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

## Other caveats in this report

- ADP1 NDH-2 is missing from the deletion collection and has no growth data. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]
- The cross-species NDH-2 analysis excludes organisms with >2 NDH-2 hits as likely misannotated Complex I subunits and retains organisms with 1-2 hits. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]
- The report cautions that ADP1 respiratory wiring may be species-specific rather than general. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]
- Of ADP1 respiratory-chain genes, 36 have growth data and 26 do not, with missing data including NDH-2 and several ATP synthase subunits. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]
- The report states that FBA fails to predict condition-specific respiratory requirements because optimal-pathway assumptions miss capacity constraints that force alternative suboptimal pathways at high flux. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]
