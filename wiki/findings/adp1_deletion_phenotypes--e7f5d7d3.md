# Eight conditions provide about five independent phenotypic dimensions

Pairwise Pearson correlations between conditions are moderate at best (highest: acetate–butanediol, r = 0.58; median across all 28 pairs: r = 0.25), from which the report infers that the 8 conditions provide approximately 5 independent dimensions of phenotypic information, far more than a 2-group (demanding vs robust) model would suggest. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Evidence

> Pairwise Pearson correlations between conditions are moderate at best (highest: acetate–butanediol, r = 0.58; median across all 28 pairs: r = 0.25). This means the 8 conditions provide approximately 5 independent dimensions of phenotypic information — far more than the 2-group (demanding vs robust) model would suggest. ## Caveat

## Other caveats in this report

- The report contrasts its lack of discrete ADP1 modules, apart from the 24-gene quinate module, with published E. coli chemical-genetic modules; differing perturbation methods or organismal architecture are proposed, not established, explanations. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- This analysis uses single-gene deletions rather than transposon sequencing; the report compares its 625 condition-specific genes with findings from RB-TnSeq. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- Growth ratios come from single-timepoint measurements with unknown technical noise, and condition-specificity results assume cross-condition differences are biological. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- The 2,034-gene complete matrix excludes 499 essential genes and 316 genes with incomplete data, biasing analysis toward dispensable genes with successful deletions. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- The species-level A. baylyi pangenome assignment may have less resolution than a population-level analysis. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
