# Core genes are more often burdens when deleted

Core genes are more likely than accessory genes to be burdens, with 24.4% showing positive fitness when deleted (mutant grows better) vs 19.9% for accessory genes. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Key figures

- **24.4%**: core genes with positive fitness when deleted (scope core genes) [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- **19.9%**: accessory genes with positive fitness when deleted (scope accessory genes) [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Evidence

> **Core genes are MORE likely to be burdens** -- 24.4% show positive fitness when deleted (mutant grows better) vs 19.9% for accessory genes [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

From the report [[summaries/conservation_fitness_synthesis__REPORT]].

## Caveat

A gene that shows positive fitness when deleted in rich media may be essential for survival in soil, biofilm, host tissue, or other natural conditions. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

> A gene that shows positive fitness when deleted in rich media may be essential for survival in soil, biofilm, host tissue, or any of the thousands of conditions bacteria face in their natural habitats. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report describes the positive relationship between gene fitness importance and conservation as modest. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The report states that laboratory conditions are an impoverished proxy for natural environments. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The report attributes the absence of a module-family breadth gradient to an already high baseline core fraction. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The report cautions that condition-specific fitness effects do not imply niche specificity, because core genes have more detectable effects. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The identities of the 5,526 costly and dispensable genes, including whether they are mobile elements or recently acquired genes being lost, remain open questions. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
