# Fitness modules are enriched in core genes

ICA fitness modules are enriched in core genes (86% core vs 81.5% baseline, OR=1.46, p=1.6e-87). [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Key figures

- **86%**: core fraction of modules (scope ICA fitness modules) [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- **81.5%**: baseline core fraction [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- **OR=1.46**: enrichment odds ratio [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Evidence

> These modules are enriched in core genes (86% core vs 81.5% baseline, OR=1.46, p=1.6e-87). [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Caveat

The baseline is already so high there's no room for a gradient. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

> The baseline is already so high there's no room for a gradient. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report describes the positive relationship between gene fitness importance and conservation as modest. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The report states that laboratory conditions are an impoverished proxy for natural environments. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- A gene whose deletion gives positive fitness in rich media may nevertheless be essential in soil, biofilm, host tissue, or other natural conditions. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The report cautions that condition-specific fitness effects do not imply niche specificity, because core genes have more detectable effects. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The identities of the 5,526 costly and dispensable genes, including whether they are mobile elements or recently acquired genes being lost, remain open questions. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
