# T4SS machinery is common in high-quality environmental MAGs

21.8% of 30,497 high-quality environmental MAGs carry T4SS/conjugative machinery (6,652 MAGs; multi-marker definition: VirB4/6/8/9/10/11, VirD4, TraI/D, TrwB, TraG). [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Key figures

- **21.8%**: share of MAGs carrying T4SS/conjugative machinery (denominator 30,497 high-quality environmental MAGs; scope environmental MAGs) [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- **6,652 MAGs**: MAGs with T4SS machinery [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report's environmental T4SS–CAZy analysis is preliminary, with core analyses complete (NB01–NB04) but NB05 threshold validation and NB06 manuscript figures pending. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- The environmental T4SS–CAZy synteny threshold permutation test remains pending and requires Spark unfiltered data. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- BLAST validation of environmental GT2 HGT event Node_4915 remains pending and requires BLAST against NCBI nr. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- A housekeeping gene null baseline remains pending for the environmental T4SS–CAZy analysis. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- Biome enrichment factorisation using θ = OR(T4SS-CAZy) / [OR(T4SS) × OR(CAZy)] remains pending in the environmental T4SS–CAZy analysis. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
