# Uncertain-class ADP1 genes look like fragments or pseudogenes

The 313 ADP1 uncertain-class genes have 361 bp mean length, 42% annotation coverage, and 31% core membership, consistent with gene fragments or pseudogenes rather than true essential genes. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Key figures

- **313 genes**: uncertain-class genes [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- **361 bp**: mean length (scope uncertain-class genes) [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- **31%**: core membership (scope uncertain-class genes) [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Evidence

> The 313 uncertain-class genes are even more distinctive: 361 bp mean length, 42% annotated, 31% core — consistent with gene fragments or pseudogenes rather than true essential genes. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

From the report [[summaries/adp1_deletion_phenotypes__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Pangenome core/accessory status comes from the species-level A. baylyi pangenome, which may have limited resolution compared to a population-level analysis. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

> The pangenome core/accessory status comes from BERDL's species-level pangenome for *A. baylyi*, which may have limited resolution compared to a population-level analysis. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report suggests that the difference between Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 and Escherichia coli module structure may reflect deletion versus chemical perturbation methods or greater interconnectedness of ADP1 metabolism. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 growth ratios are single-timepoint measurements with unknown technical noise, and the specificity analysis assumes across-condition variation is biological rather than measurement error. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- The complete Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 matrix (2,034 genes) excludes essential genes (499) and genes with incomplete data (316), biasing it toward dispensable genes with successful deletion mutants. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- Only 8 carbon sources were tested in Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1, so the ~5 independent dimensions may increase with additional conditions. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
