# Many genes are both important and burdensome depending on condition

In the Fitness Browser analysis, 25,271 genes (17.8%) are true trade-off genes, important (fit < -1) in some conditions and burdensome (fit > 1) in others. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]

## Key figures

- **25,271 genes**: true trade-off genes [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]
- **17.8%**: share of genes that are trade-off genes [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]

## Evidence

> 25,271 genes (17.8%) are true trade-off genes -- important (fit < -1) in some conditions, burdensome (fit > 1) in others. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]

## Caveat

Burden (fit > 1) may reflect trade-offs rather than true dispensability. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]

> "Burden" (fit > 1) may reflect trade-offs rather than true dispensability [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]

## Other caveats in this report

- Lab conditions in the report capture only a fraction of the environmental conditions bacteria face in nature. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]
- The report cautions that the 90% identity threshold for DIAMOND matching may miss rapidly evolving genes. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]
- Fitness Browser condition types are biased toward experimental convenience rather than ecological relevance. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]
