# Plant-growth genes are mostly core, rejecting the accessory/HGT hypothesis

Mean accessory fraction across PGP genes was 29.7% versus 53.2% genome-wide, which the report interprets as rejecting its horizontal-transfer hypothesis (H3) in favour of vertical inheritance with the core genome. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Key figures

- **29.7%**: mean accessory fraction across PGP genes (scope PGP genes) [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- **53.2%**: genome-wide accessory fraction (scope genome-wide baseline) [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Evidence

> The mean accessory fraction across all PGP genes is **29.7%**, compared to the genome-wide 53.2%. The HGT hypothesis (H3) is clearly rejected: PGP genes are inherited vertically with the core genome, not acquired laterally. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

From the report [[summaries/pgp_pangenome_ecology__REPORT]].

## Caveat

pqqD is an outlier (55.5% core versus 63–81% for other PGP genes) with 27.5% singletons, suggesting occasional standalone horizontal spread. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

> An important caveat: pqqD is an outlier (55.5% core vs 63–81% for other PGP genes) and shows the highest singleton fraction (27.5%), suggesting it does occasionally spread horizontally as a standalone gene. But the functional pqq operon (pqqB–pqqC as a unit) is predominantly core. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- The reported ranking is internally awkward: pqqC + acdS is called most common at n = 153, while nifH + pqqC is called next at n = 225. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- The report gives pqqC–acdS co-occurrence as n = 286 in one section but pqqC + acdS as n = 153 in its trait-combination section; it does not explain the differing counts. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- The report attributes overall soil depletion of nifH partly to database composition: most nitrogen fixers represented are aquatic/marine, including cyanobacteria and Azotobacter, or host-associated rhizobia. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- The proposed plant-exudate explanation for the soil-specific trp–ipdC reversal is explicitly speculative. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- The phylum-plus-soil logistic model failed because of quasi-complete separation; ipdC occurred in 214 species (1.9%), so conclusions rely on simpler models. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
