# Every tested organism shows positive AMR knockout fitness shift

All 25 of 25 organisms show a positive shift (AMR > background), making this one of the most consistent signals observed across the Fitness Browser compendium. [src: amr_fitness_cost]

## Key figures

- **all 25 of 25 organisms**: organisms with positive AMR knockout shift (denominator 25 organisms tested; scope Fitness Browser compendium) [src: amr_fitness_cost]

## Evidence

> Remarkably, **all 25 of 25 organisms** show a positive shift (AMR > background), making this one of the most consistent signals observed across the Fitness Browser compendium. [src: amr_fitness_cost]

From the report [[summaries/amr_fitness_cost__REPORT]].

## Caveat

All 25 organisms are lab-adapted strains, so compensatory evolution during laboratory maintenance may cause natural-population costs to be underestimated. [src: amr_fitness_cost]

> All 25 organisms are lab-adapted strains. Compensatory evolution during laboratory maintenance may have reduced measurable costs below what wild strains experience. This makes our positive result more convincing but may underestimate the true cost in natural populations. [src: amr_fitness_cost]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report suggests that the 43% of AMR genes without an antibiotic fitness flip likely reflect narrow-spectrum resistance tested against non-matching antibiotics rather than non-functional genes. [src: amr_fitness_cost]
- The report attributes the nonsignificant class-matched antibiotic result likely to small per-class sample sizes and organism heterogeneity, with strong flips in some organisms and near-zero flips in others. [src: amr_fitness_cost]
- The RB-TnSeq organisms are lab-adapted strains with extensive evolutionary time to compensate for resistance costs. [src: amr_fitness_cost]
- Most Fitness Browser species have few genomes in GTDB (median 9, range 2–399), making the ≥95% prevalence threshold for core AMR gene designation imprecise. [src: amr_fitness_cost]
- With only 2–9 genomes, a gene present in all 9 can be labeled core yet prove accessory with greater sampling depth. [src: amr_fitness_cost]
