# Core RNA Metabolism genes are disproportionately burdensome in the lab

In the Fitness Browser analysis, core genes are disproportionately burdensome in RNA Metabolism (+12.9pp), alongside Protein Metabolism (+6.2pp) and Motility (+7.8pp). [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]

## Key figures

- **+12.9pp percentage points**: burden excess of core RNA Metabolism genes (scope Fitness Browser, RNA Metabolism) [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]
- **+6.2pp percentage points**: burden excess of core Protein Metabolism genes (scope Fitness Browser) [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]
- **+7.8pp percentage points**: burden excess of core Motility genes (scope Fitness Browser) [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]

## Evidence

> Core genes are disproportionately burdensome in Protein Metabolism (+6.2pp), Motility (+7.8pp), and RNA Metabolism (+12.9pp). [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]
