# SSO groundwater community patterns were stable across nine days

The SSO groundwater variation hierarchy was temporal median BC = 0.351 < filter 0.750 < spatial 0.917, supporting spatial pattern stability at the 9-day scale. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Key figures

- **0.351**: temporal median Bray-Curtis (scope 9-day scale) [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- **0.917**: spatial median Bray-Curtis [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence

> The variation hierarchy — temporal (median BC = 0.351) < filter (0.750) < spatial (0.917) — confirms that spatial patterns are temporally stable at the 9-day scale. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

From the report [[summaries/enigma_sso_asv_ecology__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Only 5 of 9 wells have groundwater ASV data (L7, L9, M4, M6, U2), missing the critical wells M5 (denitrification hotspot) and U3 (plume entry). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

> - **Groundwater coverage**: Only 5 of 9 wells have groundwater ASV data (L7, L9, M4, M6, U2), missing the critical wells M5 (denitrification hotspot) and U3 (plume entry). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- SSO sediment cores were collected once per well in Feb-Mar 2023, with no within-well temporal replication. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- The 18-month offset between SSO sediment sampling in 2023 and groundwater sampling in 2024 confounds material type with time and limits cross-material temporal comparisons. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- SSO geochemistry, comprising 221 sample tubes registered in CORAL, has not been loaded into the KBase Data Lakehouse, so environmental inferences rely entirely on community composition and the plume model awaits geochemical confirmation. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- SSO sediment cores (Feb-Mar 2023) and groundwater (Sep 2024) were collected 18 months apart, allowing seasonal or plume dynamics to confound the comparison. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- SSO denitrification and iron oxidation hotspot interpretations are based on sediment, and the report says groundwater validation at these wells would substantially strengthen the plume model. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
