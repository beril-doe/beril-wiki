# Ephemeral niche genes are rare but enriched in core genes

In the Fitness Browser analysis, 4,450 genes (2.7%) are neutral overall but critical in one condition. [src: fitness_effects_conservation]

## Key figures

- **4,450 genes**: ephemeral niche genes [src: fitness_effects_conservation]
- **2.7%**: fraction of genes fitting the pattern [src: fitness_effects_conservation]

## Evidence

> 4,450 genes (2.7%) fit the "ephemeral niche gene" pattern: neutral overall but critical in one condition. [src: fitness_effects_conservation]

From the report [[summaries/fitness_effects_conservation__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Lab conditions are biased toward rich media and standard stresses, and many ecological niches are unrepresented. [src: fitness_effects_conservation]

> - Lab conditions are biased toward rich media and standard stresses; many ecological niches are unrepresented [src: fitness_effects_conservation]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report states that its statistically robust 16pp gradient nevertheless means fitness importance only weakly predicts conservation. [src: fitness_effects_conservation]
- The Fitness Browser fitness measurements concern single-gene knockouts and do not capture epistatic interactions. [src: fitness_effects_conservation]
- Fitness Browser coverage of 43 bacteria, primarily Proteobacteria, limits the generalizability of the report's analysis. [src: fitness_effects_conservation]
- Singleton or novel genes may lack fitness data because of poor transposon coverage rather than true neutrality. [src: fitness_effects_conservation]
