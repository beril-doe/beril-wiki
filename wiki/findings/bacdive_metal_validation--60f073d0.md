# Metal tolerance score effect scales with contamination intensity

The report describes a dose-dependent metal tolerance score effect across BacDive isolation environments: heavy metal (+1.00) > waste/sludge (+0.57) > all contamination (+0.43) > industrial (+0.20). [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Key figures

- **+1.00**: Cohen's d, heavy metal [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- **+0.57**: Cohen's d, waste/sludge [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- **+0.43**: Cohen's d, all contamination [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- **+0.20**: Cohen's d, industrial [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Evidence

> The effect is dose-dependent across contamination intensity: heavy metal (+1.00) > waste/sludge (+0.57) > all contamination (+0.43) > industrial (+0.20). [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

From the report [[summaries/bacdive_metal_validation__REPORT]].

## Caveat

BacDive represents culturable, described strains rather than full environmental bacterial diversity, and metal-tolerant extremophiles may be under-represented. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

> - **Culture collection bias**: BacDive represents culturable, described strains — not the full diversity of environmental bacteria. Metal-tolerant extremophiles may be under-represented. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report notes that contamination-isolate sample sizes are small in Bacillota and Bacteroidota. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- Positive BacDive metal utilizers had lower metal tolerance scores, but the report cautions against overinterpreting this surprising, nonsignificant direction because the sample was tiny. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- The observed BacDive heavy metal effect d=1.00 barely exceeded the detection threshold, providing just enough power to detect the effect. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- The report states that a larger heavy metal contamination dataset is needed to precisely estimate the effect size. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- Although RESEARCH_PLAN.md referenced n=31 BacDive heavy metal isolates, only 10 matched pangenome species with metal tolerance scores after the species-name bridge. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
