# Metal-tolerance score effect appears to grade with contamination category

The report concludes that the effect is dose-dependent across contamination intensity, ordering Cohen's d as heavy metal (+1.00) > waste/sludge (+0.57) > all contamination (+0.43) > industrial (+0.20). [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Key figures

- **+1.00**: Cohen's d, heavy metal [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- **+0.57**: Cohen's d, waste/sludge [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- **+0.43**: Cohen's d, all contamination [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- **+0.20**: Cohen's d, industrial [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Evidence

> The effect is dose-dependent across contamination intensity: heavy metal (+1.00) > waste/sludge (+0.57) > all contamination (+0.43) > industrial (+0.20). [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

From the report [[summaries/bacdive_metal_validation__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The heavy-metal group had n=10 after matching, which is at the detection limit for d=1.0 and leaves the effect size estimate imprecise. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

> - **Small heavy metal group**: n=10 after matching is at the detection limit for d=1.0. The effect size estimate is imprecise. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Other caveats in this report

- Positive metal utilizers had lower predicted scores, but the direction was nonsignificant and should not be over-interpreted. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- With n=10 heavy-metal isolates versus ~5,000 baseline isolates, the estimated minimum detectable effect at 80% power was approximately d=0.93. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- The observed d=1.00 was only slightly above the reported detection threshold. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- The plan referenced n=31 heavy-metal isolates, but only 10 matched scored pangenome species after bridging. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- The report attributes an 88% core estimate for metal-tolerance genes to the separate metal_specificity project; this is secondhand evidence here. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
