# Heavy metal contamination isolates score higher on predicted metal tolerance

Organisms isolated from heavy metal contamination sites have metal tolerance scores a full standard deviation above the environmental baseline (Cohen's d = +1.00, Mann-Whitney p=0.006, n=10). [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Key figures

- **+1.00**: Cohen's d of metal tolerance score vs environmental baseline (scope BacDive heavy metal contamination isolates) [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- **0.006**: Mann-Whitney p-value [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- **n=10**: heavy metal isolates [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Evidence

> Organisms isolated from heavy metal contamination sites have metal tolerance scores a full standard deviation above the environmental baseline (Cohen's d = +1.00, Mann-Whitney p=0.006, n=10). [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Caveat

The heavy metal group has n=10 after matching, at the detection limit for d=1.0, so the effect size estimate is imprecise. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

> - **Small heavy metal group**: n=10 after matching is at the detection limit for d=1.0. The effect size estimate is imprecise. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report notes that contamination-isolate sample sizes are small in Bacillota and Bacteroidota. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- Positive BacDive metal utilizers had lower metal tolerance scores, but the report cautions against overinterpreting this surprising, nonsignificant direction because the sample was tiny. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- The observed BacDive heavy metal effect d=1.00 barely exceeded the detection threshold, providing just enough power to detect the effect. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- The report states that a larger heavy metal contamination dataset is needed to precisely estimate the effect size. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
- Although RESEARCH_PLAN.md referenced n=31 BacDive heavy metal isolates, only 10 matched pangenome species with metal tolerance scores after the species-name bridge. [src: bacdive_metal_validation]
