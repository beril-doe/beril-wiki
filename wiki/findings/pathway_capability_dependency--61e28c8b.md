# Every latent capability pathway becomes fitness-important under some condition type

Condition-type stratification of Fitness Browser fitness effects reveals that all 66 Latent Capability pathway-organism pairs become fitness-important under at least one condition type. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

## Key figures

- **66**: Latent Capability pathway-organism pairs that become fitness-important under at least one condition type [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

## Evidence

> Condition-type stratification -- separating fitness effects by nitrogen source, carbon source, stress, and other conditions -- reveals that all 66 Latent Capability pathway-organism pairs become fitness-important under at least one condition type. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

From the report [[summaries/pathway_capability_dependency__REPORT]].

## Caveat

With enough condition types and a median threshold applied to each subset, some pathways will inevitably cross the threshold, so this finding should be interpreted with that in mind. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

> The finding that "all Latent Capabilities become important under some condition" should be interpreted with this in mind: with enough condition types and a median threshold applied to each subset, some pathways will inevitably cross the threshold. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report attributes the small conservation gap between pathway categories to its 7 Fitness Browser model organisms having near-complete, well-annotated core genomes. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]
- Genome count is a critical confounder because species with more sequenced genomes may appear to have more variable pathways solely from sampling depth. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]
- Smaller within-genus sample sizes limit statistical power for many groups in the pathway-variation analysis. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]
- Only 7 of the 48 Fitness Browser organisms have matching GapMind genome data. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]
- Full phylum-level stratification would require joining the KBase Data Lakehouse GTDB taxonomy table; the report instead uses genus-level grouping from GTDB species clade names as a conservative phylogenetic control. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]
