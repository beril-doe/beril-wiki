# Deep clay isolates are enriched for sulfate-reduction markers

The 9 KBase Data Lakehouse genomes traceable to clay-confined deep-subsurface biosamples (8 from Mont Terri Opalinus boreholes, 1 from a bentonite formation) are strongly enriched for dissimilatory sulfate-reduction markers (5/9 = 56%). [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Key figures

- **5/9 = 56%**: deep clay genomes carrying dissimilatory sulfate-reduction markers (denominator 9 deep clay-confined genomes; scope KBase Data Lakehouse, Mont Terri Opalinus and bentonite) [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Evidence

> The 9 BERDL genomes traceable to clay-confined deep-subsurface biosamples (8 from Mont Terri Opalinus boreholes, 1 from a bentonite formation) are **strongly enriched for dissimilatory sulfate-reduction (SR) markers** (5/9 = 56%) and depleted for iron-reduction (IR) markers (1/9 = 11%). [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

From the report [[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]].

## Caveat

After the iron-reduction marker correction, the porewater-bias headline holds via the sulfate-reduction side alone, and the rock-attached versus porewater dichotomy is unsupported on the iron-reduction side. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

> The "porewater bias" headline (Finding 1 below) holds via the SR-side alone; the rock-attached vs porewater dichotomy interpretation is half-supported (SR side) and half-unsupported (IR side, after marker correction). [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report's correction notice states that its original clay-cohort iron-reduction analysis used misidentified markers and is withdrawn. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- KEGG has no canonical KO for the Geobacter omcS or Shewanella mtr operon multi-heme outer-surface cytochromes, and the report's original analysis substituted unrelated genes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The correction withdraws the narrative that the KBase Data Lakehouse clay cohort diverges from the Mitzscherling rock-attached community because shallow clay is iron-reduction-rich. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report cites sister-project NB04 as independently flagging unreliable KEGG KO assignment for niche genes without canonical KOs, including omcS, mtrCAB, and MGE-borne resistance cassettes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- Before correction, the report claimed 3.7-fold iron-reduction-marker enrichment in shallow clay versus the broader soil baseline (p=0.0025), a result invalidated by the marker correction. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
