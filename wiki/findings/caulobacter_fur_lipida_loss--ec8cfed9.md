# Rescue appears flux-driven or post-transcriptional, not driven by sphingolipid biosynthesis induction

The report infers that Caulobacter Δlpxc rescue must be flux-driven or post-transcriptional because lipid-A loss does not increase CPG-biosynthesis transcript, ruling out a Δfur-derepresses-sphingolipid-biosynthesis framing. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Evidence

> The cell does not respond to lipid-A loss by making more CPG-biosynthesis transcript — rescue must be flux-driven or post-transcriptional. This rules out the "Δ*fur* derepresses sphingolipid biosynthesis" framing. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

From the report [[summaries/caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The sphingolipid-machinery protein observations are single-replicate observations consistent with, but not statistically establishing, post-transcriptional stabilization when LpxC competition for the shared LptB ATPase disappears. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

> These are single-replicate observations consistent with — but not statistically establishing — post-transcriptional stabilization of sphingolipid-transport machinery when LpxC competition for the shared LptB ATPase disappears. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report regards ΔsspB-mediated respiratory protection in Caulobacter crescentus as a plausible hypothesis rather than a statistically supported mechanism of Δlpxc rescue. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
- The iron-limitation arm of H2 could not be tested because the Caulobacter fitness compendium contains zero iron-limitation experiments, so H2 was restricted to the envelope axis. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
- The pre-registered ≥10% phenotype threshold lies below the Caulobacter genome-background rate, making threshold passage alone uninformative about specific mechanistic importance. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
- Although ΔsspB buffering of respiratory-chain genes is real at the Caulobacter transcript level, fitness data do not independently support this set as mechanistically critical for Δlpxc rescue. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
- The report demotes the assertion that respiratory ATP is required for Caulobacter Δlpxc rescue from an established finding to a working hypothesis. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
