# Truly dark genes look like novel acquired biology, not annotation lag

The report interprets shorter sequences, accessory genome enrichment, higher GC deviation, mobile-element proximity, and narrow taxonomic breadth as convergent evidence that truly dark genes represent recently acquired, rapidly evolving novel biology rather than annotation lag. [src: truly_dark_genes]

## Evidence

> Truly dark genes represent genuinely novel biology, not annotation lag. The convergence of multiple independent lines of evidence — shorter sequences, accessory genome enrichment, higher GC deviation, proximity to mobile elements, and narrow taxonomic breadth — paints a consistent picture of recently acquired, rapidly evolving genes that have outpaced the growth of functional annotation databases. [src: truly_dark_genes]

From the report [[summaries/truly_dark_genes__REPORT]].

## Caveat

GC deviation is an imperfect horizontal gene transfer indicator because it can also reflect gene-specific composition biases (e.g., membrane proteins) or amelioration over time. [src: truly_dark_genes]

> **GC deviation as HGT proxy**: GC deviation is an imperfect HGT indicator — it can also reflect gene-specific composition biases (e.g., membrane proteins) or amelioration over time. [src: truly_dark_genes]

## Other caveats in this report

- An additional 17,479 Fitness Browser dark genes lack pangenome links and could not be assessed. [src: truly_dark_genes]
- The report estimates ~2,841 additional truly dark genes among 17,479 unlinked dark genes at the 16.3% truly-dark rate, including 2,208 with strong fitness phenotypes. [src: truly_dark_genes]
- The ranked list covers ~69% of the estimated total truly dark gene population. [src: truly_dark_genes]
- The report could not assess 17,479 dark genes (31%) with bakta because they lack pangenome links; an estimated ~2,841 may be truly dark, representing a 31% coverage gap in the prioritized list. [src: truly_dark_genes]
- Some bakta hypothetical-protein calls may be false negatives for genes with known functions that failed to match the PSC database version tested (v6.0). [src: truly_dark_genes]
