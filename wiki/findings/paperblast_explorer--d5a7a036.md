# The most-referenced genes are all human

The top 50 most-referenced PaperBLAST genes are all human, led by p53 (9,988 papers), TNF (6,002), and EGFR (5,895). [src: paperblast_explorer]

## Caveat

Text-mined mentions do not imply functional characterization, and the 65.6% of genes with 1 paper likely include many incidental mentions. [src: paperblast_explorer]

> - **Text mining ≠ functional characterization**: A gene mentioned in a paper may be tangential to the study. The 65.6% with 1 paper likely includes many incidental mentions. [src: paperblast_explorer]

## Other caveats in this report

- In PaperBLAST, 14,798 organisms received an Unknown domain classification, indicating many organisms are not easily classified using name heuristics alone. [src: paperblast_explorer]
- PaperBLAST includes approximately 19% of the full SwissProt database (~570K reviewed entries as of 2024), specifically the subset matched to PMC open-access literature. [src: paperblast_explorer]
- PaperBLAST mines full text only from PubMed Central, missing paywalled papers and introducing systematic bias against fields or journals with lower open-access rates. [src: paperblast_explorer]
- The report's heuristic organism-to-domain mapping classified 35% of organisms as Unknown, and a formal taxonomy lookup would improve accuracy. [src: paperblast_explorer]
- The report's 50% identity protein-family cutoff is conventional but arbitrary and not a universal biological boundary. [src: paperblast_explorer]
