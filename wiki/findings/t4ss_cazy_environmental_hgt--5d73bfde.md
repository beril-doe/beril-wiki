# No plasmid-borne CAZy genes found; transfer appears chromosomal or integrative

ICEfinder found no plasmid-borne CAZy genes (12 IMEs in top 100 accumulators), and T4SS+ genomes had 10× higher MGE density (p<0.001), which the report interprets as consistent with chromosomal/integrative transfer. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Key figures

- **12 IMEs**: IMEs among top 100 CAZy accumulators (denominator top 100 accumulators; scope ICEfinder) [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- **10×**: higher MGE density in T4SS+ genomes (scope p<0.001) [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Evidence

> **CAZy genes are not on plasmids** (ICEfinder: 12 IMEs in top 100 accumulators); T4SS+ genomes have **10× higher MGE density** (p<0.001), consistent with chromosomal/integrative transfer. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

From the report [[summaries/t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt__REPORT]].

## Caveat

All associations are observational, and T4SS-mediated dissemination remains a hypothesis requiring experimental validation. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

> These results support the hypothesis that T4SS machinery mediates environmental dissemination of carbohydrate-active enzyme diversity across phylogenetically distant bacteria. All associations are observational; mechanistic confirmation requires experimental validation. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report labels the work preliminary and says NB05 threshold validation and NB06 manuscript figures are pending, although it later presents NB05 analysis results. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- The report links CAZy–T4SS synteny to a metal-resistance niche-breadth hypothesis; describing these genomes as transfer hubs is interpretive. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- Pending checks are a synteny-threshold permutation test requiring Spark unfiltered data, Node_4915 BLAST validation against NCBI nr, a housekeeping-gene null baseline, and biome-enrichment factorisation using the stated θ formula. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
