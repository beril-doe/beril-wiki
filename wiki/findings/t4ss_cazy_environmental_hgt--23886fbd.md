# Many CAZy families co-occur with T4SS loci

In environmental MAGs, 92 CAZy families show elevated co-occurrence with T4SS loci at ≤10 kb, with threshold validation pending. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Key figures

- **92 families**: CAZy families with elevated co-occurrence with T4SS loci (scope environmental MAGs, ≤10 kb) [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Evidence

> - **92 CAZy families** show elevated co-occurrence with T4SS loci at ≤10 kb (threshold validation pending); GT2 glycosyltransferases are the top hit (767 genomes, avg 5,041 bp). [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

From the report [[summaries/t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt__REPORT]].

## Other caveats in this report

- The report's environmental T4SS–CAZy analysis is preliminary, with core analyses complete (NB01–NB04) but NB05 threshold validation and NB06 manuscript figures pending. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- All associations in the environmental T4SS–CAZy report are observational, and mechanistic confirmation requires experimental validation. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- BLAST validation of environmental GT2 HGT event Node_4915 remains pending and requires BLAST against NCBI nr. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- A housekeeping gene null baseline remains pending for the environmental T4SS–CAZy analysis. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- Biome enrichment factorisation using θ = OR(T4SS-CAZy) / [OR(T4SS) × OR(CAZy)] remains pending in the environmental T4SS–CAZy analysis. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
