# Stress dark genes are not more accessory than carbon/nitrogen genes

The hypothesis that stress genes should be more accessory than carbon/nitrogen genes is rejected. [src: functional_dark_matter]

## Evidence

> The hypothesis that stress genes should be more accessory than carbon/nitrogen genes is rejected. [src: functional_dark_matter]

From the report [[summaries/functional_dark_matter__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The coarse eggNOG breadth classification (99.9% "universal") and the sparse 48-organism Fitness Browser sampling (37/48 Proteobacteria) fail to meaningfully distinguish conservation patterns among dark genes. [src: functional_dark_matter]

> The coarse eggNOG breadth classification used in prior analyses (99.9% "universal") and the sparse 48-organism Fitness Browser sampling (37/48 Proteobacteria) fail to meaningfully distinguish conservation patterns among dark genes. [src: functional_dark_matter]

## Other caveats in this report

- The GapMind organism-pathway pairs represent organism-level co-occurrences of nearly-complete pathways and dark genes, not direct gene-to-gap enzymatic matches. [src: functional_dark_matter]
- The report suggests that co-occurring dark genes could encode missing metabolic steps but says confirmation requires EC number matching, structure prediction, or experimental validation. [src: functional_dark_matter]
- The eggNOG OG hierarchy breadth classification does not discriminate among dark gene candidates because 99.9% of dark gene clusters (30,721 of 30,756) map to universal breadth at root-level OGs present across domains of life. [src: functional_dark_matter]
- The report attributes the high NMDC abiotic correlation rate likely to confounding because carrier genera Pseudomonas, Klebsiella, and Bacteroides are abundant and ubiquitous and correlate broadly with abiotic variables regardless of condition class. [src: functional_dark_matter]
- The report considers the 4/4 pre-registered abiotic prediction rate more meaningful than the overall NMDC correlation rate because it tests specific directional hypotheses. [src: functional_dark_matter]
