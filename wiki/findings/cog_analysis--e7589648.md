# Mobile elements dominate novel genes across all species

In the bacterial pangenome analysis, novel/singleton genes show +10.88% enrichment for COG L (Mobile elements), with 100% consistency across species, the strongest signal. [src: cog_analysis]

## Key figures

- **+10.88%**: enrichment of COG L among novel/singleton genes [src: cog_analysis]
- **100%**: consistency across species [src: cog_analysis]

## Evidence

> **Novel/singleton genes consistently enriched in:** - L (Mobile elements): +10.88% enrichment, 100% consistency across species -- STRONGEST SIGNAL [src: cog_analysis]

From the report [[summaries/cog_analysis__REPORT]].

## Caveat

COG annotations cover ~70% of genes, and unassigned genes may skew distributions. [src: cog_analysis]

> - COG annotations cover ~70% of genes; unassigned genes may skew distributions [src: cog_analysis]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report counts composite, multi-letter COG categories once per gene rather than splitting them. [src: cog_analysis]
- The report's analysis uses 32 species, and a larger sample might reveal phylum-specific patterns. [src: cog_analysis]
- The report notes that eggNOG v6 annotations may differ from original COG assignments. [src: cog_analysis]
