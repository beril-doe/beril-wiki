# No significant field-specific versus lab-specific conservation difference detected in DvH

In DvH, the field-specific versus lab-specific gene comparison is not significant (Fisher exact OR=0.32, p=0.27, n=50-52 per group). [src: field_vs_lab_fitness]

## Key figures

- **0.32**: Fisher exact odds ratio [src: field_vs_lab_fitness]
- **0.27**: p-value [src: field_vs_lab_fitness]
- **n=50-52**: genes per group [src: field_vs_lab_fitness]

## Evidence

> However, lab-nutrient genes are also significantly enriched (81.4%, q=0.037), and the field-specific vs lab-specific gene comparison is not significant (Fisher exact OR=0.32, p=0.27, n=50-52 per group). [src: field_vs_lab_fitness]

## Caveat

The field-specific and lab-specific gene sets are small (n=50-52), limiting statistical power for the key comparison. [src: field_vs_lab_fitness]
