# Every analysed species shows non-random functional differentiation between gene-content ecotypes

All 12 species showed at least one significantly differentiated COG category, which the report interprets as strongly rejecting H0 (that ecotype gene content variation is functionally random). [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Key figures

- **All 12 species**: species with at least one significantly differentiated COG category (scope 12 species with valid ecotypes) [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Evidence

> All 12 species showed at least one significantly differentiated COG category, strongly rejecting H0 (that ecotype gene content variation is functionally random). [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

From the report [[summaries/ecotype_functional_differentiation__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Without controlling for within-species phylogeny (e.g., using core-genome trees), the report cannot distinguish adaptive ecotypes from demographic subpopulations. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

> **Phylogenetic confounding**: Gene-content ecotypes may reflect phylogenetic substructure within species rather than ecological specialization. Without controlling for within-species phylogeny (e.g., using core-genome trees), we cannot distinguish adaptive ecotypes from demographic subpopulations. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report describes the significance-rate advantage for adaptive COG categories as modest because housekeeping categories also differentiate in most species. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- Two sampled species experienced transient Spark S3 read errors, and one (Limisoma sp.) had insufficient structure for valid clustering. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The report interprets the moderate mean silhouette score of 0.215 as overlapping ecotype boundaries rather than discrete populations. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The identified bacterial ecotypes represent statistical gene-content tendencies rather than sharply defined lineages, with ongoing auxiliary gene acquisition/loss. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The ecotype analysis retained 12 species from 15 sampled (80%), out of 456 eligible, and its pangenome-size-stratified sample may not capture phylogenetic or ecological breadth. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
