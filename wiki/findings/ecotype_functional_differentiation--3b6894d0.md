# Adaptive COG categories differentiate ecotypes with larger effect sizes than housekeeping categories

The magnitude of differentiation (effect size) is 2.1x larger for adaptive categories than housekeeping categories, and this difference is highly significant (p = 2.53 x 10^-6, Mann-Whitney U, one-sided). [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Key figures

- **2.1x**: ratio of effect size, adaptive vs housekeeping COG categories [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- **p = 2.53 x 10^-6**: one-sided Mann-Whitney U p-value [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Evidence

> However, the *magnitude* of differentiation (effect size) is 2.1x larger for adaptive categories, and this difference is highly significant (p = 2.53 x 10^-6, Mann-Whitney U, one-sided). [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

From the report [[summaries/ecotype_functional_differentiation__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The largest mean effect sizes (S: 0.039, L: 0.034) represent ~3-4 percentage point differences in COG category proportions between ecotypes, so the biological magnitude is modest despite statistical significance. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

> **Effect sizes are small**: The largest mean effect sizes (S: 0.039, L: 0.034) represent ~3-4 percentage point differences in COG category proportions between ecotypes. While statistically significant due to large sample sizes, the biological magnitude is modest. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report describes the significance-rate advantage for adaptive COG categories as modest because housekeeping categories also differentiate in most species. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- Two sampled species experienced transient Spark S3 read errors, and one (Limisoma sp.) had insufficient structure for valid clustering. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The report interprets the moderate mean silhouette score of 0.215 as overlapping ecotype boundaries rather than discrete populations. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The identified bacterial ecotypes represent statistical gene-content tendencies rather than sharply defined lineages, with ongoing auxiliary gene acquisition/loss. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
- The ecotype analysis retained 12 species from 15 sampled (80%), out of 456 eligible, and its pangenome-size-stratified sample may not capture phylogenetic or ecological breadth. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]
