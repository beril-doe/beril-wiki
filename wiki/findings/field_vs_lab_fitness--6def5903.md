# Fitness magnitude predicted conservation more than condition type in DvH

In Desulfovibrio vulgaris Hildenborough, the finding that condition type matters less than fitness magnitude for conservation prediction was not anticipated and suggests that the core genome reflects general functional importance rather than niche-specific selection. [src: field_vs_lab_fitness]
