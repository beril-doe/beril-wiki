# Lanmodulin is restricted to three alpha-proteobacterial methylotroph families

Every bakta-validated lanmodulin-positive genome, 62 / 62 = 100 %, falls within Beijerinckiaceae, Acetobacteraceae, or Hyphomicrobiaceae, the three α-Proteobacterial methylotroph families pre-specified in H3. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Key figures

- **62 / 62 = 100 %**: lanmodulin-positive genomes in the three families [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Evidence

> Every one of them — **62 / 62 = 100 %** — falls within Beijerinckiaceae, Acetobacteraceae, or Hyphomicrobiaceae, the three α-Proteobacterial methylotroph families pre-specified in H3. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

From the report [[summaries/lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The conclusion that lanmodulin is not phylogenetically widespread holds only at the 62-genome resolution available. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

> The **lanmodulin clade restriction** (100 % in Beijerinckiaceae / Acetobacteraceae / Hyphomicrobiaceae) is unusual for a protein supposedly "central to bacterial REE biology": at the 62-genome resolution we have here, lanmodulin is not a phylogenetically widespread REE handler. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report acknowledges that its per-phylum binomial framework does not formally control phylogenetic non-independence. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- The report suggests that the 13/62 lanmodulin-without-xoxF genomes may reflect annotation incompleteness or genuine alternative lanthanide-handling pathways, since lanmodulin can bind REEs without co-location with a lanthanide-MDH operon. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- The report says REE-impacted samples show a 3.5-fold descriptive xoxF elevation (10.8 % vs 3.7 % baseline), but n=37 is too small to clear the FDR threshold (p_BH = 0.082). [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- The 897 genomes (24 % of all xoxF carriers) lacking PQQ evidence from either source are candidates for assembly incompleteness, pseudogenization or community-acquired PQQ dependence, not established cases of any one explanation. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
- Discriminating assembly incompleteness, pseudogenization and community-acquired PQQ dependence requires sequence-level inspection outside the report's scope. [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
