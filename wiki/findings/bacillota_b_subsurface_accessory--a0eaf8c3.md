# Within cultivable Firmicutes, subsurface gene-content expansion refutes streamlining

The report presents its population-scale within-Bacillota_B comparison as showing that gene-content expansion, not streamlining, is the signal at this phylum's scale, with statistical support. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Evidence

> 2. **Direct refutation of "subsurface = streamlining" within cultivable Firmicutes.** Existing literature emphasizes Patescibacteria streamlining (Tian 2020) or *Ca.* Desulforudis self-sufficiency (Becraft 2021) as case studies. Our population-scale within-Bacillota_B comparison shows that gene-content **expansion** is the genuine signal at this phylum's scale, with statistical support. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

From the report [[summaries/bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The apparent contradiction with Tian 2020 is limited because that finding was specific to host-dependent episymbiotic Patescibacteria/CPR, and Tian notes that non-CPR phyla retain larger genomes. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

> - **H2 contradicts Tian 2020's "small is mighty"** ([PMC7137472](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7137472/)) at first glance — but Tian's finding was explicit to Patescibacteria/CPR (an episymbiotic superphylum that depends on hosts for many functions) and Tian explicitly notes that non-CPR phyla retain larger genomes. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report states that its keyword scanner missed a substantial fraction of anaerobic-niche signals in enriched Bacillota_B OGs because these were encoded by gene-family or domain names rather than pre-registered keywords. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The report contrasts Bacillota_B gene-content expansion with Tian et al. (2020)'s streamlining finding, noting that Tian's claim was specific to Patescibacteria/CPR with ≤1 Mbp genomes and an episymbiotic lifestyle. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The report states that clay_confined_subsurface's iron-reduction analysis used mismatched markers: K07811, K17324, and K17323 encode TMAO reductase, glycerol ABC transport, and glycerol permease respectively. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The report concludes that the clay project's porewater-bias headline remains supported via SR alone, while the Mitzscherling rock-attached versus Bagnoud porewater framing is unsupported on the corrected IR side. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- The KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome contains 334 Bacillota_B genomes, much smaller than the v1.1 plan's 6,700 estimate. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
