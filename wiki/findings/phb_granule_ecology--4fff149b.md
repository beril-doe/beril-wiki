# PHB prevalence falls steeply from plant and soil to animal environments

PHB prevalence differed by >10-fold between high-variability plant (44%) and soil (43.6%) environments and low-variability clinical (7.4%) and animal (3.3%) environments, with chi2 = 1,656.36 and p ~ 0. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Key figures

- **44%**: PHB prevalence in plant environments (scope GTDB species) [src: phb_granule_ecology]
- **43.6%**: PHB prevalence in soil environments (scope GTDB species) [src: phb_granule_ecology]
- **7.4%**: PHB prevalence in clinical environments (scope GTDB species) [src: phb_granule_ecology]
- **3.3%**: PHB prevalence in animal environments (scope GTDB species) [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Evidence

> The gradient from high-variability environments (plant 44%, soil 43.6%) to low-variability environments (clinical 7.4%, animal 3.3%) represents a >10-fold difference in PHB prevalence, with a highly significant chi-squared association (chi2 = 1,656.36, p ~ 0). [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Caveat

34.9% of species have "other_unknown" as primary environment, so the environmental enrichment results may underestimate the true signal. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

> 3. **Environment metadata sparsity**: 34.9% of species have "other_unknown" as primary environment. The environmental enrichment results may underestimate the true signal. [src: phb_granule_ecology]
