# Horizontal gene transfer is proposed as the main innovation source

The report asserts that horizontal gene transfer, rather than vertical inheritance, is the primary innovation mechanism in bacterial pangenomes. [src: cog_analysis]

## Evidence

> - Horizontal gene transfer (HGT) is the primary innovation mechanism, not vertical inheritance [src: cog_analysis]

From the report [[summaries/cog_analysis__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Analysis uses 32 species, and a larger sample might reveal phylum-specific patterns. [src: cog_analysis]

> - Analysis uses 32 species — a larger sample might reveal phylum-specific patterns [src: cog_analysis]

## Other caveats in this report

- COG annotations cover ~70% of genes in the report's analysis, and unassigned genes may skew distributions. [src: cog_analysis]
- The report counts composite, multi-letter COG categories once per gene rather than splitting them. [src: cog_analysis]
- The report notes that eggNOG v6 annotations may differ from original COG assignments. [src: cog_analysis]
