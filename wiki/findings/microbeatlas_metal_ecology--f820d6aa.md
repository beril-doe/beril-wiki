None of the 7 leave-one-metal-out models retained significance, with all p > 0.10. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]

## Caveat

The report cannot definitively distinguish a genuinely distributed signal from an original near-threshold result pushed below significance by the compressed predictor range (max diversity 6 rather than 7) without a larger metal type vocabulary. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]

> **Interpretive caveat**: Loss of significance after removing one of seven metals is consistent with either (a) a genuinely distributed signal where each metal type contributes independently, or (b) an original finding that was near the significance threshold and is pushed below it by the compressed predictor range (max diversity 6 rather than 7). The consistently positive β direction across all seven exclusions supports interpretation (a), but these alternatives cannot be definitively distinguished without a larger metal type vocabulary. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]
