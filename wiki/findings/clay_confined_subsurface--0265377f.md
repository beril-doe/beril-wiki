# Cultivation captures the porewater fraction of clay communities

Because all 8 Opalinus genomes in the KBase Data Lakehouse trace to BRC-3 or BIC-A1 borehole isolation sources, the report interprets this as a direct, quantitative diagnostic that cultivation captures the porewater fraction, not the rock-attached fraction. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Key figures

- **8**: Opalinus genomes tracing to BRC-3 or BIC-A1 borehole sources (scope KBase Data Lakehouse) [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Evidence

> Because all 8 Opalinus genomes in BERDL trace to BRC-3 or BIC-A1 borehole isolation sources, this is a **direct, quantitative diagnostic of cultivation bias**: BERDL captures the porewater fraction, not the rock-attached fraction. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

From the report [[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The KBase Data Lakehouse has no genomes from rock-attached MAGs at Mont Terri. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

> Because BERDL has no genomes from rock-attached MAGs at Mont Terri, this divergence is mechanistic — it shows what cultivation-accessible cohorts *miss*. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report's correction notice states that its original clay-cohort iron-reduction analysis used misidentified markers and is withdrawn. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- KEGG has no canonical KO for the Geobacter omcS or Shewanella mtr operon multi-heme outer-surface cytochromes, and the report's original analysis substituted unrelated genes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The correction withdraws the narrative that the KBase Data Lakehouse clay cohort diverges from the Mitzscherling rock-attached community because shallow clay is iron-reduction-rich. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report's corrected porewater-bias headline is supported by sulfate reduction alone, leaving the rock-attached-versus-porewater dichotomy unsupported on the iron-reduction side. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report cites sister-project NB04 as independently flagging unreliable KEGG KO assignment for niche genes without canonical KOs, including omcS, mtrCAB, and MGE-borne resistance cassettes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
