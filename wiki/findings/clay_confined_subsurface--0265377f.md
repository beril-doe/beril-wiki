# Sulfate-reduction signal in Opalinus isolates read as porewater cultivation bias

Because all 8 Opalinus genomes trace to BRC-3 or BIC-A1 borehole isolation sources, the report infers that the sulfate-reduction enrichment is a direct, quantitative diagnostic of cultivation bias, with the culture collection capturing the porewater fraction rather than the rock-attached fraction. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Key figures

- **8 genomes**: Opalinus genomes tracing to BRC-3 or BIC-A1 borehole isolation sources (scope Mont Terri Opalinus) [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Evidence

> Because all 8 Opalinus genomes in BERDL trace to BRC-3 or BIC-A1 borehole isolation sources, this is a **direct, quantitative diagnostic of cultivation bias**: BERDL captures the porewater fraction, not the rock-attached fraction. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

From the report [[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Because the cohort is cultured-only, conclusions apply to cultivable porewater-cultured deep-clay isolates, not the full Mont Terri or bentonite microbial community. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

> **Cultivation bias is the headline finding, not a confounder.** Because the cohort is cultured-only, all conclusions apply to "cultivable porewater-cultured deep-clay isolates," not to the full Mont Terri / bentonite microbial community. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Other caveats in this report

- The original iron-reduction comparison used unrelated KOs, invalidating its 1/9 versus 15/30 contrast. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The cited outer-surface cytochromes lacked canonical KEGG KOs, and the original analysis substituted unrelated genes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report withdraws its iron-reduction-based account of a shallow-clay, rock-attached pattern. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The porewater-bias interpretation is supported by sulfate-reduction evidence only; its iron-reduction side is unsupported. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report identifies a broader risk of silent functional substitution when KEGG-based mining targets niche genes without canonical KOs. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
