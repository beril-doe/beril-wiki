# PHB enrichment in variable environments persists within every genome-size quartile

PHB remained enriched in high-variability environments across all four genome size quartiles (fold enrichment 1.4–4.6x, all p < 1e-11), which the report interprets as environmental selection for PHB operating independently of genome size. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Key figures

- **1.4–4.6x fold**: fold enrichment of PHB in high-variability environments (scope across four genome size quartiles) [src: phb_granule_ecology]
- **p < 1e-11**: significance across all quartiles [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Evidence

> However, genome size-stratified analysis of the environmental enrichment (H1a) showed that PHB remains enriched in high-variability environments across all four genome size quartiles (fold enrichment 1.4–4.6x, all p < 1e-11), confirming that environmental selection for PHB operates independently of genome size. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

From the report [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Although H1a environmental enrichment is robust to genome size stratification, phylogenetically controlled analyses (e.g., phylogenetic logistic regression) would further strengthen the findings by accounting for shared ancestry. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

> While the H1a environmental enrichment is robust to genome size stratification, phylogenetically controlled analyses (e.g., phylogenetic logistic regression) would further strengthen the findings by accounting for shared ancestry. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report attributes the 46.5% precursors-only category to pleiotropic phaA and phaB functions beyond PHB synthesis. [src: phb_granule_ecology]
- The report concludes that H1b, the PHB–niche breadth association, is substantially confounded by genome size and cannot independently support the feast/famine hypothesis. [src: phb_granule_ecology]
- Interpretation of PHB family environmental patterns is limited by the dominant "other_unknown" category (30/41 enriched, 26/62 depleted), reflecting sparse species environmental metadata. [src: phb_granule_ecology]
- NMDC abiotic correlations had modest effect sizes (|rho| < 0.12), likely because measurements were point-in-time snapshots rather than measures of temporal variability. [src: phb_granule_ecology]
- PHA synthase class analysis assigned all 11,792 phaC clusters to other_pfam because eggNOG supplied domain names Abhydrolase_1 and PhaC_N rather than the expected Pfam accession IDs PF00561 and PF07167. [src: phb_granule_ecology]
