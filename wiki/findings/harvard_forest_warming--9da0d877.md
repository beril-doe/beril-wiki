# RNA does not shift more than DNA under warming

The Harvard Forest dataset does not support H1 that RNA composition shifts more than DNA under warming after removing the horizon × incubation confound. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

## Evidence

> The originally proposed H1 (RNA composition shifts more than DNA under warming) is **not supported** once a horizon × incubation confound is removed. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

From the report [[summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT]].

## Caveat

All samples were collected on a single date (2017-05-24), so transcript abundances may carry diurnal and microspatial noise. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

> **Single-timepoint RNA carries diurnal and microspatial noise.** All samples were collected on a single date (2017-05-24). Transcript abundances respond to short-timescale conditions (moisture pulses, root exudate availability, diurnal temperature) that are not warming-related. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

## Other caveats in this report

- The Harvard Forest paired DNA/RNA subset confounds horizon with incubation because every organic sample is incubated and every mineral sample is direct. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- The report proposes that long-term compositional turnover could explain the comparable DNA and RNA warming responses at Harvard Forest, with DNA genome content reflecting warming-selected lineages. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- The report argues that the Harvard Forest DNA pool integrates over years and is a cleaner readout of the chronic +5°C signal in this design. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- The report proposes that the richer rare-organism functional repertoire in Harvard Forest RNA increases per-KO variance under relative-abundance analysis and reduces power to detect treatment shifts. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
- The report proposes that chronic warming-induced substrate depletion could globally down-regulate the Harvard Forest transcriptome without generating a preferred axis of treatment-driven RNA variance. [src: harvard_forest_warming]
