# Even genes with no fitness effect are mostly core

Even genes with no detectable fitness effect in any experiment are 66% core. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Key figures

- **66% core**: core fraction of always-neutral genes (scope genes with no detectable fitness effect in any experiment) [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Evidence

> Even genes with no detectable fitness effect in any experiment are 66% core. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

From the report [[summaries/conservation_fitness_synthesis__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The report describes the relationship between gene importance and conservation as modest. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

> More important genes are more conserved -- but the effect is modest. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report states that laboratory conditions are an impoverished proxy for natural environments. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- A gene whose deletion gives positive fitness in rich media may nevertheless be essential in soil, biofilm, host tissue, or other natural conditions. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The report attributes the absence of a module-family breadth gradient to an already high baseline core fraction. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The report cautions that condition-specific fitness effects do not imply niche specificity, because core genes have more detectable effects. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- The identities of the 5,526 costly and dispensable genes, including whether they are mobile elements or recently acquired genes being lost, remain open questions. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]
