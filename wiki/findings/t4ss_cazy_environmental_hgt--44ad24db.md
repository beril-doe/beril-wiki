# GT2-neighbourhood genomes carry more metal resistance genes

GT2-neighbourhood MAGs (n=376) have mean 0.045 metal resistance types vs 0.004 for non-GT2 MAGs (n=260,276; Mann-Whitney p=8.6e-27); genomes with GT2 in T4SS-proximal neighbourhoods carry 11× more metal resistance genes. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Key figures

- **0.045 types**: mean metal resistance types in GT2-neighbourhood MAGs (scope n=376) [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- **0.004 types**: mean metal resistance types in non-GT2 MAGs (scope n=260,276) [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- **8.6e-27**: Mann-Whitney p-value [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- **11×**: more metal resistance genes in genomes with GT2 in T4SS-proximal neighbourhoods [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Evidence

> GT2-neighbourhood MAGs (n=376) have mean 0.045 metal resistance types vs 0.004 for non-GT2 MAGs (n=260,276); Mann-Whitney p=8.6e-27. Genomes with GT2 in T4SS-proximal neighbourhoods carry **11× more metal resistance genes**. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Caveat

Linking CAZy-T4SS synteny to the metal resistance niche-breadth hypothesis, and calling these genomes hubs for GT2 transfer, is interpretive. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

> This is an independent link between CAZy-T4SS synteny and the metal resistance niche breadth hypothesis: genomes that serve as hubs for GT2 horizontal transfer are also enriched for metal resistance functions. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report labels the work preliminary and says NB05 threshold validation and NB06 manuscript figures are pending, although it later presents NB05 analysis results. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- T4SS-mediated dissemination of carbohydrate-active enzymes is presented as a hypothesis, not a demonstrated mechanism; the associations are observational and require experimental validation. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
- Pending checks are a synteny-threshold permutation test requiring Spark unfiltered data, Node_4915 BLAST validation against NCBI nr, a housekeeping-gene null baseline, and biome-enrichment factorisation using the stated θ formula. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
