# No testable metabolite was fully discordant across databases

No testable Web of Microbes metabolite of Pseudomonas FW300-N2E3 was fully discordant across matched databases; 4/21 (19%) were partially concordant. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

## Key figures

- **4/21 (19%)**: partially concordant metabolites [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

## Evidence

> Of the 58 metabolites produced or increased by FW300-N2E3 (Web of Microbes), 21 could be cross-referenced against at least one other database. Among these testable metabolites, 17/21 (81%) were fully concordant across all matched databases, 4/21 (19%) were partially concordant, and none were fully discordant. The mean concordance score was 0.94. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

From the report [[summaries/fw300_metabolic_consistency__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Only 21/58 WoM metabolites (36%) could be tested against another database, only 3 had four-way coverage, and the untested 64% may contain additional discordances. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

> **Low cross-database overlap**: Only 21/58 WoM metabolites (36%) could be tested against any other database, and only 3 metabolites achieved four-way coverage. Results are robust where data exists but the untested 64% may harbor additional discordances. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

## Other caveats in this report

- The remaining 37 metabolites (64%) of Pseudomonas FW300-N2E3 were observed only in Web of Microbes and were not tested in BacDive, predicted by GapMind, or used as Fitness Browser conditions for this organism. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- The report describes the 94% concordance for Pseudomonas FW300-N2E3 as largely structural because Fitness Browser (21/21 = 100%) and GapMind (13/13 = 100%) are invariably concordant with Web of Microbes production. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- The FW300-N2E3 crosswalk includes two approximate Fitness Browser base-to-nucleoside matches, Cytosine→Cytidine and Uracil→Uridine, flagged with fb_match_quality=approximate. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- The report interprets pleiotropic amino acid biosynthesis fitness genes in FW300-N2E3 as essential for growth on any minimal medium and as housekeeping fitness rather than substrate-specific metabolism. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
- Lysine Emerged in FW300-N2E3 Web of Microbes data and had BacDive counts 0+/3-, classified Moderate confidence (n=3), with consistent negatives but a small sample. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]
