# Tryptophan–ipdC association reverses in soil species

The trp–ipdC association reversed in soil/rhizosphere species (n = 1,039; OR = 0.30, p = 0.02) while remaining positive in non-soil species (n = 10,233; OR = 3.56, p = 7.7e-13). [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Key figures

- **0.30 odds ratio**: odds ratio of trp–ipdC in soil/rhizosphere species (scope n = 1,039 species) [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- **3.56 odds ratio**: odds ratio of trp–ipdC in non-soil species (scope n = 10,233 species) [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Evidence

> The stratified analysis reveals an additional complexity: within soil/rhizosphere species (n = 1,039), the association **reverses** (OR = 0.30, p = 0.02), meaning trp-complete soil species are *less* likely to carry ipdC. In non-soil species (n = 10,233) the positive association holds strongly (OR = 3.56, p = 7.7e-13). [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

From the report [[summaries/pgp_pangenome_ecology__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Only 214/11,272 species (1.9%) carry ipdC, limiting power, so the soil reversal (OR = 0.30, p = 0.02) is hypothesis-generating rather than conclusive. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

> 4. **ipdC rarity**: Only 214/11,272 species (1.9%) carry ipdC, limiting statistical power for the H4 stratified analysis. The soil reversal (OR = 0.30, p = 0.02) should be treated as hypothesis-generating rather than conclusive. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- The reported ranking is internally awkward: pqqC + acdS is called most common at n = 153, while nifH + pqqC is called next at n = 225. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- The report gives pqqC–acdS co-occurrence as n = 286 in one section but pqqC + acdS as n = 153 in its trait-combination section; it does not explain the differing counts. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- The report attributes overall soil depletion of nifH partly to database composition: most nitrogen fixers represented are aquatic/marine, including cyanobacteria and Azotobacter, or host-associated rhizobia. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- The proposed plant-exudate explanation for the soil-specific trp–ipdC reversal is explicitly speculative. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
- The phylum-plus-soil logistic model failed because of quasi-complete separation; ipdC occurred in 214 species (1.9%), so conclusions rely on simpler models. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
