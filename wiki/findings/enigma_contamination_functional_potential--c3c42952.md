# Contamination gradient does not robustly shift inferred stress functional potential

Within this ENIGMA subset, contamination gradients did not translate into a robust community-level shift in inferred stress-related functional potential when features are aggregated at genus resolution from broad COG categories. [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential]
