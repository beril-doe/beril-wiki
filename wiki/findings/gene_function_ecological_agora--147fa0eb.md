## Caveat

M22 recent means leaf-adjacent gain on the species tree, while Alm 2006's LSE used per-genome paralog counts regardless of depth, so definitions diverge for older expansions. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

> "Recent" in M22 = leaf-adjacent gain on the species tree, which captures within-genus diversification; Alm 2006's "LSE" was operationalized at the per-genome paralog count, capturing all expansion regardless of phylogenetic depth. The two definitions converge for very-recent expansions but diverge for older ones. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]
