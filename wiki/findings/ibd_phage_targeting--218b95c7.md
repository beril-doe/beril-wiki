# One joint species–metabolite axis separates Crohn's disease from non-IBD in HMP2

In HMP2 metagenomics plus metabolomics, a canonical correlation pair (r = 0.96) on which all 6 actionable Tier-A core pathobionts load CD-positive stratifies CD from non-IBD subjects at cliff δ = +0.50 (p = 4×10⁻⁴). [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Key figures

- **0.96 r**: canonical correlation of the dominant cross-modality pair (scope HMP2 metagenomics + metabolomics) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- **+0.50 cliff δ**: cliff δ for CD versus non-IBD on the composite axis (scope HMP2) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- **4×10⁻⁴ p**: p-value for the CD versus non-IBD stratification (scope HMP2) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- **6 species**: actionable Tier-A core pathobionts loading CD-positive (scope HMP2) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Evidence

> The dominant cross-modality structure in HMP2 metagenomics + metabolomics is a canonical correlation pair (r = 0.96) on which: (a) all 6 actionable Tier-A core pathobionts (*H. hathewayi*, *M. gnavus*, *E. coli*, *E. lenta*, *F. plautii*, *E. bolteae*) load CD-positive; (b) ecotype-defining commensals (*R. bromii*, *A. putredinis*, *L. eligens*) load CD-negative; (c) urobilin and secondary bile acids load CD-negative; (d) polyamines, long-chain PUFAs, fatty-acid amides (ebf/ecf substrates), and *E. coli*-specific cadaverine load CD-positive. **A single composite axis stratifies CD from non-IBD subjects at cliff δ = +0.50 (p = 4×10⁻⁴)** [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Caveat

The pilot is not a substitute for a full three-modality MOFA+ analysis, and canonical correlations all above 0.88 may indicate the per-modality PCAs are over-aligned. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

> - **Pilot-scale** — not a substitute for a full MOFA+ analysis with 3 modalities and proper factor-relevance gating. The 4 canonical correlations are all very strong (>0.88), which suggests the per-modality PCAs are over-aligned; a proper MOFA+ would impose sparsity to gate factor count. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Other caveats in this report

- The two phage-gap species score 4.0 and 3.3; INPHARED and IMG/VR searches remain a priority rather than completed evidence. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- No E1 patient has a feasible pure-phage cocktail in the proposed design, which instead combines direct phages, alternative therapies, and limited or engineered interventions. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- F. plautii occurs in 78 % of patients, including all nine E1 patients; its bile-acid-coupling cost and phage gap motivate deprioritization. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- E. coli is present in only 35 % of UC Davis patients, limiting direct applicability of the five-phage cocktail to ~8/23 patients. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- The proposed dosing rules call for ecotype reassessment every 3-6 months, ecotype-conditional target inclusion, and full retesting after a 5-fold M. gnavus qPCR change; these are proposed clinical rules, not validated outcomes. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
