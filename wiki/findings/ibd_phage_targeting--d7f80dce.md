# E1 Tier-A candidates largely replicate in independent HMP2 cohort

Within HMP2-projected E1 samples (593 CD / 337 nonIBD), 45 / 51 (88.2 %) of the NB04e E1 Tier-A candidates are sign-concordant (both CD↑). [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Key figures

- **45 / 51 candidates**: E1 Tier-A candidates sign-concordant CD↑ in HMP2 (denominator 51 NB04e E1 Tier-A candidates; scope HMP2-projected E1 samples) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- **88.2 %**: sign-concordance of E1 Tier-A (denominator 51 candidates; scope HMP2 E1) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

- External INPHARED and IMG/VR queries and multi-cohort prospective validation remain out-of-scope clinical-translation prerequisites. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- The report states that cohort-level phage cocktails will mismatch individual patients unless ecotype is known first. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
