# E1 Crohn's candidate species replicate in held-out HMP2 cohort

Within HMP2-projected E1 samples (593 CD / 337 nonIBD), 45 / 51 (88.2 %) of the E1 Tier-A candidates were sign-concordant, both CD-enriched. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Key figures

- **88.2 %**: E1 Tier-A candidates sign-concordant (CD-up) in HMP2 (denominator 51 candidates; scope HMP2-projected E1 samples) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- **45 candidates**: candidates sign-concordant (denominator 51; scope HMP2 E1) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Evidence

> per-species CD-vs-nonIBD CLR-Δ within HMP2-projected E1 samples (593 CD / 337 nonIBD), cross-referenced against the 51-candidate NB04e E1 Tier-A list. **45 / 51 (88.2 %) are sign-concordant** (both CD↑). [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Caveat

The ecotype framework itself has real cross-study variance (leave-one-study-out ARI 0.113), although the operational Tier-A list replicated externally. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

> **The ecotype framework itself has real cross-study variance (NB04f LOSO ARI 0.113) — but the operational Tier-A is externally validated.** [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Other caveats in this report

- The two phage-gap species score 4.0 and 3.3; INPHARED and IMG/VR searches remain a priority rather than completed evidence. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- No E1 patient has a feasible pure-phage cocktail in the proposed design, which instead combines direct phages, alternative therapies, and limited or engineered interventions. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- F. plautii occurs in 78 % of patients, including all nine E1 patients; its bile-acid-coupling cost and phage gap motivate deprioritization. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- E. coli is present in only 35 % of UC Davis patients, limiting direct applicability of the five-phage cocktail to ~8/23 patients. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- The proposed dosing rules call for ecotype reassessment every 3-6 months, ecotype-conditional target inclusion, and full retesting after a 5-fold M. gnavus qPCR change; these are proposed clinical rules, not validated outcomes. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
