# Eight conditions give about five independent phenotype dimensions in ADP1

The report interprets the 8 ADP1 conditions as providing approximately 5 independent dimensions of phenotypic information rather than the 2-group demanding-versus-robust model. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Key figures

- **5 dimensions**: approximate independent dimensions of phenotypic information [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Evidence

> This means the 8 conditions provide approximately 5 independent dimensions of phenotypic information — far more than the 2-group (demanding vs robust) model would suggest. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

From the report [[summaries/adp1_deletion_phenotypes__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Only 8 carbon sources were tested, so the ~5 independent dimensions may increase with more conditions. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

> Only 8 carbon sources were tested. The ~5 independent dimensions may increase with more conditions. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report suggests that the difference between Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 and Escherichia coli module structure may reflect deletion versus chemical perturbation methods or greater interconnectedness of ADP1 metabolism. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 growth ratios are single-timepoint measurements with unknown technical noise, and the specificity analysis assumes across-condition variation is biological rather than measurement error. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- The complete Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 matrix (2,034 genes) excludes essential genes (499) and genes with incomplete data (316), biasing it toward dispensable genes with successful deletion mutants. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
- The Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 analysis uses core/accessory status from the KBase Data Lakehouse species-level Acinetobacter baylyi pangenome, which may have limited resolution compared with population-level analysis. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]
