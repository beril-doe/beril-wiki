# Module transfer yields function predictions for hypothetical essential genes

Module transfer via non-essential orthologs in ICA fitness modules generated 1,382 family-backed function predictions for hypothetical essential genes, covering 35.3% of predictable targets, with top predicted functions TIGR00254, PF00460 and PF00356. [src: essential_genome]

## Key figures

- **1,382 predictions**: function predictions for hypothetical essential genes (scope all family-backed) [src: essential_genome]
- **35.3%**: coverage of predictable targets (denominator predictable targets) [src: essential_genome]

## Evidence

> Module transfer via non-essential orthologs in ICA fitness modules generated 1,382 function predictions for hypothetical essential genes (35.3% of predictable targets). All predictions are family-backed. Top predicted functions: TIGR00254 (signal transduction), PF00460 (flagellar basal body rod), PF00356 (lactoylglutathione lyase). [src: essential_genome]

From the report [[summaries/essential_genome__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Module-transfer predictions are indirect, derived from non-essential orthologs in other organisms, and the essential gene's function may have diverged. [src: essential_genome]

> - **Module transfer predictions are indirect**: Function predictions for essential genes come from non-essential orthologs in other organisms. The essential gene's function may have diverged. [src: essential_genome]

## Other caveats in this report

- Of the hypothetical essential genes, 3,912 have orthologs and are predictable by this method, while 4,385 are orphan genes that the method cannot predict. [src: essential_genome]
- The report distinguishes its experimentally defined essential set from computationally conserved gene sets by requiring lethal transposon disruption across the tested organisms. [src: essential_genome]
- The report proposes paralogs, alternative pathways, and accessory-genome compensation as explanations for variable essentiality; its broad-spectrum antibiotic-target inference is an interpretation rather than a tested drug-efficacy result. [src: essential_genome]
- Recently acquired genes, rapid evolution, and lineage-specific innovation are proposed—not established—explanations for orphan essentials; the report compares them with 149 of 473 genes (31%) of unknown function in a minimal Mycoplasma genome. [src: essential_genome]
- Essential-gene calls are an upper bound because lack of insertions can reflect small size, AT-rich sequence, or scaffold-edge effects rather than lethality. [src: essential_genome]
