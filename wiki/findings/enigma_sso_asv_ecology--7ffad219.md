# Depth, not horizontal position, appears to determine plume exposure

Samples from the same well but different depths are nearly maximally dissimilar (median BC = 0.977) while same-zone samples from different wells are more similar (median BC = 0.835), which the report reads as consistent with the plume traveling through the saturated zone. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Key figures

- **0.977**: median Bray-Curtis dissimilarity, same well different depths [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- **0.835**: median Bray-Curtis dissimilarity, same depth zone different wells [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Evidence

> Samples from the same well but different depths are nearly maximally dissimilar (median BC = 0.977), while samples from the same depth zone in different wells are substantially more similar (median BC = 0.835). This is consistent with the contamination plume traveling through the **saturated zone**: depth controls whether a sample intersects the plume, while horizontal position is secondary. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Caveat

All environmental inferences are from community composition alone, and the plume model awaits geochemical confirmation. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

> All environmental inferences are from community composition alone. The contamination plume model generates testable predictions that await geochemical confirmation. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- NMDS stress is 0.067, but Procrustes correspondence with the physical grid is marginal (m² = 0.379, p = 0.080). [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Biogeochemical processes are inferred from taxonomy rather than directly measured; class-level annotation covers 22 classes and 78%, while genus-level annotation covers 65 genera and 21%. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Guild analysis assigns 65 annotated genera to 11 guilds and tests co-occurrence across nine wells; these correlations do not demonstrate interactions. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- Sediment cores were sampled once per well in Feb–Mar 2023 with no within-well temporal replication; the 18-month offset to groundwater sampling in 2024 confounds material and time. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
- A northeast-to-southwest saturated-zone plume is the report's explanatory model, not an independently verified flow-path measurement. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]
