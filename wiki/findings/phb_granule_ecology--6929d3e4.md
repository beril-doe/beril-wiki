# No PHB in Campylobacterota

The GTDB analysis found no PHB in Campylobacterota (0/271 species), among several major phyla entirely devoid of PHB. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Key figures

- **0/271 species**: Campylobacterota species with PHB (denominator 271 species; scope GTDB) [src: phb_granule_ecology]

## Evidence

> Several major phyla are entirely devoid of PHB: Campylobacterota (0/271), Gemmatimonadota (0/102), Nanoarchaeota (0/135), and Marinisomatota (0/90). [src: phb_granule_ecology]

From the report [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome includes both complete genomes and MAGs, which may have variable gene detection rates. [src: phb_granule_ecology]

> 8. **Genome quality**: The BERDL pangenome includes both complete genomes and MAGs, which may have variable gene detection rates. [src: phb_granule_ecology]
