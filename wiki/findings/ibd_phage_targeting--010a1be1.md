# M. gnavus dominates dysbiosis shift in one longitudinal patient

In UC Davis CD patient 6967, M. gnavus expands 14× (0.53 → 7.45 reads) across an ecotype drift over 2 visits and is the dominant signature. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Key figures

- **14× fold**: M. gnavus fold expansion (scope UC Davis CD patient 6967) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- **0.53 → 7.45 reads**: M. gnavus reads from first to second visit (scope patient 6967) [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Evidence

> Patient 6967 shows clear **E1→E3 ecotype drift** across 2 visits with ***M. gnavus* 14× expansion** (0.53 → 7.45 reads) as the dominant signature; *E. lenta* 3.1×, *F. plautii* 1.9×, *E. bolteae* 2.1×, *H. hathewayi* 1.3× also expand reflecting general dysbiosis worsening, but the *M. gnavus* fold-change dominates. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

From the report [[summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT]].

## Caveat

The state-dependent dosing rule rests on a single patient, because patient 6967 is the only multi-timepoint UC Davis patient with biological-replicate samples. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

> **State-dependent dosing rule is n=1** (NB16). Patient 6967 is the only multi-timepoint UC Davis patient with biological-replicate samples. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report identifies only temperate phage evidence for M. gnavus, comprising 6 phages. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- H. hathewayi and F. plautii are phage-coverage-gap species in the actionable Tier-A set. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- The NB13 5-phage E. coli cocktail is directly applicable to only ~8/23 UC Davis patients. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- External INPHARED and IMG/VR queries and multi-cohort prospective validation remain out-of-scope clinical-translation prerequisites. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
- The ecotype framework has real cross-study variance despite Tier-A external replication. [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
