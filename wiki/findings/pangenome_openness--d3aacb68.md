# Uncorrelated openness suggests opportunistic rather than ecologically directed gene transfer

The report interprets the absence of correlation between openness and environment effects as consistent with opportunistic rather than ecologically directed horizontal gene transfer. [src: pangenome_openness]

## Evidence

> Our finding that openness is uncorrelated with environment effects is consistent with HGT being opportunistic rather than ecologically directed. [src: pangenome_openness]

From the report [[summaries/pangenome_openness__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Core/accessory classification may not capture functional adaptation, because the genes that vary may not be the ones responding to environment. [src: pangenome_openness]

> **Core/accessory classification may not capture functional adaptation**: The genes that vary may not be the ones responding to environment [src: pangenome_openness]

## Other caveats in this report

- The pangenome openness analysis is limited to species with both pangenome statistics and ecotype analysis results. [src: pangenome_openness]
- Pangenome openness is a single summary metric that may not capture the full complexity of pangenome structure. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The environment and phylogeny effects in the report derive from partial correlations that may not fully disentangle confounded variables. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The upstream ecotype analysis may have limited statistical power for species with few genomes. [src: pangenome_openness]
