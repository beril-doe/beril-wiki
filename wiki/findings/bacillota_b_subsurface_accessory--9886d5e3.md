# Corrected iron-reduction detector erases the clay project's shallow-over-deep pattern

With the corrected detector, no clay-project cohort comparison is significant: shallow (40%) is lower than deep (56%) without significance, versus the original shallow >> deep pattern (50% vs 11%). [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Key figures

- **50%**: original shallow IR rate [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- **11%**: original deep IR rate [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- **40%**: corrected shallow IR rate [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- **56%**: corrected deep IR rate [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

## Evidence

> **No comparison is significant after correction.** The original clay-project analysis showed shallow >> deep (50% vs 11%) — a "Mitzscherling rock-attached IR-rich pattern in shallow clay" interpretation. With the corrected detector, shallow (40%) is *lower* than deep (56%) and the difference is not statistically significant; multi-heme cytochrome content is similar across all three clay-project cohorts and similar to soil baseline (41%). [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
