# Callable catabolic capacity is concentrated in Burkholderiales and is a specialist trait

Callable catabolic capacity among these compounds is phylogenetically concentrated in Burkholderiales and is a specialist trait: 675 single-capacity genomes vs only 18 generalists (≥3 capacities). [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Key figures

- **675 genomes**: single-capacity genomes [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- **18 genomes**: generalist genomes with ≥3 capacities [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Evidence

> Callable catabolic capacity among these compounds is **phylogenetically concentrated in Burkholderiales** and is a **specialist** trait: 675 single-capacity genomes vs only 18 generalists (≥3 capacities). [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

From the report [[summaries/enigma_carbon_census_1__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Per-clade conservation fractions are denominated over depot genomes already carrying some catabolic call, so they describe within-called-set specialization rather than absolute prevalence. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

> **These conservation fractions are denominated over depot genomes that already carry some catabolic call — not a census of all genomes of each genus — so they describe within-called-set specialization, not absolute prevalence:** e.g. Castellaniella and Hylemonella carry 3-hydroxybenzoic-acid capacity in ~100% of such genomes, Sphingomonads (Novosphingobium/Sphingobium) carry 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde capacity at ~92–100%, Paenarthrobacter carries phthalic-acid capacity at 100%, and Comamonas spans both 3-hydroxybenzoate (92%) and 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde (85%). [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]

## Other caveats in this report

- Lauric acid's Fitness Browser organism is a reference bacterium rather than an ENIGMA isolate, so it does not change the ENIGMA-isolate deliverable. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- Organism-dark status in the ENIGMA Carbon Census means resource-darkness, not absence from science; class-level catabolic literature exists for compounds including monoterpenes and nicotine. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- The ENIGMA Carbon Census literature-rescue channel NB02b returned zero because it used a shallow PubMed-title screen, which the report calls a method floor rather than evidence of absence. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- The ENIGMA Carbon Census mis-scores xanthine as carbon-catabolic because its allowlisted R02107 xanthine-to-urate xanthine oxidase reaction belongs to nitrogen acquisition in the purine pathway, making the effective carbon-callable set 8 rather than 9. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
- With only ~9 callables across 83 compounds, the ENIGMA Carbon Census class-gradient design is underpowered and its directional pattern is confounded by KEGG/ModelSEED/genome_depot annotation coverage rather than clean biological degradability. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]
