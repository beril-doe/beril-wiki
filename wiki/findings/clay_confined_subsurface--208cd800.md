# Corrected iron-reduction markers show no significant cohort difference

After the iron-reduction marker correction, the original "shallow >> deep IR" pattern reverses (deep is slightly higher than shallow) and none of the cohort comparisons is statistically significant (all Fisher p ≥ 0.46). [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Caveat

The n=9 deep cohort is power-limited; only large effects (Cohen's d > 0.7) are reliably detectable in unfiltered comparisons, and marginal p-values are descriptive. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

> **Small anchor cohort.** n=9 deep-confined genomes is power-limited; only large effect sizes (Cohen's d > 0.7) are reliably detectable in unfiltered comparisons. Reported p-values for marginal effects (e.g., H1 within-Bacillota_B p=0.07) should be treated as descriptive. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
