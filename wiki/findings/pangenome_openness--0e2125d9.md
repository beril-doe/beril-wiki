# Pangenome openness does not correlate with environment effect

Across species with pangenome statistics and ecotype analysis results, pangenome openness was not significantly correlated with the environment effect (Spearman rho -0.05, p-value 0.54). [src: pangenome_openness]

## Key figures

- **-0.05**: Spearman rho of openness vs environment effect (scope species with pangenome statistics and ecotype analysis results) [src: pangenome_openness]
- **0.54**: p-value of the correlation [src: pangenome_openness]

## Evidence

> | Openness vs Environment effect | -0.05 | 0.54 | [src: pangenome_openness]

From the report [[summaries/pangenome_openness__REPORT]].

## Caveat

Sample size is limited to species with both pangenome statistics and ecotype analysis results. [src: pangenome_openness]

> Sample size is limited to species with both pangenome statistics and ecotype analysis results [src: pangenome_openness]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report suggests that core/accessory classification may not capture functional adaptation because variable genes may not be those responding to environment. [src: pangenome_openness]
- Pangenome openness is a single summary metric that may not capture the full complexity of pangenome structure. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The environment and phylogeny effects in the report derive from partial correlations that may not fully disentangle confounded variables. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The upstream ecotype analysis may have limited statistical power for species with few genomes. [src: pangenome_openness]
