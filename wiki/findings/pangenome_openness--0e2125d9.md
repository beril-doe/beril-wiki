# Pangenome openness is not associated with the environment effect

Pangenome openness versus the environment effect gave a correlation of -0.05 with a p-value of 0.54, so no significant relationship was found. [src: pangenome_openness]

## Other caveats in this report

- The report suggests that an open pangenome need not imply environment-driven adaptation; this is an interpretation of the null result, not a separately tested finding. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The report raises, but does not test, the possibility that horizontal gene transfer is opportunistic rather than niche-specific. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The report raises the possibility that core/accessory classification misses environmentally responsive functional adaptation. [src: pangenome_openness]
- The null association is described as consistent with opportunistic horizontal gene transfer, but does not establish that mechanism. [src: pangenome_openness]
- A single openness metric may not represent the full complexity of pangenome structure. [src: pangenome_openness]
