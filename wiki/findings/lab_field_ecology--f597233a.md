# Five Fitness Browser genera track uranium, suggesting geochemistry sorts communities

The report interprets H2 as partially supported: genus-level relative abundance correlates with uranium concentration for 5 of 11 tested FB genera after FDR correction (q<0.05), in both directions, which is consistent with geochemistry shaping community structure. [src: lab_field_ecology]

## Key figures

- **5 of 11 genera**: tested FB genera with significant uranium correlation after FDR correction (denominator 11 tested FB genera; scope Oak Ridge) [src: lab_field_ecology]
- **q<0.05**: FDR significance threshold [src: lab_field_ecology]

## Evidence

> - **H2 partially supported**: Genus-level relative abundance correlates with uranium concentration for 5 of 11 tested FB genera after FDR correction (q<0.05). The correlations go in both directions: *Herbaspirillum* and *Bacteroides* increase at contaminated sites while *Caulobacter*, *Sphingomonas*, and *Pedobacter* decrease. This ecological sorting is consistent with geochemistry shaping community structure. [src: lab_field_ecology]

## Caveat

Confounding variables (pH, dissolved oxygen, carbon sources) are not controlled for. [src: lab_field_ecology]

> - Confounding variables (pH, dissolved oxygen, carbon sources) are not controlled for [src: lab_field_ecology]

## Other caveats in this report

- Shewanella, Dechlorosoma, and Marinobacter were excluded from Oak Ridge uranium correlation analysis because their prevalence was <10 sites. [src: lab_field_ecology]
- The report states that the predicted positive trend between lab tolerance and abundance at contaminated Oak Ridge sites has statistical power limited by the small number of genera. [src: lab_field_ecology]
- The report proposes that variation in pH, redox potential, carbon sources, sulfate, nitrate, and other field parameters can disconnect laboratory metal tolerance from Oak Ridge abundance because tolerant organisms may lack site-specific metabolic capabilities. [src: lab_field_ecology]
- The report notes that field competition, cross-feeding, and syntrophy are absent from single-organism laboratory fitness measurements. [src: lab_field_ecology]
- The report notes that genus-level amplicon resolution does not match strain-specific Fitness Browser organisms, with Pseudomonas containing thousands of species with different ecologies. [src: lab_field_ecology]
