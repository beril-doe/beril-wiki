# Wood–Ljungdahl and hydrogenase enrichment track lineage, not deep-clay habitat

Within Bacillota_B, deep cohort versus baseline comparisons for Wood–Ljungdahl (5/5 vs 15/19, p=0.54) and [NiFe]-hydrogenase (5/5 vs 14/19, p=0.54) are not significant, so these markers track the Bacillota_B lineage rather than deep-clay habitat per se. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Key figures

- **5/5 vs 15/19**: Wood–Ljungdahl-positive genomes, deep versus baseline (denominator Bacillota_B genomes; scope within Bacillota_B) [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- **5/5 vs 14/19**: [NiFe]-hydrogenase-positive genomes, deep versus baseline (denominator Bacillota_B genomes; scope within Bacillota_B) [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- **0.54**: p-value for each comparison (scope within Bacillota_B) [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Evidence

> Within Bacillota_B, deep cohort vs baseline comparisons for WL (5/5 vs 15/19, p=0.54) and NiFe (5/5 vs 14/19, p=0.54) are not significant — these markers track the Bacillota_B lineage, not deep-clay habitat per se. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Caveat

Bacillota_B, which contains the lineages dominating the anchor cohort, carries Wood–Ljungdahl and [NiFe]-hydrogenase at high background rates even in soil (toolkit mean 1.65 in Bacillota_B baseline). [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

> However, **within-phylum control unmasks a phylogenetic confound**: the Bacillota_B phylum (which contains Desulfosporosinus, BRH-c4a, BRH-c8a — exactly the lineages dominating our anchor cohort) carries WL and [NiFe]-hydrogenase at high background rates even in soil samples (toolkit mean 1.65 in Bacillota_B baseline). [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Other caveats in this report

- The original iron-reduction comparison used unrelated KOs, invalidating its 1/9 versus 15/30 contrast. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The cited outer-surface cytochromes lacked canonical KEGG KOs, and the original analysis substituted unrelated genes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report withdraws its iron-reduction-based account of a shallow-clay, rock-attached pattern. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The porewater-bias interpretation is supported by sulfate-reduction evidence only; its iron-reduction side is unsupported. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report identifies a broader risk of silent functional substitution when KEGG-based mining targets niche genes without canonical KOs. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
