# Wood–Ljungdahl carriage tracks Bacillota_B lineage, not deep-clay habitat

Within Bacillota_B, deep cohort versus baseline comparison for Wood–Ljungdahl carriage (5/5 vs 15/19, p=0.54) is not significant, so this marker tracks the Bacillota_B lineage, not deep-clay habitat per se. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

## Caveat

## Other caveats in this report

- The report's correction notice states that its original clay-cohort iron-reduction analysis used misidentified markers and is withdrawn. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- KEGG has no canonical KO for the Geobacter omcS or Shewanella mtr operon multi-heme outer-surface cytochromes, and the report's original analysis substituted unrelated genes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The correction withdraws the narrative that the KBase Data Lakehouse clay cohort diverges from the Mitzscherling rock-attached community because shallow clay is iron-reduction-rich. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report's corrected porewater-bias headline is supported by sulfate reduction alone, leaving the rock-attached-versus-porewater dichotomy unsupported on the iron-reduction side. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- The report cites sister-project NB04 as independently flagging unreliable KEGG KO assignment for niche genes without canonical KOs, including omcS, mtrCAB, and MGE-borne resistance cassettes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
