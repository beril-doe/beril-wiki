# No tested substrate gives commensals a clear advantage over PA14

PA14 outgrows the average commensal on every tested substrate, and the most selective substrates (cysteine, threonine, methionine) have ratios of only 0.77–0.96, so there is no amino acid or simple sugar where commensals have a clear growth advantage. [src: cf_formulation_design]

## Key figures

- **0.77–0.96**: selectivity ratio (commensal mean OD / PA14 OD) for the most selective substrates (scope cysteine, threonine, methionine) [src: cf_formulation_design]

## Evidence

> PA14 outgrows the average commensal on **every tested substrate**. The most selective substrates (cysteine, threonine, methionine) have ratios of only 0.77–0.96. There is no amino acid or simple sugar where commensals have a clear growth advantage. [src: cf_formulation_design]

## Caveat

The report concludes that among the 22 tested substrates no selective prebiotic exists, so competitive exclusion must work through community-level resource depletion, and it turned to a genomic extension to search for untested substrates. [src: cf_formulation_design]

> **Conclusion**: Among the 22 tested substrates, no selective prebiotic exists. Competitive exclusion must work through **community-level resource depletion** — multiple organisms collectively consuming the resource pool faster than PA14 alone — rather than individual substrate advantage. This motivated our genomic extension analysis (Section 2.10) to search for untested substrates. [src: cf_formulation_design]

## Other caveats in this report

- Competitive metabolic exclusion is the study's working theory; the listed CF-sputum amino acids are proposed shared resources. [src: cf_formulation_design]
- Selective prebiotic biomass pre-loading is explicitly a hypothesis. [src: cf_formulation_design]
- GTDB taxonomy is used; Pseudomonas_E is a non-aeruginosa clade and should not be merged with P. aeruginosa. [src: cf_formulation_design]
- The three named organisms have the largest reported isolate counts; pathogen oversampling limits interpretation of collection frequencies as natural prevalence. [src: cf_formulation_design]
- Acinetobacter baumannii and Klebsiella pneumoniae are other named reporter pathogens; their distinct profiles limit generalization of the PA14-targeted formulation. [src: cf_formulation_design]
