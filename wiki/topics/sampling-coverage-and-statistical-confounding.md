# Sampling Coverage and Statistical Confounding

Every comparative claim in this corpus — that an environment shapes gene content, that contamination selects for defense functions, that a phenotype tracks metal tolerance — rests on a collection of genomes, reads, or strains that someone chose to sequence. This topic hub covers what happens when that choice, rather than biology, drives the result. The corpus speaks to it with unusual force because several projects went looking for a biological signal, found one under permissive validation, and then watched it evaporate under a design that held out whole studies: a gradient-boosted model of eukaryotic read fraction scored R²=0.35 for environment under random cross-validation but R²=−0.30 when complete studies were held out, with an out-of-study detection AUC of 0.56, approximately chance [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. Other projects found the reverse — that a suspected sampling artifact, when tested directly, failed to explain the weak signal attributed to it [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis]. Nine member concepts and seven conflict pages sit beneath this hub; the four sub-themes below organize what they jointly establish.

## Literature Context

The field has long held that reference databases are incomplete in ways that bias what metagenomics can see. A survey of 184 viral assemblages from 68 sites across four oceanic regions found that most viral sequences were not similar to anything in the databases of the time [PMID 17090214](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17090214/), and longitudinal sewage sequencing from seven treatment plants in five European cities recovered 2332 prokaryotic metagenome-assembled genomes of which 1334 were previously undescribed, with the authors framing unknown species similar to known genomes as a source of database bias [PMID 39215001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39215001/). Binning methods were developed precisely because supervised approaches "rely heavily on reference genomes of known microorganisms and phylogenetic marker genes," whose limited availability and instability restrict applicability [PMID 20406503](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20406503/), and reviews of microbiome resources note that heterogeneous sampling, preprocessing and annotation methods make studies hard to compare and data hard to recycle [PMID 37362854](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37362854/). This corpus is consistent with that consensus and sharpens it into an arithmetic form: GOTTCHA2 detected eukaryotic reads in 77% of 2,759 runs while Kraken2 and Centrifuge returned approximately 0 domain-level Eukaryota because their reference databases were prokaryote-restricted — a database that cannot represent a taxon cannot testify to its absence.

That a methodological choice moves the estimate is established. A full factorial comparison of 48 protocols on stool samples found methodological bias similar in magnitude to real biological differences, varying substantially between taxa even among closely related genera [PMID 39807898](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39807898/). In a full-scale swine wastewater plant, V6–V8 primers overestimated archaeal methanogens by over 30 times relative to primer-bias-free metagenomic data [PMID 37303742](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37303742/); primer non-coverage was under 6% in the Ribosomal Database Project (RDP), a curated 16S rRNA gene sequence repository, for all primers except 27F, but above 10% for most primers in the metagenomic datasets [PMID 22554309](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22554309/); and a GAAS re-analysis of one dataset, the Sargasso Sea virome, indicated that standard methodologies "may dramatically underestimate" small-genome organisms [PMID 20011103](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20011103/). The corpus extends this from assay protocols to analysis-design choices: using all embedded genomes rather than diversity-maximizing downsampling at a maximum of 250 per species moved the all-species median partial correlation from 0.003 to 0.081, a difference characterized as 27x with the caution that absolute values are not comparable across the two methodologies, while strict and relaxed genus-to-clade mapping retained a mean mapped abundance fraction of 0.343 against 0.031 for the finer unique-clade species-proxy mode.

Where the corpus is novel is in refusing to let a documented bias stand as an explanation. The literature above quantifies bias but does not test whether an acknowledged skew accounts for a specific weak biological result; here that test was run and returned null (U=1536, p=0.83; rho=−0.085, p=0.25), and held-out-study validation collapsed an environment model from R²=0.35 to R²=−0.30 with detection AUC 0.56. The nearest published analogue is a meta-analysis that screened 1050 publications, included 10, and reported no significant alpha-diversity difference between bronchoalveolar lavage and tracheal sampling while calling for further high-quality studies [PMID 41130610](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41130610/) — a methodological null left null, as this corpus leaves its own.

## What the Corpus Shows

**Reference collections are not samples of nature, and the shortfall is quantified.** Of 97,334 BacDive strains, 42,227 (43.4%) matched 6,426 GTDB species while 55,107 (56.6%) remained unmatched, and only 25,089 matched strains carried isolation-source metadata [src: bacdive_metal_validation]. A second bridge from the same database matched 37,368 strains (38.4%) to a pangenome species and metal score, covering 5,647 of 27,702 GTDB species (38.4%) [src: bacdive_phenotype_metal_tolerance]. Plant-association labels existed for only 7,995 of 293,059 genomes (2.7%), endophytes for 29 species [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]. AlphaEarth embeddings — numeric vectors summarizing satellite imagery around each sampling coordinate — covered 28.4% of genomes [src: ecotype_analysis]. Occurrence atlases carry the same signature: sequenced-genome coverage correlated with inferred metal-type diversity (r = 0.35, p = 2.6×10⁻¹⁹), and single-genome genera had lower metal-type counts than multi-genome genera (Mann–Whitney p = 5.1×10⁻¹⁰) [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]. The Genomic Discovery Index — OTU (operational taxonomic unit, a sequence cluster treated as one taxon) Richness / (Mean Genome Completeness + 1) — flagged frontier areas with GDI > 1000 at mean pH = 6.74 versus 5.94 in mapped areas, a +0.8 pH unit gap [src: soil_frontier_genomics]. See [[concepts/cultivation-collection-bias-in-ecological-genomics]] and [[concepts/genomic-under-representation]].

**Skew is real; whether it explains a weak result is a separate, testable question.** The corpus's cleanest methodological lesson is that documenting a bias does not license blaming it. Among 83,287 genomes with AlphaEarth embeddings, 38% were human-associated, including 16,390 Human clinical (20%), 13,466 Human gut (16%), and 1,669 Human other (2%), against Soil at 6,073 (7%), Marine at 5,850 (7%), and Freshwater at 5,840 (7%) [src: env_embedding_explorer]. Of 224 species selected with ≥20 embedded genomes and ≥30% coverage, 106 (47%) were majority human-associated, 47 (21%) majority environmental, 71 (32%) Mixed/Other [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis]. Tested directly, the skew did not do the work attributed to it: Environmental species had a median environment–gene-content partial correlation of 0.051 against 0.084 for Human-associated, a one-sided Environmental > Human-associated Mann–Whitney gave U=1536, p=0.83, and the continuous fraction-of-environmental-genomes analysis gave rho=−0.085, p=0.25 [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis]. This is a null result and stays one. See [[concepts/sampling-depth-and-downsampling-effects]].

**Sampling depth, aggregation grain, and classifier scope each change the number you get.** Using all embedded genomes (up to 3,505 per species) rather than diversity-maximizing downsampling at a maximum of 250 per species moved the all-species median partial correlation from 0.003 to 0.081, a difference the report characterizes as 27x, while cautioning that absolute values are not comparable across the two methodologies [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis]. Grain matters equally: strict and relaxed genus-to-clade mapping retained a mean mapped abundance fraction of 0.343, whereas a unique-clade species-proxy mode retained 0.031, trading ambiguity for abundance [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential]. Ambiguity was itself concentrated — 380 mapped genera were multi-clade, maximum 433 clades per genus, led by `pseudomonas` at 433 [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential]. And a database that cannot represent a taxon cannot testify to its absence: GOTTCHA2 detected eukaryotic reads in 77% of 2,759 runs (median fraction 2.7%, mean 13.3%, 20% of runs above 20%), while Kraken2 and Centrifuge returned approximately 0 domain-level Eukaryota because their reference databases were prokaryote-restricted [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. See [[concepts/classifier-database-compatibility-in-taxonomic-quantification]] and [[concepts/taxonomic-resolution-dependent-functional-inference]].

**Confounding by study, lineage, and feature reuse can manufacture or reverse an effect.** Each NMDC (National Microbiome Data Collaborative) biome was approximately 80–100% nested within a single study, confounding environment with batch; a study_id-only model reached R²=0.24 under random cross-validation against environment's R²=0.35, and adding sequencing metadata worsened out-of-study performance to R²=−0.39 [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. Lineage is parallel: across 172 species, median partial correlation was 0.0025 for environment against 0.0143 for phylogeny, with significant positive environmental effects in 12 species (7.0%), negative in 4 (2.3%), none in 156 (90.7%) [src: ecotype_analysis]. Composition alone can inflate: a compartment R² of 0.527 fell to 0.072 after excluding three genome-rich species per compartment, an 86% loss [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]. Feature reuse: held-out-species sensitivity on K=4 latent Dirichlet allocation (LDA, which sorts samples into K latent groups) ecotypes gave Jaccard 0.230 for E1 and 0.064 for E3, against that project's thresholds (above 0.5 bounded leakage, below 0.3 leakage dominating), specific to that sensitivity procedure rather than universal statistical guarantees; independent within-substudy evidence cut a 33-species Tier-A list to 3 [src: pitfalls]. Pooling is opposite-signed: 1,067 of 2,759 runs pooled multiple biosamples, forcing run-level analysis with metadata inherited from one `MIN(biosample_id)` representative — label noise the report calls a conservative bias that can weaken rather than manufacture associations [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. See [[concepts/study-batch-confounding-of-environmental-associations]], [[concepts/selection-on-outcome-leakage]], and [[concepts/pooled-run-pseudoreplication-and-metadata-label-noise]].

## Tensions and Caveats

The sharpest disagreement is arithmetic. The original ecotype analysis places its environment median at 0.0025 [src: ecotype_analysis]; the reanalysis places its all-species median at 0.081 across 183 species [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis]; and the reanalysis's own quotation of the original, 0.003, does not match the original page's number [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis, ecotype_analysis]. This is recorded, unaveraged, at [[conflicts/conflict--confirmatory-exploratory-ecological-association-discordance--c1d44874]] and again — framed as a measurement-and-sampling tension deliberately *not* attributed to feature reuse by default — at [[conflicts/conflict--selection-on-outcome-leakage--c66707fc]].

Two tensions ask whether a documented sampling defect is the explanation. Missingness — NaN ('not a number') gaps in AlphaEarth embedding vectors or derived statistics — was uneven: Environmental species 21% NaN against 7% for Human-associated, yet filtering out more Environmental species left no stronger environmental signal [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis]; whether that sets missingness aside is [[conflicts/conflict--sampling-depth-and-downsampling-effects--8542320b]]. Whether the agreed clinical skew explains the weak environment–gene-content signal is disputed at [[conflicts/conflict--sampling-depth-and-downsampling-effects--e2e1ad79]]; the direct test of that skew was null [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis].

Resolution cuts both ways. Within-species gene-content ecotypes showed 170 of 257 COG (Clusters of Orthologous Groups, a coarse functional binning) tests significant after BH-FDR — the Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction — with adaptive categories at mean effect 0.0136 against housekeeping at 0.0064 (Mann–Whitney p = 2.53 x 10^-6), but without within-species phylogenetic controls [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation]. Whether that differentiation is ecological or lineage structure is [[conflicts/conflict--phenotype-database-coverage-bias--539e6d8e]]; whether refining resolution should recover contamination-linked function at all is [[conflicts/conflict--taxonomic-resolution-dependent-functional-inference--326d40ea]], and its COG-specific form, consistent core-versus-novel partitioning across 32 species [src: cog_analysis] beside a genus-resolution null [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential], is [[conflicts/conflict--taxonomic-resolution-dependent-functional-inference--4b4aa6ed]].

Load-bearing caveats: confirmatory genus-level tests of site defense score against contamination were non-significant in both mapping modes (relaxed rho = 0.0587, p = 0.546, FDR q = 0.862; strict rho = 0.0682, p = 0.483, q = 0.849), robust across four index definitions, with all eight variant tests at q = 0.546 [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential]. Pangenome openness predicted neither environment (rho = −0.05, p = 0.54) nor phylogeny effects (rho = 0.03, p = 0.73) — a null about one summary metric, not about pangenome biology [src: pangenome_openness]. Small denominators remain dangerous: an H₂S effect of d = −0.87 rests on 8 H₂S-negative species [src: bacdive_phenotype_metal_tolerance]. And annotation coverage caps everything downstream — approximately 38% of gene clusters had COG annotations [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation], and GTDB r214 propagation raised dark-gene conservation coverage from 32,791 (57.5%) to 37,997 (66.6%) [src: functional_dark_matter].
