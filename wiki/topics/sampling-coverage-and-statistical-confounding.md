# Sampling Coverage and Statistical Confounding

Many apparent ecological and genomic patterns in this corpus depend on who was sampled, what the reference databases could represent, and which observations were treated as independent. The corpus speaks to this because many projects reuse the same public genome, metagenome and fitness collections, and several of them audited their own caveats directly. This hub groups the member concepts into four themes, then lists the open disagreements.

## What the Corpus Shows

**Who was sampled: clinical, cultivated and literature skew.** Human clinical and gut genomes dominate the genome sets analysed; 38% of genomes in the AlphaEarth subset are human-associated, though that figure describes one subset only [src: env_embedding_explorer]. See [[concepts/clinical-and-human-associated-sampling-bias-in-genome-collections]]. In the ecotype reanalysis the bias was real but did not explain the weak environment–gene content effect, so the finding is "bias present, not confounding" for that comparison [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis]; see [[concepts/clinical-sampling-bias-in-environment-embedding-analyses]]. AMR (antimicrobial resistance) surveys inherit the same skew, plus sparse metadata [src: amr_pangenome_atlas, amr_environmental_resistome]: [[concepts/sampling-bias-and-metadata-sparsity-in-amr-resistome-surveys]]. Stratifying embedding distance by habitat shows that environmental genomes decay steeply (mean cosine distance 0.27 at <100 km, 0.90 at >10,000 km) and that the pooled curve hides this [src: discoveries, env_embedding_explorer]: [[concepts/distance-decay-of-environmental-embeddings-with-geographic-distance]]. Cultivation has its own skew, toward porewater rather than rock-attached communities [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory, clay_confined_subsurface]: [[concepts/cultivation-bias-toward-porewater-versus-rock-attached-subsurface-communities]]. Literature is also concentrated: Homo sapiens alone accounts for 46.7% of gene-paper records [src: paperblast_explorer] ([[concepts/literature-coverage-bias-and-text-mining-limits]]). Exometabolomic data are small, single-laboratory and frozen at 2018 [src: pitfalls, webofmicrobes_explorer] ([[concepts/taxonomic-and-single-laboratory-coverage-limits-of-exometabolomic-data]]). Planktonic assays on a single reference strain that represents <5% of CF isolates have similar limits [src: cf_formulation_design] ([[concepts/generalizability-limits-of-planktonic-single-strain-inhibition-assays]]).

**What the references can represent: database coverage and bridging.** Reference collections under-represent some niches. NCBI appears to under-represent ENIGMA subsurface isolates, a hypothesis resting on one report [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma] ([[concepts/representativeness-of-reference-genome-collections-for-environmental-field-isolates]]). In NEON soil the share of MAGs (metagenome-assembled genomes) lacking a GTDB genus call rises from 7.6% to 37.0% across pH bins; reading this as database bias is the report's untested favoured hypothesis [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery] ([[concepts/lineage-novelty-and-reference-database-culture-bias-across-environmental-gradients]]). Annotation-gap estimates depend on which organisms the databases cover well [src: alphafold_msa_annotation, annotation_gap_discovery, functional_dark_matter] ([[concepts/reference-database-coverage-and-phylogenetic-bias-in-annotation-gap-estimates]]); the Genomic Discovery Index tries to quantify under-representation [src: soil_frontier_genomics] ([[concepts/genomic-discovery-gap-indices-and-database-representation-bias]]). The Fitness Browser is Proteobacteria-heavy (37 of 48 organisms in Pseudomonadota) [src: functional_dark_matter], which shapes organism selection ([[concepts/organism-selection-and-taxonomic-coverage-bias-for-experimental-characterization-of-dark-genes]]), leaves one organism with the full SNIPE architecture [src: snipe_defense_system] ([[concepts/fitness-data-coverage-gaps-for-defense-genes]]), and thins metal coverage [src: metal_fitness_atlas, counter_ion_effects] ([[concepts/metal-coverage-gaps-and-proposed-validation-experiments]], [[concepts/organism-coverage-and-attrition-in-cross-organism-fitness-conservation-analyses]]). Linking records across databases loses entries: genus bridging left 862 of 1,392 genera unmapped in the ENIGMA contamination analysis [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential] ([[concepts/genus-level-taxonomy-bridging-to-pangenome-clades-and-its-coverage-limits]], [[concepts/taxonomy-bridging-between-metagenomic-classifiers-and-reference-pangenomes]]). Exact species-name matching captures only 34.5% of BacDive strains [src: pitfalls] ([[concepts/taxonomic-synonymy-and-strain-name-collisions-in-cross-database-matching]], [[concepts/coverage-and-bias-in-linking-strain-phenotype-databases-to-pangenomes]], [[concepts/reliability-of-species-level-bacdive-consensus-phenotypes]]). Related limits: [[concepts/genus-level-amplicon-resolution-and-rare-taxon-limits-in-matching-reference-organisms-to-field-data]], [[concepts/isolation-metadata-versus-metagenomic-cross-validation-of-plant-associated-taxa]], [[concepts/spatial-matching-of-environmental-samples-to-genomes]].

**Annotation and metadata as hidden filters.** Annotation labels can flip a presence/absence result, as the lanmodulin and Psl cases show [src: discoveries, pitfalls] ([[concepts/annotation-based-gene-detection-limits-in-pangenome-trait-surveys]]). Annotation is uneven: essential genes are better annotated than dispensable ones in ADP1 [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer] ([[concepts/annotation-coverage-and-annotation-density-bias-in-functional-enrichment]]). Missing metadata shrink cohorts: a gap in per-gene annotation tables cut a 246-MAG NEON cohort to 73 [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery, pitfalls] ([[concepts/metadata-and-annotation-coverage-gaps-biasing-environmental-genome-cohorts]]). MAG incompleteness and isolate-versus-MAG composition add further bias [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology, oak_ridge_cultivation_gap] ([[concepts/genome-type-and-sampling-bias-in-metagenome-assembled-genome-surveys]], [[concepts/genome-size-and-taxonomic-composition-confounding-of-cultured-versus-mag-functional-comparisons]]). Inventories themselves undercount and double-count [src: discoveries] ([[concepts/audit-blind-spots-in-inventories-of-data-and-its-use]]). Sample-scope limits appear in [[concepts/sampling-scope-and-generalizability-of-pangenome-wide-tests]], [[concepts/fitness-measurement-of-novel-singleton-genes-and-assay-limits]] and [[concepts/sources-and-prevalence-of-eukaryotic-contamination-in-prokaryote-targeted-metagenomes]] (77% of NMDC runs carry detectable eukaryotic reads [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]).

**Which observations were independent: phylogeny, study and design.** Genome count was a severe confound for pangenome cloud fraction (ρ = 0.913) in the CAZyme (carbohydrate-active enzyme) dataset [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology] ([[concepts/genome-sampling-depth-genome-size-and-phylogeny-as-confounders-of-gene-content-and-pangenome-associations]]). Using all genomes rather than diversity-maximizing downsampling raised a median partial correlation to 0.081 from 0.003 [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis] ([[concepts/genome-sampling-depth-and-downsampling-effects-on-partial-correlation-estimates]]). Phylogeny usually explains more gene-content similarity than environment [src: ecotype_analysis] ([[concepts/phylogenetic-structure-versus-ecological-adaptation-in-gene-content-patterns]]). Taxonomy absorbs phenotype–metal associations, some reversing sign within classes [src: bacdive_phenotype_metal_tolerance, discoveries] ([[concepts/phylogenetic-confounding-of-phenotype-metal-tolerance-associations]]). Environment–AMR effects survive phylum control in 5 of 6 major phyla [src: amr_environmental_resistome] ([[concepts/phylogenetic-confounding-of-environment-amr-associations]]). Anaerobic markers in clay isolates mostly track Bacillota_B, with only sulfate reduction enriched within phylum [src: clay_confined_subsurface] ([[concepts/phylum-level-confounding-of-habitat-specific-anaerobic-gene-content]]). Shared ancestry inflates gene co-occurrence [src: cofitness_coinheritance] ([[concepts/phylogenetic-confounding-of-gene-co-occurrence]]). Small cohorts compound this: 10 anchor genomes against 62 baseline [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] ([[concepts/small-cohort-and-phylogenetic-confounding-in-lineage-level-comparisons]]). Study structure matters too: eukaryotic-fraction associations vanish when whole studies are held out [src: euk_in_prok_correlates] ([[concepts/study-and-batch-confounding-of-cross-collection-metadata-correlates]]); diagnosis is collinear with study in cMD [src: pitfalls] ([[concepts/substudy-nesting-and-feature-leakage-in-pooled-cohort-microbiome-comparisons]], [[concepts/cross-cohort-portability-and-batch-dominance-in-microbiome-and-metabolomic-stratification]]); the NMDC leucine signal survived soil-only stratification in a 95% single-study dataset [src: discoveries] ([[concepts/confounding-and-robustness-in-environmental-metabolomics-genomics-association]]). Other design confounds: [[concepts/co-contamination-and-multiple-testing-confounding-in-metal-function-associations]], [[concepts/confounding-of-compound-source-with-chemical-class]], [[concepts/contrast-design-and-confounding-in-testing-a-putative-lipid-a-processing-enzyme]], [[concepts/compositional-coupling-in-metagenomic-validation-of-carrier-abundance]], [[concepts/calibration-and-denominators-in-environmental-abundance-contrasts]], [[concepts/carrier-weighted-versus-binary-metrics-for-sparse-pangenome-events]], [[concepts/genus-level-aggregation-tree-choice-and-trait-distribution-assumptions-in-phylogenetic-comparative-analyses]], [[concepts/robustness-of-exploratory-associations-to-coverage-fraction-and-site-covariate-specification]].

## Tensions and Caveats

**Bias present versus bias consequential.** Clinical skew is well documented, but its effect on conclusions is mostly argued rather than tested; the one reanalysis found it did not explain the weak effect [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis, env_embedding_explorer].

**Niche breadth and metals: separate analyses.** In the MicrobeAtlas analysis, the bacterial metal-type effect on niche breadth was not explained away by genome count alone and survived genome-size and combined controls [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]. In a different analysis, per-Mb metal-gene density against Levins' B_std niche breadth gave β = −0.0207 in a permutation test, was significant in two of four phyla, lost significance with the independent Earth Microbiome Project metric, and the Australian subset is not an independent replication [src: comprehensive_metal_ecology]. These test different exposures and are not a single result; see [[concepts/robustness-and-replication-of-niche-breadth-associations]]. Archaeal tests used only 48 genera and could neither support nor refute the bacterial finding [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology] ([[concepts/statistical-power-and-database-coverage-gap-for-archaeal-trait-analyses]]).

**Null versus model failure.** Every soil functional-gene model had negative cross-validated R²; the cause is untested, so a modelling failure cannot be separated from a biological null [src: soil_frontier_genomics] ([[concepts/interpreting-negative-out-of-sample-r-in-environmental-predictive-models]]).

**Single-site, single-report evidence.** Many pages rest on one project. The DNA-versus-RNA result is a single-site null [src: harvard_forest_warming] ([[concepts/dna-versus-rna-pool-sensitivity-to-environmental-perturbation]]); groundwater stability is one two-date comparison at 5 wells [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology] ([[concepts/temporal-stability-of-groundwater-communities]]); n=3 sets a permutation floor [src: lignin_community_enrichment] ([[concepts/small-sample-limits-in-community-amplicon-statistics]]); proteomics has one replicate per strain [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] ([[concepts/transcript-protein-discordance-and-single-replicate-proteomics-limits]]). Training-set size changed what a model learned [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma] ([[concepts/training-set-size-and-the-shift-from-genome-scale-to-gene-specific-predictive-features]]), and the active-learning ranking remains only partially supported pending a test against random selection [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma] ([[concepts/active-learning-for-selecting-growth-experiments-from-model-errors]]). Validation of metal-tolerance predictions is underpowered (d=1.00 barely exceeded the detection threshold for n=10) [src: bacdive_metal_validation] ([[concepts/validating-metal-tolerance-predictions-against-culture-collection-metadata]]).
