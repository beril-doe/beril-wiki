# Data Infrastructure, Provenance, and Research Practice

This topic covers the layer beneath every biological result in the corpus: how data are found, how their origin and currency are established, how records from separate owners are joined, how identifiers are reconciled, and how a finished claim is attacked before it is believed. The corpus speaks to it directly because the observatory it describes is a shared, multi-owner store — an audit of it inventories 1,740 deduplicated tables across 119 databases, 17 tenants, and 10 funding agencies or programs [src: berdl_data_atlas] — and because one audit showed that 20 database names containing `nmdc` resolve to only 7 real, maintained resources spanning three tenants and six provenance classes [src: nmdc_context_audit]. A *tenant* here is an organizational namespace owning a group of databases; it is a governance boundary, not a topic boundary. The six member concepts below argue that infrastructure facts of this kind are evidence about biological claims, not plumbing beneath them.

## Literature Context

The published literature treats provenance and metadata as prerequisites for reuse rather than as findings in their own right. A commentary from the International Neuroinformatics Coordinating Facility Task Force on Neuroimaging Datasharing argues that barriers are as much cultural as technical — absent standard infrastructure, uncertainty about how to prepare data, and fear of unattributed use — and insists shared data carry standards for metadata, provenance and other descriptors ([PMID 23587272](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23587272/)); it is a commentary, not an audit. A FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) review in plant biology reframes FAIR as a readiness spectrum, not a binary property, and finds readiness strongly method- and context-dependent: task-focused AI can succeed at low readiness, while integrative, transferable and reproducible analyses consistently require richer metadata, explicit semantics and traceable provenance ([PMID 42467611](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42467611/)). On the engineering side, a hospital Medical Data Integration Center reports a fully automated, open-source framework with a metadata schema for data provenance and a process validation concept, while conceding it is "not a panacea" for FAIR compliance ([PMID 37189148](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37189148/)); a neuroimaging Provenance Service built on CRISTAL captures workflow and dataset evolution for Alzheimer's biomarker studies, though the authors note few deployed Grid-based medical systems provide such traceability and none had been evaluated in practice ([PMID 23763909](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23763909/)); and a comparison of Nextflow, CWL and WDL — workflow description languages — and Swift/T, run on a variant-calling pipeline and a scalability-testing framework, treats provenance as an emerging tool category rather than a solved one ([PMID 34737383](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34737383/)).

This corpus is consistent with that consensus but sits one layer downstream of it. Existing work secures provenance *within* a pipeline or institution; the corpus measures what breaks *between* owners, and its central claims — that 20 database names containing `nmdc` resolve to only 7 real resources, that a co-hosted mirror holding 51,711,888 biosamples sits beside a 16,640-sample genuine universe, that 12 of 32 pangenome linkages through `ncbi_strain_identifiers` were incorrect genus matches, and that unreconciled taxon names manufactured a log₂FC approximately 28 — have no direct counterpart in these papers. That is an extension, and in the case of the quantified value-space failures documented in [[concepts/cross-tenant-data-bridging]] and [[concepts/taxonomic-nomenclature-reconciliation]], novel: the literature names provenance as a requirement, the corpus prices its absence.

Two further alignments are partial. Governance-as-multiagent and patient-centric stewardship in clinicogenomics ([PMID 39695714](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39695714/)) parallels the tenant boundary and the ownership concentration the corpus treats as a covariate, though that symposium report addresses underrepresentation and barriers of knowledge, trust, access and technology, not join keys. The corpus's adversarial finding — 5 critical and 6 important issues in work standard review had called exceptionally sophisticated with no critical issues — echoes arguments that data credibility must be assessed rather than assumed, made there for four randomized trials of antenatal corticosteroids that lack clear governance, which makes the provenance and reliability of their data difficult to verify ([PMID 33940424](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33940424/)), and in a narrative-review framework urging clinicians to audit systems for data provenance, transparency and failure modes ([PMID 42537692](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42537692/)). Neither supplies the citation-verification protocol that [[concepts/adversarial-research-quality-assurance]] makes load-bearing.

## What the Corpus Shows

**A resource name is not a dataset boundary, and scale and currency are selection variables.** The `nmdc` tenant is neither exclusively NMDC nor the only home of NMDC-related resources: `kbase.nmdc_neon` represents NEON rather than NMDC, and misreading that namesake would create an agency-attribution error [src: nmdc_context_audit]. The genuine biosample universe holds 16,640 samples in `nmdc.metadata.biosample_set` while a co-hosted NCBI mirror holds 51,711,888 biosamples — a ~3,000× scale trap that discovery interfaces should pre-empt by showing row counts and resource role [src: nmdc_context_audit]. Currency was legible only through table-format snapshot ages, spanning approximately four months, with latest commits from `2026-03-09` to `2026-07-02`, so `max(committed_at)` belongs in discovery tooling [src: nmdc_context_audit]. Two other resources sharpen the point: the analyzed Web of Microbes export is a frozen 2018 snapshot with 37 organisms and 589 metabolites, 332 (56.4%) of them unidentified [src: webofmicrobes_explorer], and the PaperBLAST collection holds 12.4 million rows across 14 tables and contains papers from 1951 to 2026, yet its mining of PubMed Central full text misses paywalled literature and the report estimates that only 19% of SwissProt is represented [src: paperblast_explorer]. Composite metrics need the same treatment: the Genomic Discovery Index, defined as OTU (operational taxonomic unit) Richness / (Mean Genome Completeness + 1), can equal 902 even with zero genomes, and forest (902.36) and cropland (890.82) differ by only 1.3% with no bootstrap intervals, so they are jointly highest, not ranked [src: soil_frontier_genomics]. See [[concepts/provenance-aware-resource-discovery]].

**Schema compatibility is the weakest of four evidence states.** [[concepts/cross-tenant-data-bridging]] separates shared schema keys, overlapping values, an executable join, and a scientifically interpretable result. The atlas found 536 unordered cross-tenant bridges at tenant-by-topic-cell granularity through 29 canonical keys, with `sample_id` occurring across 10 tenants and `genome_id` and `ncbi_taxon_id` across 9 [src: berdl_data_atlas]. Only UC1 was later sample-validated; UC2–UC5 still require live execution [src: berdl_data_atlas]. Value-space failure is measurable, not hypothetical: among 32 pangenome linkages made through `ncbi_strain_identifiers`, 12 were incorrect genus matches [src: pitfalls].

**Identifier and taxonomy reconciliation is where joins fail silently.** ENIGMA MT20 refers to *Rhodanobacter glycinis* while GTDB MT20 refers to *Streptococcus pneumoniae*; the erroneous match involved 8,434 genomes, including 1,751 clinical genomes [src: pitfalls]. Unreconciled taxon names produced a log₂FC approximately 28, equivalent to approximately 2.68 × 10⁸ fold, because one cohort's abundance collapsed to a pseudocount — a biological extreme manufactured by naming, not measurement [src: pitfalls]. A hand-curated 23-entry synonym map sufficed for a targeted battery, but full-taxonomy aggregation needs an NCBI-taxid-backed, GTDB-version-aware layer [src: pitfalls]. See [[concepts/taxonomic-nomenclature-reconciliation]].

**Who owns the data shapes what looks biologically true.** DOE accounts for approximately 78% of tables (DOE-BER 63%, DOE BRaVE 14%), and six topics are more than 75% single-owner, including mobile_phage at 96% PhageFoundry ownership and pangenome at 79% KBase ownership [src: berdl_data_atlas]. Coverage also hides in metadata: 83,227 of 293,059 genomes (28.4%) have AlphaEarth environmental embeddings, and 52.7% of 180,025 genomes have unknown per-genome NCBI environment labels — gaps that reflect missing coordinates or unstructured metadata rather than biological absence [src: pitfalls]. See [[concepts/data-landscape-ownership-and-coverage-bias]].

**Integration repays matching evidence at organism and metabolite level, and its honest output is sometimes a documented gap.** For *Pseudomonas* FW300-N2E3, exometabolomics, mutant fitness, utilization phenotypes and pathway predictions agreed for 17/21 (81%) testable metabolites fully, 4/21 (19%) partially, none fully discordant, mean concordance 0.94 — while 37/58 metabolites (64%) occurred only in Web of Microbes [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]. A tiered chemical census resolved all 83 enrichment compounds structurally and linked 54 to KEGG, yet 74/83 (89%) remained organism-dark [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. Genotype-to-phenotype alignment of 46,389 genome × condition pairs across 727 genomes and 363 conditions predicted binary capability but not continuous kinetics under genus-blocked holdout [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. See [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]].

**Adversarial review finds a different error class than standard review.** Standard review called one project exceptionally sophisticated with no critical issues; adversarial review of the same work found 5 critical and 6 important issues, including feature leakage, hard-coded positive readings of non-significant results, absent confounder adjustment, missing null distributions and sample-size overclaims [src: discoveries]. Across 9 adversarial review rounds, PMID hijacking and hallucinated author lists recurred, so load-bearing citations need author, title, journal, PMID and DOI verified in PubMed [src: discoveries]. Correction must target real hypothesis families: of revisions M1–M26, the 4 pre-registered hypotheses took Bonferroni α=0.0125 and all 4 survived, while only M14, M21 and M23 were genuine post-hoc metric corrections [src: discoveries]. See [[concepts/adversarial-research-quality-assurance]].

## Tensions and Caveats

The load-bearing limitation is observability. The realized-use audit mined project README files, so tenant breadth and reuse are lower bounds, and "zero realized use" for the five high-leverage bridges means zero use found in audited documentation, not proof of non-use [src: berdl_data_atlas]. Across-tenant deduplication was not performed, and two tenant-to-agency mappings (evaluation, lambda, 4 tables total) remain unverified [src: berdl_data_atlas].

Four recorded conflicts sit inside bridging. [[conflicts/conflict--cross-tenant-data-bridging--8325c1f4]] asks whether aggregating genes into multi-gene modules rescues a weak gene-level bridge: pairwise co-fitness gave delta = +0.003 with Mann-Whitney p=1.66e-29 while coordinated modules from independent component analysis (a decomposition of multivariate covariance) gave delta phi=+0.053 [src: cofitness_coinheritance]; the conflict page records a second project reaching the opposite verdict at module level, leaving "go to modules" either a genuine remedy or a reframing of the same weak signal. [[conflicts/conflict--cross-tenant-data-bridging--34478c5a]] concerns whether an IBD result with a dominant taxonomy–metabolite canonical correlation of r = 0.964, whose clinical rules remain hypotheses needing prospective validation [src: ibd_phage_targeting], is under-validated or structurally unidentifiable. [[conflicts/conflict--cross-tenant-data-bridging--d89b8efa]] asks whether tryptophan production, pathway completeness, fitness significance and growth are the same claim when a metabolite name is used as a join key. [[conflicts/conflict--cross-tenant-data-bridging--dd5b9877]] asks whether one summary number per entity is portable across a join at all. Finally, [[conflicts/conflict--provenance-aware-resource-discovery--f0c8f1e3]] records that scale read successfully through the table format's own metadata for the audited NMDC tables — `nmdc.results.annotation_kegg_orthology` with 1.83B rows returned instantly [src: nmdc_context_audit] — while a REST (Representational State Transfer, an HTTP endpoint interface) surface showed conditional unreliability: `/count` for loops over many tables, and `/schema` prone to timeout on large tables [src: pitfalls]. The corpus treats these as different access surfaces rather than a contradiction and requires the interface and query path be recorded with each claim [src: nmdc_context_audit, pitfalls]; which condition — interface, workload shape, or table size — carries the failure stays open. Operationally, archived reports and notebooks are historical records, not automatically valid instructions, since schemas, namespaces and permissions change [src: pitfalls].
