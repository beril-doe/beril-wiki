# Pangenome Structure and Genome Evolution

A pangenome is the full set of gene clusters across the genomes of a species. Genes found in nearly all genomes are "core", and the rest are "accessory", including singletons found in one genome. This corpus speaks to the topic because many projects ask the same question from different angles. They ask which genes sit in which compartment, what drives gene gain, copy number and loss, and how far those patterns can be trusted. The page has 4 sub-themes and links all 17 member concepts.

## Literature Context

The candidate literature frames pangenomes as the set of all genes in a species, split into a core genome present in all genomes and an accessory genome present in only some. Reviews describe them as arising from horizontal gene transfer (HGT) and differential gene loss, and note that current understanding is phenomenological and incomplete [PMID 31639358](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31639358/). Single-species studies repeat the same functional sorting. In Burkholderia pseudomallei (37 isolates), metabolism genes were overrepresented in the core genome, while mobile-element, disease and motility genes were mostly accessory. The pangenome was open, with approximately 136 new genes per added genome [PMID 26484663](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26484663/). In Aggregatibacter, accessory genes were often poorly characterized by COG and were organized into genomic islands (n = 387) whose base composition biases suggest HGT [PMID 26420795](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26420795/). In Bacillus paranthracis, core and accessory genomes showed different evolutionary dynamics, with accessory plasticity linked to HGT, gene loss and weak selection [PMID 39376581](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39376581/).

The literature also shows that accessory content often follows vertical descent and that HGT is constrained. In Streptomyces (n = 17 strains), recombination was constrained mainly by phylogeny rather than geography. Shell accessory genes were over three times more likely to be shared between the same species than with non-related geographical neighbors [PMID 41404872](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41404872/). Deep-sea mussel symbiont pangenomes evolved mainly by vertical inheritance [PMID 35731940](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35731940/). Accessory virulence genes in the Bacillus cereus group tracked core-genome phylogeny [PMID 39452755](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39452755/). In Bacillus transformation experiments, the rate of orthologous replacement fell exponentially with core-genome divergence [PMID 37971327](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37971327/). Coral-associated Vibrio studies describe a conserved core plus horizontally acquired accessory modules, offered as hypotheses needing validation (13 sequenced strains) [PMID 42909470](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42909470/). Methodologically, PEPPAN was reported to outperform four other pangenome pipelines on empirical and simulated data. Separately, it excludes paralogs using tree- and synteny-based approaches [PMID 33055096](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33055096/).

The corpus's core/accessory sorting is consistent with this literature. Its COG finding of a "two-speed genome" matches the enrichment of metabolism in core genes and of mobile elements in accessory genes [PMID 26484663](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26484663/). Its accessory-biased prophage markers (83.8%) and AMR clusters (69.7%) fit the picture of accessory content tied to mobile elements [PMID 31639358](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31639358/). The corpus's caution that horizontal acquisition is mostly inferred, not tested, is supported by the vertical-inheritance results above. Non-core AMR genes showed stronger phylogenetic signal than core ones (median r 0.222 vs 0.117), which the report reads as clonal inheritance. This agrees with the phylogenetic tracking of accessory genes in Streptomyces and Bacillus cereus group genomes [PMID 41404872](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41404872/), [PMID 39452755](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39452755/). The transformation work also suggests that core genes can be exchanged between close relatives [PMID 37971327](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37971327/). That bears on how the corpus reads core-gene signal, though it was tested only in Bacillus.

The corpus extends this literature in scale and in its links to fitness data. It covers 32 species for COG, 2,810 species for pathway variability and 177,863 fitness-to-pangenome links. The candidates are mostly single-species or single-genus studies. The corpus's openness correlates, such as the CAZyme link confounded by sampling depth (ρ = 0.913), and its Black Queen tests have no direct counterpart in the candidates, so they are novel here.

## What the Corpus Shows

**Functions sort into core and accessory compartments.** A clusters of orthologous groups (COG) analysis of 32 species finds a "two-speed genome". Translation, nucleotide, coenzyme, amino acid and energy categories are enriched in core genes. Mobile-element (COG L) and defense (COG V) categories are enriched among novel or singleton genes [src: cog_analysis, discoveries]. Six of seven anti-phage defense systems are strongly accessory-biased [src: phage_defense_arsenal]. Prophage markers are 83.8% accessory and AMR gene clusters 69.7% accessory [src: prophage_amr_comobilization]. Plant growth-promoting genes are the opposite case, predominantly core [src: discoveries, pgp_pangenome_ecology]. See [[concepts/core-versus-accessory-partitioning-of-gene-functions-and-trait-genes]]. Copy number follows the same split. Adaptive COG categories have higher multi-copy rates than housekeeping ones in 24 of 24 species, and the effect is concentrated in accessory clusters [src: gene_copy_number_variation]. Among fixation-level core clusters the 8× species-level effect collapses to 2× [src: gene_copy_number_variation]. See [[concepts/paralog-expansion-and-gene-copy-number-constraint]]. Dark genes are widely conserved: 55.9% of root orthologous groups are kingdom-level [src: functional_dark_matter]. The report reads this as a fundamental limit on understanding broadly conserved biology, though this is interpretation and not a tested result [src: functional_dark_matter]. See [[concepts/phylogenetic-breadth-and-pangenome-conservation-of-dark-genes]].

**Gene gain is measurable, but inferring it is hard.** Sankoff parsimony (a tree-based method that counts the fewest gain events) attributed gains on the GTDB-r214 species tree to acquisition-depth bins. CRISPR-Cas had a recent-to-ancient ratio of 24.5×, and strict housekeeping classes were far lower [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. See [[concepts/horizontal-gene-transfer-inference-metrics-and-control-class-calibration]]. Clade-level innovation tests gave verdicts that depend on taxonomic rank. Cyanobacteria photosystem II (PSII) was an Innovator-Exchange signal at class rank only, and stable at genus, family and order ranks [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. See [[concepts/clade-level-innovation-signals-and-their-rank-dependence]]. A test of the complexity hypothesis found regulatory genes slightly more vertically inherited than metabolic genes (d=−0.21). This fell short of the pre-registered d ≥ 0.3 threshold, so the strong-form asymmetry was not supported [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. See [[concepts/complexity-hypothesis-regulatory-versus-metabolic-gene-transfer-asymmetry]]. Phylogenetic signal differs by trait. Within species, 55.6% (701 of 1,261) show significant AMR phylogenetic signal [src: amr_strain_variation]. Across genera, bacterial niche breadth has λ of 0.787, against about 0.26–0.44 for metal AMR traits [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]. See [[concepts/phylogenetic-signal-of-gene-content-and-traits]].

**Openness, loss and genome size.** Pangenome openness correlates with antimicrobial resistance (AMR) count within 8/10 phyla but not overall (rho=0.006) [src: amr_pangenome_atlas]. Its link to CAZyme (carbohydrate-active enzyme) density is modest (ρ = 0.153) and falls to ρ = 0.101 after controls [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology]. Pathway variability correlates with openness at rho=0.327 across 2,810 species [src: discoveries]. See [[concepts/pangenome-openness-and-its-correlates]]. For gene loss, latent metabolic capabilities correlated with openness across 22 clades (ρ = 0.69) [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]. Pathway-level conservation did not separate the predicted Black Queen classes (p = 0.94) [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]. See [[concepts/gene-loss-and-black-queen-hypothesis-tests]]. Streamlining is not universal. Deep-clay Bacillota_B genomes were larger than soil relatives (4,110,038 bp vs 3,046,124 bp), a within-phylum comparison with a small anchor group (n=10) [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. See [[concepts/genome-streamlining-and-genome-size-in-ecological-specialists]]. Within-species GC shifts of 0.1–0.9% are tied to ecology. Their proposed mechanisms (biased gene conversion, oxidative damage, codon-usage selection) are untested [src: gc_ecotype_ecology]. See [[concepts/mechanisms-of-ecology-linked-gc-shifts]]. NEON metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs) carry accessory KEGG orthologs absent from cultured pangenomes of the same genus. The median is 343 field-only KOs per MAG [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery]. See [[concepts/field-only-accessory-gene-content-absent-from-cultured-pangenomes]].

**Linking pangenomes to other data.** Fitness Browser genes were mapped to pangenome clusters, giving 177,863 links, and 44 of 48 organisms mapped [src: conservation_vs_fitness]. See [[concepts/linking-fitness-assay-genes-to-pangenome-clusters]]. A 1,286-term metal signature scored across 27,702 species. Normalization recovers bioleaching genera, but simple presence/absence did not validate [src: metal_fitness_atlas]. See [[concepts/pangenome-scale-prediction-of-metal-tolerance-from-gene-content]]. Exometabolite phenotypes could be cross-referenced to pangenome context, but this is proposed and not run [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]. See [[concepts/linking-exometabolic-phenotypes-to-pangenome-gene-content]]. Strain diversity matters for reference strains. PA14 is rare among cystic fibrosis isolates, which are 94% ExoS+ [src: pitfalls, cf_formulation_design]. See [[concepts/pseudomonas-aeruginosa-strain-diversity-and-representativeness-of-reference-strains]].

## Tensions and Caveats

**Core ceilings limit co-occurrence tests.** Most assayed genes are core with >95% prevalence. In Korea 95.2% of cofit pairs had NaN phi (not a number, from zero variance), so the negative delta is treated as noise and not biology [src: cofitness_coinheritance]. See [[concepts/core-gene-prevalence-ceiling-on-co-occurrence-signal]]. The ceiling is the report's own interpretation, not a directly tested mechanism [src: cofitness_coinheritance].

**Horizontal transfer is often inferred, not tested.** The reading of accessory status as horizontal acquisition is mostly interpretation [src: amr_environmental_resistome]. The COG report asserts horizontal gene transfer as the primary innovation mechanism [src: discoveries]. Yet non-core AMR genes show stronger phylogenetic signal than core ones (median r 0.222 vs 0.117), which the report reads as clonal inheritance. Core genes' near-zero Jaccard distances partly suppress their signal [src: amr_strain_variation]. Parsimony has not been benchmarked against modern duplication-transfer-loss reconciliation [src: gene_function_ecological_agora].

**Black Queen support is mixed.** Clade-level openness supports it, and pathway-level conservation does not [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]. Candidate costly genes are predominantly selfish elements, which limits the reading [src: costly_dispensable_genes].

**Sampling and confounding.** Openness is severely confounded by sampling depth in the CAZyme data (ρ = 0.913) [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology]. Small clades make "core" a weak label, so results for species with fewer than 20 genomes need caution [src: amr_fitness_cost]. Ten organisms with under 90% coverage were excluded from the fitness-conservation phase [src: conservation_vs_fitness]. The NEON analysis inherits 53% cohort attrition [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery]. PSII class-rank evidence has a small sample (n=21) [src: gene_function_ecological_agora].
