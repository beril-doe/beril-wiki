# Pangenome Structure and Genome Evolution

A bacterial pangenome is the union of genes found across the sequenced genomes of a clade, conventionally split into a *core* (present in effectively all sampled genomes) and an *auxiliary* or accessory fraction (present in only some). This topic asks what that partition actually means: which functions sit on each side, how genes move between lineages, whether "openness" — the propensity of a repertoire to keep expanding as genomes are added — carries ecological signal, and how much of any observed pattern is biology versus sampling. The corpus speaks at unusual scale from both ends: comparative-genomic censuses over hundreds of thousands of genomes (KBase pangenome data contain 1,011,650,903 gene rows spanning 293,059 genomes, 27,690 GTDB (Genome Taxonomy Database) species clades or pangenomes, and 132.5M gene clusters [src: berdl_data_atlas]) joined to gene-level laboratory fitness measurements from a few dozen organisms (approximately 194,000 genes from 43 bacteria [src: fitness_effects_conservation]). The sections below argue across the member concept pages rather than summarizing each.

## What the Corpus Shows

**The core/accessory split has a reproducible functional signature, but it is a two-speed model, not a two-class one.** Across 32 species spanning 9 phyla and 357,623 genes, novel or singleton genes were enriched in COG L — the Clusters of Orthologous Groups category for replication, recombination and mobile elements — by +10.88% with 100% consistency across species; they were also enriched in COG V (defense) by +2.83% with 100% consistency and COG S (unknown function) by +1.64% with 69% consistency, while core genes were depleted in COG J (translation) by -4.65% with 97% consistency [src: cog_analysis]. That is the backbone of [[concepts/two-speed-bacterial-genome]]. But the model leaks at both edges. Essentiality tracks core status only modestly: among 17,222 ortholog families, 859 (5.0%) were universally essential, 4,799 (27.9%) variably essential and 11,564 (67.1%) never essential, and 7,084 essential genes had no detectable orthologs in the tested organisms [src: essential_genome]. Conserved genes are also not uniformly cheap — 25,271 genes, or 17.8% of those examined, had fitness below -1 in some conditions and above 1 in others, and such trade-off genes were 1.29 times more likely to be core than non-core (OR 1.29, p=1.2e-44) [src: core_gene_tradeoffs].

**Mobile-element association is the strongest evidence that horizontal gene transfer (HGT) drives innovation, yet almost always indirect.** [[concepts/horizontal-gene-transfer-driven-innovation]] gathers convergent lines: of 142,190 genes from 43 bacteria, 5,526 were costly in the laboratory yet dispensable, carrying mobile-element keywords 7.45x more often than costly+conserved genes (OR=7.45, p=4.6e-71), with 11.7x SEED-category enrichment for "Phages, Prophages, Transposable elements, Plasmids" (false-discovery-rate FDR=1.3e-17) [src: costly_dispensable_genes]. One line is transfer-resolved: of 30,497 high-quality environmental metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs), 6,652 (21.8%) carried type IV secretion system (T4SS) or conjugative machinery; T4SS-positive genomes had 10× higher mobile-genetic-element density (p<0.001); the GT2 glycosyltransferase tree yielded 77 HGT events, of which 65 spanned 2 phyla and 12 spanned ≥3 phyla (15.6%), and 32 were normalized high-confidence cross-phylum events [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]. [[concepts/chromosomal-and-integrative-gene-transfer]] keeps the mechanism hypothetical: ICEfinder found no CAZy (carbohydrate-active enzyme) genes on plasmids, while 12 integrative mobilizable elements sat among the top 100 accumulators, a detection-dependent negative that cannot establish a chromosomal route [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt].

**Acquisition depth is measurable phylogenetically, but what it measures is attributed gains rather than observed transfers.** [[concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth]] rests on a GTDB-r214 atlas of 18,989 species representatives with 17,073,194 Sankoff-parsimony gain events — Sankoff parsimony infers the minimum number and placement of state changes on a tree — binned by recipient rank [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. Recent-to-ancient gain ratios differ sharply by function: CRISPR-Cas had 58.7% recent and 2.4% ancient gains (24.5×), versus clean tRNA-synthetase controls at 24.7% recent and 10.7% ancient (2.3×); these ratios describe phylogenetically attributed gains, not direct donor transfers [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. Genomic proxies point the same way at hypothesis strength only: truly dark genes (hypothetical under two annotation pipelines) had mean absolute GC deviation 0.047 versus 0.038 for annotation-lag genes, core-genome fraction 43.1% versus 72.7%, and 12.0% were within 2 genes of a mobile element — supporting recent acquisition or rapid evolution as a hypothesis, though GC deviation is an imperfect HGT proxy and mobile-element proximity does not establish transfer direction or mechanism [src: truly_dark_genes].

**Openness, by contrast, is the weakest link in the chain.** Openness showed no significant relationship to environment effect (Spearman rho = -0.05, p = 0.54) or phylogeny effect (rho = 0.03, p = 0.73) [src: pangenome_openness]. What does correlate with openness is metabolic variability: variable metabolic pathways at rho=0.327, p=7.2e-71, rising to partial rho=0.530, p=2.83e-203 after controlling for genome count [src: discoveries], and latent capability rate at Spearman rho=0.69, p=0.0004 across n=22 clades [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]. [[concepts/pangenome-openness-determinants]] treats metabolic variability as a reproducible correlate and environment as specification-dependent.

**Sampling is a first-class confounder, not a footnote.** [[concepts/pangenome-core-boundary-and-clade-size-bias]] shows the core boundary bending with design choices: 145,821 linked genes (82.0%) fell in core clusters and 32,042 (18.0%) in auxiliary, yet clades with only 2 genomes can have trivially high core fractions, and Dyella79 was dropped on a locus-tag mismatch producing a 0% join rate [src: conservation_vs_fitness]. [[concepts/phylogenetic-confounding-of-pangenome-associations]] extends this across lineages: openness correlated positively with AMR (antimicrobial resistance) count in 8/10 tested phyla while the pooled correlation was rho=0.006 [src: amr_pangenome_atlas]. [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] documents the joins themselves — 177,863 gene-to-cluster links at 100.0% median protein identity and 94.2% median gene coverage [src: conservation_vs_fitness] — and their failure modes: one erroneous MT20 strain match involved 8,434 genomes, and of 32 pangenome linkages through `ncbi_strain_identifiers`, 12 were incorrect genus matches [src: pitfalls]. [[concepts/within-species-conservation-between-species-functional-divergence]] adds the layered reading: metal-important genes were 87.4% core versus 76.9% baseline (OR=2.08, p=4.3e-162) [src: metal_fitness_atlas], yet general-stress, metal-shared and metal-specific tiers differed only modestly at 92.0%, 91.0% and 89.8% mean core fraction [src: metal_cross_resistance].

## Tensions and Caveats

The sharpest unresolved question is whether openness means anything ecologically. [[conflicts/conflict--pangenome-openness-determinants--813fc4ab]] and [[conflicts/conflict--pangenome-openness-determinants--0ec0a9e1]] set the null (rho = -0.05, p = 0.54; rho = 0.03, p = 0.73 [src: pangenome_openness]) against the strong metabolic and ecological-breadth associations [src: discoveries]. The corpus does not average these: the reports attribute the discrepancy to differences in predictor definition, genome-count control, species inclusion and environmental coverage [src: discoveries], and only 6.8% of species — 1,872 of 27,690 — had sufficient AlphaEarth coverage for the breadth analysis [src: discoveries]. [[conflicts/conflict--pangenome-core-boundary-and-clade-size-bias--f5ff24c2]] and [[conflicts/conflict--phylogenetic-confounding-of-pangenome-associations--5a54a7b7]] narrow this to a scope question: a null on one response variable neither licenses nor forbids reading openness as signal on another [src: pangenome_openness, amr_pangenome_atlas].

A second tension is magnitude, not direction. [[conflicts/conflict--core-genome-burden-paradox--52b853b5]] contrasts essential genes at 86.1% core versus 81.2% non-essential, median odds ratio 1.56, with 18 of 33 organisms significant after Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction [src: conservation_vs_fitness], against 82% core for essential versus 66% for always-neutral genes [src: fitness_effects_conservation]. Both point the same way; neither resolves whether the gap's width is bacterial biology or category definition.

Third, whether resistance patterns are ecology or ancestry. [[conflicts/conflict--phylogenetic-confounding-of-pangenome-associations--782017fa]] records five of six phyla with significant within-phylum environment effects but only 20 of 141 testable families surviving FDR correction [src: amr_environmental_resistome], while a within-species follow-up found only 2 species with sufficient environmental diversity and 52.7% of genomes with no classifiable isolation_source [src: amr_strain_variation]. [[conflicts/conflict--phylogenetic-confounding-of-pangenome-associations--7b9c5a6c]] adds prophage density: rho=0.572 with AMR breadth, partial rho=0.464 after genome-count control, yet local accessory-AMR enrichment near prophage markers was only OR 1.10 at 10 genes with a median species-level OR of 0.85 [src: prophage_amr_comobilization]. [[conflicts/conflict--composite-resistance-score-limitations--a9245bd1]] pits Cohen's *d* = +1.00 for heavy-metal isolates [src: bacdive_metal_validation] against a species-scale Spearman rho ≈ -0.02, p > 0.8 — a null the report calls underpowered, because matching and strain collapsing left 20 independent species [src: metal_cross_resistance].

Load-bearing limits throughout: COG annotations covered approximately 70% of genes [src: cog_analysis]; "burden" was defined as max_fit > 1 in any experiment, so one noisy assay can classify a gene as costly [src: costly_dispensable_genes]; and 44 of 48 Fitness Browser organisms mapped to pangenome clades but only 33 entered downstream analysis [src: conservation_vs_fitness].
