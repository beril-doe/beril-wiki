# Metabolic Traits, Auxotrophy, and Community Dependence

This topic asks what organisms can make, what they can use, and what they depend on others for. It also asks how far genome-based predictions of those traits hold up against measured growth, fitness and environmental data. The corpus speaks to it from several directions: pathway-completeness predictions from GapMind (a tool that scores whether a genome carries a complete biosynthetic or catabolic pathway), gene-fitness data, growth-curve screens, community metabolomics and habitat surveys [src: essential_metabolome, pathway_capability_dependency, genotype_to_phenotype_enigma, nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]. This hub has four sections, and the main lesson is that predicted capability, measured dependency and realized activity are three different things.

## Literature Context

The candidate literature is about gut and host-associated communities, and its recurring theme is that metabolic function is distributed across species through cross-feeding. Reviews of short-chain fatty acid formation stress cross-feeding of intermediates such as lactate, succinate and 1,2-propanediol between gut bacteria [PMID 27928878](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27928878/). Honeybee gut bacteria likewise engage in cross-feeding and antagonistic interactions that likely contribute to community stability [PMID 38049554](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38049554/). Experimental studies show dependence of a concrete kind. Lactobacillus johnsonii cannot use β-glucan and requires cross-feeding with Bacteroides uniformis, which degrades it and supplies nicotinamide [PMID 39300532](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39300532/). Bacteroides xylanisolvens supplies folate to Clostridium butyricum, which returns pABA (para-aminobenzoic acid) for folate biosynthesis [PMID 40190016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40190016/). Enterocloster aldenensis makes indole-3-propionate only from indole-3-lactate, not from tryptophan, in a co-culture with Lachnospira eligens, based on fecal cultures from 16 adults [PMID 40336187](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40336187/). A three-species defined community showed that tryptophan metabolite output was not predominantly set by the abundance of tryptophan-metabolizing bacteria but by substrate-dependent pathway regulation [PMID 38918470](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38918470/). On methods, one review of Phenotype MicroArray practice advises a systematic check for auxotrophies in strains before phenotype experiments [PMID 25343861](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25343861/).

Against this background, the corpus's central message, that predicted capability, measured dependency and realized activity are different things, is consistent in spirit with the cross-feeding literature. There, in one defined three-species community, metabolite output was not predominantly set by the abundance of the producing bacteria but by substrate-dependent pathway regulation [PMID 38918470](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38918470/). The weak-to-moderate Black Queen signal in the NMDC metabolomics, with only leucine and arginine passing false-discovery-rate correction, is also consistent with the idea that dependencies are specific rather than general. Those cited cross-feeding studies, however, are mostly single-pair or small-community experiments in gut or host models, and the corpus tests the idea statistically across environmental communities, so the two lines of evidence are complementary rather than directly comparable.

Several corpus findings are not addressed by these papers. The GapMind-versus-fitness comparison, the Latent Capability fractions (pathways predicted complete but without significant fitness defects), the clay-subsurface cultivation-bias argument and the habitat structure of pathway completeness have no counterpart in the candidate set, so they should be treated as novel here. The growth-screen results sit nearer to the phenomics literature, whose advice to check auxotrophies before phenotyping [PMID 25343861](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25343861/) is a methodological reminder that auxotrophy can confound growth phenotypes. It is relevant to the corpus's serine-gap hypothesis, though that advice concerns Phenotype MicroArray experiments and does not change the report's classification of its no-growth wells as biological. The papers on host signalling and AMPK in pathogens [PMID 27812985](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27812985/) concern host energy sensing, and they do not bear on these questions.

## What the Corpus Shows

**Predicted capability is not dependency.** In a seven-organism pilot, 17 of 18 amino-acid pathways were complete in all 7 organisms, and the one gap was serine in *Desulfovibrio vulgaris* [src: essential_metabolome]. That gap supports only a hypothesis of serine auxotrophy (dependence on an externally supplied nutrient), because it is computational and may reflect detection limits [src: essential_metabolome]. Pairing predictions with fitness data sharpens the point. Across 161 organism–pathway pairs, 66 (41.0%) were Latent Capability: complete but without significant fitness defects, and all 66 became fitness-important under at least one condition type [src: pathway_capability_dependency]. A median-based threshold can produce that result by construction [src: pathway_capability_dependency]. A larger analysis of 1,695 complete pathway–organism pairs found 267 (15.8%) latent, with carbon-utilization pathways more often latent than amino-acid pathways (24.3% versus 6.5%) [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]. See [[concepts/biosynthetic-prototrophy-and-auxotrophy]].

**Cultured genomes may miss the most self-sufficient lineages.** Deep-subsurface life is often described as biosynthetically self-sufficient. In the clay-confined cohort, however, the 6 quality-filtered deep genomes had lower amino-acid pathway completeness than the 137 baseline genomes (15.50/18 versus 17.14/18; Cohen's d = −0.84) [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. The report treats this as a statement about the cultured cohort, not about deep life. It suggests the hypothesis that extreme self-sufficiency lies in uncultivated lineages [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. The shallow cohort showed the opposite sign, which the report reads as cultivation-quality selection [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. See [[concepts/biosynthetic-self-sufficiency-and-cultivation]].

**Utilization capacity is concentrated and specialist-dominated.** Among genomes carrying at least one of 8 callable aromatic or alkaloid capacities, 675 carried exactly 1 and 18 carried at least 3 [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. Generalists clustered in Burkholderiales genera such as Paraburkholderia and Burkholderia [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. Measured growth fits this picture: Pseudomonas anchor strains grew in 43-61% of tested conditions, while Pedobacter (23.5%) and Acidovorax (22.4%) grew in far fewer [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. These are realized growth rates for anchor strains, so they do not directly test the census's eight callable capacities [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. For 74/83 (89%) census compounds, no degrading organism could be linked in the queried resources. The report calls this resource-darkness, not proof of absence [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. See [[concepts/metabolic-capacity-specialization]] and [[concepts/organism-dark-compounds]].

**Capability is easier to predict than kinetics, and prediction is condition-dependent.** A seven-strain model reached AUC 0.633 (area under the ROC curve, a threshold-free measure of discrimination) but mostly learned that big genomes grow on amino acids [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. With 46,389 genome × condition pairs and genus-blocked holdout, overall AUC was 0.620 [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. In the growth corpus, 15,227 of 27,632 wells (55.1%) showed no detectable growth, which the report classes as biological [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. Active learning then ranks 343 testable conditions to propose 50 next experiments. That benefit is a projection, and retrospective subsampling against random selection has not been done [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. See [[concepts/capability-versus-kinetic-predictability]] and [[concepts/active-learning-experiment-selection]].

**Community dependence shows a weak-to-moderate Black Queen signal.** The Black Queen Hypothesis holds that costly functions can be lost when community members supply them as public goods [src: costly_dispensable_genes]. In NMDC metabolomics, 11 of 13 testable amino-acid pathways trended negative (sign test p=0.011). Only leucine (q = 0.022) and arginine (q = 0.049) passed false-discovery-rate correction [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]. Habitat adds structure: Soil and Freshwater communities separate on a PC1 dominated by carbon-utilization pathways, and 17 of 18 amino-acid pathways differ across ecosystems (q < 0.05) [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]. See [[concepts/community-metabolic-interdependence]] and [[concepts/habitat-structured-community-metabolic-potential]].

**Occurrence, energetics and pathway ecology constrain what traits mean.** Detecting a genus in an environment does not show that it degrades a compound there. The census atlas, for example, measured abundance only [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. Respiratory dependence in *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 may track NADH load more than substrate identity, a hypothesis: Complex I fitness defects are strongest on acetate and succinate, not only on aromatics [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]. Different carbon sources select different respiratory wiring, though the proposed flux mechanism rests on theoretical stoichiometry, not measured flux [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]. Polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB, a carbon-storage polymer) synthase (phaC, the presence marker) occurs in 21.9% of 27,690 GTDB species, with 21.7% carrying a complete pathway, and the pathway is are enriched in variable environments, but those tests are correlational and do not control for phylogeny, and the niche-breadth link is largely explained by genome size [src: phb_granule_ecology]. Species-level phenotype rates need strain denominators: only 37,368 of 97,334 BacDive strains (38.4%) matched a pangenome species and metal score [src: bacdive_phenotype_metal_tolerance]. See [[concepts/occurrence-versus-catabolic-activity]], [[concepts/respiratory-capacity-and-nadh-load]], [[concepts/phb-granule-ecology]] and [[concepts/sample-size-aware-phenotype-consensus]].

## Tensions and Caveats

**Self-sufficiency in deep Bacillota_B.** Genome size and orthologous-group content in deep Bacillota_B are consistent with self-sufficiency, yet the GapMind comparison finds no elevated pathway completeness [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory, clay_confined_subsurface]. The two measure different aspects of capacity, so neither settles it ([[conflicts/conflict--biosynthetic-self-sufficiency-and-cultivation--0f75deea]]). The projects also disagree on whether *Ca.* Desulforudis audaxviator-style lineages are in the cohorts at all ([[conflicts/conflict--biosynthetic-self-sufficiency-and-cultivation--c7d73f43]]).

**What "latent" means.** One analysis finds all Latent Capabilities become fitness-important under condition-specific analyses [src: discoveries]. The broader analysis finds a threshold-sensitive latent fraction, ranging from 4.7% to 21.1% across 16 threshold combinations [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] ([[conflicts/conflict--community-metabolic-interdependence--cdc57135]]).

**How far the Black Queen signal generalizes.** The 11/13 directional result may be coverage-limited because of sparse annotation, untestable pathways and proxy metabolites [src: discoveries]. A leucine signal strengthened after a mapping correction [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] ([[conflicts/conflict--community-metabolic-interdependence--732543c8]]). Of 131 samples, 125 (95%) come from one study [src: discoveries]. Leucine and arginine are read either as limits on generality or as not being artefacts of study mixing [src: discoveries, nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] ([[conflicts/conflict--community-metabolic-interdependence--96c2ce9b]]). Tyrosine is positive rather than negative, with unresolved causes [src: discoveries] ([[conflicts/conflict--community-metabolic-interdependence--e7db3c77]]). The plant-microbiome redundancy effect size of Cohen's d = −7.54 is described later as largely a formula error [src: discoveries, plant_microbiome_ecotypes] ([[conflicts/conflict--community-metabolic-interdependence--6a81cf0f]]).

**Complex I and NDH-2.** One reading attributes Complex I dispensability on glucose and lactate to NDH-2 compensation [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]. An ADP1 analysis finds no significant cross-species compensation pattern [src: respiratory_chain_wiring] ([[conflicts/conflict--respiratory-capacity-and-nadh-load--3d728916]]). The NADH-flux mechanism rests on theoretical stoichiometry [src: respiratory_chain_wiring].

**Load-bearing limits.** Most evidence is predicted completeness, not measured activity. The pilot covers only 7 organisms [src: essential_metabolome], and the fitness comparison covers 48 organisms against roughly 293,000 genomes with predictions [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]. The active-learning rationale and its own AUC table disagree about which class is weakest [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma].
