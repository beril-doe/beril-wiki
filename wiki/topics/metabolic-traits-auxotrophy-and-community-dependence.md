# Metabolic Traits, Auxotrophy, and Community Dependence

This topic asks what organisms can make, what they can use, and what they depend on others for. The corpus speaks to it through genome-based pathway predictions (mostly GapMind, a pathway-completeness caller), genome-wide fitness data, exometabolomics and community surveys. Its 22 member concepts are all linked below.

## What the Corpus Shows

**Biosynthesis is widely complete, but not always where it is needed.** In a seven-organism GapMind analysis, 17 of 18 amino acid pathways were present in every organism, and the exception was serine in Desulfovibrio vulgaris [src: essential_metabolome]. See [[concepts/conservation-of-amino-acid-biosynthesis-pathways-across-bacteria]]. The sample is small and the result is a prediction, not an essentiality test [src: essential_metabolome]. Across species, however, completeness depends on accessory genes. Leucine, valine, arginine, lysine and threonine show the largest core-versus-all-genes gaps, about 14% of species-level completeness [src: pathway_capability_dependency]. The report reads this as a precondition for Black Queen dynamics (gene loss when others supply the product), not a direct test [src: pathway_capability_dependency]. See [[concepts/black-queen-dynamics-in-amino-acid-biosynthesis]].

**Capability is not dependency.** Fitness data show that complete pathways are often dispensable. The 7-organism analysis gives 41.0% latent capabilities [src: discoveries], and the 48-organism threshold analysis gives 15.8% [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]. See [[concepts/latent-metabolic-capability-versus-fitness-dependency]]. In Pseudomonas FW300-N2E3, tryptophan is produced and used by the strain, but 0 of 50 P. fluorescens strains use it as a carbon source in BacDive. The report reads this as possible overflow for cross-feeding, an interpretation only [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]. See [[concepts/metabolite-production-versus-utilization-discordance]]. Aromatic amino acid pathways also predict the auxin gene ipdC, but the tyrosine negative control did so too [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. See [[concepts/aromatic-amino-acid-biosynthesis-and-auxin-pathway-gene-coupling]].

**What organisms can use is clade- and habitat-structured.** Catabolic capacity for aromatics is mostly a specialist trait. 675 genomes have one capacity and 18 have at least 3 [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. See [[concepts/taxonomic-distribution-of-aromatic-compound-catabolism]] and [[concepts/co-occurrence-and-modularity-of-catabolic-capacities-across-genomes]]. Host-associated Pseudomonas lost plant-derived sugar catabolism: 43 of 62 pathways differ between Pseudomonas s.s. and Pseudomonas_E [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. See [[concepts/host-adaptation-pathway-loss-in-pseudomonas-carbon-catabolism]]. CAZyme (carbohydrate-active enzyme) classes differ across 18 biomes [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology]; see [[concepts/biome-level-carbohydrate-active-enzyme-repertoire-differentiation]]. In NMDC samples, Soil and Freshwater separate on carbon-utilization pathways [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]; see [[concepts/ecosystem-differentiation-of-community-metabolic-potential]]. PHB synthesis (phaC) occurs in 21.9% of 27,690 GTDB species, concentrated in a few phyla [src: phb_granule_ecology]; see [[concepts/phylogenetic-distribution-of-phb-synthesis-across-bacteria-and-archaea]]. In BacDive, oxygen tolerance and metabolite breadth show no meaningful link to metal tolerance [src: bacdive_phenotype_metal_tolerance]; see [[concepts/metabolic-versatility-oxygen-tolerance-and-metal-tolerance]].

**Community dependence is inferred from co-occurrence and coverage.** A 3–5 organism consortium covers 100% of PA14's metabolic niche, while no individual commensal outgrows PA14 on any tested substrate [src: cf_formulation_design]. See [[concepts/community-level-niche-coverage-and-resource-depletion-for-competitive-exclusion]]. In the SSO wells, guilds couple positively (nitrifier–iron oxidizer ρ = +0.95) or segregate by redox (sulfate reducers vs aerobic heterotrophs ρ = −0.75) [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]; see [[concepts/metabolic-guild-co-occurrence-and-redox-driven-segregation]]. Labile carbon lowered bacterial Shannon diversity to 2.41 from 3.16 in lignin enrichments [src: lignin_community_enrichment]; see [[concepts/labile-carbon-effects-on-lignin-degrading-community-structure]]. Co-occurring plant genera look redundant, not complementary, after the effect size shrank from d = −7.54 to |d| ≈ 0.4 [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]; see [[concepts/functional-redundancy-versus-complementarity-among-co-occurring-genera]]. Crohn's pathobionts cluster in one module, and the cross-feeding reading with commensals was not supported by paired metabolite data [src: ibd_phage_targeting]; see [[concepts/pathobiont-co-occurrence-modules-versus-cross-feeding-with-commensals]]. Some pathobionts appear to 7α-dehydroxylate bile acids, which implies an ecological cost of removing them, though the evidence is correlational [src: ibd_phage_targeting, discoveries]; see [[concepts/bile-acid-7-dehydroxylation-deficit-and-the-ecological-cost-of-targeting-bile-acid-transforming-species]].

**Sampling and measurement shape what we see.** Cultured isolates differ from subsurface MAGs in 8,482 of 10,845 KOs tested, but most enrichment reflects larger cultivable genomes [src: oak_ridge_cultivation_gap]; see [[concepts/cultivation-bias-in-genome-collections-versus-uncultivated-lineages]]. Deep-clay genomes show no higher amino acid pathway completeness than soil [src: clay_confined_subsurface]; see [[concepts/biosynthetic-self-sufficiency-as-a-deep-subsurface-adaptation]]. Exometabolomic action codes need the actor to be known, and the 2018 snapshot lacks organism consumption [src: discoveries, webofmicrobes_explorer]; see [[concepts/production-versus-consumption-coverage-in-exometabolomic-databases]]. At n=6 strains, a multivariate model gave AUC 0.500 while univariate correlation found 940 associations [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]; see [[concepts/gene-metabolite-association-discovery-in-small-exometabolomic-datasets]].

## Tensions and Caveats

**Self-sufficiency.** Pathway completeness in cultured deep-clay genomes is not above soil (16.22 vs 16.66, unfiltered) [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. Yet Bacillota_B deep-clay genomes encode about 25% more orthogroups, read as consistent with self-sufficiency [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. This is an orthogroup count, not a pathway measure, and the deep cohort is very small [src: clay_confined_subsurface].

**Latent fractions.** 41.0% and 15.8% come from different organism sets and importance definitions, so they should not be reconciled by averaging [src: discoveries, metabolic_capability_dependency].

**Interpretation versus test.** Black Queen, tryptophan overflow, TyrR regulation of ipdC and syntrophy readings are the reports' proposals, not direct tests [src: pathway_capability_dependency, fw300_metabolic_consistency, pgp_pangenome_ecology, enigma_sso_asv_ecology]. The soil reversal for ipdC rests on few positive species [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology].

**Cross-feeding.** The first cross-feeding reading of Anaerostipes caccae coupling was not supported by paired metabolite data, which favored shared environment [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. Redundancy conclusions in NMDC depend on a corrected formula [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes].

**Scope.** Many results rest on a single project, a single organism or genome-predicted potential rather than measured activity. Others use direct measurements such as fitness, metabolite and community profiles. Check each page's evidence type.
