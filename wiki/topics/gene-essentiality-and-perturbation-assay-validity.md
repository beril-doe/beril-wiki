# Gene Essentiality and Perturbation-Assay Validity

Gene essentiality is dependence of growth on a gene's function under a defined condition. Whether a gene looks essential depends on how it is perturbed (transposon insertion, deletion, knockdown), the medium, and how absence of data is treated. This corpus speaks to the topic because it holds Fitness Browser RB-TnSeq (random barcode transposon sequencing) data for 48 bacteria, plus an *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 system where TnSeq, deletion knockouts and proteomics can be compared directly. [src: essential_genome, adp1_triple_essentiality]

## Literature Context

The candidate papers frame essentiality as conditional and graded. A review argues that binary essential/non-essential classifications leave no room between the two classes. It says the quantitative properties of essentiality depend on the encoded process, the environment and the genetic background, and it proposes using them to prioritize antibacterial targets [PMID 35104846](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35104846/). Condition-specific TnSeq studies support this. In Bordetella pertussis, some cell wall genes, including the entire mre/mrd locus, were essential in the avirulent Bvg-minus phase but not the virulent Bvg-plus phase [PMID 33295860](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33295860/). In Klebsiella pneumoniae, a TraDIS screen found 35 non-essential genes needed for growth under colistin. Only 1 gene each was found for imipenem and ciprofloxacin [PMID 28198411](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28198411/). The MtbTnDB collates about 150 standardized Mycobacterium tuberculosis TnSeq screens to compare conditional essentiality [PMID 40527579](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40527579/). Clostridium autoethanogenum was also screened under heterotrophic and autotrophic growth [PMID 35285680](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35285680/).

The papers also describe technical limits of transposon calls. Himar1 insertion frequency at TA sites is shaped by the surrounding nucleotides, contrary to the usual assumption of random insertion. The authors modeled this bias and built the TTN-Fitness method, which classifies many small genes previously called uncertain [PMID 34665010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34665010/). TnseqDiff uses insertion-level evidence to call conditional essentiality and was tested on simulated data and on one Serratia marcescens dataset [PMID 28683752](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28683752/). Orthogonal validation uses conditional alleles. These include a temperature-sensitive lpxH allele in Pseudomonas aeruginosa, which confirmed a TnSeq essentiality call [PMID 38927720](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38927720/). They also include promoter-replacement mutants in Burkholderia cenocepacia [PMID 23389959](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23389959/) and conditional-expression mutants used to validate selected essential genes in Streptococcus pyogenes. That study predicted 227 and 241 essential genes in two strains, and 177 were conserved in the core genome of 20 genomes [PMID 25996237](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25996237/).

The corpus is consistent with this literature on several points. Its split into 859 universally essential, 4,799 variably essential and 11,564 never-essential families fits the conditional and graded view. The same is true of its finding that 499 ADP1 genes are essential on minimal medium versus 346 on LB. Its caution that insertion-missing calls for short genes may reflect missed insertions agrees with the documented insertion biases [PMID 34665010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34665010/). Its preference for continuous measures, such as inverted fitness with AUC 0.700 in rich medium, fits the gradient argument [PMID 35104846](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35104846/). Its inference that essential-gene conservation is modest (86.1% core versus 81.2%) is in line with the 177 conserved essential genes in S. pyogenes, though that study did not make this comparison.

The corpus extends the literature in one way. The ADP1 comparison found negative Cohen's kappa between RB-TnSeq and knockout essentiality at all five thresholds. TnSeq caught only 18 of 229 knockout-essential genes at the 0.05 threshold. None of the candidate papers makes a direct, genome-wide comparison of this kind. The P. aeruginosa lpxH result is a single-gene concordance and does not bear on that discordance. The ADP1 result is from one organism, so it remains a hypothesis for other bacteria. The proposed CRISPRi tests of dark essential genes are untested, and the candidate papers say nothing about them. The counter-ion and Complex I tensions likewise have no counterpart in the candidate papers.

## What the Corpus Shows

**Essentiality is mostly context-dependent, with a small universal core.** Of 17,222 ortholog families, 859 (5.0%) are universally essential, 4,799 (27.9%) are variably essential and 11,564 (67.1%) are never essential. [src: discoveries, essential_genome] Fifteen families are essential in all 48 organisms, mainly ribosomal proteins plus groEL, pyrG, fusA, valS and SelGGPS. [src: essential_genome, discoveries] The report reads variability as genomic context (paralogs, alternative pathways, accessory functions), which is its interpretation. [src: essential_genome] It also argues that only the 859 universal families are reliable broad-spectrum antibiotic targets, an assertion not yet tested. [src: essential_genome] See [[concepts/universal-versus-variable-gene-essentiality-across-bacteria]].

**Essential genes are better annotated and modestly more core.** In ADP1, 92% of essential genes have KO (KEGG ortholog) assignments versus 53% of dispensable genes. [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer] Essential genes are 86.1% core versus 81.2% for non-essential genes, and the core enrichment is modest. [src: conservation_vs_fitness] Orphan essential genes are 58.7% hypothetical, the highest uncharacterized fraction of any category. [src: essential_genome] Medium matters: 499 ADP1 genes are essential on minimal medium versus 346 on LB. [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer] See [[concepts/annotation-and-pangenome-correlates-of-gene-essentiality]]. Collection gaps follow the same pattern: of 2,593 TnSeq-dispensable ADP1 genes, 272 (10.5%) lack deletion-collection data, and these are 76.5% core versus 93.3% for those present. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes] The report links this to evolutionary retention pressure, which is a suggestion and not a tested mechanism. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes] See [[concepts/coverage-gaps-in-deletion-collections-and-their-link-to-pangenome-conservation]].

**A transposon insertion is not a deletion.** In ADP1, Cohen's kappa between RB-TnSeq and knockout essentiality was negative at all five thresholds. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality] At the 0.05 threshold TnSeq caught only 18 of 229 knockout-essential genes, while 293 genes (15.2%) were knockout-dispensable but TnSeq-essential. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality] The report suggests RB-TnSeq reflects fitness cost more than lethality, and that truncated protein or read-through transcription may let disrupted alleles retain function; this mechanism is a suggestion from one organism. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality] See [[concepts/transposon-insertion-fitness-versus-gene-deletion-lethality]] and [[concepts/limits-of-transposon-based-essentiality-calls]].

**Continuous measures beat binary ones.** Inverted fitness gave ROC (receiver operating characteristic) AUC (area under curve) 0.700 in rich and 0.725 in minimal medium, and proteomics gave 0.743, while essentiality fraction scored 0.344 and 0.403, below chance. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality] See [[concepts/continuous-versus-binary-predictors-of-gene-essentiality]].

**Absence-based calls hide genes and bias downstream analyses.** The Fitness Browser has no essentiality column, so essentials are inferred as protein-coding genes absent from genefitness, an upper bound that is about 14.3% of protein-coding genes. [src: pitfalls] They are excluded from fitness modules (0 essential genes in 1,116 modules) and make core-enrichment estimates conservative. [src: discoveries, metal_specificity] Among functionally dark genes, 9,557 are essential, and the report ranks them without fitness data and proposes CRISPRi (CRISPR interference) knockdown tests that have not been run. [src: functional_dark_matter] See [[concepts/essential-genes-as-a-blind-spot-of-transposon-fitness-screens]]. In AMR (antimicrobial resistance) analyses, about 4.6% of AMR genes are absent from fitness matrices, so the +0.086 cost may be a lower bound. [src: amr_fitness_cost] Polar effects and the environmental bias of the organisms remain unresolved. [src: amr_fitness_cost, amr_pangenome_atlas] See [[concepts/essentiality-and-annotation-caveats-in-transposon-based-resistance-gene-fitness-analysis]].

**Assay validity extends beyond essentiality.** Threshold calibration matters: a ≥10% phenotype threshold sat below a 33.25% background, so it passed any random gene set, and under a recalibrated test one Fur-regulon path was supported while the other was not. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The compendium also had zero iron-limitation experiments. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] See [[concepts/calibration-of-fitness-phenotype-thresholds-against-genome-background]]. Overlap between metal and NaCl fitness genes (39.8%) did not scale with chloride delivered, arguing against counter-ion confounding. [src: counter_ion_effects] See [[concepts/counter-ion-confounding-in-metal-fitness-assays]]. Complex I dependence shows how condition choice shapes a requirement: the data point to high-NADH substrates rather than aromatics alone. [src: aromatic_catabolism_network] See [[concepts/substrate-dependent-complex-i-requirement-and-ndh-2-compensation]].

## Tensions and Caveats

- **Modest versus strong conservation signal.** Core fractions of 86.1% versus 81.2% and a weak penetrance–core correlation (rho=0.123) show a small effect, though universally essential genes reach 91.7% core. [src: conservation_vs_fitness, essential_genome]
- **Rates are not matched.** The 4.6% AMR essential rate is compared with a ~14% background from a different organism set, so the reading that censoring is small is only suggestive. [src: amr_fitness_cost]
- **Denominators.** Essential genes were 82% core using all genes and 86% using mapped genes only. [src: pitfalls]
- **Insertion bias.** Essential genes were shorter (median 675 bp versus 885 bp), so some calls may reflect missed insertions. [src: essential_genome]
- **Sampling.** The 48 organisms are biased toward culturable Proteobacteria, and universality across 43 organisms remains an open question elsewhere. [src: essential_genome, conservation_fitness_synthesis]
- **Single-organism evidence.** The TnSeq–knockout discordance and predictor comparison come from ADP1 alone, and the proteomics and fitness AUCs use different gene sets. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality]
- **Counter-ion test.** The report calls zinc sulfate a definitive rejection, yet its only within-metal comparison is confounded by aerobic versus anaerobic growth and the report says it only partially supports H0. [src: counter_ion_effects]
- **NDH-2 compensation.** One report supports it, while another finds NDH-2 presence does not predict Complex I dispensability (p = 0.52). [src: aromatic_catabolism_network, respiratory_chain_wiring]
