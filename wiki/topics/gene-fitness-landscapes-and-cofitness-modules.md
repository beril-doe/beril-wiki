# Gene Fitness Landscapes and Cofitness Modules

A gene fitness landscape is the set of growth effects that disrupting each gene produces across many experimental conditions. Genes whose landscapes move together are called cofitness partners, and independent component analysis (ICA, a matrix decomposition that separates mixed signals into independent components) groups them into fitness modules. This corpus speaks to the topic through ICA modules built across 32 organisms, deletion-collection screens in Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1, and metal, stress and dark-gene analyses on Fitness Browser data (RB-TnSeq, random barcode transposon sequencing, mutant fitness). [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis, adp1_deletion_phenotypes, fitness_modules]

## What the Corpus Shows

**Modules are built by thresholding, and the threshold decides validity.** Robust ICA (30-50 runs with DBSCAN clustering) gives 1,116 stable modules across 32 organisms, all with >=100 experiments. [src: fitness_modules, discoveries] Membership selection, not the ICA components, caused the early failure. The initial D'Agostino K-squared approach gave 100-280 genes per module. An absolute |Pearson r| ≥ 0.3 cutoff with a maximum of 50 genes fixed it, and cofitness enrichment rose from 59% to 94.2%. [src: discoveries, fitness_modules] The pitfalls digest calls this the single most impactful parameter in the pipeline. [src: pitfalls] Within-module mean |r| is 0.34 against a background of 0.12, and genomic adjacency enrichment averages 22.7x, which the reports read as operon-like groups. [src: discoveries, fitness_modules] See [[concepts/construction-and-validity-of-cofitness-gene-groups]] and [[concepts/fitness-modules-essential-genes-and-condition-specific-gene-function]]. Modules capture process-level co-regulation, not specific molecular function, and they contain no essential genes. [src: discoveries]

**Modules are mostly core and recur across organisms.** ICA modules are 86% core against an 81.5% baseline (odds ratio 1.46). [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis, module_conservation] There are 156 module families spanning 2+ organisms, 28 spanning 5+, and the largest spans 21 of 32 organisms. [src: fitness_modules, discoveries] Ortholog scope matters. A 5-organism pilot gave 27 families and none spanning 5+ organisms, so partial extraction underestimates conservation. [src: discoveries, pitfalls] See [[concepts/conservation-of-cofitness-modules-across-genomes-and-organisms]]. Metal-responsive modules have a mean core fraction of 0.826, with a median of 0.929. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] See [[concepts/metal-responsive-ica-fitness-modules]]. Only 24% of AMR (antimicrobial resistance) genes (192/801) fall in modules, but those modules are larger, with a median of 46 genes against 27, and 99% (208/209) of assignments lie in conserved families. [src: amr_cofitness_networks] See [[concepts/amr-genes-in-ica-fitness-modules]].

**Modules and cofitness support guilt-by-association inference.** The fitness_modules analysis produced 6,691 function predictions for hypothetical proteins, of which 2,455 (37%) were family-backed. [src: fitness_modules] It warns that module-ICA should not be used to predict specific molecular functions. [src: fitness_modules] The strongest dark-gene candidates combine several independent lines of evidence. [src: functional_dark_matter] See [[concepts/guilt-by-association-inference-of-dark-gene-function-from-modules-operons-synteny-and-co-fitness]] and [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness-of-dark-genes]]. Dark-gene concordance across 65 testable ortholog groups was statistically indistinguishable from annotated genes, which supports dark genes behaving like functional genes. [src: discoveries, functional_dark_matter] See [[concepts/cross-organism-fitness-concordance-of-dark-gene-orthologs]].

**Condition dependence is the core of the landscape.** In ADP1, the 8 carbon sources fall into demanding, moderate and robust tiers. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes] The mean pairwise defect correlation was 0.38. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality] Urea is largely independent of other conditions. [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer] The 8 conditions give about 5 independent dimensions. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes] Genes with condition-specific importance (625, 31%) recover expected pathways. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes] Respiratory chain requirements also differ qualitatively by carbon source. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring, discoveries] See [[concepts/condition-specific-gene-importance-across-carbon-sources]], [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness-correlation-across-carbon-sources]], [[concepts/continuous-versus-discrete-structure-of-condition-dependent-gene-essentiality]], [[concepts/condition-specific-gene-phenotypes-and-functional-annotation-yield]], [[concepts/condition-dependent-respiratory-chain-configuration]], [[concepts/support-gene-networks-around-catabolic-pathways]] and [[concepts/operon-organization-versus-functional-coupling-of-pathway-support-genes]]. In FW300-N2E3, the top 18 genes were significant in all 21 conditions, while about 370 were substrate-specific. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency] See [[concepts/housekeeping-versus-substrate-specific-fitness-genes]] and [[concepts/matching-exometabolite-profiles-to-gene-fitness-experiments]]. Transporter specificity can be read directly: ManXYZ knockouts are defective on mannose and glucosamine but dispensable on fructose. [src: discoveries, snipe_defense_system] See [[concepts/substrate-specificity-of-the-mannose-pts-manxyz-from-genome-wide-fitness]].

**Stress and metal landscapes overlap, and core genes can be burdens.** 98.1% of metal-pair gene-level correlations were positive (311/317). [src: metal_cross_resistance] 4,304 of 10,821 metal-important gene records (39.8%) were also important under NaCl. [src: counter_ion_effects] See [[concepts/universal-positivity-and-chemistry-specific-magnitude-of-metal-cross-resistance]], [[concepts/shared-metal-and-osmotic-stress-fitness-determinants]], [[concepts/metal-specific-versus-pleiotropic-stress-gene-classification]] and [[concepts/metal-fitness-breadth-and-magnitude-across-metals-and-organisms]]. Core genes are more often burdens: 24.4% show positive fitness when deleted, against 19.9% for accessory genes. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis] See [[concepts/function-specific-burden-of-core-genes]].

**Regulatory dissection and structure.** In Caulobacter, the ChvG-ChvI regulon splits into three cohorts: 20 early, 10 both-phase and 49 late genes. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] See [[concepts/chvg-chvi-envelope-stress-regulon-phase-structure]] and [[concepts/fur-derepression-and-transcript-buffering-by-regulator-co-deletion]]. The join of fitness data to AlphaFold models covers 55,454 genes, but the structural analyses have not yet been run. [src: berdl_data_atlas] See [[concepts/structure-fitness-integration-of-mutant-fitness-data-with-predicted-protein-structures]].

## Tensions and Caveats

**Shared dispensability.** Cofitness enrichment may reflect genes that are all dispensable under laboratory conditions, not co-regulation. The AMR permutation matched conservation class but not mean fitness, and the proposed tests were not run. [src: amr_cofitness_networks] See [[concepts/shared-dispensability-as-a-confound-in-cofitness-enrichment]].

**Metal specificity depends on definition.** The sick-rate analysis called 54.9% of metal-important records metal-specific. [src: metal_specificity] The atlas report calls its broad definition mostly general stress genes. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] The NaCl overlap was 39.8% in one report and 14.7% in another, a 2.7x discrepancy that the later report attributes mostly to threshold stringency. [src: counter_ion_effects, metal_specificity] The metal_specificity analysis found 0 metal-specific modules, and per-module z-normalization limits may bear on that. [src: metal_specificity]

**Scope limits.** ADP1 results come from one organism and 8 conditions, and the reports' correlations (0.44, 0.38, 0.58) come from different analyses and are not directly comparable. [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer, adp1_triple_essentiality, adp1_deletion_phenotypes] Truly dark genes are depleted in stress conditions (OR=0.53), but the interpretation is a hypothesis. [src: discoveries, truly_dark_genes] The Caulobacter ΔsspB buffering has no independent fitness support. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Rare metals are tested in only 1-2 organisms. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]
