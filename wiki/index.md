# BERIL Knowledge Wiki

Most of these projects analysed data already in the [KBase Data Lakehouse](https://hub.berdl.kbase.us); a few brought their own. See [[about|About This Wiki]] for what that means for citing anything here.

The BERIL Research Observatory is a corpus of AI-conducted microbial-biology research run over the KBase Data Lakehouse. Each project wrote a report, and the wiki compiles those reports and two cross-project digests into linked pages.

Start from the topics below. Each topic hub states a question, summarizes what the corpus shows, notes where projects disagree, and links every concept assigned to it. Concept pages are the reference layer that argues across projects. Entity pages cover single organisms, genes, compounds, methods and datasets. Summary pages cover individual projects and the two digests.

Claims on concept and entity pages end with citations to their source projects. Follow those citations back to the project summaries when you need the original context.

## Topics

- [[topics/gene-fitness-landscapes-and-cofitness-modules|Gene Fitness Landscapes and Cofitness Modules]] (27 concepts): Growth effects of disrupting each gene across many conditions, and how independent component analysis groups cofitness partners into fitness modules.
- [[topics/gene-essentiality-and-perturbation-assay-validity|Gene Essentiality and Perturbation-Assay Validity]] (11 concepts): How apparent essentiality depends on the perturbation method, the medium and the treatment of missing data, using Fitness Browser RB-TnSeq data for 48 bacteria.
- [[topics/laboratory-fitness-versus-natural-selection-and-gene-conservation|Laboratory Fitness Versus Natural Selection and Gene Conservation]] (16 concepts): Whether a gene's laboratory growth effect explains why the gene is core or accessory across a species' genomes.
- [[topics/pangenome-structure-and-genome-evolution|Pangenome Structure and Genome Evolution]] (17 concepts): Which genes sit in the core or accessory compartment, and what drives gene gain, copy number and loss.
- [[topics/ecotypes-and-environmental-gene-content-differentiation|Ecotypes and Environmental Gene-Content Differentiation]] (21 concepts): Whether within-species groups defined by accessory gene content exist, and how much gene content environment explains compared with ancestry.
- [[topics/metabolic-traits-auxotrophy-and-community-dependence|Metabolic Traits, Auxotrophy, and Community Dependence]] (22 concepts): What organisms can make, what they can use, and what they depend on others for, from pathway predictions, fitness data, exometabolomics and community surveys.
- [[topics/metabolic-models-and-pathway-level-evidence|Metabolic Models and Pathway-Level Evidence]] (26 concepts): How far genome-derived metabolic predictions can be trusted.
- [[topics/lanthanide-dependent-methylotrophy|Lanthanide-Dependent Methylotrophy]] (4 concepts): Rare-earth cofactors for PQQ methanol dehydrogenases, drawn from a single pangenome-scale atlas of the xoxF cassette.
- [[topics/functional-dark-matter-and-annotation-resolution|Functional Dark Matter and Annotation Resolution]] (21 concepts): Genes without functional annotation, such as hypothetical proteins, DUFs and uncharacterized proteins.
- [[topics/resistance-defense-systems-and-mobile-elements|Resistance, Defense Systems, and Mobile Elements]] (41 concepts): Antimicrobial-resistance genes, metal tolerance, anti-phage defense and the mobile genetic elements that can move them between genomes.
- [[topics/sampling-coverage-and-statistical-confounding|Sampling Coverage and Statistical Confounding]] (63 concepts): How sampling, reference database coverage and non-independent observations shape apparent ecological and genomic patterns.
- [[topics/data-infrastructure-provenance-and-research-practice|Data Infrastructure, Provenance, and Research Practice]] (15 concepts): How data are organized, labelled and joined in the KBase Data Lakehouse, and how review, code history and ingestion decide whether a result can be trusted.
- [[topics/distributed-computation-and-analysis-execution|Distributed Computation and Analysis Execution]] (2 concepts): How analyses run against the KBase Data Lakehouse through Spark, pandas frames, notebooks, sessions and credentials.
- [[topics/translational-microbiome-and-phage-therapeutics|Translational Microbiome and Phage Therapeutics]] (10 concepts): How microbiome measurements become interventions, including a Crohn's disease phage-targeting project.
- [[topics/subsurface-and-soil-community-ecology|Subsurface and Soil Community Ecology]] (26 concepts): What structures microbial communities in soils and the subsurface, from Oak Ridge surveys, a Harvard Forest warming experiment, NEON soil metagenomes and lignin enrichment cultures.
