# BERIL Knowledge Wiki

Most of these projects analysed data already in the [KBase Data Lakehouse](https://hub.berdl.kbase.us); a few brought their own. See [[about|About This Wiki]] for what that means for citing anything here.

The BERIL Research Observatory is a body of AI-conducted microbial-biology research. The research runs over the KBase Data Lakehouse, and each project wrote up its results as a report. This wiki compiles those reports, plus two cross-project digests of discoveries and pitfalls, into linked pages.

Start from the topics below. Each topic gathers a set of concept pages that argue across projects: where the evidence agrees, where it conflicts, and what analyses would settle the open questions. Concept pages cite the projects behind every claim. Entity pages describe the organisms, genes, compounds, methods and datasets that recur across reports, and summary pages cover one project each.

Use the topics as entry points and the other page types as reference. If you know a project, go to its summary. If you know a gene, organism or dataset, go to its entity page.

## Topics

- [[topics/gene-fitness-landscapes-and-cofitness-modules|Gene Fitness Landscapes and Cofitness Modules]] (11 concepts): the growth effects of disrupting each gene across many conditions, and the cofitness modules of genes that share fitness patterns.
- [[topics/gene-essentiality-and-perturbation-assay-validity|Gene Essentiality and Perturbation-Assay Validity]] (6 concepts): direct perturbation and measured growth count as the strongest evidence, while conservation, annotation and modeling count as predictors.
- [[topics/laboratory-fitness-versus-natural-selection-and-gene-conservation|Laboratory Fitness Versus Natural Selection and Gene Conservation]] (6 concepts): whether a gene's growth effect in the laboratory explains its conservation across genomes, using Fitness Browser data linked to pangenomes.
- [[topics/pangenome-structure-and-genome-evolution|Pangenome Structure and Genome Evolution]] (9 concepts): the core and auxiliary gene sets of species, and how projects define and use them.
- [[topics/ecotypes-and-environmental-gene-content-differentiation|Ecotypes and Environmental Gene-Content Differentiation]] (7 concepts): whether environment shapes the genes bacteria carry, with one broad test across 172 species and many targeted ones.
- [[topics/metabolic-traits-auxotrophy-and-community-dependence|Metabolic Traits, Auxotrophy, and Community Dependence]] (12 concepts): what organisms can make, use and depend on others for, and how well GapMind-style genome predictions match measured growth.
- [[topics/metabolic-models-and-pathway-level-evidence|Metabolic Models and Pathway-Level Evidence]] (6 concepts): how far flux balance analysis and GapMind pathway-completeness calls can be trusted.
- [[topics/lanthanide-dependent-methylotrophy|Lanthanide-Dependent Methylotrophy]] (3 concepts): rare-earth cofactors for PQQ methanol dehydrogenases, scanned across 293,059 GTDB genomes.
- [[topics/functional-dark-matter-and-annotation-resolution|Functional Dark Matter and Annotation Resolution]] (9 concepts): genes with no functional annotation, and the separate layer of literature darkness.
- [[topics/resistance-defense-systems-and-mobile-elements|Resistance, Defense Systems, and Mobile Elements]] (7 concepts): antimicrobial resistance, metal tolerance, anti-phage defense and the mobile elements that carry them.
- [[topics/sampling-coverage-and-statistical-confounding|Sampling Coverage and Statistical Confounding]] (14 concepts): how uneven sampling, database scope, batch structure and pseudoreplication shape apparent patterns.
- [[topics/data-infrastructure-provenance-and-research-practice|Data Infrastructure, Provenance, and Research Practice]] (8 concepts): how Lakehouse data are organized and labelled, and how review and code history decide whether a result can be trusted.
- [[topics/distributed-computation-and-analysis-execution|Distributed Computation and Analysis Execution]] (9 concepts): Spark, pandas frames, notebooks, sessions and credentials, drawn from the pitfalls digest.
- [[topics/translational-microbiome-and-phage-therapeutics|Translational Microbiome and Phage Therapeutics]] (13 concepts): how microbiome measurements become interventions, through a Crohn's disease phage-targeting case and a cystic-fibrosis airway case.
- [[topics/subsurface-and-soil-community-ecology|Subsurface and Soil Community Ecology]] (6 concepts): what structures soil and subsurface communities, from Oak Ridge groundwater surveys to a soil-warming experiment.
