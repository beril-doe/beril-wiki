# Does Anaerostipes caccae get its lactate from Bacteroides rather than Crohn's pathobionts?

Where does Anaerostipes caccae get the lactate it converts to butyrate in a Crohn's gut, and do the pathobionts Flavonifractor plautii and Enterocloster bolteae supply it under defined growth conditions?

## Hypothesis

In defined lactate-limited medium, A. caccae growth and butyrate output rise when it is paired with Bacteroides or Bifidobacterium, but not when it is paired with F. plautii or E. bolteae. That would make the opposite-direction lactate pattern across the cohort consistent with these two pathobionts not supplying lactate to A. caccae under the tested conditions.

## Why it matters

Pathobiont-targeting phage cocktails assume that removing a pathobiont may cost the gut a butyrate supply. If A. caccae does not depend on these pathobionts, that cost is smaller for these partners, and the real lactate suppliers become the taxa to protect.

## How to test it

Grow A. caccae in defined lactate-limited medium, alone and paired with F. plautii, E. bolteae, a Bacteroides species, or Bifidobacterium infantis. Measure lactate, butyrate and A. caccae counts over time. Spent-medium and transwell controls separate secreted lactate from contact effects. Then repeat in a synthetic community in which each partner is removed in turn, and vary the carbon source and pH to see whether any result depends on condition.

## What would support or refute it

Support: A. caccae butyrate rises only with the Bacteroides or Bifidobacterium partners, and the pathobionts add nothing under the conditions tried. Refutation: pathobiont partners raise A. caccae growth or butyrate as much as the known partners, so the cohort pattern came from something other than missing cross-feeding. A null result would apply only to these partners and conditions, not to cross-feeding in general.

## Data

Approach: experiment. Readiness: needs new measurements. The paired-partner co-culture data needed are not shown in the catalog; only cohort-level metagenome and metabolite data were used, and those gave the null.
