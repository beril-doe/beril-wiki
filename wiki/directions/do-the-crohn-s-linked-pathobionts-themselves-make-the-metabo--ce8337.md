# Do the Crohn's-linked pathobionts themselves make the metabolites that separate Crohn's from non-IBD?

Are the metabolites that load with the six core pathobionts on the joint species-metabolite axis produced by those species, or only correlated with them?

## Hypothesis

At least some of the top metabolites on the axis are made in pure culture by the pathobionts that load on it, while others are produced by non-pathobiont members of the community.

## Why it matters

The axis separates Crohn's from non-IBD, but a correlation cannot say whether removing the pathobionts would change the metabolite profile. Production by the pathobionts would make the axis a mechanism-linked readout; absence would mean it tracks community state instead.

## How to test it

Grow the six core pathobionts, singly and in a pooled community, in a stool-derived anaerobic medium. Run untargeted metabolomics on the supernatants and compare them with the metabolites loading on the axis in the paired metagenome and metabolome data. Compare the pooled community with a version lacking the pathobionts.

## What would support or refute it

Support: a substantial share of the axis metabolites appear in pathobiont cultures and fall when the pathobionts are dropped from the community. Refutation: the axis metabolites are absent from pathobiont cultures and unaffected by their removal.

## Data

Approach: experiment. Readiness: needs new measurements. It needs cultured isolates and a metabolomics run; the axis itself comes from a pilot analysis that its authors say is not a substitute for a full three-modality analysis.
