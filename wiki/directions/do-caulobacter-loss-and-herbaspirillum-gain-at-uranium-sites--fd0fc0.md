# Do Caulobacter loss and Herbaspirillum gain at uranium sites reflect uranium tolerance or co-varying chemistry?

Are the lower Caulobacter and higher Herbaspirillum abundances at high-uranium Oak Ridge sites caused by uranium itself, or by correlated factors such as pH?

## Hypothesis

Uranium alone does not set the growth advantage of these genera. In a factorial test of uranium and pH, pH or co-occurring factors will explain growth differences more than uranium does.

## Why it matters

It would show whether field uranium trends can guide which organisms to test for uranium tolerance. It would also show whether they reflect general contamination.

## How to test it

Grow Oak Ridge Caulobacter and Herbaspirillum isolates in a factorial design of uranium concentration and pH, with and without a second metal. Compare their growth responses with the field direction: lower for Caulobacter and higher for Herbaspirillum at high-uranium sites. Desulfovibrio is left out because its rarity made its field test unreliable.

## What would support or refute it

Support: pH or the second metal shapes growth more than uranium, or the lab responses do not match the field direction. Refute: uranium alone reproduces the field direction for both genera.

## Data

Approach: experiment. Readiness: needs new measurements. The field correlations exist, but the factorial growth tests are new measurements.
