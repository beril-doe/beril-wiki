# Heather MacGregor

ORCID: [0000-0003-1112-3009](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1112-3009)

## Contributions

Metal-gene density and niche breadth. In [[summaries/comprehensive_metal_ecology__REPORT]], across 1,574 bacterial genera, per-Mb metal-gene KO (KEGG ortholog) density was negatively associated with standardised Levins' niche breadth (β = −0.021, SE = 0.0037, p = 2.1×10⁻⁸, r² = 0.046). [src: comprehensive_metal_ecology] The pre-specified negative-control gene sets (ribosomal proteins, amino acid biosynthesis, DNA repair) also showed significant negative associations, so specificity to metal genes is not demonstrated. [src: comprehensive_metal_ecology] The archaeal replication (n = 95) was directionally consistent but non-significant. [src: comprehensive_metal_ecology] Resistance/Detoxification KOs were null within the metal set (β = +0.003, FDR p = 0.656). [src: comprehensive_metal_ecology] In [[summaries/microbeatlas_metal_ecology__REPORT]], across 606 bacterial genera in PGLS (phylogenetic generalized least squares), metal type diversity was the only metal AMR predictor to survive Bonferroni correction (β = +0.021 per SD, p = 1.5×10⁻⁴). [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology] AMR cluster count and core fraction did not predict niche breadth in either the simple or the multi-predictor models. [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]

Soil metals and functional genes. [[summaries/soil_metal_functional_genomics__REPORT]] found 2,355 significant COG-metal associations at FDR < 0.05 across 9 metals in 51,748 soil samples. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics] The conditional db-RDA gave R² = 0.799 (p = 0.005). The report cautions that it may describe residual rather than total variance, and the unconditional R² is unreported. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics] The copper-specific analysis identified 116 COGs at FDR < 0.05 using 7,566 samples with nearby genomes at 10 km. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics] The report is preliminary, and co-contamination confounding remains unresolved. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics]

Soil genomic gaps. [[summaries/soil_frontier_genomics__REPORT]] reported that the clay shield hypothesis was not supported at global scale. All predictive models had negative out-of-sample R², and the shield-efficiency difference was 0.024 [95% CI: −0.423, 0.161], which includes zero. The cause of the poor prediction is not diagnosed. [src: soil_frontier_genomics] Its Genomic Discovery Index was highest for Forest (902.36) and Cropland (890.82), and the report treats these as not meaningfully distinguishable without bootstrap CIs. [src: soil_frontier_genomics]

Global biogeography and horizontal transfer. [[summaries/metal_resistance_global_biogeography__REPORT]] found that 2.8% of 22,356 coordinate-bearing MAGs carry at least one metal resistance type. Soil was enriched (5.8%, OR=5.05) and marine depleted (1.2%, OR=0.20). [src: metal_resistance_global_biogeography] Its 11 hotspots and 3 coldspots are provisional pending sampling-effort correction. [src: metal_resistance_global_biogeography] [[summaries/t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt__REPORT]] found T4SS machinery in 21.8% of 30,497 environmental MAGs (6,652). [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt] The GT2 gene tree contained 77 detected HGT events, 32 of them high-confidence cross-phylum. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt] GT2-neighbourhood MAGs (n=376) had a mean of 0.045 metal resistance types, against 0.004 for non-GT2 MAGs (Mann-Whitney p=8.6e-27). [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt]
