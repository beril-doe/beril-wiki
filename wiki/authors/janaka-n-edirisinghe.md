# Janaka N. Edirisinghe

ORCID: [0000-0003-2493-234X](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-2493-234X)

## Contributions

[[summaries/annotation_gap_discovery__REPORT]] integrated metabolic-model gapfilling, Fitness Browser phenotypes, pangenome annotations, GapMind pathway evidence, and BLAST homology across 14 organisms and 18 carbon sources to assign candidate genes to gapfilled reactions. Of 201 gapfilled enzymatic reaction-organism pairs, the project resolved 96 (47.8%) with confidence-scored candidate genes — 44 high-confidence (21.9%), 19 medium-confidence (9.5%), and 33 low-confidence (16.4%) — leaving 105 pairs (52.2%) unresolved, and exceeding the pre-specified H1 threshold of 30% [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. Leave-one-out cross-validation in the same project found no single evidence stream sufficient: the full pipeline gave 96 resolved pairs (47.8%) versus 86 (42.8%) without NB03 EC matching, 80 (39.8%) without NB04 Bakta annotations, and 73 (36.3%) without NB06 BLAST, while individual streams alone resolved 51 pairs (25.4%) for NB03, 22 (10.9%) for NB04, and 70 (34.8%) for BLAST [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. The project reported that resolution varied 3.5-fold across organisms, from 20.0% in *Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron* (3 of 15 gaps) to 71.4% in *Klebsiella michiganensis* (5 of 7), with *Marinobacter* at 8/12 (66.7%), *Azospirillum brasilense* at 13/21 (61.9%), *Herbaspirillum seropedicae* at 10/17 (58.8%), and *E. coli* Keio at 4/7 (57.1%) [src: annotation_gap_discovery].

The same project characterised where the approach failed. It designated 50 of 201 gapfilled reactions (24.9%) as "dark reactions" lacking an EC number in ModelSEED, and resolved only 8 of those 50 (16%) against 88 of 151 (58.3%) for reactions with known EC numbers [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. Two reactions — rxn02185, 2-acetolactate pyruvate-lyase (EC 2.2.1.6), and rxn03436, acetohydroxy acid isomeroreductase (EC 1.1.1.86), sequential steps in branched-chain amino acid biosynthesis — were each resolved at high confidence in 9 of 14 organisms [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. Across 104 GapMind-gapfill pathway pairings, GapMind frequently reported incomplete pathways (`not_present` or `steps_missing`) where ModelSEED required gapfilling, but exact concordance was limited because GapMind reports pathway-level step counts rather than individual step identities in the available data [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. The report recorded that the dataset was phylogenetically biased, with 12 of 14 organisms Proteobacteria, and that the sole Bacteroidetes organism had the lowest resolution rate [src: annotation_gap_discovery].

Methodologically, the project built draft models from ModelSEED/RAST annotations and COBRApy and evaluated them with flux-balance analysis (FBA), a constraint-based method for predicting metabolic flux and growth; across 574 organism-carbon source combinations, baseline FBA reached 42.5% overall accuracy with 86.5% recall (244 of 282 growth-positive conditions) and 42.5% precision (244 of 574 growth predictions), producing 330 false positives [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. Conditional gapfilling of 38 false-negative cases added 219 reactions (201 enzymatic, 14 transport, 12 exchange), averaging 5.8 per case [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. The BLAST stage retrieved 328 reviewed bacterial Swiss-Prot exemplar sequences for 75/84 unique ECs via the UniProt REST API and identified 154 hits with DIAMOND v2.1.16 blastp (`--evalue 1e-5 --max-target-seqs 20 --id 25 --query-cover 50 --outfmt 6`), applying high-confidence thresholds of ≥30% identity, ≥70% coverage, and e-value ≤1e-10 [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. For validation the project inserted 23 gene-protein-reaction (GPR) rules into SBML models, but reported the knockout simulations as inconclusive: wildtype growth on minimal carbon-source media was zero because the models required the gapfilled reactions themselves, making single-gene knockout analysis circular in that setting [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. The report also noted gapfilling non-uniqueness — default ModelSEED gapfilling minimizes added reactions without guaranteeing biological optimality — and that manual curation of 109 carbon-source-to-compound mappings could generate spurious false negatives [src: annotation_gap_discovery]. These results feed [[concepts/metabolic-model-gapfilling]], [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]], and [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]] [src: annotation_gap_discovery].
