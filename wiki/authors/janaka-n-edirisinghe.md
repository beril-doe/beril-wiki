# Janaka N. Edirisinghe

ORCID: [0000-0003-2493-234X](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-2493-234X)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/annotation_gap_discovery__REPORT]] project integrated metabolic-model gapfilling, Fitness Browser phenotypes, pangenome annotations, GapMind pathway evidence, and BLAST homology across 14 organisms and 18 carbon sources, using flux-balance analysis (FBA) to evaluate draft models and confidence-score candidate genes for gapfilled reactions. [src: annotation_gap_discovery] The project examined 201 gapfilled enzymatic reaction–organism pairs and assigned candidate genes to 96 (47.8%), including 44 high-confidence, 19 medium-confidence, and 33 low-confidence assignments, while 105 pairs (52.2%) remained unresolved. [src: annotation_gap_discovery] Leave-one-out analysis found that the full pipeline resolved 96 pairs (47.8%), compared with 86 (42.8%) without NB03 EC matching, 80 (39.8%) without NB04 Bakta annotations, 73 (36.3%) without NB06 BLAST, and 70 (34.8%) with BLAST alone. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]

The project found that resolution rates ranged from 20% in *Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron* to 71.4% in *Klebsiella michiganensis*, and that reactions rxn02185 and rxn03436 were each resolved with high confidence in 9 of 14 organisms. [src: annotation_gap_discovery] It found that only 8 of 50 dark reactions lacking ModelSEED EC numbers were resolved (16%), compared with 88 of 151 reactions with known EC numbers (58.3%). [src: annotation_gap_discovery] The project compared 104 GapMind–gapfill pathway pairings and found frequent pathway-level incompleteness where ModelSEED required gapfilling, but limited exact concordance because the available GapMind data reported step counts rather than individual step identities. [src: annotation_gap_discovery] It identified 154 BLAST hits from 328 reviewed bacterial Swiss-Prot exemplar sequences covering 75 of 84 unique ECs, with resolved cases clustering around the stated high-confidence thresholds of at least 30% identity, at least 70% coverage, and an e-value of at most 1e-10. [src: annotation_gap_discovery]
