# Dileep Kishore

## Contributions

The [[summaries/pathway_capability_dependency__REPORT]] project crossed GapMind pathway-completeness predictions with RB-TnSeq fitness data from the Fitness Browser to classify 161 organism–pathway combinations across 7 model bacteria and found 57 Active Dependency pairs (35.4%), 66 Latent Capability pairs (41.0%), 24 Incomplete but Important pairs (14.9%), and 14 Missing pairs (8.7%). [src: pathway_capability_dependency] The project found that all 66 Latent Capability pairs became fitness-important under at least one tested condition type, with nitrogen limitation, stress, and carbon limitation the most frequent triggers, while cautioning that median-based thresholds can cause reclassification by construction. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] It also found mean core gene completeness of 0.986 for Active Dependencies and 0.975 for Latent Capabilities. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

The [[summaries/pathway_capability_dependency__REPORT]] project analyzed 2,810 GTDB species with at least 10 genomes and found that variable pathway count correlated with pangenome openness (raw Spearman rho=0.327, p=7.2e-71; partial Spearman rho=0.530 controlling for genome count, p=2.83e-203). [src: pathway_capability_dependency] The project found a positive pathway-variation–openness signal in 13 of 18 genera, significant at p<0.05 in 5 genera: Clostridium, Eubacterium, Mesorhizobium, Pseudomonas, and Streptomyces. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] It compared core-only with all-gene GapMind completeness and found the largest accessory-dependent gaps in amino acid biosynthesis, including gaps of 0.146 for leucine and valine, 0.141 for arginine, 0.140 for lysine, and 0.140 for threonine. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]
