# Dileep Kishore

## Contributions

The project [[summaries/pathway_capability_dependency__REPORT]] crossed GapMind pathway-completeness predictions with random barcode transposon sequencing (RB-TnSeq) fitness data from the Fitness Browser. Only 7 of the 48 Fitness Browser organisms have matching GapMind data. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] Across those 7 organisms, 23 GapMind pathways had sufficient gene-level annotation, giving 161 organism-pathway pairs. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] Only 35.4% (57/161) of the pairs were Active Dependencies. 41.0% (66/161) were Latent Capabilities, 14.9% (24/161) were Incomplete but Important, and 8.7% (14/161) were Missing. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] The importance threshold is median-based, so the category split is partly built in. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

Stratifying fitness effects by condition type showed that all 66 Latent Capability pairs become fitness-important under at least one condition type. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] Nitrogen limitation, stress and carbon limitation most often triggered reclassification. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] The report cautions that applying the median threshold to each condition-type subset may let some pathways inevitably cross it. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] Active Dependencies had mean core gene completeness of 0.986, against 0.975 for Latent Capabilities. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

The pan-bacterial analysis covered 2,810 GTDB species with at least 10 genomes and 80 GapMind pathways (18 amino acid biosynthesis and 62 carbon source utilization). [src: pathway_capability_dependency] The raw Spearman correlation between pathway variability and pangenome openness was rho=0.327 (p=7.2e-71, n=2,810 species). [src: pathway_capability_dependency] After controlling for genome count, the partial Spearman correlation was rho=0.530 (p=2.83e-203). [src: pathway_capability_dependency] Within genera the signal was weaker. It was significant at p<0.05 in 5 of 18 genera tested and positive in 13 of 18. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] It was strongest in Clostridium (rho=0.534) and Eubacterium (rho=0.506). [src: pathway_capability_dependency]

Comparing GapMind runs on core pangenome genes only against all genes showed that amino acid biosynthesis pathways had the largest gaps. [src: pathway_capability_dependency] Leucine and valine each had 0.614 with all genes against 0.468 with core only, a gap of 0.146. Arginine had 0.613 against 0.472, a gap of 0.141. [src: pathway_capability_dependency]
