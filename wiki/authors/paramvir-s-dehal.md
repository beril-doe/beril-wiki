# Paramvir S. Dehal

ORCID: [0000-0001-5810-2497](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5810-2497)

## Contributions

[[summaries/acinetobacter_adp1_explorer__REPORT]] analyzed a SQLite database of 15 tables, 461,522 rows and 135 MB for *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 and 13 related genomes, and found that no single gene of the 5,852 in `genome_features` has data across all six modalities, although pairwise overlaps are substantial [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. [[summaries/adp1_triple_essentiality__REPORT]] compared flux balance analysis (FBA, constraint-based prediction of metabolic fluxes), TnSeq (transposon-insertion sequencing), knockout and growth data, and found that all 478 triple-covered genes were TnSeq-dispensable [src: adp1_triple_essentiality]. [[summaries/adp1_deletion_phenotypes__REPORT]] analyzed 2,034 deletion mutants across 8 carbon sources and found mean growth ratios spanning a 3.3-fold range, from 0.41 on urea to 1.36 on quinate [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]. In that analysis, 97.9% of genes showed severe defects (growth ratio < 0.5) on urea, and only 1.6% were defective on quinate [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]. [[summaries/aromatic_catabolism_network__REPORT]] defined a 51-gene quinate support network around the β-ketoadipate pathway and, by co-fitness, assigned 44/51 genes (86%) to four functional subsystems plus 6 transcriptional regulators, with the regulators counted within the 44-gene total and 7 genes left unassigned because of low co-fitness with all subsystems [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]. [[summaries/respiratory_chain_wiring__REPORT]] mapped 62 respiratory-chain genes across 8 subsystems and found that quinate requires only Complex I, whereas lactate specifically requires cytochrome bo3 [src: respiratory_chain_wiring].

[[summaries/conservation_vs_fitness__REPORT]] mapped 44 of 48 Fitness Browser organisms to pangenome clades, producing 177,863 gene-to-cluster links, and found 27,693 putative essential genes (18.6%) across 33 organisms [src: conservation_vs_fitness]. [[summaries/fitness_effects_conservation__REPORT]] and [[summaries/conservation_fitness_synthesis__REPORT]] reported a modest conservation gradient across 194,216 genes in 43 organisms, with essential genes 82% core and always-neutral genes 66% core [src: fitness_effects_conservation, conservation_fitness_synthesis]. The synthesis also found that 24.4% of core genes, compared with 19.9% of accessory genes, show positive fitness when deleted [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis]. [[summaries/core_gene_tradeoffs__REPORT]] found that this burden pattern is function-specific: it runs +12.9 percentage points for RNA Metabolism but -14.1 percentage points for Cell Wall [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]. [[summaries/costly_dispensable_genes__REPORT]] characterized 5,526 costly and dispensable genes and found them dominated by mobile elements and poorly annotated DNA, though provenance was not measured directly [src: costly_dispensable_genes]. [[summaries/cog_analysis__REPORT]] found that novel or singleton genes were enriched in COG (Clusters of Orthologous Groups) category L by +10.88% across 32 species [src: cog_analysis]. [[summaries/essential_genome__REPORT]] identified 15 gene families essential in all 48 organisms and found that 859 of 17,222 ortholog families (5.0%) were universally essential [src: essential_genome]. [[summaries/fitness_modules__REPORT]] applied independent component analysis (ICA, a statistical decomposition into independent signals) across 32 organisms and found that it recovers co-regulated process modules, while ortholog transfer remains the stronger gene-level predictor [src: fitness_modules]. [[summaries/module_conservation__REPORT]] found that module genes were 86.0% core, compared with 81.5% for all genes (odds ratio, OR=1.46, p=1.6e-87) [src: module_conservation]. [[summaries/cofitness_coinheritance__REPORT]] found that pairwise co-fitness only weakly predicts co-occurrence, and the across-organism Wilcoxon test was not significant (W=9, p=0.13) [src: cofitness_coinheritance].

[[summaries/amr_pangenome_atlas__REPORT]] found that 30.3% of antimicrobial-resistance (AMR) genes were core, compared with 46.8% for the baseline (OR=0.49) [src: amr_pangenome_atlas]. [[summaries/amr_fitness_cost__REPORT]] found a pooled baseline AMR fitness cost of +0.086 [95% CI: +0.074, +0.098] across 25 organisms, of which only two remained significant after false discovery rate (FDR, the expected share of false positives among significant calls) correction [src: amr_fitness_cost]. [[summaries/amr_environmental_resistome__REPORT]] found that clinical species carry a median of 5 AMR clusters, compared with 2 for soil, aquatic and host-associated species, though the study is observational and subject to sampling, classification and annotation limitations [src: amr_environmental_resistome]. [[summaries/amr_strain_variation__REPORT]] found that 51.3% of 37,444 AMR gene-species records were rare, present in <=5% of strains within a species [src: amr_strain_variation]. [[summaries/amr_cofitness_networks__REPORT]] found that AMR-containing ICA modules had a median of 46 genes, compared with 27 for non-AMR modules, and cautioned that enrichments may reflect shared dispensability [src: amr_cofitness_networks]. [[summaries/metal_fitness_atlas__REPORT]] found that metal-important genes were 87.4% core, compared with 76.9% for baseline genes (OR=2.08, p=4.3e-162) [src: metal_fitness_atlas]. [[summaries/metal_cross_resistance__REPORT]] found that 98.1% (311/317) of gene-level metal fitness correlations were positive [src: metal_cross_resistance]. [[summaries/metal_specificity__REPORT]] classified 4,177 of 7,609 metal-important records (54.9%) as metal-specific, meaning significant fitness defects under metal stress but a <5% sick rate across 5,945 non-metal experiments; the fraction was approximately 41% at a 2% threshold and approximately 67% at 10% [src: metal_specificity]. That analysis could not process seven of 31 metal-tested organisms because their locusIds did not match the fitness-matrix index format, so the 24 included organisms accounted for 7,609 of 12,838 metal-important gene records (59.3%) [src: metal_specificity]. [[summaries/counter_ion_effects__REPORT]] found that 39.8% of metal-important records were also NaCl-important, but found no evidence that chloride dose is the primary driver of the overlap [src: counter_ion_effects]. [[summaries/bacdive_metal_validation__REPORT]] found that heavy-metal-site isolates scored Cohen's d = +1.00 above the baseline (p=0.006), but the n=10 group makes this estimate imprecise [src: bacdive_metal_validation]. [[summaries/bacdive_phenotype_metal_tolerance__REPORT]] found that 7 of 10 BacDive phenotype features were significant after FDR correction [src: bacdive_phenotype_metal_tolerance].

[[summaries/lab_field_ecology__REPORT]] found that 14 of 26 Fitness Browser genera were detected at Oak Ridge and that five genera were significantly associated with uranium, while aggregate tolerance was only suggestive [src: lab_field_ecology]. [[summaries/field_vs_lab_fitness__REPORT]] found that the deficits for antibiotic and heavy-metal genes in *Desulfovibrio vulgaris* Hildenborough were not significant after FDR correction, which leaves resistance accessory-enrichment as a single-organism hypothesis [src: field_vs_lab_fitness]. [[summaries/enigma_contamination_functional_potential__REPORT]] found, in confirmatory genus-level tests across 108 samples, no robust monotonic association between contamination and broad functional scores, while exploratory coverage-aware models detected positive defense associations that were sensitive to coverage, covariate specification, taxonomic resolution and multiple-testing correction [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential]. [[summaries/ecotype_analysis__REPORT]] found a median environment partial correlation of 0.0025, compared with 0.0143 for phylogeny, across 172 species [src: ecotype_analysis]. [[summaries/ecotype_env_reanalysis__REPORT]] found that environmental species did not show stronger correlations than human-associated species (median 0.051 versus 0.084) [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis]. [[summaries/pangenome_openness__REPORT]] detected no significant relationship between openness and environment effects (rho = -0.05, p = 0.54) [src: pangenome_openness]. [[summaries/env_embedding_explorer__REPORT]] characterized AlphaEarth embeddings for 83,287 genomes, 28.4% of 293,059 [src: env_embedding_explorer]. [[summaries/paperblast_explorer__REPORT]] found that *Homo sapiens* accounts for 46.7% of PaperBLAST gene-paper records [src: paperblast_explorer]. [[summaries/webofmicrobes_explorer__REPORT]] characterized a 2018 Web of Microbes snapshot and flagged several internally inconsistent counts [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]. [[summaries/fw300_metabolic_consistency__REPORT]] found that 17/21 testable metabolites (81%) were fully concordant across databases for *Pseudomonas* FW300-N2E3 [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]. [[summaries/essential_metabolome__REPORT]] found that 17 of 18 amino-acid pathways were present in all 7 organisms, in a pilot limited to 7 of 45 organisms [src: essential_metabolome]. [[summaries/pathway_capability_dependency__REPORT]] classified 161 organism-pathway pairs and found 57 (35.4%) Active Dependency pairs, complete pathways containing fitness-important genes, and 66 (41.0%) Latent Capability pairs, complete pathways whose genes showed no significant fitness defects under standard conditions [src: pathway_capability_dependency].
