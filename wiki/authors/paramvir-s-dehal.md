# Paramvir S. Dehal

ORCID: [0000-0001-5810-2497](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5810-2497)

## Contributions

Several projects analyzed *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 using integrated multi-omics data. [[summaries/acinetobacter_adp1_explorer__REPORT]] characterized a SQLite database of 15 tables, 461,522 rows and 135 MB covering ADP1 and 13 related genomes, and found that four of five tested connection types matched KBase Data Lakehouse collections at greater than 90% — 13/13 genome IDs, 1,210/1,330 reactions, 230/230 compounds, and 4,891/4,891 cluster IDs — while ADP1 was absent from the Fitness Browser with 0 matches [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. [[summaries/adp1_triple_essentiality__REPORT]] compared flux balance analysis (FBA), TnSeq, knockout, growth and proteomics data and found that FBA class was not associated with growth-defect status among 478 triple-covered genes (chi-squared = 0.93, p = 0.63, 2 df), while FBA showed moderate concordance with knockout lethality in minimal media (recall = 65.6%, precision = 69.2%, Cohen's kappa = 0.493); it also reported that RB-TnSeq (random barcode transposon sequencing) essentiality disagreed with knockout essentiality in rich media, with Cohen's kappa = -0.081 at the 0.05 threshold across 1,933 genes [src: adp1_triple_essentiality]. [[summaries/adp1_deletion_phenotypes__REPORT]] analyzed a 2,034-gene × 8-carbon-source growth matrix and found a three-tier essentiality landscape spanning mean growth ratios from 0.41 on urea to 1.36 on quinate, with 5 principal components capturing 82% of variance and a single discrete 24-gene module of quinate-specific aromatic-degradation genes (mean quinate z-score -7.28) [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes]. [[summaries/aromatic_catabolism_network__REPORT]] defined a 51-gene quinate-catabolism support network in which Complex I accounted for 21/51 genes (41%) and 30/51 genes had no FBA reaction mappings, with FBA capturing 1.76× higher Complex I flux on aromatic substrates (0.55 versus 0.31) yet predicting 0% essentiality [src: aromatic_catabolism_network]. [[summaries/respiratory_chain_wiring__REPORT]] mapped 62 respiratory-chain genes across 8 subsystems and found condition-specific configurations — quinate requiring only Complex I, glucose requiring no specific component, and ACIAD3522 showing a 0.013 growth ratio on acetate versus 1.39 on quinate and glucose [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]. These projects feed [[concepts/gene-essentiality]] and [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]].

A second group of projects linked Fitness Browser fitness measurements to pangenome conservation. [[summaries/conservation_vs_fitness__REPORT]] mapped 44 of 48 Fitness Browser organisms to pangenome species clades, producing 177,863 gene-to-cluster links, and found essential genes were 86.1% core versus 81.2% for non-essential genes (median odds ratio 1.56), with 18 of 33 organisms significant after Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction [src: conservation_vs_fitness]. [[summaries/fitness_effects_conservation__REPORT]] and [[summaries/conservation_fitness_synthesis__REPORT]] quantified a conservation gradient across 194,216 protein-coding genes from 43 bacteria in which essential genes were 82% core and always-neutral genes 66% core, and reported a core-genome burden pattern in which 24.4% of core genes showed positive fitness when deleted versus 19.9% of accessory genes [src: fitness_effects_conservation, conservation_fitness_synthesis]. [[summaries/core_gene_tradeoffs__REPORT]] identified 25,271 trade-off genes (17.8%, OR=1.29, p=1.2e-44) and a selection-signature matrix of 28,017 Costly+Conserved, 5,526 Costly+Dispensable, 86,761 Neutral+Conserved and 21,886 Neutral+Dispensable genes, with burden differences of +12.9 percentage points for RNA Metabolism and -14.1 for Cell Wall genes [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]. [[summaries/costly_dispensable_genes__REPORT]] characterized the 5,526 costly+dispensable genes as 7.45× enriched for mobile-element keywords (p=4.6e-71), 44.5% orphan genes, and shorter at median 615 bp versus 765 bp [src: costly_dispensable_genes]. [[summaries/fitness_modules__REPORT]] used independent component analysis (ICA) to identify 1,116 stable modules across 32 organisms with 94.2% showing elevated within-module cofitness and 22.7× genomic-adjacency enrichment, while held-out benchmarking gave ortholog transfer 95.8% strict precision versus <1% for Module-ICA [src: fitness_modules]. [[summaries/module_conservation__REPORT]] found module genes were 86.0% core versus an 81.5% baseline (OR=1.46, p=1.6e-87), that 577/974 modules (59%) were >90% core, and that zero essential genes appeared in any module [src: module_conservation]. [[summaries/cofitness_coinheritance__REPORT]] tested 2,253,491 cofit pairs against 22,534,910 random pairs across 9 organisms, finding a mean delta phi of +0.011 pairwise and stronger module-level co-inheritance (mean delta +0.053 across 195 modules; accessory modules +0.108) [src: cofitness_coinheritance]. [[summaries/essential_genome__REPORT]] analyzed 221,005 genes from 48 bacteria and found 15 gene families essential in all 48 organisms, 859/17,222 ortholog families (5.0%) universally essential, and 7,084 orphan essential genes that were 49.5% core versus 91.7% for universally essential genes [src: essential_genome]. [[summaries/cog_analysis__REPORT]] examined 357,623 genes across 32 species and found novel/singleton genes enriched in COG L (mobile elements) by +10.88% with 100% consistency [src: cog_analysis].

Further projects addressed antimicrobial resistance, metal stress, and environment–genome relationships. [[summaries/amr_pangenome_atlas__REPORT]] identified 83,008 AMRFinderPlus hits across 14,723 species and found AMR genes only 30.3% core versus a 46.8% baseline (OR=0.49), with beta-lactamases 54.9% core and regulatory genes 6.5% core [src: amr_pangenome_atlas]. [[summaries/amr_fitness_cost__REPORT]] meta-analyzed 1,352 AMR genes across 43 organisms and found a pooled baseline fitness shift of +0.086 [95% CI +0.074, +0.098], z = 14.3, with 25/25 organisms positive and a mechanism-dependent antibiotic flip of +0.094 for efflux versus −0.001 for enzymatic inactivation [src: amr_fitness_cost]. [[summaries/amr_environmental_resistome__REPORT]] found clinical species carried a median of 5 AMR clusters versus 2 for soil, aquatic and host-associated species (H = 781.9, p = 9.4×10⁻¹⁶⁷), and that metal resistance made up 45.0% of aquatic AMR but 6.1% of human-gut AMR [src: amr_environmental_resistome]. [[summaries/amr_strain_variation__REPORT]] analyzed 1,305 species and 180,025 genomes, finding 51.3% of AMR gene-species records rare and 1,517 resistance islands across 705 species with mean phi = 0.827 [src: amr_strain_variation]. [[summaries/amr_cofitness_networks__REPORT]] found 192/801 AMR genes assigned to ICA modules, AMR-containing modules larger at median 46 versus 27 genes (p = 1.7×10⁻⁸), and InterProScan GO annotations yielding 35/3,193 significant enrichment tests versus 0/280 with SEED/KEGG [src: amr_cofitness_networks]. On metals, [[summaries/metal_fitness_atlas__REPORT]] combined 559 experiments across 31 organisms and 16 metals and found metal-important genes 87.4% core versus 76.9% baseline (OR=2.08, p=4.3e-162), with essential metals showing a mean core delta of +0.148 versus +0.081 for toxic metals [src: metal_fitness_atlas]; [[summaries/metal_cross_resistance__REPORT]] found 311/317 organism-metal-pair correlations positive (98.1%), strongest for Fe-Zn (r = 0.61) and Co-Ni (r = 0.56, n = 28) [src: metal_cross_resistance]; [[summaries/metal_specificity__REPORT]] classified 4,177/7,609 metal-important gene records (54.9%) as metal-specific and found them less core-enriched than general sick genes (Cochran-Mantel-Haenszel p=0.011) [src: metal_specificity]; and [[summaries/counter_ion_effects__REPORT]] found 39.8% metal–NaCl overlap but no chloride-dose explanation, since zinc sulfate with 0 mM chloride showed 44.6% overlap [src: counter_ion_effects]. Validation and field-linkage projects included [[summaries/bacdive_metal_validation__REPORT]], which matched 42,227 of 97,334 BacDive strains and found heavy-metal-contamination isolates scoring Cohen's d = +1.00 above baseline (p=0.006, n=10) [src: bacdive_metal_validation]; [[summaries/bacdive_phenotype_metal_tolerance__REPORT]], which found taxonomy alone explained 35.4% of metal tolerance variance with 7 of 10 phenotype features significant after FDR [src: bacdive_phenotype_metal_tolerance]; [[summaries/lab_field_ecology__REPORT]], which tested 11 Fitness Browser genera across 108 Oak Ridge sites and found five significant uranium associations including *Caulobacter* (rho=-0.411) and *Herbaspirillum* (rho=+0.336), with the aggregate tolerance correlation non-significant (rho=0.503, p=0.095, n=12) [src: lab_field_ecology]; [[summaries/field_vs_lab_fitness__REPORT]], which classified 757 *Desulfovibrio vulgaris* Hildenborough experiments and found field-stress genes 83.6% core (OR=1.58, q=0.026) while heavy-metal genes were 71.2% core [src: field_vs_lab_fitness]; and [[summaries/enigma_contamination_functional_potential__REPORT]], whose confirmatory contamination–defense tests across 108 samples were null (rho = 0.0587, q = 0.862) [src: enigma_contamination_functional_potential]. [[summaries/ecotype_analysis__REPORT]] found phylogeny dominated gene-content signal in 60.5% of 172 species with no significant environment effect in 156 species (90.7%) [src: ecotype_analysis]; [[summaries/ecotype_env_reanalysis__REPORT]] tested and rejected clinical sampling bias as the explanation (U=1536, p=0.83) [src: ecotype_env_reanalysis]; [[summaries/pangenome_openness__REPORT]] found no relationship between openness and environment (rho = -0.05, p = 0.54) or phylogeny (rho = 0.03, p = 0.73) effects [src: pangenome_openness]; and [[summaries/env_embedding_explorer__REPORT]] characterized AlphaEarth embeddings for 83,287 genomes, reporting a 3.4× geographic gradient for environmental samples versus 2.0× for human-associated samples [src: env_embedding_explorer]. Database-integration projects included [[summaries/paperblast_explorer__REPORT]], which found *Homo sapiens* accounting for 46.7% of gene-paper records and an organism-level Gini coefficient of 0.967 [src: paperblast_explorer]; [[summaries/webofmicrobes_explorer__REPORT]], which documented WoM action semantics across 10,744 observations and two direct Fitness Browser strain matches [src: webofmicrobes_explorer]; [[summaries/fw300_metabolic_consistency__REPORT]], which found 17/21 metabolites (81%) fully concordant across four databases with tryptophan the strongest discordance [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]; [[summaries/essential_metabolome__REPORT]], which found 17 of 18 amino-acid biosynthesis pathways complete in all 7 organisms with serine absent in DvH [src: essential_metabolome]; and [[summaries/pathway_capability_dependency__REPORT]], which classified 161 organism-pathway pairs as 35.4% Active Dependency and 41.0% Latent Capability [src: pathway_capability_dependency].
