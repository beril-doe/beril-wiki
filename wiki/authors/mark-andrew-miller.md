## Contributions

[[summaries/nmdc_context_audit__REPORT]] audited KBase Data Lakehouse resources whose names contain `nmdc` and found that 20 database names resolve to 7 real, maintained resources across three tenants and six provenance classes, so the label does not reliably mark a dataset boundary [src: nmdc_context_audit]. The audit found that two of the four databases in the `nmdc` tenant are external re-hosts: an NCBI BioSample harvest with 51,711,888 biosamples, and 27,481 Pfam terms [src: nmdc_context_audit]. It also found that `kbase.nmdc_neon` represents NSF's NEON rather than NMDC [src: nmdc_context_audit]. It found that the freshest NMDC resource, `kbase.nmdc_mags` with 62,346 MAGs (metagenome-assembled genomes), sits in the `kbase` tenant with nothing cross-linking it to the `nmdc` tenant [src: nmdc_context_audit]. It identified a ~3,000× scale trap between the 16,640 genuine NMDC samples and the co-hosted NCBI mirror [src: nmdc_context_audit]. It found that Iceberg snapshot ages span approximately four months across NMDC-labeled resources and that this currency signal is not exposed at discovery [src: nmdc_context_audit]. The audit delivered a 15-file Open-Knowledge-Format directory and proposed, but did not apply, seven fixes [src: nmdc_context_audit]. The audit did not directly observe any user choosing the wrong resource, so its link between label overload and sub-optimal selection is inferred [src: nmdc_context_audit]. Its provenance classes are inferred without an ingestion manifest [src: nmdc_context_audit]. These findings feed [[concepts/provenance-aware-resource-discovery]] [src: nmdc_context_audit].

[[summaries/euk_in_prok_correlates__REPORT]] quantified eukaryotic read fractions across 2,759 NMDC ReadbasedAnalysis runs from 9 studies using GOTTCHA2 [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. It found eukaryotic reads in 77% of runs, a median fraction of 2.7% and a mean of 13.3%, and that plastid sequences made up a median 100% of detected eukaryotic signal [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. The project found that Kraken2 and Centrifuge produced approximately 0 Eukaryota signal because their NMDC reference databases were prokaryote-restricted [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. GOTTCHA2 was therefore the only usable estimator, and its values are relative rather than calibrated absolute contamination [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. It found that eukaryotic fraction differed by matrix (Kruskal–Wallis H=77.8, p=1.3×10⁻¹⁷) [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. However, each biome was approximately 80–100% nested within a single study, and an environment model scored R²=−0.30 under GroupKFold out-of-study validation (cross-validation that holds out complete studies as groups), with an out-of-study detection AUC (area under the ROC curve) of 0.56, approximately chance [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. The report's statement that environment explained no more variance than `study_id` alone conflicts with its own random cross-validation (CV) table, which gives environment R²=0.35 against `study_id`-only R²=0.24 [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. This inconsistency was left unresolved [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. Within the NEON soil study of 1,186 runs, the project found that local vegetation (H=119.1, p=7.6×10⁻²¹) and geography across 47 sites (H=310.4, p=2.4×10⁻⁴⁶) were predictive, with within-study R²=+0.17 ± 0.06 [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. These within-study signals remain subject to possible sub-batch confounding and were shown for one soil study only [src: euk_in_prok_correlates].

[[summaries/pseudomonas_carbon_ecology__REPORT]] analyzed GapMind carbon-pathway predictions for 12,732 genomes across 433 *Pseudomonas* species clades (GTDB r214) [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. Comparing 7 *Pseudomonas* s.s. species and 189 *Pseudomonas_E* species with at least 5 genomes, it found that 43 of 62 pathways differed significantly (q < 0.05), including xylose completeness of 0.0% versus 74.4% and arabinose of 0.0% versus 62.6% [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. Among 54 free-living and plant-associated species with at least 5 genomes and at least 60% majority-environment agreement, the project found a significant but modest ecological signal, with a permutation test giving p = 0.006 [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. A Random Forest classifier trained on 51 species in four environment classes reached a balanced accuracy of 0.408 +/- 0.169 against a 0.250 chance baseline, and the report judged ecology prediction only partially supported [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. It found that the primary axis of a principal component analysis (PCA) separated the two subgenera rather than lifestyles and noted that the analyses do not explicitly control for phylogenetic non-independence [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. The report also flagged two further limitations: *P. aeruginosa* comprised 53% of genomes (6,760/12,732), and GapMind omits aromatic degradation pathways [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. It treated the link between pathway loss and a transition to animal hosts as an untested hypothesis [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology].
