## Contributions

The [[summaries/nmdc_context_audit__REPORT]] project audited KBase Data Lakehouse resources whose names contain `nmdc` and found that 20 NMDC-named database entries resolve to 7 real, maintained resources spread across three tenants and six provenance classes, including genuine NMDC resources (`nmdc.metadata`, `nmdc.results`), an NCBI re-host, a Pfam re-host, an Arkin Lab derivative, an NMDC-derived MAG catalog, and a namesake collision (`kbase.nmdc_neon`) that represents the NSF National Ecological Observatory Network rather than the DOE-BER National Microbiome Data Collaborative [src: nmdc_context_audit]. It measured the resulting scale trap directly: the genuine NMDC biosample universe holds 16,640 samples in `nmdc.metadata.biosample_set` while the co-hosted NCBI mirror holds 51,711,888 biosamples and 756,112,544 attribute rows, a difference the report describes as ~3,000× [src: nmdc_context_audit]. The audit also found that the freshest NMDC resource, `kbase.nmdc_mags` with 62,346 MAGs (metagenome-assembled genomes), sits in the `kbase` tenant rather than the `nmdc` one, that Iceberg snapshot ages span roughly four months (latest commits `2026-07-02` for `kbase.nmdc_mags` and `kbase.nmdc_neon` through `2026-03-09` for `nmdc.ncbi_biosamples`), and that `DESCRIBE DATABASE EXTENDED kbase.nmdc_*` raises `ForbiddenException` for a `kesciencero`/`microbialdiscoveryforge` principal even where `COUNT(*)` on the same tables succeeds [src: nmdc_context_audit]. Its deliverable was a 15-file Open-Knowledge-Format directory, and it proposed but did not apply seven documentation and tooling fixes; the report states plainly that it never observed a user choosing the wrong resource, so its claim that label overload drives sub-optimal selection remains inferred from gap analysis and prior-project reuse skew [src: nmdc_context_audit]. These findings slot into [[concepts/cross-tenant-data-bridging]] [src: nmdc_context_audit].

The [[summaries/euk_in_prok_correlates__REPORT]] project quantified eukaryotic read fractions across 2,759 NMDC ReadbasedAnalysis runs from 9 studies using native `nmdc.results` GOTTCHA2 classifications and found eukaryotic reads in 77% of runs, with a median eukaryotic fraction of 2.7%, a mean of 13.3%, 23% of runs at zero, and 20% of runs exceeding 20% eukaryotic reads; among runs with detectable signal, plastid sequences were a median 100% of that signal [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. It reported that Kraken2 and Centrifuge returned approximately zero domain-level Eukaryota because their NMDC reference databases were prokaryote-restricted, leaving GOTTCHA2 the only usable estimator [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. The project's central methodological result was that cross-study environment associations are batch-confounded: each biome was approximately 80–100% nested within a single study, and a gradient-boosted model gave R²=0.24 for `study_id` alone and R²=0.35 for environment under random cross-validation but R²=−0.30 for environment under GroupKFold out-of-study validation (holding whole studies out as groups), with out-of-study detection AUC of 0.56 [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. Within the batch-controlled NEON soil study of 1,186 runs, local vegetation was associated with eukaryotic fraction at H=119.1, p=7.6×10⁻²¹ and site geography across 47 sites at H=310.4, p=2.4×10⁻⁴⁶, with within-study five-fold R²=+0.17 ± 0.06 [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. The analysis was run at `workflow_run_id` level because 1,067 of the 2,759 runs were pooled from multiple biosamples, and the report flags that NMDC did not populate DNA-extraction kit, filtration, host-depletion, or library-prep fields, leaving the strongest literature-linked wet-lab factors as unmeasured residuals [src: euk_in_prok_correlates]. It slots into [[concepts/environment-embedding-geography]] and [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]] [src: euk_in_prok_correlates].

The [[summaries/pseudomonas_carbon_ecology__REPORT]] project analyzed GapMind carbon-pathway predictions from the `kbase_ke_pangenome` collection across 12,732 genomes and 433 *Pseudomonas* species clades (GTDB r214) and found that 43 of 62 pathways differed significantly between *Pseudomonas* s.s. and *Pseudomonas_E* by Mann-Whitney U testing with Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction (q < 0.05), with the largest gaps in plant-derived sugars — xylose 0.0% versus 74.4%, ribose 27.9% versus 92.0%, arabinose 0.0% versus 62.6%, and myo-inositol 0.0% versus 58.8% — while amino-acid and core organic-acid pathways stayed above 99% in both groups [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. It found carbon profiles carried a statistically significant but modest ecological signal among 54 free-living and plant-associated species: a 999-permutation PERMANOVA-like test gave p = 0.006 with a between/within distance ratio of 1.087, and a Random Forest over four environment classes reached balanced accuracy 0.408 +/- 0.169 against a 0.250 chance baseline, with D-serine (importance 0.132) and arabinose (0.094) most discriminating [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. The report identified the deep subgenus division, not lifestyle, as the dominant PCA axis, noted that *P. aeruginosa* comprised 53% of genomes (6,760/12,732) and that isolation-source metadata were classifiable for 64.2% of genomes, and proposed PGLS (phylogenetic generalized least squares, regression accounting for relatedness) on the GTDB tree plus cross-reference with RB-TnSeq fitness data as controls it did not itself apply [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]. These results slot into [[concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content]] and [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] [src: pseudomonas_carbon_ecology].
