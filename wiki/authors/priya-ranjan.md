# Priya Ranjan

ORCID: [0000-0002-0357-1939](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0357-1939)

## Contributions

[[summaries/pgp_pangenome_ecology__REPORT]] analyzed plant-growth-promoting (PGP) gene distribution across the KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome, covering 27,702 total species of which 11,272 carried at least one of 13 PGP gene markers, 32,736 PGP gene clusters, 27,690 species with GapMind pathway scores, and 291,279 genomes with isolation-source metadata [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. The project tested co-occurrence among 10 focal-gene pairs and found 8 significant after Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate (BH-FDR) correction — five positive, three negative — with the strongest positive association being pqqC × acdS (OR = 7.24, n = 286 co-occurring species, q = 1.2e-83); pqqC also co-occurred with hcnC (OR = 1.91) and ipdC (OR = 1.55) [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. It found nifH negatively associated with hcnC (OR = 0.23, q = 5.8e-29) and pqqC (OR = 0.57, q = 2.9e-19) and not significantly associated with ipdC (OR = 1.13, q = 0.54), reporting that only 157 species (1.4%) carried at least three focal traits, with pqqC + acdS (n = 153) and nifH + pqqC (n = 225) as the most common multi-trait genotypes [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology].

The same project compared 1,039 soil/rhizosphere species with 10,233 species from other environments and found acdS, pqqC, and hcnC significantly soil-enriched after BH-FDR correction: acdS at 15.8% versus 2.6% prevalence (OR = 7.02, q = 5.1e-62), pqqC at 43.8% versus 21.2% (OR = 2.90, q = 2.8e-53), and hcnC at 11.3% versus 6.4% (OR = 1.85, q = 6.1e-08), while nifH was depleted (12.9% versus 19.7%, OR = 0.60, q = 5.5e-08) and ipdC was not significantly enriched (1.5% versus 1.9%, OR = 0.79, q = 0.47) [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. It showed the acdS enrichment persisted under phylum-level fixed effects (OR = 6.98, p = 4.8e-61) and in a strict rhizosphere-only sensitivity analysis (OR = 10.6, q = 7.6e-38), with Bacillota_A an exception for nifH (OR = ∞, q = 2.5e-4) [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. The report recorded that environment classification was conservative and noisy — only 1,637 species (5.9% of species with an environment label) were classified as soil/rhizosphere dominant, with 93.5% of the 291,279 metadata-bearing genomes classifiable — and that NCBI's bias toward clinical and host-associated sampling may cause the acdS and pqqC effects to underestimate true rhizosphere enrichment [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. These results feed [[concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content]] and [[concepts/environmental-resistome]] [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology].

The project also rejected the hypothesis that PGP genes are predominantly horizontally transferred accessory genes: all 13 PGP genes had significantly higher core fractions than the genome-wide baseline of 46.8% core (BH-FDR q < 0.05 for all), including pqqC at 81.5% core (q = 0.0), pqqB at 78.1% (q = 3.0e-247), hcnA at 78.5% (q = 9.2e-37), ipdC at 76.5% (q = 3.4e-19), acdS at 70.4% (q = 7.9e-25), nifH at 63.8% (q = 4.3e-71), and pqqD at 55.5% core with the highest singleton fraction, 27.5% (q = 2.2e-86) [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. It reported a mean PGP accessory fraction of 29.7% versus 53.2% genome-wide, and a negative correlation between singleton fraction and PGP gene richness (Spearman ρ = −0.195, p = 2.0e-97, n = 11,272 species with ≥2 genomes), findings that slot into [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] and [[concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth]] [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. On aromatic-amino-acid metabolism, the project found ipdC present in 2.5% of species with a complete GapMind tryptophan pathway (score ≥ 0.9) versus 0.9% with an incomplete pathway (Fisher OR = 2.81, p = 6.3e-10; logistic OR = 2.81, 95% CI 1.97–4.01, p = 1.4e-08, n = 11,272; OR = 2.87, p = 7.0e-09 with soil status added), but also found a similar tyrosine effect (OR = 3.62, p = 2.3e-11), so the result did not support a tryptophan-specific mechanism [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. The association reversed within soil/rhizosphere species (n = 1,039, OR = 0.30, p = 0.02) while remaining positive in non-soil species (n = 10,233, OR = 3.56, p = 7.7e-13); the report treated the reversal as hypothesis-generating given that ipdC occurred in only 214 of 11,272 species (1.9%), a rarity that also caused a phylum-plus-soil logistic model to fail through quasi-complete separation [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]. Further caveats logged were that PGP detection relied on Bakta exact gene-name matches and could miss product-only or variant annotations, that clusters were not functionally validated for truncations, frameshifts, or pseudogenization, that GapMind completeness may proxy for overall metabolic capacity, and that co-occurrence did not establish physical linkage between pqqC and acdS; the nifH count was 2,756 rather than the ~1,913 estimated in the research plan, reflecting growth in the GTDB r214 pangenome without affecting the analyses [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology].
