# Sierra Moxon

ORCID: [0000-0002-8719-7760](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-8719-7760)

## Contributions

The project [[summaries/metabolic_capability_dependency__REPORT]] quantified latent metabolic capabilities in 48 Fitness Browser organisms. Of 1,695 pathway-organism pairs, 881 (51.9%) were active, 547 (32.3%) intermediate and 267 (15.8%) latent, meaning genomically complete but showing no detectable fitness importance under the conditions tested. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] The latent fraction varied by organism from 0 to 31.6%, with mean 15.0%. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] The latent fraction depends on classification thresholds and on tested conditions. Varying the thresholds by ±25% gave latent fractions of 4.7% to 21.1% (SD = 5.9 percentage points across 16 threshold combinations). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] Pathway category strongly predicted dependency class (χ²=163.6, df=4, p=2.5×10⁻³⁴). Carbon source utilization pathways were 3.7× more likely to be latent than amino acid biosynthesis pathways (24.3% vs 6.5%). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The same project tested the [[entities/black-queen-hypothesis|Black Queen]] hypothesis at two levels. At the pathway level, conservation did not differ between latent and active pathways. Mean conservation was 0.869 for latent and 0.829 for active pathways, with Mann-Whitney U p = 0.94 and rank-biserial r = 0.052. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] At the clade level, latent capability fraction correlated positively with [[entities/pangenome-openness|pangenome openness]] (Spearman ρ = 0.69, p = 0.0004, n = 22 clades). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The project also examined within-species metabolic ecotypes. All 10 target species (≥50 genomes, ≥15 variable pathways) showed metabolic clustering with silhouette scores above 0.2, ranging from 0.35 (PALSA-747 sp.) to 0.89 (*Salmonella enterica*). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] Clusters correlated with isolation environment in [[entities/salmonella-enterica|Salmonella enterica]] (χ²=1570.2, df=25, p<0.0001) and in *Phenylobacterium* sp. (χ²=12.2, df=1, p=0.0005). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] Environment-cluster associations were non-significant in the marine organisms Stutzerimonas, Alteromonas, Pelagibacter and Prochlorococcus. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] The most heterogeneous pathways across species were all amino acid biosynthesis pathways: valine/leucine (heterogeneity=0.60), tryptophan (0.51) and lysine/threonine (0.49). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]
