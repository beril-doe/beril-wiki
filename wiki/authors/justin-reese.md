# Justin Reese

ORCID: [0000-0002-2170-2250](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2170-2250)

## Contributions

The CAZyme pangenome project compared carbohydrate-active enzyme (CAZyme) content across 49K genomes in 18 biomes. All 6 non-zero CAZy classes differed across biomes (Kruskal-Wallis tests, all p < 10^-150 after BH-FDR correction). The effect survived genome-size correction and was significant within all 5 top phyla. [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology] Glycoside hydrolase genes averaged 37.7 in barley-rhizosphere and 28.9 in sheep-rumen genomes, against 9.0 in marine genomes. [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology] Across 9,347 species, CAZyme density correlated only modestly with pangenome cloud fraction (Spearman ρ = 0.153). Genome count strongly confounded cloud fraction (ρ = 0.913), and the partial correlation fell to ρ = 0.101. Only 3 of 10 tested phyla were significant after BH correction. [src: cazyme_pangenome_ecology] Details are in [[summaries/cazyme_pangenome_ecology__REPORT]].

Two projects examined gene-content variation within species. The ecotype functional differentiation project found valid ecotypes in 12 of 15 sampled species, with mean silhouette 0.215. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation] It found 170 of 257 species × COG tests (66.1%) significant after BH-FDR correction. Adaptive categories showed a mean effect 2.1x larger than housekeeping categories (p = 2.53 x 10^-6). The report treats its hypothesis as partly supported, because the significance rates were 79.8% for adaptive and 68.8% for housekeeping categories. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation] See [[summaries/ecotype_functional_differentiation__REPORT]]. The gene copy number project found higher multi-copy rates for adaptive COG categories in 24 of 24 species across 5 phyla, with a median ratio of 8.14× (paired Wilcoxon p = 5.96 × 10⁻⁸). [src: gene_copy_number_variation] The 8× effect fell to 2× when restricted to core clusters. [src: gene_copy_number_variation] See [[summaries/gene_copy_number_variation__REPORT]].

The GC ecotype project tested for an ecological GC signal within species after controlling for ANI clusters. Of 108 species, 40 (37%) showed isolation-source-associated GC variation at FDR < 0.05. Effect sizes were small, with a median max-minus-min GC of 0.15% among the significant species. [src: gc_ecotype_ecology] In the label-permutation null, 39 of 40 species survived. In the continuous phylogenetic regression, 32 of 39 testable species stayed significant. With geographic covariates, 12 of 16 testable species stayed significant. [src: gc_ecotype_ecology] An independent AlphaEarth test found 53 of 131 species significant. Agreement with the isolation-category test was 59% (10 of 17). [src: gc_ecotype_ecology] See [[summaries/gc_ecotype_ecology__REPORT]] and [[concepts/genome-gc-content-as-an-ecological-signal]].

Three projects covered mobile elements and defense. The phage defense arsenal project detected defense systems in 27,626 of 27,690 species-level pangenomes. Defense count correlated with prophage burden after control for genome size and phylum (partial ρ = 0.301), and 27 of 28 tested system pairs co-occurred more than a phylum-preserving null predicts. [src: phage_defense_arsenal] The report says no phylogenetically corrected regression was run. [src: phage_defense_arsenal] See [[summaries/phage_defense_arsenal__REPORT]]. The prophage-AMR co-mobilization project found that proximal AMR instances (≤10 genes from a prophage marker) were 67.6% accessory and distal instances 65.5% (OR=1.10, p=0.005). [src: prophage_amr_comobilization] Across 4,770 species, prophage density and AMR breadth correlated (Spearman rho=0.572). The evidence is correlational, and the fitness-cost hypothesis (H3) could not be tested. [src: prophage_amr_comobilization] See [[summaries/prophage_amr_comobilization__REPORT]].

The Oak Ridge cultivation gap project compared 3,110 cultured isolates with 2,279 subsurface MAGs. Of 10,845 KOs tested, 8,482 (78.2%) differed significantly, with 7,669 enriched in cultured genomes and 813 depleted. [src: oak_ridge_cultivation_gap] The report says genome size and taxonomy drive much of this asymmetry, and it did not run a genome-size-normalized analysis. [src: oak_ridge_cultivation_gap] Four of 5 Wood-Ljungdahl marker KOs were depleted in cultured genomes. NiFe-hydrogenase was instead enriched, contrary to the prediction. [src: oak_ridge_cultivation_gap] See [[summaries/oak_ridge_cultivation_gap__REPORT]].
