## Contributions

The [[summaries/acinetobacter_adp1_explorer__REPORT]] project analyzed a user-provided SQLite database of 15 tables, 461,522 rows, and 135 MB covering *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 and 13 related genomes, and found that its central `genome_features` table holds 5,852 genes with 51 annotation columns across six modalities — TnSeq essentiality at 58% coverage, flux balance analysis (FBA) flux at 15%, mutant growth fitness on 8 carbon sources at 39%, proteomics across 7 strains at 41%, pangenome classification at 54%, and COG/KO/Pfam/UniRef annotations at 34–55% — with no single gene carrying data across all six [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. The project documented 7 engineered ADP1 strains (wild-type plus 6 aromatic-amino-acid-pathway derivatives involving ΔaroF and ΔaroG or dgoA variants), measured protein abundance for 2,383 genes, and reported high cross-strain correlation, indicating targeted rather than global effects of the modifications [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. It also reported that the database covers only *A. baylyi*, so cross-species comparison would require comparable databases for other organisms [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer].

On platform connectivity and pangenome linkage, the project tested five connection types against the KBase Data Lakehouse and found four matching above 90%: 13 of 13 genome IDs to the pangenome, 1,210 of 1,330 reactions to biochemistry, 230 of 230 compounds to biochemistry, and 4,891 of 4,891 cluster IDs to the pangenome via mapping; ADP1 returned 0 matches in 1 Fitness Browser organism query and was absent from that resource [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. It found that all 13 lakehouse-format genomes belong to *s__Acinetobacter_baylyi* and the clade `s__Acinetobacter_baylyi--RS_GCF_000368685.1`, whose pangenome holds 3,207 core and 1,684 accessory clusters, and that the 120 unmatched reactions (9% of 1,330) may be custom or draft reactions absent from ModelSEED [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. Because ADP1's mmseqs2-style cluster IDs and the lakehouse centroid gene IDs share 0% direct string match, the project built a bridge through the `gene_genecluster_junction` table, mapping all 4,891 lakehouse clusters onto 4,081 unique ADP1 clusters for a 100% gene-level match across 43,754 genes, and emitted `data/cluster_id_mapping.csv`; it noted the mapping is indirect, passing through 3 tables, which could create edge cases where clusters split or merge [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. This work feeds [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] and [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]].

On essentiality, fitness, and metabolic modeling, the project compared 866 genes having both FBA flux predictions and TnSeq (transposon-sequencing) essentiality calls and found 639, or 73.8%, concordant and 227 discordant; it found 499 genes essential on minimal media versus 346 on LB, and FBA flux classes changing between rich and minimal media for 177 of 866 genes, or 20% [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. It found essential genes more annotation-rich than dispensable ones (33% versus 5% with COG assignments; 92% versus 53% with KEGG KO assignments), with roughly 8% of essential genes lacking KO assignments and flagged as potential novel essential functions, and essential genes more likely to be core pangenome members [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. Mutant fitness across 8 carbon sources had a mean pairwise correlation of 0.44, with urea nearly uncorrelated with quinate (r = 0.11) and weakly correlated with the rest (r = 0.12–0.28), while butanediol-acetate and butanediol-lactate correlated most strongly (r = 0.58 and r = 0.53) [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer], evidence that slots into [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]] and [[concepts/gene-essentiality]]. In metabolic reconstruction the project found 1,248 of 1,330 reactions (94%) shared across all 14 genomes, 62 variable and 20 genome-unique, gapfilling accounting for 7.7% of reactions on average with 243 missing functions cataloged, and 105,376 of 121,519 growth phenotype predictions (87%) requiring at least one gapfilled reaction — a dependence the report states limits interpretation of those predictions [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. The report listed pathway enrichment of the 227 discordant genes, cross-species fitness comparison, PhageFoundry cross-referencing, urea-specific gene identification, and pangenome-informed gapfill confidence assessment as future rather than completed analyses [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer].
