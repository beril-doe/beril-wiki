## Contributions

The [[summaries/acinetobacter_adp1_explorer__REPORT]] project analyzed a user-provided SQLite database of 15 tables, 461,522 total rows, and 135 MB of data covering *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 and 13 related genomes, and found that its central `genome_features` table holds 5,852 genes and 51 annotation columns spanning six modalities — TnSeq (transposon-insertion sequencing) essentiality at 58% coverage, FBA (flux balance analysis, constraint-based prediction of metabolic fluxes) at 15%, mutant growth fitness on 8 carbon sources at 39%, proteomics across 7 strains at 41%, pangenome classification at 54%, and COG (Clusters of Orthologous Groups), KO (KEGG Orthology), Pfam and UniRef functional annotations at 34-55% [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. The project reported that no single gene carries data across all six modalities, though pairwise overlaps are substantial, particularly among essentiality, pangenome, and proteomics [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. It documented 7 engineered ADP1 strains — wild-type plus 6 derivatives with aromatic amino acid pathway modifications involving ΔaroF and ΔaroG or dgoA variants — with proteomics for all 7, protein abundance measured for 2,383 genes, and high cross-strain correlation indicating targeted rather than global effects of the engineered modifications [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. These results feed [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]].

On connectivity to the KBase Data Lakehouse, the project tested five connection types and found four matched at greater than 90%: 13 of 13 genome IDs matched the pangenome, 1,210 of 1,330 reactions matched biochemistry, 230 of 230 compounds matched biochemistry, and 4,891 of 4,891 cluster IDs matched the pangenome through mapping; ADP1 returned 0 matches among 1 Fitness Browser organism query and was absent from the Fitness Browser [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. It found all 13 lakehouse-format genomes belong to *s__Acinetobacter_baylyi* and the clade `s__Acinetobacter_baylyi--RS_GCF_000368685.1`, with a pangenome of 3,207 core and 1,684 accessory gene clusters, and noted the 120 unmatched reactions — 9% of 1,330 — may be custom or draft reactions not yet present in ModelSEED [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. The project found that ADP1's mmseqs2-style cluster IDs such as `NHSXFYEX_mmseqsCluster_NNNN` and the lakehouse's centroid gene IDs such as `NC_005966.1_1024` have 0% direct string match, and built a bridge through the `gene_genecluster_junction` table that mapped all 4,891 lakehouse clusters to 4,081 unique ADP1 clusters for a 100% gene-level match across 43,754 genes, emitted as `data/cluster_id_mapping.csv` [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. The report cautions that this mapping is indirect, passing through 3 tables, so cluster splits or merges between the two pangenome analyses could introduce edge cases [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]; see [[concepts/pangenome-integration]].

The project compared essentiality predictors and found that of 866 genes with both FBA flux predictions and TnSeq essentiality calls, 639, or 73.8%, were concordant and 227 discordant, with the discordant set proposed as candidates for metabolic-model refinement or as regulatory effects not represented by FBA [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. It found essentiality to be condition-specific, with 499 genes essential on minimal media versus 346 on LB, and FBA flux classes changing between rich and minimal media for 177 of 866 genes, or 20% [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. It further found essential genes to be more annotation-rich than dispensable genes — 33% versus 5% with COG assignments and 92% versus 53% with KEGG KO assignments — with approximately 8% of essential genes lacking KO assignments and flagged as potential novel essential functions, and essential genes more likely to belong to the core pangenome [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. On fitness, the project found a mean pairwise correlation of 0.44 across the 8 carbon sources, with urea nearly uncorrelated with quinate at r = 0.11 and weakly correlated with all other conditions at r = 0.12-0.28, suggesting urea catabolism draws on a largely independent gene set, while butanediol-acetate and butanediol-lactate correlated most strongly at r = 0.58 and r = 0.53 [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. Because ADP1 is absent from the Fitness Browser, the project reported these 8-carbon-source measurements as a resource not otherwise available in the lakehouse [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]; these results feed [[concepts/gene-essentiality]] and [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]].

On metabolic reconstruction, the project found that of 1,330 unique metabolic reactions, 1,248, or 94%, were shared across all 14 genomes and classified as core, 62 were variable across 2-13 genomes, and 20 were genome-unique, with gapfilling accounting for 7.7% of reactions on average and 243 missing functions cataloged [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. It found that of 121,519 growth phenotype predictions across 14 genomes, 105,376, or 87%, required at least one gapfilled reaction, that prediction accuracy was therefore tightly coupled to gapfilling quality, and that false negatives had higher mean gap counts than correct predictions — a dependence the report states limits interpretation of those predictions [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. The project connected the database to the `kbase_ke_pangenome`, `kbase_msd_biochemistry`, and `kescience_fitnessbrowser` collections and identified a 37-table Acinetobacter genome browser in `phagefoundry_acinetobacter_genome_browser` without deeply querying it [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]. The report states its FBA-TnSeq concordance analysis is limited to 866 genes because FBA flux data cover only 15% of genes, that the database covers only *A. baylyi* so cross-species comparison requires comparable databases for other organisms, and that pathway enrichment of the 227 discordant genes, cross-species fitness comparison, PhageFoundry cross-referencing, urea-specific gene identification, and pangenome-informed gapfill confidence assessment are future analyses rather than completed findings [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer]; these results feed [[concepts/metabolic-model-gapfilling]].
