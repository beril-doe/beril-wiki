# Chris Mungall

ORCID: [0000-0002-6601-2165](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6601-2165)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT]] project examined the 25-year +5°C soil warming experiment at Harvard Forest Barre Woods (n=42 biosamples, one sampling date). It found a real but modest community response. In organic soil, Actinobacteria rose (log2 FC +0.341, q 0.049) and Acidobacteria fell (log2 FC −0.549, q 0.049), which reproduces DeAngelis et al. 2015. The mineral horizon showed no phylum-level changes after FDR. [src: harvard_forest_warming] PERMANOVA on Bray-Curtis genus distances gave treatment R²=7.6% (p=0.069) and horizon R²=30.6% (p=0.0002). [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The same project did not support the hypothesis that RNA composition shifts more than DNA. The paired n=25 subset showed DNA R²=11.9% (p=0.020) and RNA R²=3.2% (p=0.60), but that subset confounds horizon with incubation. In the direct-only sensitivity analysis, DNA × mineral gave R²=12.7% (p=0.081), RNA × mineral gave R²=11.4% (p=0.254) and RNA × organic gave R²=10.0% (p=0.190). [src: harvard_forest_warming] The project concluded that the DNA and RNA pools show comparable treatment R² (10-13%). [src: harvard_forest_warming] For carbon-cycling genes, heated-up DNA KOs in organic soil were enriched for the curated 62-KO list (5 / 57 hits, OR 2.78, p 0.042). No individual RNA-pool KO survived FDR (q<0.10). [src: harvard_forest_warming] The pmoA and pmoB methanotrophy signals and the glyoxylate-cycle signals (icl and ms) were nominal only; for example, pmoA in organic soil was +0.730 (p=0.009). [src: harvard_forest_warming] Heated mineral samples had fewer detectable metabolites than control (155 vs 167 ChEBI per sample, MW p=0.012), and no individual metabolite passed BH-FDR. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

The [[summaries/neon_mag_functional_discovery__REPORT]] project analysed 7,298 MAGs from three NMDC-rehosted NEON studies, of which 1,609 pass MQ+HQ quality. [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery] The novel fraction of soil MAG records, meaning those with no GTDB genus call, rose from 7.6% below pH 4.5 to 37.0% above pH 6.5. This is opposite to the literature prior, and the report favors a GTDB coverage gap over true novelty. Kendall τ on four bin midpoints was 1.0 but p = 0.083. [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery] Functional gene content partitioned by habitat (PERMANOVA pseudo-F = 137, p = 0.001). [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery] Soil horizon explained 2.2% of variance, below the 5% effect-size threshold. [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery] The community-level KO-union proxy for accessory content was uninformative: pooled NEON-only KOs covered 9,547 / 9,584 of the universe. [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery] The per-MAG re-analysis found a median of 343 field-only KOs per MAG, and every MAG had at least 10. [src: neon_mag_functional_discovery]

The [[summaries/snipe_defense_system__REPORT]] project surveyed the KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome for the SNIPE phage-defense system. It found DUF4041 homologues in 1,696 species across 33 phyla and in 4,572 gene clusters. [src: snipe_defense_system] Of these, 86.7% were accessory or singleton. [src: snipe_defense_system] SNIPE-bearing species differed in 22/64 AlphaEarth dimensions, with small-to-medium effects (|d| = 0.11–0.26). AlphaEarth covers only 28.4% of genomes. [src: snipe_defense_system] The project also established that the SNIPE nuclease is PF13455, not canonical GIY-YIG PF01541. A search for PF01541 co-occurrence with DUF4041 gave zero hits. [src: snipe_defense_system]
