# Chris Mungall

ORCID: [0000-0002-6601-2165](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6601-2165)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT]] project analyzed a 25-year +5°C soil-warming experiment at Harvard Forest Barre Woods (NMDC `nmdc:sty-11-8ws97026`, 42 biosamples) and reported a modest community-level response: in 14 control and 14 heated direct samples, kraken2 read-based relative abundance showed Actinobacteria rising from 0.249 to 0.315 in organic soil (log2 FC +0.341, q=0.049) and Acidobacteria falling from 0.035 to 0.024 (log2 FC −0.549, q=0.049), while mineral soil showed no phylum-level changes after FDR (false discovery rate) correction [src: harvard_forest_warming]. PERMANOVA on Bray–Curtis genus distances attributed 7.6% of variance to treatment (p=0.069), 30.6% to horizon (p=0.0002), and 41% to the treatment × horizon four-cell factor (p=0.0002) [src: harvard_forest_warming]. The project tested and did not support the proposed hypothesis that RNA shifts more than DNA: in the paired n=25 subset treatment pseudo-F was 3.12 in DNA (R²=11.9%, p=0.020) versus 0.76 in RNA (R²=3.2%, p=0.60), but that subset confounded horizon with incubation, and direct-sample sensitivity analyses gave comparable magnitudes (DNA × mineral n=14, F=1.75, R²=12.7%, p=0.081; RNA × mineral n=11, F=1.15, R²=11.4%, p=0.254; RNA × organic n=14, F=1.33, R²=10.0%, p=0.190) [src: harvard_forest_warming].

The same project reported gene- and metabolite-level results and their limits. A curated 62-KO carbon-cycling list was enriched among heated-up DNA KOs in organic soil (5 of 57 carbon-cycling hits versus 428 of 12,806 other hits; Fisher odds ratio 2.78, one-sided p=0.042), with no such enrichment in DNA × mineral, RNA × organic, or RNA × mineral [src: harvard_forest_warming]. RNA signals for particulate methane monooxygenase were directionally positive in both horizons — pmoA (K10944) log2 FC +0.730 organic (p=0.009) and +0.743 mineral (p=0.029); pmoB (K10945) +0.669 organic (p=0.012) and +0.880 mineral (p=0.054) — and glyoxylate-cycle genes aceA/icl (K01637, log2 FC +0.460) and aceB/glcB (K01638, log2 FC +0.268, p=0.037 each) increased in heated mineral RNA, though these nominal signals did not survive FDR across 14K KOs [src: harvard_forest_warming]. Heated mineral samples yielded fewer detectable metabolites (155 ± 4 ChEBI identifications per sample versus 167 ± 9 in controls, Mann–Whitney p=0.012) with no individual metabolite passing BH-FDR [src: harvard_forest_warming]. The report records that the study used a single sampling date (2017-05-24), that `abiotic_features` was all zeros because of an NMDC parsing artifact so the +5°C label was the only environmental contrast, and that metatranscriptome KO counts came from contig annotations rather than TPM-quantified expression [src: harvard_forest_warming]. Its findings slot into [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]] and [[concepts/ecotype-environment-gene-content]] [src: harvard_forest_warming].

The [[summaries/snipe_defense_system__REPORT]] project surveyed the SNIPE phage-defense system across the KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome and corrected its domain architecture, identifying PF13250 as the Pfam assignment for DUF4041 (InterPro IPR025280) and PF13455/Mug113 — in GIY-YIG clan CL0418 but distinct from canonical GIY-YIG PF01541 — as the nuclease family, with the 558 aa *E. coli* protein A0A0A1A5Z2 carrying PF13250 at 232–333 and PF13455 at 443–520; zero surveyed gene clusters contained both DUF4041/PF13250 and PF01541 [src: snipe_defense_system]. DUF4041/PF13250 occurred in 4,572 gene clusters across 1,696 species and 33 bacterial and archaeal phyla, of which 13.3% were core, 30.7% accessory, and 56.1% singleton (86.7% accessory-plus-singleton), and 3,675/4,572 (80.4%) carried T5orf172 or DUF4041 descriptions [src: snipe_defense_system]. Direct RB-TnSeq (random barcode transposon sequencing) data from the Fitness Browser covered 168 *E. coli* K-12 experiments per ManXYZ gene, with strongest defects on D-glucosamine (*manX* −3.79, *manY* −2.79, *manZ* −3.63) and D-mannose (−2.75, −3.00, −2.74) versus *fruA* −1.44 on D-fructose, contradicting the UniProt "fructose-specific" ManX annotation; cofitness was 0.851 for *manX*↔*manZ*, 0.705 for *manX*↔*manY*, and 0.725 for *manY*↔*manZ* [src: snipe_defense_system]. The project also compared AlphaEarth satellite-derived embeddings for 1,069 SNIPE-bearing versus 13,977 non-SNIPE species, finding Bonferroni-significant differences in 22 of 64 dimensions with effect sizes |d| = 0.11–0.26 (largest A19, d=0.26, p=5.0e-14) at 28.4% genome coverage biased toward environmental isolates, and reported PhageFoundry results in which a model over 1,582 gene-cluster presence/absence features reached AUC=0.883 and 84.3% accuracy and Lambdavirus phage 411_P1 infected 1 of 188 *E. coli* strains (0.5%) [src: snipe_defense_system]. These results feed [[concepts/phage-defense-syndromes-and-arms-race]], [[concepts/pangenome-integration]], [[concepts/environment-embedding-geography]], and [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]] [src: snipe_defense_system].
