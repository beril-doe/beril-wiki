# Adam Deutschbauer

ORCID: [0000-0003-2728-7622](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-2728-7622)

## Contributions

The Pan-Bacterial Metal Fitness Atlas reported 383,349 gene × metal fitness records across 24 organisms and 14 metals. The report calls it the first cross-species genome-wide metal-tolerance fitness atlas. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] The Fitness Browser holds 559 metal-related experiments, 8.2% of 6,804 total, covering 16 metals. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] Significant defects (fit < -1, |t| > 4) occur in 3.3% of gene × metal records (12,838 / 383,349). [src: metal_fitness_atlas] Full results are in [[summaries/metal_fitness_atlas__REPORT]].

The project found that metal-fitness genes are enriched in the core genome, which contradicts hypothesis H1a (accessory enrichment). [src: metal_fitness_atlas] 21 of 22 organisms show metal-important genes as more core than baseline, and 14 are significant at p<0.05. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] 12 of 14 metals are individually significant at p<0.05. Cadmium (1 organism, delta=-0.010, p=0.92) and uranium (2 organisms, delta=+0.035, p=0.34) are not significant, likely because of limited organism coverage. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] Essential-metal tolerance genes have a mean core-fraction delta of +0.148, against +0.081 for toxic metals (Mann-Whitney U=39, p=0.015, one-sided). [src: metal_fitness_atlas] Removing 4 duplicate *P. fluorescens* FW300 strains (22 to 18 organisms) left enrichment robust (OR=2.065, p=5.9e-141). The report still lists phylogenetic non-independence as a caveat. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] See [[concepts/core-genome-enrichment-of-metal-stress-fitness-genes]].

The atlas found 1,182 of 2,891 ortholog groups with metal phenotypes conserved across ≥2 organisms. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] It also identified 149 novel metal-biology candidates: 89 truly unknown families, 43 with DUF/UPF domains and 17 with partial functional hints. These are function predictions based solely on cross-species fitness data. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] Independent component analysis yielded 600 metal-responsive module records at |z| > 2.0 among 19,453 records (3.1%). [src: metal_fitness_atlas]

In pangenome-scale prediction, a signature of 1,286 KEGG ortholog terms was used to score 27,702 species. After normalization, *Leptospirillum* ranked at the 91st percentile. Bioleaching genera as a group were not significantly enriched over background (Mann-Whitney p=0.17). [src: metal_fitness_atlas] The report also proposes experiments to close coverage gaps for critical metals. These are proposals, not results. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] See [[concepts/metal-coverage-gaps-and-proposed-validation-experiments]].
