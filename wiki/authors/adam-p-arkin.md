# Adam P. Arkin

ORCID: [0000-0002-4999-2931](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4999-2931)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/berdl_data_atlas__REPORT]] project inventoried 1,740 deduplicated tables across 119 databases, 17 tenants, and 10 funding agencies or programs on the KBase Data Lakehouse, mapping 536 schema-level cross-tenant bridges defined by 29 canonical join keys and realized use across 66 BERIL projects [src: berdl_data_atlas]. Its audit of 65 curated headline tables reported 1,011,650,903 KBase pangenome gene rows spanning 293,059 genomes and 27,690 GTDB species clades or pangenomes, alongside 241,070,489 AlphaFold predicted structures, 215,130,942 UniProt proteins, and 27,410,721 FitnessBrowser measurements [src: berdl_data_atlas]. It found that 51 of 66 audited projects (77%) span multiple tenants, that KBase appears in 53/66 (80%) and KEScience in 35/66 (53%), and that ENIGMA holds 36% of tables but appears in only 6 projects [src: berdl_data_atlas]. The project corrected its own proposed UC1 join after SQL probing — FitnessBrowser does not expose `protein_id`, so the validated path joins `genefitness` to `besthitswissprot` on the composite `orgId`, `locusId` key and then `sprotAccession` to `uniprot_accession` in `alphafold_entries` — and found 78,753 of 79,180 SwissProt best hits represented among AlphaFold entries, while cautioning that schema-level compatibility does not establish value-space overlap and that UC1 was the only bridge sample-executed [src: berdl_data_atlas].

Working on unannotated gene space, [[summaries/functional_dark_matter__REPORT]] found that 57,011 of 228,709 genes (24.9%) across 48 Fitness Browser organisms lack functional annotation, of which 7,787 show strong fitness effects (|fitness| ≥ 2 in at least one condition) and 9,557 are essential, giving 17,344 genes with experimentally measurable phenotypes [src: functional_dark_matter]. That project's GapMind analysis identified 1,256 organism–pathway pairs across 44 species with nearly complete pathways co-occurring with fitness-active dark genes, but stated these are organism-level co-occurrences rather than gene-to-enzyme assignments, and its conservation-by-ignorance classification assigned 6.0% of dark genes to strong testable hypotheses, 52.5% to weak leads, and 41.5% to true knowledge gaps [src: functional_dark_matter]. It also reported a pre-registered lab–field mapping in which 29 of 47 testable clusters were concordant (61.7%), with only 10 of 137 accessory dark-gene clusters significantly enriched by environmental category at FDR (false discovery rate) < 0.05 [src: functional_dark_matter]. Reannotating the same 57,011 dark genes with Bakta v1.12.0, [[summaries/truly_dark_genes__REPORT]] decomposed them into 33,105 annotation-lag genes, 6,427 truly dark genes, and 17,479 unlinked genes that could not be assessed, and found truly dark genes shorter (median 121 versus 194 amino acids, d = −0.432) and less conserved (ortholog presence 29.3% versus 63.7%, OR = 0.236) [src: truly_dark_genes]. That project rejected the hypothesis that truly dark genes are enriched in stress conditions, reporting stress at 28.7% versus 43.2% with OR = 0.53 and p < 0.001 in one comparison, and noted that 12.0% of truly dark genes lie within 2 genes of a mobile genetic element while treating GC deviation as an imperfect horizontal-gene-transfer proxy [src: truly_dark_genes].

At GTDB scale, [[summaries/gene_function_ecological_agora__REPORT]] built an atlas over 18,989 species representatives with 13.74M rank × clade × KO (KEGG ortholog) scores and 17,073,194 Sankoff-parsimony gain events, confirmed two of four pre-registered hypotheses, and failed to reproduce the Alm 2006 point estimate of r ≈ 0.74, obtaining Pearson r of 0.288, 0.157, 0.100, and 0.105 across four framings [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. That project supported Cyanobacteriia × PSII at class rank (producer d = +1.50, consumer d = +0.70) but found its Mycobacteriaceae family-level mycolic-acid result to be a mixture of sub-clades, with leaf_consistency of 0.15 against an atlas reference of 0.20 [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. Across 27,690 GTDB species, [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]] found 21.9% of species carry phaC and 21.7% have a complete polyhydroxybutyrate pathway, with a chi-squared test of PHB presence against environmental variability giving chi2 = 1,656.36, p ~ 0, dof = 2; after partial Spearman control for genome size the PHB–niche-breadth association fell from rho = 0.106 to partial rho = −0.047 (p = 0.037), a 56.3% reduction with sign reversal [src: phb_granule_ecology]. Analyzing 4,005,537 prophage-associated gene clusters across 27,702 species, [[summaries/prophage_ecology__REPORT]] ran PERMANOVA (a permutation-based multivariate analysis of variance) on a 1,773-species subsample and found effects for genome-size quartile (F = 212.99), environment (F = 30.04), and family-level phylogeny (F = 6.17), each at p = 0.01; clustering 38,085 TerL sequences from 11,789 species into 10,991 lineages, it found no individual TerL lineage significantly environment-enriched in 0/500 tests after FDR (false discovery rate) correction [src: prophage_ecology]. Across 293,059 GTDB r214 genomes, [[summaries/plant_microbiome_ecotypes__REPORT]] identified 1,136 plant-associated species and 7,995 genomes, and its Phase 2b adversarial review cut the compartment effect from R² = 0.527 on a 25-marker panel to pseudo-F = 23.2, R² = 0.071 on a refined 17-marker panel, attributing most of the original headline effect to taxonomic sampling [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]. The same project found beneficial plant-growth-promotion gene clusters 64.6% core versus 45.2% for pathogenic clusters (U = 83,567,419, p = 3.38e-125), and found co-occurring plant-associated genus pairs slightly *less* complementary than random pairs (Cohen's d ≈ −0.4, permutation p < 0.001) [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes].

On ENIGMA and subsurface data, [[summaries/genotype_to_phenotype_enigma__REPORT]] assembled 46,389 genome × condition pairs across 727 genomes and 363 conditions and obtained overall binary-growth AUC (area under the receiver-operating-characteristic curve) 0.620 with LightGBM, rising to 0.653 with KO × condition interaction features, while nitrogen conditions scored AUC 0.435 across 152 pairs [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. That project also found that a strain-name collision caused 12 of 32 genus-level mismatches when ENIGMA strains were matched to the KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome through short identifiers such as MT20, and that one collision matched *Rhodanobacter glycinis* to *Streptococcus pneumoniae* and introduced 1,751 spurious clinical genomes into environmental profiles; using CORAL brick 522 GTDB-Tk assignments and checking genus consistency eliminated all false matches and reduced verified linkages from 32 to 20 [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. The [[summaries/enigma_carbon_census_1__REPORT]] project resolved all 83 enrichment compounds to structures with InChIKeys, linked 54 to KEGG, and found 74/83 (89%) organism-dark, with its chemical-class coverage hypothesis unsupported (χ² = 5.07, p = 0.53, df = 6) and xanthine mis-scored as carbon-catabolic because reaction R02107 represents purine nitrogen acquisition [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. Across 9 wells spanning approximately 6 m, [[summaries/enigma_sso_asv_ecology__REPORT]] found hydrogeological zone explained 27.5% of community variance (F = 4.05, p = 0.0001) while well identity explained 19.2% and was not significant (F = 0.80, p = 0.979), and reported significant distance-decay (Mantel Spearman ρ = 0.323, p = 0.029), stating that its contamination-plume interpretation remains unconfirmed because direct SSO geochemistry has not been loaded [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]. In a single-organism mechanism study, [[summaries/caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss__REPORT]] found the 32-gene concordant-strong Fur signature enriched for RB-TnSeq (random barcode transposon sequencing) envelope-stress phenotypes at 53.1% versus a 33.25% genome background (1.60×, hypergeometric p = 0.016) while the 26-gene SspB-buffered set was indistinguishable from background (34.6%, p = 0.515), rejected the CtpA hypothesis at the preregistered bar, and found sphingolipid biosynthesis constitutive rather than induced (0/6 genes UP; *spt* −0.64, FDR 0.002) [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss].

For host-associated community design, [[summaries/cf_formulation_design__REPORT]] analyzed 4,949 isolates from 175 patient samples and found metabolic overlap with PA14 predicted planktonic inhibition (r = 0.384, p = 2.3×10⁻⁶), with a multivariate metabolic model at R² = 0.274 rising to R² = 0.360 with genus but cross-validating at only CV R² = 0.145 ± 0.142 [src: cf_formulation_design]. That project identified a k=3 formulation (*M. luteus* + *N. mucosa* + *S. salivarius*) as the global optimum over 127,598 valid triples with composite score 0.562, yet recommended k=2 (*R. dentocariosa* + *N. mucosa*) as the primary clinical candidate on 84% mean inhibition and combined engraftability 0.820, and found bootstrap composite-score intervals for k=2 through k=5 overlapping [src: cf_formulation_design]. Using 8,489 curatedMetagenomicData samples, [[summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT]] selected four ecotypes at consensus K = 4 (cross-method ARI = 0.131 versus 0.140 at K = 7, resolved by a smallest-K-within-0.02 parsimony rule) and reported only 48.9% per-sample LDA/GMM agreement [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. That project retracted its original within-ecotype *C. scindens* result, its original Jaccard framing, and its original 33-species Tier-A list, replacing them with a confound-free within-IBD-substudy meta-analysis in which *C. scindens* was CD-up at pooled CLR-Δ = +1.18, FDR = 1e-8, and it named six actionable Tier-A targets scoring ≥ 2.5, led by *Hungatella hathewayi* (4.0) and *Mediterraneibacter gnavus* (3.8) [src: ibd_phage_targeting].
