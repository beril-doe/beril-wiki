# Adam P. Arkin

ORCID: [0000-0002-4999-2931](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4999-2931)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/berdl_data_atlas__REPORT]] project inventoried 1,740 deduplicated tables across 119 databases, 17 tenants, and 10 funding agencies or programs, and identified 536 schema-level cross-tenant bridges defined by 29 canonical join keys; among 66 audited BERIL projects, 51 (77%) spanned multiple tenants [src: berdl_data_atlas]. It sample-validated the UC1 structural fitness atlas after SQL probing showed FitnessBrowser exposes no protein_id, joining genefitness on orgId and locusId to besthitswissprot and then from sprotAccession to uniprot_accession in alphafold_entries, recovering 55,454 genes across 48 organisms with both fitness data and an AlphaFold model, of which 6,635 were essential at min_fit ≤ −4 [src: berdl_data_atlas]. [[summaries/functional_dark_matter__REPORT]] found that 57,011 of 228,709 genes across 48 Fitness Browser organisms (24.9%) lack functional annotation, that 17,344 of these have experimentally measurable phenotypes (7,787 with |fitness| ≥ 2 and 9,557 essential), and that a pre-registered lab–field mapping was concordant for 29 of 47 testable clusters (61.7%, one-sided binomial p = 0.072, Fisher's combined p = 0.031), with all four testable NMDC abiotic predictions confirmed, including pH carriers correlating with pH at ρ = +0.157, n = 4,366, FDR = 7.4e-25 [src: functional_dark_matter]. [[summaries/truly_dark_genes__REPORT]] reannotated those dark genes with Bakta v1.12.0 and decomposed them into 33,105 annotation-lag genes, 6,427 truly dark genes, and 17,479 unlinked genes, reporting that truly dark genes have a median length of 121 versus 194 amino acids and ortholog presence of 29.3% versus 63.7% (OR = 0.236), and ranking 100 top candidates with scores of 8–10 across 19 organisms [src: truly_dark_genes]. That project also rejected the hypothesis that truly dark genes are enriched in stress conditions, reporting two differing in-report figures for the same comparison — stress conditions at 28.7% versus 43.2% for annotation-lag genes (OR = 0.53, p < 0.001) in one comparison, and 43.3% versus 54.7% in the results section, which additionally reports carbon-source conditions at 13.7% versus 21.7% and enrichment in mixed-community (7.5% versus 0%) and iron (0.7% versus 0%) conditions [src: truly_dark_genes].

[[summaries/gene_function_ecological_agora__REPORT]] built a GTDB-r214 atlas of 18,989 species representatives with 13.74M rank × clade × KO scores and 17,073,194 Sankoff-parsimony gain events, confirmed Cyanobacteriia × PSII at class rank (producer d = +1.50, p = 2×10⁻⁵) and Mycobacteriaceae × mycolic acid at family rank (producer d = +0.31), and failed to reproduce the Alm 2006 r ≈ 0.74 estimate, obtaining Pearson r of 0.288, 0.157, 0.100, and 0.105 across four framings [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]] evaluated PHB biosynthesis across 27,690 GTDB species, found 21.9% carry phaC and 21.7% have a complete pathway, reported a chi-squared test of PHB presence against environmental variability of chi2 = 1,656.36, p ~ 0, dof = 2, and showed the apparent PHB–niche-breadth association (rho = 0.106) reversed to partial rho = −0.047, p = 0.037 after controlling for genome size [src: phb_granule_ecology]. [[summaries/prophage_ecology__REPORT]] analyzed 4,005,537 eggNOG-annotated prophage-associated gene clusters across 27,702 species, found packaging and lysogenic regulation modules in 100.0% of species, and reported that environment (F = 30.04) exceeded family-level phylogeny (F = 6.17) in PERMANOVA while no individual TerL lineage showed significant environment-specific enrichment in 0/500 tests [src: prophage_ecology]; the same project reported tail (log2(OR) = 2.21), head morphogenesis (log2(OR) = 1.98), and anti-defense (log2(OR) = 1.70) enrichment in human-associated environments, which it presents as consistent with the [[concepts/phage-defense-syndromes-and-arms-race]] interpretation rather than a mechanistic demonstration [src: prophage_ecology]. [[summaries/plant_microbiome_ecotypes__REPORT]] identified 1,136 plant-associated species and 7,995 genomes among 293,059 GTDB r214 genomes, measured compartment effects on the refined 17-marker panel at PERMANOVA R² = 0.071 (pseudo-F = 23.2, p = 0.001), and separately reported that the Phase 1 25-marker-panel PERMANOVA gave R² = 0.527 (pseudo-F = 235.1) but dropped to R² = 0.072 when the three genome-rich species per compartment were excluded; it also found beneficial plant-growth-promotion clusters at 64.6% core fraction versus 45.2% for pathogenic clusters (U = 83,567,419, p = 3.38e-125) [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes].

[[summaries/genotype_to_phenotype_enigma__REPORT]] assembled 46,389 genome × condition training pairs from 727 genomes and 363 conditions, achieved binary-growth AUC 0.620 rising to 0.653 with KO × condition interaction features, and found continuous µmax, lag, and max_A unpredictable under genus-blocked holdout with negative R²; it also documented a strain-name collision that matched Rhodanobacter glycinis to Streptococcus pneumoniae and introduced 1,751 spurious clinical genomes [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. [[summaries/enigma_carbon_census_1__REPORT]] resolved all 83 enrichment compounds to InChIKey structures, linked 54 to KEGG, and found 74/83 (89%) organism-dark, while its H1 chemical-class coverage test was not supported (χ² = 5.07, p = 0.53, df = 6) [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. [[summaries/enigma_sso_asv_ecology__REPORT]] analyzed 23,458 ASVs across 9 wells, detected distance-decay by Mantel test (Spearman ρ = 0.323, p = 0.029) and found hydrogeological zone explained 27.5% of community variance (F = 4.05, p = 0.0001) versus a non-significant 19.2% for well identity [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]. [[summaries/caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss__REPORT]] analyzed Δ*fur* Δ*sspB*-permitted Δ*lpxc* viability in *Caulobacter crescentus*, finding Fur-signature Path A enriched for envelope-stress phenotypes at 53.1% versus a 33.25% background (p = 0.016) while the SspB-buffered Path B was indistinguishable from background (p = 0.515), and rejected the CtpA hypothesis at the preregistered bar [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]. [[summaries/cf_formulation_design__REPORT]] analyzed 4,949 isolates from 175 patient samples, found metabolic overlap with PA14 predicted planktonic inhibition (r = 0.384, p = 2.3×10⁻⁶) with R² = 0.274, and recommended a k=2 formulation of *R. dentocariosa* + *N. mucosa* at 84% mean inhibition [src: cf_formulation_design]. [[summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT]] derived four ecotypes from 8,489 curatedMetagenomicData samples, identified six Tier-A pathobionts scoring ≥ 2.5 led by *Hungatella hathewayi* at 4.0, and found *E. coli* alone carried the iron-plus-genotoxin MIBiG signature with 54 iron BGCs and 25 genotoxin BGCs [src: ibd_phage_targeting].
