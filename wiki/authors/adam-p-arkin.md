# Adam P. Arkin

ORCID: [0000-0002-4999-2931](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4999-2931)

## Contributions

[[summaries/berdl_data_atlas__REPORT]] inventoried the KBase Data Lakehouse [src: berdl_data_atlas]. It catalogued 1,740 deduplicated tables across 119 databases, 17 tenants and 10 funding agencies or programs, and mapped 536 schema-level cross-tenant bridges defined by 29 canonical join keys [src: berdl_data_atlas]. The BERDL Data Atlas project found that DOE accounts for approximately 78% of the tables and that the largest entity table, `kbase_ke_pangenome.gene`, holds 1,011,650,903 rows [src: berdl_data_atlas]. It also sample-validated UC1, a bridge joining FitnessBrowser to AlphaFold through SwissProt best hits, and the report describes itself as a catalog of data systems rather than a test of biological hypotheses [src: berdl_data_atlas].

[[summaries/functional_dark_matter__REPORT]] found that 57,011 of 228,709 genes (24.9%) across 48 Fitness Browser organisms lack functional annotation [src: functional_dark_matter]. Of those, 7,787 show strong fitness effects (|fitness| ≥ 2 in at least one condition) and 9,557 are essential because no viable transposon mutants were recovered, so 17,344 have measurable phenotypes [src: functional_dark_matter]. The same project identified 1,256 organism–pathway pairs across 44 species in which GapMind (a tool that scores pathway completeness from genome sequence) found nearly complete pathways co-occurring with strongly fitness-active dark genes [src: functional_dark_matter]. The report presents these as organism-level co-occurrences supporting the hypothesis that dark genes may encode the missing steps, not as gene-to-enzyme assignments [src: functional_dark_matter].

[[summaries/truly_dark_genes__REPORT]] reannotated dark genes with Bakta v1.12.0 [src: truly_dark_genes]. Of the 39,532 pangenome-linked dark genes, 33,105 (83.7%) were reclassified and 6,427 remained hypothetical, while the other 17,479 could not be assessed [src: truly_dark_genes]. The project found that truly dark genes are shorter than annotation-lag genes (median 121 vs 194 amino acids) and less often core (43.1% vs 72.7%) [src: truly_dark_genes]. However, the report's claim that every effect exceeded its pre-registered thresholds is internally inconsistent for the essential-fraction comparison, whose odds ratio (OR) is 1.420 against a threshold of OR ≥ 1.5 [src: truly_dark_genes]. It also found that 12.0% of truly dark genes lie within 2 genes of a mobile genetic element, a figure limited to the 4,394 genes in organisms with ICA (independent component analysis) fitness-module data [src: truly_dark_genes]. That proximity supports horizontal transfer but does not establish it [src: truly_dark_genes].

[[summaries/gene_function_ecological_agora__REPORT]] built an atlas on the Genome Taxonomy Database release r214 (GTDB-r214) spanning 18,989 species representatives, with 13.74M producer/participation scores, which classify clade × function tuples by paralog-expansion (producer) and participation behavior into Innovator-Isolated, Innovator-Exchange, Sink/Broker-Exchange and Stable categories [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. The atlas also holds 17.07M Sankoff-parsimony gain events, Sankoff parsimony being a weighted-parsimony reconstruction of gene gains on a tree [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. Two of its four pre-registered hypotheses were confirmed: Mycobacteriaceae × mycolic-acid as Innovator-Isolated, and Cyanobacteria × photosystem II (PSII) as Innovator-Exchange at class rank [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]. One further hypothesis was qualified or reframed, the Alm 2006 point estimate was not reproduced at GTDB scale, and the pre-registered Bacteroidota hypothesis remains falsified at the pre-registered absolute-zero criterion [src: gene_function_ecological_agora].

[[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]] found that 21.9% of 27,690 GTDB species carry phaC and 21.7% have a complete polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB) pathway, with the highest prevalence in Pseudomonadota at 60.9% [src: phb_granule_ecology]. PHB presence was enriched in variable environments (chi2 = 1,656.36), and this held within genome-size quartiles, for example a 4.4x enrichment in Q1 with p = 1.18 x 10^-11 [src: phb_granule_ecology]. The PHB–niche-breadth association (rho = 0.106), however, became partial rho = -0.047 after controlling for genome size, so the report treats genome size as the primary correlate [src: phb_granule_ecology].

[[summaries/prophage_ecology__REPORT]] found prophage-associated gene clusters, 4,005,537 in total, in all 27,702 analyzed species, with 34.9% of species carrying all 7 modules [src: prophage_ecology]. PERMANOVA (permutation-based multivariate analysis of variance) on a 1,773-species subsample gave F=212.99 for genome size, F=30.04 for environment and F=6.17 for phylogeny [src: prophage_ecology]. After controlling for genome size, niche breadth correlated with prophage module count at partial rho=0.468 [src: prophage_ecology]. The report notes that its module interpretations are limited by the absence of dedicated prophage detection [src: prophage_ecology].

[[summaries/plant_microbiome_ecotypes__REPORT]] identified 1,136 plant-associated species and 7,995 genomes [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]. On 607 compartment species it found PERMANOVA R² = 0.071 and db-RDA (distance-based redundancy analysis) location-only R² = 0.060, alongside significant dispersion heterogeneity (PERMDISP F = 15.6, p = 0.001), so the PERMANOVA R² is treated as an upper bound and the compartment hypothesis was rated weakly supported [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]. Adversarial review showed that the earlier Phase 1 R² = 0.527 fell to 0.072 after excluding the three genome-rich species per compartment [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes]. Under within-genus label shuffling, only 3/15 markers survived [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes].

[[summaries/genotype_to_phenotype_enigma__REPORT]] assembled 27,632 ENIGMA growth curves across 123 strains and 195 molecules [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. Of these, 15,227 (55.1%) showed no detectable growth, and fit-ok curves had median µmax = 0.028 h⁻¹ [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. The project built a 46,389-pair modeling corpus and distinguished predictable binary growth capability from poorly predictable continuous kinetics [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. It also identified 8 metabolic guilds from KO (KEGG Orthology) profiles [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma]. Across 587 Oak Ridge communities it reported two anti-correlated genus clusters, whose members averaged pH 6.78 versus pH 5.43 in global 16S samples [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma].

[[summaries/enigma_carbon_census_1__REPORT]] resolved all 83 enrichment compounds to structures [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. It found 9 callable and 74/83 (89%) organism-dark, a resource-defined gap meaning their genetic determinants of utilization were not linkable through the queried the KBase Data Lakehouse and curated resources [src: enigma_carbon_census_1]. One of the 9, lauric acid, was callable only through a reference bacterium, and one isolate call, xanthine, was later identified as a carbon-category error [src: enigma_carbon_census_1].

[[summaries/enigma_sso_asv_ecology__REPORT]] analyzed 23,458 sediment ASVs (amplicon sequence variants, exact error-corrected amplicon sequences) across 9 SSO wells [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]. A Mantel test, which correlates ecological and geographic distance matrices, detected distance-decay (Spearman ρ = 0.323, p = 0.029), and the project found that hydrogeological zone explained 27.5% of variance, while well identity explained 19.2% and was not significant [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]. The project's contamination-plume interpretation of the U3–M6–L7 corridor remains unconfirmed because no geochemistry was analyzed [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology].

[[summaries/caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss__REPORT]] found that the Δ*fur* Δ*sspB* contrast correlated with a published Δ*fur* signal across 93 DEGs (differentially expressed genes) at Spearman ρ = 0.315, with 71% sign concordance, and that 53 of those genes were buffered [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]. Using RB-TnSeq (random barcode transposon sequencing) data, the concordant Fur signature was marginally enriched for envelope-stress phenotypes (1.60×, p = 0.016), while the SspB-buffered set was indistinguishable from background (1.04×, p = 0.515) [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]. The mechanistic role of the buffered respiratory program therefore remains a hypothesis [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss].

[[summaries/cf_formulation_design__REPORT]] integrated 4,949 isolates from 175 patient samples [src: cf_formulation_design]. It found that the *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* reporter strain PA14 grew best on amino acids under synthetic cystic-fibrosis sputum conditions, reaching an endpoint optical density (OD) of 0.60 on proline, but noted that other reporter pathogens have distinct profiles, so its design is PA14-targeted and not shown to generalize [src: cf_formulation_design]. The report leaves its substrate counts (21, 22, and 20 amino acids plus glucose and lactate) unreconciled [src: cf_formulation_design].

[[summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT]] selected four ecotypes from 8,489 metagenomic samples using consensus K = 4, with a cross-method adjusted Rand index (ARI, a chance-corrected measure of clustering agreement) of 0.131 [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. It reported that Crohn's disease in species–metabolite space is dominated by one canonical correlation axis (r = 0.96) and identified six actionable Tier-A pathobionts [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. From these it produced per-patient cocktail drafts for 14 of 23 UC Davis patients [src: ibd_phage_targeting]. Its clinical-covariate classifier reached a macro one-vs-rest (OvR) area under the ROC curve (AUC) of 0.799, but agreed with metagenomic ecotype calls in only 41% (9/22) of cases [src: ibd_phage_targeting].
