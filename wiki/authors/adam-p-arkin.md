# Adam P. Arkin

ORCID: [0000-0002-4999-2931](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4999-2931)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/berdl_data_atlas__REPORT]] project inventoried 1,740 deduplicated tables across 119 databases and 17 tenants, identified 536 schema-level cross-tenant bridges defined by 29 canonical join keys, and found that 51 of 66 audited BERIL projects spanned multiple tenants. [src: berdl_data_atlas] The [[summaries/functional_dark_matter__REPORT]] project analyzed 228,709 genes across 48 organisms, identified 57,011 dark genes, and found 17,344 with experimentally measurable phenotypes through strong fitness effects or essentiality. [src: functional_dark_matter] The [[summaries/truly_dark_genes__REPORT]] project reannotated those dark genes with Bakta and classified 6,427 as remaining hypothetical in both pipelines, finding that these genes had median length 121 amino acids, core-genome fraction 43.1%, and ortholog breadth median 1. [src: truly_dark_genes] The [[summaries/gene_function_ecological_agora__REPORT]] project built a GTDB-r214 atlas spanning 18,989 species representatives and reported 17.07 million Sankoff-parsimony gain events, supporting family- or class-level signals for mycolic-acid and PSII functions while failing to reproduce the Alm 2006 correlation of approximately 0.74. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

The [[summaries/genotype_to_phenotype_enigma__REPORT]] project integrated ENIGMA growth curves with genome, phenotype, fitness, and environmental data, assembled 46,389 genome-by-condition training pairs, and found that binary growth capability was more predictable than continuous growth kinetics, with matched-condition Carbon Source Phenotypes transfer achieving AUC 0.800 at 23% coverage. [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma] The [[summaries/enigma_carbon_census_1__REPORT]] project resolved 83 enrichment compounds to structures and found that 74/83, or 89%, were organism-dark because their utilization determinants were not linkable through the queried resources. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1] The [[summaries/enigma_sso_asv_ecology__REPORT]] project analyzed subsurface ASV communities and found that hydrogeological zone explained 27.5% of community variance, while its proposed northeast-to-southwest contamination-plume corridor remained an inference pending direct geochemical measurements. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology] The [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]] project surveyed PHB biosynthesis across 27,690 GTDB species and found phaC in 21.9% of species, with high-variability environments enriched for phaC-positive species even after genome-size stratification. [src: phb_granule_ecology] The [[summaries/plant_microbiome_ecotypes__REPORT]] project analyzed 293,059 GTDB genomes and found that refined plant-compartment effects explained R² = 0.071 of marker-profile variation, while beneficial plant-growth-promotion gene clusters had a 64.6% core fraction versus 45.2% for pathogenic clusters. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] The [[summaries/prophage_ecology__REPORT]] project analyzed 4,005,537 prophage-associated gene clusters across 27,702 bacterial species and found that environmental effects on prophage-module composition remained significant beyond genome size and host phylogeny. [src: prophage_ecology]

The [[summaries/cf_formulation_design__REPORT]] project evaluated 4,949 isolates from 175 cystic-fibrosis patient samples and found that metabolic overlap with *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* PA14 predicted planktonic inhibition with r = 0.384 and p = 2.3×10⁻⁶; its recommended two-species formulation of *Rothia dentocariosa* plus *Neisseria mucosa* achieved 84% mean inhibition and combined engraftability of 0.820. [src: cf_formulation_design] The [[summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT]] project analyzed 8,489 microbiome samples and 23 UC Davis patients, identified six actionable Tier-A pathobionts, and found that iron/heme acquisition was enriched among Crohn’s-disease-up pathways with OR = 8.11 and FDR = 7.4e-6. [src: ibd_phage_targeting] The [[summaries/caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss__REPORT]] project integrated transcriptomics, proteomics, and RB-TnSeq to study Δ*fur* Δ*sspB* Δ*lpxc* viability in *Caulobacter crescentus*, finding statistically supported Fur derepression but rejecting the preregistered CtpA mechanism and finding no enrichment for the SspB-buffered respiratory gene set. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
