# David Lyon

ORCID: [0000-0002-1927-3565](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1927-3565)

## Contributions

[[summaries/lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas__REPORT]] built a 293,059-genome atlas of lanthanide-dependent methanol oxidation across the KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome, testing xoxF (rare-earth-element-dependent methanol dehydrogenase), mxaF (Ca-dependent methanol dehydrogenase), lanmodulin, and PQQ-biosynthesis markers against GTDB-r214 taxonomy and environmental metadata [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. The project found eggNOG K00114 (xoxF) in 3,690 genomes versus K14028 (mxaF) in 195, an 18.92 : 1 ratio (Clopper-Pearson 95 % CI [13.07, 27.69]), with a one-sided binomial test against the pre-registered 10/11 ≈ 0.909 threshold giving p = 7.6 × 10⁻²²; a Bayesian binomial GLMM with phylum and family random intercepts returned a phylogeny-corrected xoxF fraction of 0.993 [95 % CI 0.992, 0.994], or roughly 143 : 1 [95 % credible interval 122, 169] [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. It reported high-rate carriers outside canonical methylotrophs — Acidobacteriota at 28.3 % xoxF (285 xoxF, 3 mxaF of 1,006 genomes, p_BH = 1.2 × 10⁻⁷⁹) and Gemmatimonadota at 25.4 % (98 xoxF, 1 mxaF of 386, p_BH = 1.5 × 10⁻²⁷) — while noting that annotation-based detection does not establish enzyme activity [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. Environmentally, soil_sediment (13,779 genomes, 6.84 % xoxF) was enriched over generic_environmental (21,538 genomes, 3.69 %) at OR = 1.92, p_BH = 6.1 × 10⁻³⁹, marine at OR = 1.31, p_BH = 7.8 × 10⁻⁷, and host-associated depleted at OR = 0.058, p_BH = 0.0, whereas the REE-impacted class (37 genomes, 10.81 %, OR = 3.51) did not clear FDR at p_BH = 0.082 [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. The project also found all 62 Bakta-validated lanmodulin genomes confined to Beijerinckiaceae, Acetobacteraceae, or Hyphomicrobiaceae (p = 9.8 × 10⁻⁷), but xoxF co-occurrence in only 49/62 = 79.0 % (p = 0.65, hypothesis not formally supported), and traced 505 eggNOG lanM calls to false positives in unrelated gut Bacillota, recommending Bakta product calls exclusively for lanmodulin — a marker-source calibration that feeds [[concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth]] [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas].

[[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]] compared 9 deep-confined, 30 shallow-clay, and 150 phylum-stratified soil-baseline cultured genomes (137 after quality filtering) for biosynthetic self-sufficiency, an anaerobic toolkit, and cultivation bias [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. It found dissimilatory sulfate-reduction markers in 5/9 deep genomes against a Mitzscherling (2023) rock-attached null expectation of 0.018 positives (binomial p = 4.0 × 10⁻¹²), and concluded that the cultured Mont Terri cohort matches the Bagnoud porewater paradigm rather than the rock-attached community [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. Anaerobic-toolkit means were 1.889 for anchor_deep (n = 9), 0.033 for anchor_shallow (n = 30), and 0.393 for the soil baseline (n = 140), with deep-versus-baseline Fisher tests giving Wood–Ljungdahl OR = 10.4 (p_BH = 0.004), group 1 [NiFe]-hydrogenase OR = 10.5 (p_BH = 0.004), and sulfate reduction OR = 33.8 (p_BH = 2.5 × 10⁻⁴); within Bacillota_B only sulfate reduction survived (5/5 versus 4/19, OR = ∞, p = 0.003, p_BH = 0.044), so Wood–Ljungdahl and [NiFe] tracked lineage instead [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. GapMind amino-acid pathway completeness (18-pathway universe) was not elevated in the deep cohort — 16.22/18 versus 16.66/18 unfiltered (Mann–Whitney p = 0.153, d = −0.17) and 15.50/18 versus 17.14/18 after the CheckM ≥80 % completeness/≤5 % contamination filter (p = 0.009, d = −0.84) — while anchor_shallow exceeded the baseline at 17.87/18 (p = 0.006, d = +0.52), results that slot into [[concepts/metabolic-model-gapfilling]] [src: clay_confined_subsurface].

[[summaries/bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory__REPORT]] extended that cohort by comparing 10 deep-clay Bacillota_B genomes with 62 soil-baseline Bacillota_B genomes across 14,109 Firmicutes-level eggNOG orthologous groups, identifying 547 enriched OGs at q < 0.05 under Benjamini–Hochberg correction with a ≥3-fold difference and ≥3 positive anchors — including 42 anaerobic-respiration, 24 sporulation-revival, 12 mineral-attachment or exopolysaccharide, 4 anaerobic-regulator, and 3 osmoadaptation OGs, with manual inspection suggesting the true anaerobic-respiration-related total was closer to 80–100 [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. It rejected the prediction of genome streamlining in the opposite direction: anchor genomes averaged 4,110,038 bp versus 3,046,124 bp (Cohen's d = +1.39, p = 0.025) and 2,630 versus 2,106 eggNOG OGs (d = +1.30, p = 0.022), with near-identical CheckM completeness (94.7 % versus 94.3 %, p = 0.93), ruling out a quality artifact [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. Both projects reported that the clay project's original iron-reduction markers K07811, K17324, and K17323 are TMAO reductase and glycerol ABC transport genes rather than iron-reduction markers; a corrected triple-signal multi-heme cytochrome detector (PFAM PF02085, PFAM PF22678, and ≥4 CXXCH heme-binding motifs) raised anchor_deep from 11.1 % to 55.6 %, lowered anchor_shallow from 50.0 % to 40.0 %, and raised the soil baseline from 20.1 % to 40.9 %, leaving all corrected cohort comparisons non-significant (Fisher p ≥ 0.46) and withdrawing the original shallow-greater-than-deep narrative while the sulfate-reduction finding stood [src: clay_confined_subsurface, bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. Both projects also documented cohort limits: rock-attached Geobacter and Geothrix lineages and CPR/DPANN episymbionts are essentially absent from the cultured clay cohort [src: clay_confined_subsurface], the Bacillota_B universe held 334 genomes rather than the planned estimate of 6,700, and the 10-genome anchor is clumped among 3 BRH-c8a, 2 BRH-c4a, and 2 Desulfosporosinus genomes, so some enriched OGs may be lineage markers [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory].
