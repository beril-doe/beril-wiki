# David Lyon

ORCID: [0000-0002-1927-3565](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1927-3565)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas__REPORT]] project built a 293,059-genome atlas of lanthanide-dependent methanol oxidation across the KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome and found that eggNOG K00114 (xoxF, the REE-dependent methanol dehydrogenase) occurs in 3,690 genomes against 195 genomes for K14028 (mxaF, the Ca-dependent enzyme), an 18.92 : 1 ratio with a Clopper-Pearson 95% CI of [13.07, 27.69] and a one-sided binomial p = 7.6 × 10⁻²² against the pre-registered 10/11 ≈ 0.909 threshold [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. The project ran three phylogenetic controls and found the direction held in all of them: naive genome-level pooling gave an xoxF fraction of 0.950 [95% CI 0.942, 0.956] over 3,885 MDH-informative genomes, equal weighting across 271 GTDB families gave 0.960 [95% CI 0.941, 0.976], and a Bayesian binomial GLMM with phylum and family random intercepts gave 0.993 [95% CI 0.992, 0.994], equivalent to ~143 : 1 with a credible interval of [122, 169] [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. The same project found high xoxF rates in lineages beyond the classical methylotrophs — 28.3 % in Acidobacteriota (285 xoxF, 3 mxaF among 1,006 genomes; p_BH = 1.2 × 10⁻⁷⁹) and 25.4 % in Gemmatimonadota (98 xoxF, 1 mxaF among 386; p_BH = 1.5 × 10⁻²⁷) — while stating that annotation-based detection does not establish enzyme activity or methylotrophic physiology [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. It found Bakta-validated lanmodulin in 62 genomes spanning 10 species, all 62/62 within Beijerinckiaceae, Acetobacteraceae, or Hyphomicrobiaceae (one-sided binomial p = 9.8 × 10⁻⁷ against an 80 % threshold), but xoxF co-occurrence in only 49/62 = 79.0 %, just below the pre-registered 80 % threshold, so that hypothesis (H3b, p = 0.65) was not formally supported [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas].

That project also reported environmental and annotation-quality results. It found soil_sediment (13,779 genomes, 6.84 % xoxF rate) enriched over generic_environmental (21,538 genomes, 3.69 %) with OR = 1.92 and p_BH = 6.1 × 10⁻³⁹, and marine (15,554 genomes, 4.76 %) enriched with OR = 1.31 and p_BH = 7.8 × 10⁻⁷, while REE-impacted samples (37 genomes, 10.81 %, OR = 3.51, p_BH = 0.082) did not clear the FDR (false-discovery-rate) threshold and host-associated samples (107,600 genomes, 0.22 %) were depleted at OR = 0.058 [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. It found the 37 REE-acid-mine-drainage MAGs (metagenome-assembled genomes) dominated by stress biology rather than lanthanide metabolism — only 4/37 carry any xoxF and 0/37 carry Bakta-validated lanmodulin or xoxJ, whereas RecN occurs in 33, RadA in 32, and MerR-family heavy-metal-responsive regulators in 30 — and flagged this anchor as descriptive only, drawn from a single bioproject [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. The project found that apparent PQQ-biosynthesis absence is largely an annotation gap: of 2,185 xoxF genomes with no eggNOG pqq annotation, 1,288 (59 %) carry at least 1 Bakta PQQ product, leaving 897 genomes (24 % of all xoxF carriers) without PQQ evidence from either source [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. It found 505 eggNOG `Preferred_name = lanM` calls concentrated in unrelated gut Bacillota — Streptococcus pneumoniae (10), Blautia_A wexlerae (9), Enterococcus faecalis (8) — and concluded that Bakta product should be used exclusively for lanmodulin, a marker-calibration result that feeds [[concepts/gene-function-acquisition-depth]] [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas].

The [[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]] project compared 9 deep-confined, 30 shallow-clay, and 150 phylum-stratified soil-baseline cultured genomes (137 after quality filtering) and found 5/9 deep genomes carried dissimilatory sulfate-reduction markers against 0.018 expected under the Mitzscherling (2023) rock-attached null, binomial p = 4.0×10⁻¹², concluding the cultured Mont Terri cohort matches the Bagnoud porewater paradigm rather than the rock-attached community [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. It found the anaerobic toolkit strongly enriched at cohort level against the soil baseline (Wood–Ljungdahl OR = 10.4, p_BH = 0.004; group 1 [NiFe]-hydrogenase OR = 10.5, p_BH = 0.004; sulfate reduction OR = 33.8, p_BH = 2.5×10⁻⁴; nitrogenase OR = 5.4, p_BH = 0.035; iron reduction OR = 0.46, p_BH = 0.69), but found that within Bacillota_B only sulfate reduction survived — 5/5 deep versus 4/19 baseline, OR = ∞, p = 0.003, p_BH = 0.044 — while Wood–Ljungdahl and [NiFe]-hydrogenase tracked the lineage [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. The project reported a null on biosynthetic self-sufficiency: GapMind amino-acid completeness in the deep cohort was 16.22/18 versus 16.66/18 for the baseline unfiltered (Mann–Whitney p = 0.153, Cohen's d = −0.17) and 15.50/18 versus 17.14/18 after CheckM filtering (p = 0.009, d = −0.84), with shallow genomes instead higher than baseline (17.87/18 versus 16.66/18, p = 0.006, d = +0.52); it noted the 18-pathway metric saturates near 18 and that the negative result does not reject self-sufficiency in deep subsurface life, only in this cultivable cohort [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. It withdrew its own original shallow-greater-than-deep iron-reduction narrative after K07811, K17324, and K17323 were identified as TMAO reductase and two glycerol ABC transport genes rather than iron-reduction markers [src: clay_confined_subsurface].

The [[summaries/bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory__REPORT]] project compared 10 deep-clay Bacillota_B genomes with 62 soil-baseline Bacillota_B genomes and found 547 eggNOG orthologous groups (OGs) enriched at q<0.05 across 14,109 Firmicutes-level OGs under Benjamini–Hochberg correction, a ≥3 fold-difference threshold, and ≥3 positive anchors, exceeding the preregistered prediction of ≥10 [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. Keyword scanning assigned 42 OGs to anaerobic respiration, 24 to sporulation-revival, 12 to mineral attachment or exopolysaccharide, 4 to anaerobic regulators, and 3 to osmoadaptation, with 462 other or unannotated; manual inspection found that category undercounted, with COG1977 in 10/10 anchors versus 11/62 baselines and the DsrE/DsrF/DsrH-like OG 1UIFM in 7/10 versus 0/62, suggesting a true anaerobic-respiration total nearer 80–100 OGs [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. The project rejected its H2 streamlining prediction in the opposite direction, finding anchor genomes larger at 4,110,038 bp versus 3,046,124 bp (d=+1.39, p=0.025) and richer in OGs at 2,630 versus 2,106 (d=+1.30, p=0.022), with CheckM completeness effectively identical at 94.7% versus 94.3% (p=0.93), ruling out a quality artifact [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. Applying a corrected triple-signal detector (PFAM PF02085, PFAM PF22678, and a CXXCH heme-binding motif count of ≥4), it found corrected iron-reduction rates of 55.6% for anchor_deep, 40.0% for anchor_shallow, and 40.9% for soil_baseline against originals of 11.1%, 50.0%, and 20.1%, with all pairwise Fisher tests non-significant (deep versus shallow OR 1.88, p=0.46; deep versus baseline OR 1.80, p=0.49; shallow versus baseline OR 0.96, p=1.0), a [[concepts/functional-marker-validation]] correction that removes the original contrast while leaving the sulfite-reduction side supported [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. It also reported that the Bacillota_B universe held 334 genomes in the KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome against the v1.1 plan's estimate of 6,700, and that CXXCH motif counting carried most of the corrected signal because multi-heme cytochrome PFAMs were sparse (PF02085 4 hits, PF22678 1 hit, PF14537 0 hits) [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory].
