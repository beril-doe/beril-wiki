# Markus de Raad

ORCID: [0000-0001-8263-9198](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-8263-9198)

## Contributions

The project [[summaries/lignin_community_enrichment__REPORT]] analyzed user-provided 16S V3–V4 and ITS2 amplicon data from 21 samples across 7 groups with 3 replicates per group, using 97% OTU clustering, Bray–Curtis distances, PCoA, and PERMANOVA (a permutation-based test of community-composition differences); all 42 paired-end libraries had Q30 > 0.94 [src: lignin_community_enrichment]. It found that one round of lignin enrichment restructured the bacterial community from a base dominated by unclassified taxa (Incertae Sedis, 44.3%) with Pseudomonas at <0.1% to communities in which Pseudomonas reached 39.3% and Acinetobacter 25.2%, together >64% of 16S reads, while Shannon diversity fell from 6.46 to 3.16 and observed OTUs from 1,594 to 163, a 90% reduction [src: lignin_community_enrichment]. The global PERMANOVA attributed 97.9% of community variance to treatment (F=111.35, R²=0.979, p=0.001) and the Base-versus-Round-1 model gave F=394.42, R²=0.992, p=0.008 [src: lignin_community_enrichment]. The project also reported that adding labile carbon to lignin raised Acinetobacter from 25.2% to 41.7%, lowered Pseudomonas from 39.3% to 23.0%, raised Aeromonas from 0.1% to 20.2%, and reduced Shannon diversity from 3.16 to 2.41 and Pielou's evenness from 0.62 to 0.49 [src: lignin_community_enrichment].

The same project tested sequential passaging and found that Round-2 communities segregated by their Round-1 carbon history: lignin-history groups L-L and L-LC were Pseudomonas-dominated at 52–53%, whereas lignin-plus-labile-history groups LC-L and LC-LC were Acinetobacter-dominant at 31–34% with Pseudomonas at 24–26% and Enterobacter at 2.7–18.1% [src: lignin_community_enrichment]. In the 12-sample Round-2 factorial PERMANOVA, Round-1 history explained 58.9% of variance (F=14.31, R²=0.589, p=0.002) versus 32.7% for current Round-2 carbon source (F=4.85, R²=0.327, p=0.018), and communities with different histories did not converge under identical Round-2 conditions (mean Bray–Curtis 0.507 for L-L versus LC-L and 0.486 for L-LC versus LC-LC, against 0.441 for L-L versus L-LC and 0.306 for LC-L versus LC-LC), with a reported memory index of ~0.50 and OTU retention of 81% from L to L-L and 91% from LC to LC-LC [src: lignin_community_enrichment]. These results feed [[concepts/ecological-memory]] and [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]], the latter also via genus-level responses: Flavobacterium rose from 0.1% to 14.1% under lignin-only enrichment (CLR effect +11.2 in the Base-versus-L comparison) but fell to 1.4% with labile-carbon co-supplementation, Comamonas rose from 0.1% to 3.6–5.1% in Round-2 groups (17–51× enrichment), and Aminobacter, absent from base and Round-1 groups, reached 7.6% in L-L and 6.4% in LC-L [src: lignin_community_enrichment].

On the fungal side, the project reported strong but poorly reproducible ITS responses: the base community had 253 OTUs and Shannon 3.50 with Fusarium at 26.5%; lignin-only enrichment left 8 OTUs and Shannon 1.37 (Fusarium 43.4%, Fusicolla 30.3%), lignin plus labile carbon left 6 OTUs and Shannon 0.62 (Chrysosporium 61.4%, Aspergillus 27.2%), and in Round 2 Malassezia reached 63.4% in L-L and 50.0% in LC-L while Pleurotus reached 50.0% in LC-LC [src: lignin_community_enrichment]. Within-group Bray–Curtis distances reached 0.99–1.00 for several Round-2 ITS groups against 0.09 for 16S, the ITS Round-2 history effect was not statistically detectable (R²=0.142, p=0.090), and the preregistered Procrustes comparison of 16S and ITS ordinations was not completed because the extreme ITS variability would make the rotation uninformative — a cross-marker limitation slotted into [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]] [src: lignin_community_enrichment]. The project documented power and processing limits explicitly: with n=3 per group, pairwise Mann–Whitney U tests have a minimum achievable p-value of 0.10 and pairwise PERMANOVA a permutation floor near p~0.10, so no individual OTUs reached FDR (false-discovery-rate) significance, though global Kruskal–Wallis tests were significant across all 7 groups (16S p=0.006–0.010; ITS p=0.015–0.041) [src: lignin_community_enrichment]. It further recorded that PERMDISP was significant for both markers (16S p=0.0004; ITS p=0.0001), so PERMANOVA signal reflects dispersion as well as location differences; that sample LL_1 was excluded after retaining 74 reads, reducing L-L to n=2; that ITS taxonomy used NCBI ITS_RefSeq_Fungi with 19,375 reference sequences rather than UNITE; and that the lignin-degradation interpretation rests on taxonomic association and literature context rather than measured gene-level pathway enrichment, with future cross-referencing of Pseudomonas, Acinetobacter, and Comamonas proposed against [[concepts/pangenome-integration]] resources [src: lignin_community_enrichment].
