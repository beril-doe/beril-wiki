# Markus de Raad

ORCID: [0000-0001-8263-9198](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-8263-9198)

## Contributions

[[summaries/lignin_community_enrichment__REPORT|Lignin Enrichment and Ecological Memory in Microbial Communities]] analyzed 42 paired-end 16S V3–V4 and ITS2 amplicon libraries from 21 samples across 7 groups using 97% OTU clustering, Bray–Curtis distances, PCoA, and PERMANOVA. [src: lignin_community_enrichment] The project found that one round of lignin enrichment reduced bacterial Shannon diversity from 6.46 to 3.16 and observed OTUs from 1,594 to 163, while [[entities/pseudomonas-aeruginosa|Pseudomonas]] reached 39.3% and [[entities/acinetobacter-baylyi-adp1|Acinetobacter]] reached 25.2% of 16S reads. [src: lignin_community_enrichment] The project found that labile-carbon co-supplementation increased Acinetobacter from 25.2% to 41.7% and Aeromonas from 0.1% to 20.2%, while reducing Pseudomonas from 39.3% to 23.0%. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The project tested whether Round-1 carbon history affected Round-2 bacterial communities and found that history explained 58.9% of variance (F=14.31, R²=0.589, p=0.002), compared with 32.7% for the current Round-2 carbon source (F=4.85, R²=0.327, p=0.018); its reported memory index was approximately 0.50. [src: lignin_community_enrichment] The project found stronger fungal compositional changes but poorer replicate consistency than in bacteria: fungal Shannon diversity declined from 3.50 in the base community to 1.37 under lignin-only enrichment and 0.62 under lignin plus labile carbon, while several Round-2 groups had within-group Bray–Curtis distances of 0.99–1.00 compared with 0.09 for 16S. [src: lignin_community_enrichment] The fungal global PERMANOVA gave R²=0.582 and p=0.001, but the Round-2 history effect was not statistically detectable (R²=0.142, p=0.090). [src: lignin_community_enrichment]
