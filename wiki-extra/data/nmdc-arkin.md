# NMDC Multi-omics

Provider: NMDC · [Website](https://microbiomedata.org/)

## Description

Multi-omics analysis data from the National Microbiome Data Collaborative. Includes functional annotations, embeddings, metabolomics, proteomics, lipidomics, and microbial trait data for integrated microbiome analysis.

## Purpose

Enable integrated microbiome analysis across multiple omics layers. Combine metabolomics, proteomics, and metagenomics with standardized annotations and embeddings for comprehensive sample characterization.

## Scale

- studies: 48
- metabolomics_records: 3M+
- lipidomics_records: 1.4M+
- tables: 60+
