# Pangenome Collection

Provider: KBase, DOE · [Website](https://www.kbase.us/) · DOI: [10.1038/nbt.4163](https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.4163)

## Description

Pangenome data for 293,059 genomes across 27,690 microbial species derived from GTDB r214. Includes core/accessory gene classification, functional annotations, and ANI relationships.

## Purpose

Enable comparative genomics at scale. Understand core vs accessory genome content, functional distributions, and evolutionary patterns across bacterial and archaeal species. Answer questions about what makes species unique and what they share.

## Scale

- genomes: 293,059
- species: 27,690
- genes: 1B+
- tables: 14
