# Biological Evidence Integration

Biological evidence integration is the deliberate combination of measurements, annotations, metadata, models, and experimental phenotypes so that a biological claim is supported by more than one evidentiary route. This corpus speaks directly to the problem because its projects range from cross-tenant data discovery and multi-omics experiments to ecological surveys, metabolic models, phage targeting, and adversarial review. Across these settings, the central lesson is that integration increases explanatory power only when provenance, identifier meaning, statistical units, and biological endpoints remain explicit.

## Literature Context

Published microbiome research establishes that community composition and function are dynamic, context-dependent, and linked to host phenotypes. Diet can alter the human gut microbiome rapidly and reproducibly, while antibiotic exposure can perturb community structure and host immune responses, including vaccine immunity. [PMID 24336217](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24336217/) [PMID 37491458](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37491458/) [PMID 31491384](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31491384/) Succession across the human lifespan and microbiome patterns associated with healthy ageing further show that biological interpretation depends on temporal and ecological context rather than on taxonomic presence alone. [PMID 35906422](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35906422/) [PMID 33619379](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33619379/) Multi-omics studies have connected microbial genes, metabolites, proteins, and host disease states across inflammatory bowel disease, ulcerative colitis, atherosclerosis, and other conditions, including links between *Bacteroides vulgatus* proteases and disease severity. [PMID 37932270](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37932270/) [PMID 35087228](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35087228/) [PMID 40785047](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40785047/)

This literature is consistent with the corpus’s emphasis on conditional mechanisms and on separating occurrence, encoded capability, metabolite association, and demonstrated activity. Work mapping gut-bacterial drug metabolism shows why genes and pathways can be biologically consequential without being interchangeable with direct measurements of substrate use or phenotype. [PMID 31158845](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31158845/) Likewise, the corpus’s ecological-memory result—that prior carbon history explained more bacterial community variance than the subsequent carbon source—is compatible with a field that treats microbiomes as histories shaped by perturbation and succession, but it extends that framing beyond host-associated disease into experimentally manipulated environmental communities. Its respiratory-chain and model–fitness analyses add a more explicit test of condition dependence: the same organism and machinery can have different apparent importance across carbon sources, and model agreement does not guarantee measured flux or growth.

The corpus is most distinctive in making the infrastructure and auditability of integration part of the biological evidence itself. Multi-cohort and multi-omics studies demonstrate the value of combining modalities, but the BERDL analyses expose failure modes that are often upstream of biological modeling: ambiguous resource identity, tenant-specific provenance, rank-mismatched identifiers, pooled workflow units, and joins that are executable yet biologically wrong. This complements contamination-aware microbiome work, which showed the importance of distinguishing tissue-resident organisms from contaminants, and transmission studies that require careful definition of shared microbial entities. [PMID 33382980](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33382980/) [PMID 36653448](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36653448/) The corpus therefore extends the literature’s multi-omics integration agenda from analytical combination to evidence-chain validation. Its caution that selected concordance can coexist with broader false positives is also a useful tension with studies presenting integrated functional signatures: it argues that reproducible joins, statistical associations, and mechanistic claims should remain separate endpoints, verified through provenance checks, independent sample units, and adversarial review.

## What the Corpus Shows

**Integration is a chain of evidence, not a successful join.**  
The corpus distinguishes four increasingly strong states: shared schema keys, overlapping values, an executable join, and a scientifically interpretable result. A schema key is a field such as `genome_id` or `sample_id`; its presence indicates structural compatibility but not that the records refer to the same biological object. The BERDL atlas identified 536 potential cross-tenant bridges across 1,740 tables, 119 databases, 17 tenants, and 29 canonical keys, but only one audited use case was sample-validated. [src: berdl_data_atlas] Among 66 audited projects, 51 (77%) spanned multiple tenants, showing that integration is operationally common while not proving that every bridge is biologically meaningful. [src: berdl_data_atlas] This distinction is developed in [[concepts/cross-tenant-data-bridging]].

Resource discovery is therefore part of evidence integration. Twenty database names containing `nmdc` resolved to seven maintained resources across three tenants and six provenance classes, including NMDC resources, re-hosted NCBI and Pfam data, KBase derivatives, and a NEON namesake collision. [src: nmdc_context_audit] The genuine NMDC biosample universe contained 16,640 samples, whereas a co-hosted NCBI mirror contained 51,711,888 biosamples, creating a ~3,000× scale trap. [src: nmdc_context_audit] [[concepts/provenance-aware-resource-discovery]] explains why authority, scale, tenant, access, and freshness must accompany a resource name.

Identifier reconciliation is equally load-bearing. In one linkage, 12 of 32 pangenome joins through `ncbi_strain_identifiers` were incorrect genus matches; an MT20 label referred to *Rhodanobacter glycinis* in one resource and *Streptococcus pneumoniae* in another. [src: pitfalls] The resulting erroneous match involved 8,434 genomes, including 1,751 clinical genomes. [src: pitfalls] Stable assembly accessions, taxids, taxonomy versions, and rank-specific join keys are therefore evidence controls rather than clerical details, as shown in [[concepts/taxonomic-nomenclature-reconciliation]].

**Multiple modalities expose conditional mechanisms.**  
The strongest biological integrations do not merely stack datasets; they test whether different measurement types agree under a defined condition. In *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1, genome features, TnSeq—random barcode transposon sequencing—flux-balance analysis (FBA), mutant growth, proteomics, pangenome status, and annotations were combined. [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer] One ADP1 analysis found 73.8% FBA/TnSeq concordance for 866 genes, while a broader gapfilling evaluation found 42.5% baseline FBA accuracy and 330 false positives. [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer, annotation_gap_discovery] These results support models as useful conditional evidence, not as substitutes for phenotype measurement. [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]] develops this framework.

The respiratory-chain integration makes the condition dependence concrete. On glucose, Complex I had a growth ratio of 1.44; on quinate it had a ratio of 0.37, despite quinate producing 0.57 NADH per carbon and glucose producing 1.50 NADH per carbon. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring] Similar protein levels for Complex I, NDH-2, and ACIAD3522 under standard conditions supported passive, flux-based wiring, but the proposed interpretation of concentrated quinate-derived NADH exceeding alternative dehydrogenase capacity remained theoretical rather than a measured flux distribution. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring] The member page [[concepts/outer-membrane-lipid-homeostasis]] shows a related transcript–protein problem: Lpt-associated transcripts increased while detected LptD and LptE protein abundances moved in the opposite direction, although the proteome had a single replicate. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] RNA, protein, model, and fitness measurements can therefore describe different regulatory layers.

Integration also links molecular state to ecological history. In a lignin-enrichment experiment, Round-1 carbon history explained 58.9% of Round-2 bacterial community variance, compared with 32.7% for the current Round-2 carbon source. [src: lignin_community_enrichment] The reported memory index was ~0.50, while the fungal history effect was not statistically detectable (R²=0.142, p=0.090). [src: lignin_community_enrichment] This supports [[concepts/ecological-memory]] as a bacterial community-composition result, while showing why marker type and taxonomic resolution must remain explicit.

**Occurrence, capability, and activity must not be collapsed.**  
The corpus repeatedly separates what an organism is found near, what its genome could encode, what it produces, and what it actually consumes or performs in situ. The ENIGMA carbon census structure-resolved 83 compounds, linked 54 to KEGG, made 9 callable through predictions or measured fitness, and left 74/83 (89%) organism-dark. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1] “Organism-dark” indicates a limitation of the integrated resources, not proof that no organism uses the compound. [[concepts/provenance-aware-resource-discovery]] and [[concepts/cross-tenant-data-bridging]] provide the relevant discovery safeguards.

The Web of Microbes bridge contained 37 organisms and 589 metabolites, but the 2018 snapshot lacked organism consumption data and had ambiguous compound matching. [src: webofmicrobes_explorer] A linked Fitness Browser analysis could therefore support gene–metabolite hypotheses without establishing environmental utilization. In a matched FW300 comparison, GapMind agreed with 13/13 comparisons and Fitness Browser with 21/21, while BacDive agreed with only 3/7. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency] The selected concordance is informative but cannot be generalized to all pathways, organisms, or conditions; this boundary is central to [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]].

Ecological metadata has its own measurement grain. Of 2,759 NMDC workflow runs, 1,067 were pooled from multiple biosamples, so analysis at `workflow_run_id` avoided pseudo-replication—the artificial inflation of sample size by treating one pooled measurement as several independent observations. [src: euk_in_prok_correlates] Each pooled run inherited metadata from the biosample with the minimum identifier, introducing possible label noise. [src: euk_in_prok_correlates] Thus, even a 99%+ metadata linkage rate does not guarantee a uniquely correct environmental label, as discussed in [[concepts/pooled-run-pseudoreplication-and-metadata-label-noise]].

**Independent scrutiny determines whether integration is trustworthy.**  
Adversarial review adds a distinct quality-assurance layer by challenging citations, feature construction, confounding, null models, sample-size claims, and provenance. Standard review described one project as exceptionally sophisticated with no critical issues, whereas adversarial review found 5 critical and 6 important issues. [src: discoveries] Across 9 adversarial review rounds, recurring citation failures included PMID hijacking and hallucinated author lists; load-bearing references therefore require verification of author, title, journal, PMID, and DOI through PubMed. [src: discoveries]

The same scrutiny applies to statistical integration. The 4 pre-registered hypotheses used Bonferroni α=0.0125 and all survived that correction, whereas only M14, M21, and M23 were genuine post-hoc metric corrections. [src: discoveries] This supports correcting actual hypothesis-test families rather than treating every documented methodological revision as a separate test. [src: discoveries] [[concepts/adversarial-research-quality-assurance]] further requires leakage checks, explicit confounder adjustment, empirical or simulated null distributions, independently counted sample units, and execution checks for large joins.

## Tensions and Caveats

The corpus does not support a universal hierarchy in which one evidence type always outranks another. The conflict [[conflicts/conflict--acinetobacter_adp1_explorer--adp1_triple_essentiality--alphafold_msa_annotation--cc872022]] captures disagreement over whether conservation and essentiality reliably predict function. Broad associations exist: metal-important genes were 87.4% core versus 76.9% for baseline genes. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] Yet pathway-level comparisons can fail to distinguish active dependencies from latent capabilities, and FBA performance can be substantially lower in broader evaluations. [src: annotation_gap_discovery, discoveries] Conservation, essentiality, capability, and measured dependency should therefore remain separate endpoints.

A related conflict, [[conflicts/conflict--acinetobacter_adp1_explorer--adp1_triple_essentiality--annotation_gap_discovery--8f009ad9]], concerns model predictions versus phenotypes. Selected ADP1 and FW300 comparisons show substantial agreement, but broader evaluations include false positives and respiratory mismatches. [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer, annotation_gap_discovery, fw300_metabolic_consistency] Model–data disagreement may reflect media, objective functions, organism scope, endpoint definitions, or missing reactions rather than a single model failure.

Integration can also reveal associations that do not transport. Within-study environmental models achieved R²=0.35 under random cross-validation and R²=+0.17 ± 0.06 within NEON, but out-of-study validation produced R²=−0.30 and detection AUC=0.56. [src: euk_in_prok_correlates] The conflict [[conflicts/conflict--ecotype_analysis--ecotype_env_reanalysis--euk_in_prok_correlates]] therefore cautions that real within-collection structure may coexist with poor cross-study generalization. Environmental association is not automatically portable prediction.

Finally, integration is constrained by coverage and ownership. DOE accounts for approximately 78% of BERDL tables, while pangenome data are 79% KBase-owned and mobile-phage data are 96% PhageFoundry-owned. [src: berdl_data_atlas] These concentrations can make apparent biological coverage reflect funding, tenancy, sampling, and curation history. The conflict [[conflicts/conflict--berdl_data_atlas--cofitness_coinheritance--ecotype_env_reanalysis--ff2b02eb]] emphasizes that schema-level connectivity and documented use are not equivalent to validated biological evidence.
