# Integrative Microbial Phenotyping

Integrative microbial phenotyping connects what microbes are genetically capable of doing with what they actually do under defined conditions, across molecular measurements, ecological context, and fitness assays. The corpus speaks directly to this problem because it combines genome annotation, pangenomes, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, metabolic models, community composition, and experimental growth phenotypes—while repeatedly showing that an apparent phenotype depends on measurement scale, condition, representation, and validation design. [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer, adp1_triple_essentiality, berdl_data_atlas]

## Literature Context

Published work increasingly treats phenotype as a multidimensional and context-dependent state rather than as the output of a single molecular layer. Multimodal and spatiotemporal atlases have integrated transcriptomic, proteomic, cellular, and spatial measurements to resolve heterogeneity during ageing, tissue homeostasis, regeneration, and injury repair. [PMID 38649488](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38649488/) [PMID 38627598](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38627598/) [PMID 38627596](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38627596/) Integrative multiomics has also been used to connect molecular patterns with a mechanistic candidate, as in the proposed protective role of asporin in pulmonary arterial hypertension. [PMID 39167456](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39167456/) Cross-ancestry regulatory atlases further show that gene expression, isoform usage, and splicing can represent distinct but related layers of biological state. [PMID 38781368](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38781368/) The [[concepts/multi-omics-integration]] framework is therefore consistent with a broader literature in which integration is valuable precisely because different assays capture different dimensions of phenotype.

The corpus extends that literature into microbial systems by aligning genome annotation, pangenome status, metabolic modeling, RB-TnSeq fitness, knockout growth, and proteomics around experimentally defined carbon sources. Its ADP1 analyses make a stronger microbial case for distinguishing metabolic capability from condition-specific requirement, while the [[concepts/respiratory-chain-wiring]] results show how apparently similar growth phenotypes can arise from different respiratory constraints. This emphasis is consistent with bacterial studies showing that transcriptional states vary with environmental and physiological conditions, and that growth measurements themselves are shaped by experimental context. [PMID 24068961](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24068961/) [PMID 33082255](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33082255/) Studies of piezophilic growth and transient methylation states likewise support the corpus claim that a microbial phenotype is not necessarily an invariant property of a strain or gene. [PMID 36985211](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36985211/) [PMID 36454087](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36454087/)

The corpus also extends ecological work on history dependence. Experimental studies have shown that bacterial communities can preserve prior resistance states, and that social or signaling phenotypes depend on environmental conditions and population context. [PMID 34819498](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34819498/) [PMID 30536518](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30536518/) [PMID 35294222](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35294222/) The lignin-enrichment results add a quantitative community-level example in which prior carbon history explains more subsequent bacterial community variance than the current carbon source, while the absence of a comparable fungal signal qualifies simple generalization across taxa. This taxon-specific contrast is a distinctive contribution of the corpus rather than a direct contradiction of the prior bacterial literature.

Finally, the corpus is in tension with any interpretation of multiomics as automatically mechanistic. Its ADP1 model concordance coexists with limited predictive accuracy, and its Caulobacter case shows transcript–protein discordance that is compatible with—but does not establish—post-transcriptional regulation. These cautions are compatible with literature demonstrating that regulatory control can operate through signaling and RNA-processing layers beyond steady-state transcript abundance. [PMID 28489820](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28489820/) [PMID 34121356](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34121356/) The corpus’s explicit focus on representation, portability, negative-search validation, and unresolved model–fitness conflicts therefore goes beyond the candidate literature by treating analytical sensitivity and provenance as components of biological interpretation, not merely technical afterthoughts.

## What the Corpus Shows

**Phenotypes emerge from aligned evidence layers, not from a single assay.** Integrative phenotyping is strongest when independent modalities interrogate the same biological object. In *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1, one framework connected genome features, transposon-sequencing fitness, flux-balance analysis (FBA; a constraint-based model of possible metabolic fluxes), mutant growth, proteomics, pangenome classification, and functional annotations. [src: acinetobacter_adp1_explorer] A related deletion-phenotype analysis joined growth measurements with TnSeq classifications, annotations, pangenome status, 2,034 genes, and 8 carbon sources. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes] Direct comparison of FBA, RB-TnSeq, complete-gene knockouts, mutant growth rates, and proteomics extended that design from parallel evidence to concordance analysis. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality]

This integration can distinguish capability from condition-specific requirement. In ADP1, carbon sources selected different respiratory configurations: quinate and acetate required Complex I, acetate additionally required cytochrome bo3 and ACIAD3522, lactate specifically required cytochrome bo3, glucose had no specifically required respiratory component, and urea was broadly demanding. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring] These results show why total metabolic output is not enough: quinate produced 4 NADH, or 0.57 NADH per carbon, yet Complex I had a growth ratio of 0.37; glucose produced 9 NADH, or 1.50 NADH per carbon, while Complex I had a growth ratio of 1.44. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring] The stoichiometric interpretation—that concentrated NADH production can exceed alternative dehydrogenase capacity—is proposed by the model and is not a direct measurement of intracellular flux. [src: respiratory_chain_wiring]

**Condition and history are part of the phenotype.** A microbial phenotype is not necessarily an intrinsic property of a strain or gene; it can depend on the current environment and on prior exposure. In a lignin-enrichment experiment, ecological memory—the persistence of community differences caused by earlier conditions after current conditions change—was observed in bacterial communities. Round-1 carbon history explained 58.9% of Round-2 community variance (F=14.31, R²=0.589, p=0.002), while the current Round-2 carbon source explained 32.7% (F=4.85, R²=0.327, p=0.018). [src: lignin_community_enrichment] Under identical Round-2 conditions, history-associated Bray–Curtis distances were 0.507 and 0.486 in the reported comparisons, and the memory index was ~0.50. [src: lignin_community_enrichment] Lignin-history groups were Pseudomonas-dominated at 52–53%, whereas lignin-plus-labile-carbon histories were Acinetobacter-dominant at 31–34%. [src: lignin_community_enrichment]

The bacterial result did not generalize automatically to fungi: fungal history was not statistically detectable (R²=0.142, p=0.090), and fungal within-group Bray–Curtis distances reached 0.99–1.00 for several groups compared with 0.09 for 16S. [src: lignin_community_enrichment] Thus, community phenotype requires attention to taxonomic compartment, marker system, and ecological timescale. The same principle appears in cell-level stress responses: the Caulobacter envelope response separated into early-only, shared, and late-consequence ChvI regulatory cohorts rather than one uniform transcriptional program. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The published ChvI-induced regulons contained 488 genes in the tested universe, partitioned into 20 early-only genes, 10 present in both phases, and 49 late-consequence genes. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

**Cross-modal discordance is itself phenotypic information.** Agreement between RNA, protein, genotype, and fitness is useful, but disagreement can reveal regulation between transcription and observable function. In Caulobacter lipid-A-loss rescue, no canonical Lpt component was down at the transcript level; the MsbA-like CCNA_00307 was up by +0.89 with FDR 0.01 and LptC-related CCNA_03716 was up by +0.56 with FDR 0.005. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] Yet detected LptD and LptE proteins changed in the opposite direction, by -0.47 and -0.78, respectively, in the rescued-versus-intermediate comparison. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] This pattern is compatible with translational control, altered protein stability, or complex assembly effects, but it does not identify the mechanism.

Other components showed concordance: Pal increased by +2.08 at the transcript level and +2.84 at the protein level. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] The sphingolipid transporter CCNA_01226 showed transcript -0.60 with FDR 0.034 but protein log2(4672/4659) = +1.08, while CCNA_01217 protein increased by +0.77 in the same comparison. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] These observations support a component-specific response rather than a globally uniform RNA-to-protein relationship. However, the outer-membrane proteome had a single replicate per strain, so the protein-level directions lack per-protein statistical tests. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]

**Representation determines whether integration transfers.** Integrative phenotyping requires compatible feature spaces: the same biological object must be represented consistently across cohorts, annotation systems, and analytical categories. In pooled HMP2 and Franzosa metabolomics, PCA and K-means with K=4 separated samples completely by cohort rather than diagnosis; PC1 explained 79% of variance, and leave-one-study-out adjusted Rand index was 0.000 for metabolomics versus 0.113 for taxonomic ecotypes. [src: discoveries] Relative-abundance features transferred more robustly than absolute-intensity metabolomics, which required explicit correction approaches such as ComBat, surrogate variable analysis, RUV, or quantile normalization. [src: discoveries]

Namespace and category choices can also alter biological interpretation. A CLR-plus-PCA Gaussian-mixture projection assigned all 26 Kuehl samples to E3 at confidence greater than 0.97 even though Kuehl detected only 54% of training species; an alternative LDA projection across MetaPhlAn3 and Kaiju namespaces produced proportions of 27/42/31% across ecotypes. [src: discoveries] In pathway analysis, regex categories identified 3 of 52 CD-up pathways in 7 themes and produced a structurally degenerate result, whereas a MetaCyc hierarchy assigned 262/409 pathways to at least one of 12 IBD themes and supported iron/heme acquisition with OR=8.1, FDR=7e-6, and 15/52 CD-up pathways. [src: discoveries] Annotation systems likewise measured different spaces: eggNOG had COG coverage of 51% versus 8.2% for Bakta, while Bakta had GO coverage of 15% versus 7.4% for eggNOG; their union raised any-functional-annotation coverage to 77.3%. [src: discoveries]

## Tensions and Caveats

The corpus does not support treating integration as automatic evidence of mechanism. The conflict between [[conflicts/conflict--acinetobacter_adp1_explorer--adp1_triple_essentiality--annotation_gap_discovery]] shows that model concordance can coexist with limited predictive accuracy: baseline FBA accuracy was 42.5% with 330 false positives, despite reported FBA/TnSeq concordance of 73.8% for 866 genes. [src: annotation_gap_discovery, acinetobacter_adp1_explorer] Pathway completeness can also overstate use: a matched FW300-N2E3 subset showed 100% concordance across 21 Fitness Browser and 13 GapMind comparisons, while a broader analysis classified 66 of 161 complete pathways as complete-but-latent. [src: fw300_metabolic_consistency]

A related disagreement concerns respiratory interpretation. [[conflicts/conflict--aromatic_catabolism_network--respiratory_chain_wiring]] contrasts an earlier transferred-fitness interpretation in which Complex I was dispensable on glucose and lactate with an ADP1-specific analysis reporting a glucose growth ratio of 1.44, zero predicted NDH-2 flux, and no significant cross-species compensation pattern (p = 0.52). [src: aromatic_catabolism_network, respiratory_chain_wiring] These results may reflect different fitness metrics, model assumptions, species architectures, or conditions rather than a simple biological contradiction.

Integration is also vulnerable to ecological confounding and data provenance. Broad environmental AMR associations explained 2–13% of composition variance, but only 20 of 141 testable families showed significant within-family effects after FDR correction; whole-genome environmental effects were absent in 156 species (90.7%). [src: amr_environmental_resistome] The conflict [[conflicts/conflict--amr_environmental_resistome--amr_fitness_cost--amr_pangenome_atlas]] therefore cautions against reading ecological association as exposure-to-selection causality. Similarly, [[conflicts/conflict--nmdc_context_audit--paperblast_explorer--pitfalls]] records that successful joins and resource coverage do not guarantee provenance or biological authority: 20 NMDC entries resolved to 7 resources, while only 65.6% of genes with a paper link had exactly one paper and 25.6% of genes had no text-mined paper link. 

Finally, absence claims require sensitivity validation. PaperBLAST returned 0 hits for several Caulobacter lipid-A genes that had 11–18 independent NCBI hits, an approximately 80% false-negative rate for the tested set. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] A negative search should therefore mean “not detected by this method” unless independent annotation, profile searches, pathway alternatives, or functional evidence agree. [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss]
