# Bacterial Fitness-Conservation Dynamics

Bacterial fitness–conservation dynamics describe how measured mutant fitness, gene essentiality, condition dependence, and pangenome retention relate—and where they diverge. Across the corpus, conserved genes are somewhat more likely to be important or conditionally active in laboratory assays, but conservation is not a proxy for universal essentiality, natural selection, ecological activity, or complete functional annotation. The most useful interpretation is therefore a joint one: pangenome conservation supplies evolutionary context, while perturbation fitness supplies condition-specific evidence. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis, fitness_effects_conservation, conservation_vs_fitness]

## What the Corpus Shows

**Conservation carries a real but modest fitness signal.** Across 194,216 protein-coding genes from 43 bacteria, essential genes were 82% core, compared with 66% of genes that were always neutral in the tested experiments. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis] An independent analysis found the same direction: essential genes were 82% core, genes affecting 20 or more experiments were 79% core, and genes with no measured effects were 66% core; nevertheless, fitness breadth had only a weak association with core status (Spearman rho=0.086, p=8.1e-230). [src: fitness_effects_conservation] A separate 33-organism linkage found 86.1% of essential genes core versus 81.2% of non-essential genes, with a median odds ratio of 1.56; 18 of 33 organisms showed significant enrichment after Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction. [src: conservation_vs_fitness] These estimates use different cohorts and definitions, but together support conservation as a prioritization feature rather than a standalone importance score. The distinction is developed in [[concepts/comparative-conservation-metric-calibration]] and [[concepts/pangenome-conservation-fitness-decoupling]].

**Fitness is conditional, graded, and often asymmetric.** Gene essentiality means dependence on a gene under a defined assay condition, not an invariant property of the organism.  In an *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 deletion matrix spanning 8 carbon sources, mean growth ratios ranged from 0.41 on urea to 1.36 on quinate, and 625 genes, or 31% of the complete matrix, had condition-specificity scores ≥ 1.0. [src: adp1_deletion_phenotypes] In a related analysis, 333 of 478 genes (70%) showed defects on at least one of the 8 carbon sources, but only 10 (2%) were defective across all 8. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality] A trade-off gene is one with fitness below -1 in some conditions and above 1 in others; 25,271 genes, or 17.8% of those examined, met this definition. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs]

Condition dependence is not confined to accessory DNA. Genes with strong condition-specific phenotypes were 77.3% core versus 70.3% without such annotations, with OR=1.78 and p=1.8e-97. [src: fitness_effects_conservation] The synthesis identified 4,450 genes (2.7%) that were neutral overall but critical in one condition; these “ephemeral-niche” candidates were 3.0% of core genes, 1.7% of auxiliary genes, and 1.6% of singleton genes. [src: fitness_effects_conservation] Core genes also had more positive deletion effects overall: 24.4% were beneficial when deleted, compared with 19.9% of accessory genes. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis] The pattern was function-specific: core genes were more burdensome in Protein Metabolism, Motility, and RNA Metabolism by +6.2, +7.8, and +12.9 percentage points, respectively, whereas non-core Cell Wall genes were more burdensome, with a difference of -14.1 percentage points. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs] See [[concepts/condition-specific-fitness]], [[concepts/condition-dependent-gene-tradeoffs]], and [[concepts/core-genome-burden-paradox]].

**Laboratory burden does not negate evolutionary retention.** A positive deletion-fitness value means that the mutant grew better than the reference under the tested laboratory conditions. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis] Yet 28,017 genes were both costly in the laboratory and conserved in the pangenome, compared with 5,526 that were costly and dispensable. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis] The Costly + Conserved group is therefore consistent with retention because the assay omits environments, hosts, communities, and stresses in which the intact gene may provide benefits; it is evidence for possible purifying selection, not a direct measurement of natural selection. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis, core_gene_tradeoffs]

The contrasting Costly + Dispensable group is enriched for features consistent with mobile or recently acquired DNA. Such genes were 7.45 times more likely than Costly + Conserved genes to contain mobile-element keywords, and the SEED category “Phages, Prophages, Transposable elements, Plasmids” was enriched 11.7-fold. [src: costly_dispensable_genes] Only 50.8% had SEED annotations and 20.0% had KEGG annotations, versus 74.9% and 42.7% among Costly + Conserved genes; 44.5% were orphans with no ortholog group, compared with 13.1% of Costly + Conserved genes. [src: costly_dispensable_genes] These observations identify candidates for ongoing loss, but they do not establish that every such gene is nonfunctional or destined for deletion. [[concepts/costly-dispensable-gene-loss]] and [[concepts/laboratory-fitness-versus-natural-selection]] provide the evolutionary framing.

**Conservation is structured at gene, module, and annotation levels.** Fitness effects are not independent gene-by-gene noise. Independent component analysis (ICA), a method for decomposing correlated measurements into coordinated components, identified 1,116 fitness modules across 32 organisms. [src: fitness_modules] Module genes were 86.0% core versus 81.5% for all genes, with OR=1.46 and p=1.6e-87; 94.2% of modules showed significantly elevated within-module cofitness, and within-module mean |r| was 0.34 versus 0.12 in the background. [src: fitness_modules, module_conservation] These modules support coordinated conservation, but their 22.7-fold genomic-adjacency enrichment indicates that operon structure may contribute to the signal. [src: fitness_modules]

Module discovery is sensitive to method. Using an absolute component-weight threshold of |weight| >= 0.3 and a maximum of 50 genes produced more coherent modules than the initial 100-280-gene approach, raising reported enrichment from 59% to 94%. [src: fitness_modules] Module-ICA had less than 1% strict KEGG KO precision and 23.3% coverage, whereas ortholog transfer achieved 95.8% precision and 91.2% coverage. [src: fitness_modules] Thus, modules are useful process-level hypotheses, not reliable gene-level functional assignments. [[concepts/fitness-module-detection-sensitivity]] and [[concepts/fitness-matched-null-models]] explain how to interpret them.

Conservation also fails to guarantee characterization. Among AlphaFold-linked clusters, core genes had median multiple-sequence-alignment (MSA) depth 15,308, versus 5,299 for auxiliary-plus-singleton genes. [src: alphafold_msa_annotation] Nonetheless, 415,603 distinct core clusters had MSA depth below 10; 68.9% were annotated as hypothetical, only 0.033% had an EC annotation, and 0.083% mapped to KEGG. [src: alphafold_msa_annotation] A gene can therefore be broadly retained, experimentally active, and still poorly understood. [[concepts/core-gene-annotation-paradox]] is the next step for conserved functional dark matter.

**Measurement design determines what “fitness” can reveal.** Complete knockouts and RB-TnSeq (random-barcode transposon sequencing) do not measure identical phenotypes: the former removes the whole gene, whereas the latter estimates insertion-mutant performance. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality] In ADP1, only 18 of 1,933 genes (0.9%) were called essential by both methods at the RB-TnSeq threshold of 0.05; knockout-essential/RB-TnSeq-dispensable genes comprised 10.9%, and knockout-dispensable/RB-TnSeq-essential genes 15.2%. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality] At that threshold, continuous inverted fitness predicted knockout essentiality better than essentiality fraction, with AUC values of 0.700 and 0.344 in rich medium, respectively. [src: adp1_triple_essentiality] [[concepts/essentiality-assay-discordance]] details why continuous measurements should not be collapsed prematurely into binary calls.

Unequal condition coverage creates a second bias. The Fitness Browser collection included 48 organisms, but one organism, MR-1, had 121 historical conditions; genes in deeply profiled organisms have more opportunities to display a strong effect. [src: functional_dark_matter] Among 228,709 genes, 57,011 were dark and 7,787 had an absolute fitness effect of at least 2 in at least one condition. [src: functional_dark_matter] Near-neutral singleton genes may therefore reflect poor transposon coverage rather than true biological neutrality. [src: fitness_effects_conservation] [[concepts/fitness-condition-coverage-prioritization-bias]], [[concepts/genetic-perturbation-coverage-bias]], and [[concepts/transposon-callability-bias]] are essential safeguards when comparing gene classes.

## Tensions and Caveats

The central conflict is whether conservation and essentiality provide a reliable general guide to gene importance. [[conflicts/conflict--acinetobacter_adp1_explorer--adp1_triple_essentiality--alphafold_msa_annotation--cc872022]] captures the disagreement: essential genes are enriched in the core, but always-neutral genes can still be 66% core, lineage-restricted essential genes exist, and core genes can be burdensome or poorly annotated. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis, essential_genome, conservation_vs_fitness] The two principal essentiality estimates—82% versus 66% in the 43-bacterium synthesis and 86.1% versus 81.2% in the 33-organism linkage—must not be averaged because their cohorts and definitions differ. [src: conservation_fitness_synthesis, conservation_vs_fitness]

A second load-bearing caveat is that core status is sampling-conditioned. In the linked pangenome analysis, clades containing only 2 genomes could produce trivially high core fractions, while some organisms lacked usable pangenomes or were excluded for coverage and identifier problems. [src: conservation_vs_fitness] The conflict [[conflicts/conflict--conservation_vs_fitness--core_gene_tradeoffs--fitness_effects_conservation--99d5abbe]] therefore warns that enrichment may reflect genuine retention, clade sampling, callability, or all three.

Finally, laboratory cost is not natural selection. The conflict [[conflicts/conflict--conservation_fitness_synthesis--conservation_vs_fitness--field_vs_lab_fitness--e8524ac8]] emphasizes that field-versus-lab labels did not strongly predict conservation in *Desulfovibrio vulgaris*: field fitness alone had cross-validated AUC 0.517, laboratory fitness 0.531, and combined fitness 0.548; adding gene length raised AUC to 0.645. [src: field_vs_lab_fitness] These results support condition-aware, coverage-aware integration rather than ecological storytelling from assay labels alone.
