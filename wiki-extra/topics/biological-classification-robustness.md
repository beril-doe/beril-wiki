# Biological Classification Robustness

Biological classification robustness is the ability of a biological conclusion—such as an ecotype, functional category, metabolic capability, or environmental association—to remain interpretable when the dataset, annotation system, cohort, sampling design, or analytical definition changes. This corpus speaks directly to that problem because it compares classifications across genomes, functional annotations, microbiome cohorts, ecological metadata, and model-derived phenotypes, repeatedly showing that apparent biological structure can be genuine yet sensitive to representation and validation choices. [src: discoveries, ecotype_analysis, cog_analysis, plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

## What the Corpus Shows

**Robustness is multidimensional, not a single property.** A classification may be statistically reproducible while having a small biological effect, transferable within one cohort while failing externally, or stable at one taxonomic resolution while disappearing after phylogenetic control. The ecotype-functional analysis illustrates the distinction between statistical significance and effect size: across 257 tests covering 12 species and 23 COG categories, 170 tests were significant after Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction, while the mean effect sizes for adaptive and housekeeping categories were 0.0136 and 0.0064, respectively. [src: ecotype_functional_differentiation] Thus, widespread significance supports non-random differentiation but does not establish that every classification boundary has a large biological consequence. The broader interpretation is developed in [[concepts/adaptive-versus-housekeeping-functional-differentiation]].

This distinction also applies to ecological structure. Gene-content ecotypes showed environment-dominated variation in 39.5% of species, yet significant positive environmental effects were detected in only 12 species and significant negative effects in 4 species. [src: ecotype_analysis] The result supports localized or subset-specific ecological structure more strongly than a universal genome-wide environmental classification. Functional differentiation among ecotypes is compatible with that conclusion, but its lack of phylogenetic control leaves open whether the signal reflects adaptation, lineage structure, or both. [src: ecotype_analysis, ecotype_functional_differentiation] See [[concepts/ecotype-clustering-validity]] and [[concepts/genome-wide-versus-locus-specific-ecological-adaptation]].

**Representation determines what can be classified.** An ontology is a formal system for organizing biological entities and relationships; a category schema is the operational set of labels used for analysis. The corpus shows that changing either can change the conclusion. In an inflammatory-bowel-disease pathway analysis, a regex-based scheme identified 3 of 52 CD-up pathways in 7 themes, whereas a MetaCyc hierarchy assigned 262/409 pathways to at least one of 12 themes and supported iron/heme acquisition with OR=8.1 and FDR=7e-6. [src: discoveries] The biological observations did not change, but the measured pattern did. This is why curator-validated hierarchies should be primary analyses and simpler schemas treated as sensitivity analyses. [src: discoveries]

Functional annotations likewise define different measurable spaces. Across 132.5M gene clusters, eggNOG and Bakta had complementary coverage: eggNOG had higher COG, KEGG, and Pfam coverage, while Bakta had higher GO, product-description, and unique-UniRef50 coverage. Their union increased any-functional-annotation coverage to 77.3%, and Bakta rescued 11.2M clusters missed by eggNOG. [src: discoveries] The implication is not that one annotation system is universally correct, but that apparent functional absence may reflect coverage or identifier mapping. [[concepts/ontology-and-category-schema-sensitivity]] and [[concepts/composite-functional-annotation]] develop this issue.

Composite annotations are particularly important for robustness. A composite COG assignment contains multiple functional letters and may represent a multifunctional gene or linked biological module rather than annotation noise. In the 32-species comparison, composite assignments were retained as combined categories; the LV category showed +0.34% enrichment in novel or singleton genes with 76% consistency across species. [src: cog_analysis] Splitting such labels into independent counts could erase the joint mobile-and-defense interpretation. However, COG annotations covered approximately 70% of genes, and eggNOG v6 annotations may differ from original COG assignments, so composite-category results require annotation-version and unassigned-gene sensitivity analyses. [src: cog_analysis]

**Cross-cohort transfer is a stringent robustness test.** Portability means preserving a biological interpretation when samples come from another study, laboratory, platform, or feature namespace. Relative-abundance representations transferred more plausibly than absolute-intensity metabolomics because they are unitless and compositional. When HMP2 and Franzosa metabolomics were pooled, PCA and K-means with K=4 separated samples by cohort rather than diagnosis; PC1 explained 79% of variance, and leave-one-study-out adjusted Rand index was 0.000 for metabolomics versus 0.113 for taxonomic ecotypes. [src: discoveries] Absolute-intensity analyses therefore require explicit batch correction and should not be assumed portable merely because they cluster well in pooled data. [[concepts/cross-cohort-microbiome-portability]] provides the methodological details.

External validation exposed a second failure mode. A pooled classifier achieved macro one-versus-rest AUC of 0.80 but only 41% agreement on UC Davis patients; in that external cohort, `is_ibd=1` was constant and 19/22 patients were predicted as E1. [src: discoveries] Pooled discrimination therefore cannot substitute for cohort-held-out evaluation. Namespace changes can also create confident artifacts: a CLR-plus-PCA Gaussian-mixture projection assigned all 26 Kuehl samples to E3 at confidence greater than 0.97, while a different projection produced plausible proportions of 27/42/31%; Kuehl detected only 54% of training species. [src: discoveries] Missing feature coverage can thus generate confident but non-portable classifications.

**Biological structure survives some changes, but not all controls.** The ecotype analysis found functional differences across species, and the COG comparison found consistent core-versus-novel functional partitioning across 32 species, 9 phyla, and 357,623 genes. Novel or singleton genes were enriched in mobile elements by +10.88% and defense mechanisms by +2.83%, while core genes were depleted in translation by -4.65%. [src: cog_analysis] These results support real organization of gene content, but they do not prove that every observed association is ecological or adaptive.

Controls can materially reduce apparent effects. In plant-associated microbiome data, the refined compartment analysis gave PERMANOVA R² = 0.071, while an earlier R² = 0.527 fell to R² = 0.072 after excluding three genome-rich species per compartment. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] In the same broad analysis, only 50 of 5,671 plant-versus-non-plant ortholog groups retained enrichment after phylum-level control, despite 94.2% being significant in raw tests. [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] These results favor taxon-aware, adversarial validation over interpretation of unadjusted separation. The relevant ecological examples are collected in [[concepts/cross-cohort-microbiome-portability]].

**Conservation and functional importance are related but non-interchangeable.** Core genes are genes found broadly within the sampled species or clade; essentiality is inability to tolerate a specified perturbation under a specified condition. Across approximately 194,000 genes from 43 bacteria, essential genes were 82% core whereas always-neutral genes were 66% core. [src: fitness_effects_conservation] Yet the association was weak overall, and core genes had stronger fitness-effect tails in both negative and positive directions. [src: fitness_effects_conservation] Laboratory burden also reversed by category: core genes were more burdensome in Protein Metabolism, Motility, and RNA Metabolism, whereas non-core genes were more burdensome in Cell Wall functions. [src: core_gene_tradeoffs] Conservation therefore supports a graded prior about importance, not a universal rule that core or housekeeping functions are always essential, low-cost, or environmentally invariant. [[concepts/pangenome-core-boundary-and-clade-size-bias]] is a useful next step.

## Tensions and Caveats

The central tension is between genuine biological structure and analysis-dependent structure. The conflict [[conflicts/conflict--ecotype_analysis--ecotype_env_reanalysis--ecotype_functional_differentiation--57f3758f]] records that functional differentiation is clear, while its environmental assignment remains unresolved. Environmental associations can be localized to particular loci, prophage modules, or host-associated lineages without producing robust whole-genome ecotypes. [src: ecotype_analysis, ecotype_functional_differentiation, plant_microbiome_ecotypes]

A second tension concerns coverage-sensitive signals versus stricter nulls. Relaxed coverage adjustment yielded FDR q = 0.0462, whereas strict adjustment yielded FDR q = 0.130; confirmatory genus-level modes were null after predeclared testing. [src: discoveries] Exploratory functional tests can also be inflated by compositional coupling, so nominal significance is not sufficient evidence of a portable classification. This is anchored in [[conflicts/conflict--cog_analysis--ecotype_functional_differentiation--enigma_contamination_functional_potential--9514b16e]].

Finally, model and annotation evidence do not uniformly validate phenotype. Some organism-specific analyses reported high agreement between model predictions and measured phenotypes, but broader evaluations found baseline FBA accuracy of 42.5% and 330 false positives. [src: discoveries] The conflict [[conflicts/conflict--acinetobacter_adp1_explorer--adp1_triple_essentiality--annotation_gap_discovery--8f009ad9]] therefore matters: capability, dependency, activity, occurrence, and measured fitness should not be treated as interchangeable endpoints. Annotation coverage, perturbation coverage, clade sampling, environmental metadata, and feature namespaces remain load-bearing limitations.
