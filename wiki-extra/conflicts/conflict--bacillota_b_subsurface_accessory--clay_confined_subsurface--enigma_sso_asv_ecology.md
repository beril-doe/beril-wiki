# Broad subsurface specialization versus lineage- and metric-specific signals

The [[concepts/subsurface-bacillota-specialization]] record presents a disagreement over whether genomic differences support broad deep-clay specialization in Bacillota_B or instead reflect phylogenetic structure, cohort composition, and metric choice. This matters because the same accessory enrichment and genome-expansion results can be read either as recurrent adaptations to subsurface conditions or as properties of a limited, lineage-clumped sample.

## Evidence Sides

**Broad specialization and self-sufficiency:** The accessory-enrichment result identified 547 enriched OGs and supports broad deep-clay specialization, although the anchor cohort spans four orders while remaining clumped among 3 BRH-c8a genomes, 2 BRH-c4a genomes, 2 Desulfosporosinus genomes, Desulforudis, Ch130, and 1 other genome. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] The genome-expansion result further supports a self-sufficiency model for sampled cultivable subsurface Bacillota_B. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] The sulfite-reduction comparison remained strongly supported by binomial p=4×10⁻¹². [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]

**Lineage-, cohort-, and metric-limited interpretation:** The new deep cohort contained 9 genomes before quality filtering; within Bacillota_B, only SR remained significant after phylogenetic control, while WL and NiFe primarily tracked the Bacillota_B lineage. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] The corrected multi-heme cytochrome calls found no significant cohort differences, contradicting the original marker set’s suggestion of shallow iron-reduction enrichment. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] The Bacillota_B universe contained 334 genomes in the BERDL pangenome, substantially fewer than the v1.1 plan’s estimate of 6,700. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] The new analysis found no elevated 18-pathway GapMind completeness in deep genomes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface] Direct SSO geochemistry is unavailable, so the proposed northeast-to-southwest contamination plume, M5 mixing zone, M6 anaerobic zone, and redox ladder remain inferred from 16S composition and trait scores. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Possible Reconciliations

- **Hypothesis — phylogenetic reconciliation:** The 547 enriched OGs may indicate real cohort-level differentiation without showing that every enriched function is a recurrent subsurface adaptation, because the anchor cohort is lineage-clumped. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]
- **Hypothesis — scope reconciliation:** Genome size and OG count may capture accessory functions beyond the measured amino-acid pathways, so genome expansion and no elevated 18-pathway GapMind completeness could both be correct. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- **Hypothesis — sampling reconciliation:** The cultured cohort may omit extreme self-sufficient lineages represented by MAGs or single-cell genomes. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]
- **Hypothesis — environmental reconciliation:** The SSO gradients may be plausible but cannot yet establish that the reported accessory functions were caused by those gradients. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology]

## Resolving Work

- Assemble a larger, phylogenetically balanced Bacillota_B dataset spanning borehole, porewater, and rock-attached genomes; test whether the 547 OGs remain enriched after lineage correction.
- Reanalyze iron-reduction markers with corrected multi-heme cytochrome calls and independent functional annotations; test whether any cohort difference survives detector choice.
- Compare genome size, OG count, and GapMind completeness in matched MAG, single-cell, and cultured genomes; test whether self-sufficiency depends on the metric or genome source.
- Obtain direct SSO geochemistry alongside metagenomes and 16S profiles; test whether accessory-function abundance tracks the proposed plume, M5, M6, and redox gradients.
