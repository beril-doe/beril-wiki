# Adam Deutschbauer

ORCID: [0000-0003-2728-7622](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-2728-7622)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/metal_fitness_atlas__REPORT]] project combined 559 metal-related experiments across 31 organisms and 16 metals into an atlas containing 383,349 gene × metal fitness records, identifying 12,838 broad metal-important genes and 5,667 strict metal-important genes. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] It found that metal-important genes were 87.4% core-genome genes versus 76.9% among baseline genes across 22 organisms and 14 metals (OR=2.08, p=4.3e-162). [src: metal_fitness_atlas] The project also found that essential-metal tolerance genes had a mean core-fraction delta of +0.148 versus +0.081 for toxic metals, with the difference significant by one-sided Mann-Whitney U testing (U=39, p=0.015). [src: metal_fitness_atlas]

The [[summaries/metal_fitness_atlas__REPORT]] project analyzed 2,891 ortholog groups with metal phenotypes, finding that 1,182 were conserved across at least two organisms and 601 across at least three organisms; OG00128 was represented in 17 organisms and nine metals. [src: metal_fitness_atlas] It identified 559 metal-related experiments in the Fitness Browser, representing 8.2% of 6,804 total experiments, and found that cobalt, nickel, copper, aluminum, zinc, and iron had cross-species coverage in at least three organisms. [src: metal_fitness_atlas]
