# Mark Andrew Miller

ORCID: [0000-0001-9076-6066](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-9076-6066)

## Profile

The projects investigate how biological signal and metadata context shape interpretation of large microbial datasets. The [euk_in_prok_correlates__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/euk_in_prok_correlates__REPORT.md) quantified eukaryotic reads in 2,759 NMDC ReadbasedAnalysis runs: 77% contained detectable eukaryotic signal, the median eukaryotic fraction was 2.7%, the mean was 13.3%, and 20% exceeded 20%; plastid sequences comprised a median 100% of the eukaryotic signal. The project’s central methodological contribution is showing that strong cross-study environment associations were largely confounded with study or batch, while within the constant-protocol NEON study, local vegetation remained associated with eukaryotic fraction (H=119.1, p=7.6×10⁻²¹). [^euk_in_prok_correlates]

The [nmdc_context_audit__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/nmdc_context_audit__REPORT.md) examines provenance and discoverability rather than biological variation, showing that 20 NMDC-labeled database names resolve to 7 maintained resources spanning 3 tenants and 6 provenance classes. It identifies a roughly 3,000× scale trap between the genuine NMDC biosample universe of 16,640 samples and the 51,711,888-sample NCBI mirror, and documents that the freshest NMDC-related resource, `kbase.nmdc_mags`, contains 62,346 MAGs but is hosted in the `kbase` tenant. [^nmdc_context_audit] The [pseudomonas_carbon_ecology__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/pseudomonas_carbon_ecology__REPORT.md) uses GapMind profiles from 12,732 genomes across 433 *Pseudomonas* species clades to show substantial loss of plant-derived sugar pathways in the *P. aeruginosa* group relative to the *P. fluorescens/putida* group, while carbon profiles retain a significant but modest ecological signal among free-living and plant-associated species (permutation p=0.006; four-class balanced accuracy 0.408 ± 0.169). [^pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]

Across these projects, their work centers on making ecological and comparative-genomic conclusions reproducible by separating biological patterns from batch effects, naming and provenance errors, database scale differences, and limitations of prediction. The corpus therefore suggests research interests in microbial ecology, genome-derived metabolic traits, environmental and host-associated lifestyles, metagenomic contamination, and the design of trustworthy data infrastructure for large observational collections. [^euk_in_prok_correlates][^nmdc_context_audit][^pseudomonas_carbon_ecology]
