## Contributions

[[summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT]] analyzed a 25-year, +5°C soil-warming experiment at Harvard Forest Barre Woods using 42 biosamples and found that Actinobacteria increased from 0.249 to 0.315 while Acidobacteria decreased from 0.035 to 0.024 in warmed organic soil. [src: harvard_forest_warming] [[summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT]] used PERMANOVA to attribute 7.6% of genus-level Bray–Curtis variance to treatment, 30.6% to soil horizon, and 41% to the treatment-by-horizon factor. [src: harvard_forest_warming] [[summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT]] found comparable DNA and RNA functional responses after removing the horizon-by-incubation confound, with direct-sample treatment effects of 12.7% R² for DNA in mineral soil, 11.4% R² for RNA in mineral soil, and 10.0% R² for RNA in organic soil. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

[[summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT]] found that five of 57 curated carbon-cycling hits were among 428 heated-up DNA hits in organic soil, yielding a Fisher odds ratio of 2.78 and one-sided p=0.042. [src: harvard_forest_warming] [[summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT]] detected nominal RNA increases for pmoA of log2 fold change +0.730 in organic soil and +0.743 in mineral soil, and found that heated mineral samples had 155 ± 4 detectable metabolites per sample versus 167 ± 9 in controls. [src: harvard_forest_warming]

[[summaries/snipe_defense_system__REPORT]] evaluated [[entities/snipe-defense-system]] across the KBase Data Lakehouse pangenome and found 4,572 DUF4041/PF13250-containing gene clusters across 1,696 species and 33 bacterial and archaeal phyla. [src: snipe_defense_system] [[summaries/snipe_defense_system__REPORT]] corrected the SNIPE nuclease-family assignment to PF13455/Mug113 rather than canonical GIY-YIG PF01541, and found that zero surveyed gene clusters contained both DUF4041/PF13250 and PF01541. [src: snipe_defense_system] [[summaries/snipe_defense_system__REPORT]] used Fitness Browser RB-TnSeq measurements from 168 *E. coli* K-12 experiments per ManXYZ gene and found mean fitness values of −0.249 for *manX*, −0.171 for *manY*, and −0.082 for *manZ*. [src: snipe_defense_system]
