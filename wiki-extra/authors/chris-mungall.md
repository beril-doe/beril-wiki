# Chris Mungall

ORCID: [0000-0002-6601-2165](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6601-2165)

## Profile

Their projects investigate how microbial systems respond to environmental pressures and how genomic features shape microbial fitness. In the [harvard_forest_warming__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/harvard_forest_warming__REPORT.md), they analyzed a 25-year, +5°C soil-warming experiment at Harvard Forest and found a modest but measurable community response: Actinobacteria increased from 0.249 to 0.315 in organic soil, while Acidobacteria decreased from 0.035 to 0.024. Functional-gene composition explained approximately 12% of variance in DNA and 10–11% in RNA; after accounting for a horizon–incubation confound, the data did not support the hypothesis that RNA responds more strongly than DNA. [^harvard_forest_warming]

In the [snipe_defense_system__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/snipe_defense_system__REPORT.md), they characterized [snipe-defense-system](../../wiki/entities/snipe-defense-system.md) across the BERDL pangenome, linking phage defense to the fitness costs of losing the ManXYZ transporter and correcting its domain annotation. The analysis identified 4,572 DUF4041/PF13250-containing clusters across 1,696 species and 33 phyla, with 86.7% classified as accessory or singleton, supporting the interpretation that SNIPE is frequently mobile rather than stably core-inherited. RB-TnSeq measurements across 168 *E. coli* K-12 experiments showed strong ManXYZ defects particularly on D-glucosamine and D-mannose, while domain analysis supported PF13455/Mug113—not canonical GIY-YIG PF01541—as the relevant nuclease family. [^snipe_defense_system]

Taken together, their projects center on extracting testable biological conclusions from large, heterogeneous microbial datasets while explicitly examining confounding, annotation error, and evidence strength. The corpus suggests research interests in environment-dependent microbial community and functional responses, genome-wide fitness effects, phage–host interactions, defense-system mobility, and the use of comparative genomics to refine functional hypotheses. [^harvard_forest_warming][^snipe_defense_system]
