# Sierra Moxon

ORCID: [0000-0002-8719-7760](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-8719-7760)

## Contributions

[[summaries/metabolic_capability_dependency__REPORT]] compared genomic metabolic capability with experimentally measured metabolic dependency across 1,695 pathway–organism pairs from 48 organisms using GapMind pathway-completeness predictions, Fitness Browser gene-fitness data, and SEED subsystem annotations. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] The project classified 267 pairs (15.8%) as latent capabilities, 547 (32.3%) as intermediate, and 881 (51.9%) as active dependencies, and found that pathway category strongly predicted dependency class (χ²=163.6, df=4, p=2.5×10⁻³⁴). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] It found that carbon-source utilization pathways were latent in 217 of 892 cases (24.3%), compared with 48 of 735 amino-acid biosynthesis cases (6.5%). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

The project tested relationships between dependency class, conservation, pangenome openness, and within-species metabolic clustering. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] It found no support for a pathway-level conservation difference between active dependencies and latent capabilities (Mann–Whitney U p=0.94; rank-biserial r=0.052), while latent-capability rate correlated positively with pangenome openness across 22 species clades (Spearman ρ=0.69, p=0.0004). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] It clustered metabolic profiles in 10 species and found silhouette scores above 0.2 for all species, ranging from 0.349 in *PALSA-747* sp. to 0.894 in *Salmonella enterica*. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] It also found significant associations between metabolic clusters and isolation environment in *Salmonella enterica* (χ²=1570.2, df=25, p<0.0001) and *Phenylobacterium* sp. (χ²=12.2, df=1, p=0.0005). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]
