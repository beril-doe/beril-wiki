# Christopher Neely

## Contributions

[[summaries/metabolic_capability_dependency__REPORT|Metabolic Capability vs Metabolic Dependency]] compared genomic metabolic capability with experimentally measured metabolic dependency across 1,695 pathway–organism pairs from 48 organisms using GapMind, Fitness Browser gene-fitness data, and SEED subsystem annotations, classifying 267 pairs (15.8%) as latent capabilities, 547 (32.3%) as intermediate, and 881 (51.9%) as active dependencies. [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] The project found that carbon-source utilization pathways were latent in 217 of 892 cases (24.3%), compared with 48 of 735 amino-acid biosynthesis pathways (6.5%), while latent capability rate correlated positively with pangenome openness across 22 species clades (Spearman ρ = 0.69, p = 0.0004). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency] It also clustered metabolic profiles for 10 species, with silhouette scores ranging from 0.349 for *PALSA-747* sp. to 0.894 for *Salmonella enterica*, and found significant associations between metabolic clusters and isolation environment in *Salmonella enterica* (χ² = 1570.2, df = 25, p < 0.0001) and *Phenylobacterium* sp. (χ² = 12.2, df = 1, p = 0.0005). [src: metabolic_capability_dependency]

[[summaries/nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology__REPORT|Community Metabolic Ecology via NMDC × Pangenome Integration]] integrated NMDC community taxonomic and metabolomics data with GTDB pangenomes and GapMind records across 220 samples, 27,690 GTDB species, and 80 pathways, finding that 11 of 13 amino-acid biosynthesis pathways had negative correlations with ambient amino-acid metabolite intensity (binomial sign test p = 0.011). [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] The project found significant negative correlations for leucine biosynthesis (r = −0.390, q = 0.022, n = 62) and arginine biosynthesis (r = −0.297, q = 0.049, n = 80) after Benjamini–Hochberg correction. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] It also found that pathway-completeness profiles differentiated Soil and Freshwater communities: PCA PC1 and PC2 explained 49.4% and 16.6% of variance, respectively, and Soil versus Freshwater communities differed on PC1 with Mann–Whitney U = 3,674 and p < 0.0001. [src: nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
