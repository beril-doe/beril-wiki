# Christopher Neely

ORCID: [0000-0002-2620-8948](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2620-8948)

## Profile

Christopher Neely’s projects investigate how genomic metabolic potential relates to experimentally measured dependency at both organismal and community scales. In [metabolic_capability_dependency__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/metabolic_capability_dependency__REPORT.md), the analysis compared 1,695 pathway–organism pairs across 48 organisms and classified 267 (15.8%) complete pathways as latent capabilities, 547 (32.3%) as intermediate, and 881 (51.9%) as active dependencies; carbon-utilization pathways were latent in 24.3% of cases, compared with 6.5% for amino-acid biosynthesis. [^metabolic_capability_dependency] The same project found no pathway-level support for greater conservation of active dependencies over latent capabilities (Mann–Whitney U p = 0.94), but found a positive association between latent-capability rate and pangenome openness across 22 species clades (Spearman ρ = 0.69, p = 0.0004). [^metabolic_capability_dependency] It also identified metabolic clustering in all 10 examined species, with silhouette scores from 0.349 to 0.894, and significant associations between metabolic clusters and isolation environment in *Salmonella enterica* and *Phenylobacterium* sp. [^metabolic_capability_dependency]

In [nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology__REPORT.md), the project integrated community taxonomic, metabolomic, pangenome, and pathway-completeness data across 220 samples, 27,690 GTDB species, and 80 pathways. [^nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] Across 13 testable amino-acid biosynthesis pathways, 11 (85%) had negative correlations between community completeness and ambient amino-acid intensity; leucine (r = −0.390, q = 0.022, n = 62) and arginine (r = −0.297, q = 0.049, n = 80) remained significant after false-discovery-rate correction. [^nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] The analysis also found strong ecosystem differentiation: Soil and Freshwater communities occupied nearly non-overlapping pathway-completeness regions, with Soil and Freshwater median PC1 values of +3.86 and −6.28, respectively, and pairwise p < 0.0001. [^nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology] Across these projects, their work centers on latent metabolic capabilities, resource dependence, pangenome dynamics, community-scale Black Queen Hypothesis signals, and environment-linked metabolic organization; the corpus therefore suggests research interests in connecting genome-resolved pathway inference with fitness experiments, metabolomics, and ecological context. [^metabolic_capability_dependency][^nmdc_community_metabolic_ecology]
