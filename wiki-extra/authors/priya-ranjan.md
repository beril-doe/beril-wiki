# Priya Ranjan

ORCID: [0000-0002-0357-1939](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0357-1939)

## Contributions

[[summaries/pgp_pangenome_ecology__REPORT]] analyzed plant-growth-promoting (PGP) gene distribution, co-occurrence, environmental enrichment, and core/accessory status across 27,702 species, including 32,736 PGP gene clusters, 27,690 species with GapMind pathway scores, and 291,279 genomes with isolation-source metadata. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] The project found eight of 10 focal-gene pairs significantly associated after Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery-rate (BH-FDR) correction; the strongest positive association was pqqC × acdS (OR = 7.24, n = 286 co-occurring species, q = 1.2e-83), while nifH was negatively associated with hcnC (OR = 0.23, q = 5.8e-29) and pqqC (OR = 0.57, q = 2.9e-19). [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] It compared 1,039 soil/rhizosphere species with 10,233 species from other environments and found that acdS, pqqC, and hcnC were enriched in soil, whereas nifH was depleted; acdS prevalence was 15.8% versus 2.6% (OR = 7.02, q = 5.1e-62), and the enrichment remained strong after phylum-level fixed effects (OR = 6.98, p = 4.8e-61). [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

The project assessed PGP genes against a 46.8% genome-wide core baseline and found that all 13 genes had significantly higher core fractions, including pqqC at 81.5% core, acdS at 70.4%, nifH at 63.8%, and pqqD at 55.5%; the mean accessory fraction across PGP genes was 29.7%, compared with 53.2% genome-wide. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] It also found a negative correlation between pangenome singleton fraction and PGP gene richness (Spearman ρ = −0.195, p = 2.0e-97, n = 11,272 species with ≥2 genomes), and identified pqqD as having the highest singleton fraction among the highlighted genes at 27.5%. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] Using GapMind pathway scores, the project found that ipdC occurred in 2.5% of species with complete tryptophan biosynthesis pathways versus 0.9% with incomplete pathways (Fisher OR = 2.81, p = 6.3e-10), while tyrosine pathway completeness showed a similar association with ipdC (OR = 3.62, p = 2.3e-11). [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]
