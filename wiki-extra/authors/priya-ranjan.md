# Priya Ranjan

ORCID: [0000-0002-0357-1939](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0357-1939)

## Profile

Priya Ranjan’s project investigated the distribution, ecological associations, environmental enrichment, and pangenome status of plant-growth-promoting (PGP) genes across 27,702 species in the BERDL dataset, including 32,736 PGP gene clusters and 291,279 genomes with isolation-source metadata. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] It found that *acdS*, *pqqC*, and *hcnC* were enriched among soil/rhizosphere species, with *acdS* present in 15.8% of soil/rhizosphere species versus 2.6% of species from other environments (OR = 7.02), while *nifH* was depleted in soil-classified species (12.9% versus 19.7%; OR = 0.60). [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] The strongest positive gene association was *pqqC* × *acdS* (OR = 7.24, q = 1.2e-83), whereas *nifH* was negatively associated with *hcnC* and *pqqC*. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology]

The project also challenged the hypothesis that PGP genes are predominantly horizontally transferred accessory genes: all 13 analyzed PGP genes had higher core fractions than the 46.8% genome-wide baseline, and the highlighted genes ranged from 55.5% core for *pqqD* to 81.5% for *pqqC*. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] Its analysis further linked *ipdC* presence to pathway completeness, with *ipdC* found in 2.5% of species with complete tryptophan biosynthesis pathways versus 0.9% with incomplete pathways (OR = 2.81), although a similar association with tyrosine completeness meant the result did not establish a tryptophan-specific mechanism. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] Taken together, the supplied project material centers on comparative microbial genomics, gene–gene co-occurrence, environmental specialization, and the relationship between accessory-genome structure and PGP function, suggesting research interests in identifying ecologically structured microbial traits and testing whether genomic context predicts plant-associated functionality. [src: pgp_pangenome_ecology] The project is summarized in [[summaries/pgp_pangenome_ecology__REPORT]].
