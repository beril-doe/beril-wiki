# Aindrila Mukhopadhyay

ORCID: [0000-0002-6513-7425](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6513-7425)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/counter_ion_effects__REPORT]] project tested whether counter ions, particularly chloride delivered by metal salts, confounded genome-wide metal-fitness measurements across 71 NaCl experiments, 10,821 metal-important gene records, 86 organism × metal pairs, 14 metals, and 19 organisms, and found that 4,304 records (39.8%) overlapped with NaCl-important genes. [src: counter_ion_effects] The project found that overlap occurred for every metal, ranging from 9.2% for molybdenum to 57.6% for manganese, and declined from 39.8% to 36.7% after excluding the *Synechococcus elongatus* outlier. [src: counter_ion_effects] It compared chloride-delivered metals with non-chloride metals and found mean overlaps of 41.6% and 37.8%, respectively, while zinc sulfate, which delivers 0 mM chloride, had 44.6% overlap. [src: counter_ion_effects]

The project analyzed *DvH* metal–NaCl fitness-profile correlations across 13 metals and 6 NaCl experiments, finding correlations from zinc at r=0.715 to iron at r=0.086; the hierarchy did not follow chloride concentration. [src: counter_ion_effects] It interpreted the shared signal as consistent with cell-envelope integrity, DNA repair, and ion-homeostasis biology rather than chloride dose, while identifying these mechanistic interpretations as extrapolations from fitness profiles rather than direct biochemical tests. [src: counter_ion_effects] The project also removed shared-stress genes to test Metal Fitness Atlas robustness and found that core-genome enrichment was preserved for 12 of 14 metals, with corrected enrichment increasing for molybdenum from +0.132 to +0.145 and for iron from -0.040 to +0.182, while cadmium changed from -0.008 to -0.108. [src: counter_ion_effects]
