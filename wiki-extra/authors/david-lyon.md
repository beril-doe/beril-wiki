# David Lyon

ORCID: [0000-0002-1927-3565](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1927-3565)

## Profile

Their projects investigate how microbial genome content reflects environmental specialization, anaerobic metabolism, and lanthanide-dependent methylotrophy across large comparative datasets and clay-associated cultured genomes. In the [[summaries/lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas__REPORT]], they analyzed 293,059 genomes and found 3,690 xoxF versus 195 mxaF calls—a global ratio of 18.92:1—with xoxF dominance supported by a one-sided binomial test of p = 7.6 × 10⁻²²; they also found that Bakta-validated lanmodulin occurred in 62 genomes, all within Beijerinckiaceae, Acetobacteraceae, or Hyphomicrobiaceae, while xoxF co-occurred in 49/62 genomes (79.0%). [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas] In [[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]], they showed that 5/9 deep-clay genomes carried dissimilatory sulfate-reduction markers versus 0.018 expected under a rock-attached null (binomial p = 4.0×10⁻¹²), while the broader anaerobic toolkit was largely explained by Bacillota_B phylogeny and deep-cohort self-sufficiency was not elevated. [src: clay_confined_subsurface]

In [[summaries/bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory__REPORT]], they compared 10 deep-clay Bacillota_B genomes with 62 soil-baseline genomes and identified 547 significantly enriched eggNOG orthologous groups, including a manually reclassified estimate of roughly 80–100 anaerobic-respiration-related groups. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] The deep-clay genomes were larger rather than streamlined: mean genome size was 4,110,038 bp versus 3,046,124 bp, and mean eggNOG orthologous-group counts were 2,630 versus 2,106, with corresponding Cohen’s d values of +1.39 and +1.30. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory] The same project corrected an earlier iron-reduction comparison by replacing mismatched marker KOs with a multi-heme cytochrome detector; corrected rates were 55.6% in deep anchors, 40.0% in shallow anchors, and 40.9% in soil baseline, with no significant cohort differences (all reported pairwise Fisher p ≥ 0.46). [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory; clay_confined_subsurface]

Taken together, their projects center on testing whether genomic markers genuinely support ecological and metabolic interpretations, while separating environmental signals from phylogenetic structure and annotation artifacts. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory; clay_confined_subsurface; lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas] The corpus therefore suggests research interests in genome-content adaptation to subsurface environments, anaerobic respiratory and persistence functions, comparative validation of functional markers, and the distribution of lanthanide-linked methylotrophy across bacterial diversity—while treating gene annotations as evidence of potential rather than proof of physiology. [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory; clay_confined_subsurface; lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]
