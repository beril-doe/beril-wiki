# David Lyon

ORCID: [0000-0002-1927-3565](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1927-3565)

## Contributions

The [[summaries/lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas__REPORT]] project analyzed 293,059 genomes for lanthanide-dependent methanol-oxidation markers and found 3,690 xoxF genomes versus 195 mxaF genomes, a global xoxF:mxaF ratio of 18.92:1 and an xoxF fraction of 0.9498 [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas]. It also found that Bakta-validated lanmodulin occurred in 62 genomes across 10 species, all within Beijerinckiaceae, Acetobacteraceae, or Hyphomicrobiaceae, while xoxF co-occurred in 49/62 genomes (79.0%) [src: lanthanide_methylotrophy_atlas].

The [[summaries/clay_confined_subsurface__REPORT]] project compared 9 deep-clay, 30 shallow-clay, and phylum-stratified soil-baseline cultured bacterial genomes and found dissimilatory sulfate-reduction markers in 5/9 deep genomes versus 0.018 expected under the rock-attached null (binomial p = 4.0×10⁻¹²) [src: clay_confined_subsurface]. After correcting misidentified iron-reduction markers, it found multi-heme cytochrome detector positivity in 5/9 deep genomes, 12/30 shallow genomes, and 61/149 soil-baseline genomes, with no significant cohort differences (all corrected Fisher p ≥ 0.46), while deep-cohort enrichment of Wood–Ljungdahl, group 1 [NiFe]-hydrogenase, sulfate-reduction, and nitrogenase signals was largely explained by cohort composition except for sulfate reduction [src: clay_confined_subsurface].

The [[summaries/bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory__REPORT]] project compared 10 deep-clay Bacillota_B genomes with 62 soil-baseline genomes and identified 547 significantly enriched eggNOG orthologous groups at q<0.05, including a manually reclassified estimate of approximately 80–100 anaerobic-respiration-related groups [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory]. It also found that deep-clay genomes were larger, averaging 4,110,038 bp versus 3,046,124 bp, and contained more eggNOG groups, averaging 2,630 versus 2,106, while completeness was similar at 94.7% versus 94.3% [src: bacillota_b_subsurface_accessory].
