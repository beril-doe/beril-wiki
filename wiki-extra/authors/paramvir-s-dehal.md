# Paramvir S. Dehal

ORCID: [0000-0001-5810-2497](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5810-2497)

## Profile

Their projects investigate how bacterial gene function, fitness, metabolism, and genome conservation interact across scales—from *Acinetobacter baylyi* ADP1 to thousands of pangenome species. In ADP1, they integrated TnSeq, flux-balance analysis (FBA), deletion phenotypes, proteomics, pangenome data, and functional annotations, finding 73.8% concordance between FBA flux predictions and TnSeq essentiality among 866 genes, while also showing that FBA classes did not predict quantitative growth defects within the TnSeq-dispensable set (χ² = 0.93, p = 0.63). [acinetobacter_adp1_explorer__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/acinetobacter_adp1_explorer__REPORT.md) [adp1_triple_essentiality__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/adp1_triple_essentiality__REPORT.md) Their condition-specific analyses identified five approximately independent dimensions of growth variation across eight carbon sources, a 51-gene quinate-support network dominated by 21 Complex I genes, and respiratory-chain configurations that differ qualitatively by carbon source. [adp1_deletion_phenotypes__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/adp1_deletion_phenotypes__REPORT.md) [aromatic_catabolism_network__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/aromatic_catabolism_network__REPORT.md) [respiratory_chain_wiring__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/respiratory_chain_wiring__REPORT.md)

Across broader bacterial datasets, the projects examine how essentiality, laboratory fitness, co-fitness, and environmental context map onto pangenome structure. Across approximately 194,000 genes from 43 bacteria, essential genes were 82% core while always-neutral genes were 66% core, and core genes were more likely than auxiliary genes to show beneficial deletion effects (24.4% versus 19.9%), revealing a conservation–burden trade-off rather than a simple inert-core model. [fitness_effects_conservation__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/fitness_effects_conservation__REPORT.md) [conservation_fitness_synthesis__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/conservation_fitness_synthesis__REPORT.md) Their module analyses found 1,116 ICA fitness modules, with module genes 86.0% core versus an 81.5% genome-wide baseline, while co-fitness predicted pairwise co-occurrence only weakly but showed stronger signal for coordinated accessory modules. [module_conservation__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/module_conservation__REPORT.md) [cofitness_coinheritance__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/cofitness_coinheritance__REPORT.md) In AMR research, they mapped resistance across 27,690 pangenome species, found that only 30.3% of AMR genes were core versus 46.8% of the baseline, and measured a pooled baseline fitness shift of +0.086 across 25 organisms, while environmental and within-species analyses connected AMR abundance, accessory content, resistance islands, phylogeny, and ecological niche without treating observational associations as causal. [amr_pangenome_atlas__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/amr_pangenome_atlas__REPORT.md) [amr_fitness_cost__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/amr_fitness_cost__REPORT.md) [amr_environmental_resistome__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/amr_environmental_resistome__REPORT.md) [amr_strain_variation__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/amr_strain_variation__REPORT.md)

Taken together, their projects center on quantitative integration of experimental fitness with genome evolution, metabolic capability, and ecological metadata. Recurring interests include context-dependent gene essentiality and conditional fitness, the distinction between core cellular stress responses and specialized accessory functions, co-regulated or co-inherited gene modules, and the limits imposed by annotation quality, phylogenetic confounding, sampling bias, and database-linkage coverage. [essential_genome__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/essential_genome__REPORT.md) [metal_fitness_atlas__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/metal_fitness_atlas__REPORT.md) [metal_specificity__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/metal_specificity__REPORT.md) [amr_cofitness_networks__REPORT](../../wiki/summaries/amr_cofitness_networks__REPORT.md) Their work therefore suggests a research program focused on testing when laboratory measurements predict conservation or field ecology, using cross-database bridges and explicit null results to refine interpretations of [gene-essentiality](../../wiki/concepts/gene-essentiality.md) and [pangenome-integration](../../wiki/concepts/pangenome-integration.md).
