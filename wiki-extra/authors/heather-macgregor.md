# Heather MacGregor

## Profile

The project corpus investigates how environmental context, phylogeny, and genomic mobility shape microbial metal resistance and functional potential. [[summaries/metal_resistance_global_biogeography__REPORT]] mapped 30,497 environmental MAGs after host-associated filtering, including 22,356 with coordinates, and identified 11 significant resistance hotspots and 3 coldspots; soil showed 5.8% metal-resistance prevalence (OR=5.05, q=1.8e-82), whereas marine MAGs showed 1.2% prevalence (OR=0.20, q=9.2e-80). [src: metal_resistance_global_biogeography] [[summaries/microbeatlas_metal_ecology__REPORT]] found, across 606 bacterial genera, that metal type diversity—but not total AMR cluster count or core AMR fraction—was positively associated with ecological niche breadth after accounting for phylogeny (β=+0.021, p=1.5×10⁻⁴). [src: microbeatlas_metal_ecology]

Their projects also connect soil chemistry and gene content with horizontal gene transfer. [[summaries/soil_metal_functional_genomics__REPORT]] identified 2,355 significant COG–metal associations across 51,748 soil samples and reported a conditional db-RDA R² of 0.799 (p=0.005), while emphasizing that co-contamination and project effects limit causal interpretation. [src: soil_metal_functional_genomics] [[summaries/t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt__REPORT]] characterized T4SS or conjugative machinery in 6,652 of 30,497 environmental MAGs (21.8%), detected 32 normalized high-confidence cross-phylum GT2 HGT events, and found that T4SS-positive genomes had 10× higher mobile genetic element density. [src: t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt] [[summaries/soil_frontier_genomics__REPORT]] tested the global clay-shield hypothesis across 5,441 soil samples and found no significant difference between low- and high-clay predictive performance (ΔR²=0.024, 95% CI −0.423 to 0.161), while introducing a Genomic Discovery Index that highlighted underrepresented genomic sampling in forest and cropland soils. [src: soil_frontier_genomics]

Taken together, their projects center on environmental resistomes, spatial and biome-specific variation, phylogenetically structured ecological traits, and the movement of functional genes across microbial lineages. [src: metal_resistance_global_biogeography, microbeatlas_metal_ecology, soil_metal_functional_genomics, t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt] The corpus therefore suggests research interests in integrating metagenomic biogeography with comparative genomics, genome-resolved ecology, and statistical validation of environmental associations, while treating causal mechanisms, sampling bias, spatial dependence, and horizontal transfer routes as unresolved questions rather than established conclusions. [src: soil_frontier_genomics, soil_metal_functional_genomics, microbeatlas_metal_ecology, t4ss_cazy_environmental_hgt] These themes connect directly to [[concepts/environmental-resistome]] and [[concepts/pangenome-integration]].
