# Adam P. Arkin

ORCID: [0000-0002-4999-2931](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4999-2931)

## Profile

The projects attributed to Adam P. Arkin investigate how microbial genomes, genes, metabolites, environments, and phenotypes can be connected at scale. The [[summaries/berdl_data_atlas__REPORT]] inventories 1,740 deduplicated tables across 119 databases and 17 tenants, identifies 536 schema-level cross-tenant bridges, and reports that 51 of 66 audited BERIL projects span multiple tenants. [src: berdl_data_atlas] The [[summaries/functional_dark_matter__REPORT]] and [[summaries/truly_dark_genes__REPORT]] prioritize experimentally testable unknown genes: among 228,709 genes, 57,011 were dark, while reannotation divided the linked subset into 33,105 annotation-lag genes and 6,427 genes that remained hypothetical in both pipelines. [src: functional_dark_matter] [src: truly_dark_genes] The [[summaries/gene_function_ecological_agora__REPORT]] extends this genome-scale perspective to 18,989 GTDB species representatives and 13.74 million rank × clade × KO producer/participation scores, finding support for specific clade–function architectures while failing to reproduce the Alm 2006 correlation estimate of approximately 0.74, with reported Pearson values ranging from 0.100 to 0.288. [src: gene_function_ecological_agora]

A recurring contribution is the combination of experimental measurements with comparative genomics and ecological context, followed by explicit testing of whether an attractive interpretation survives validation. The [[summaries/genotype_to_phenotype_enigma__REPORT]] found that binary growth capability was more predictable than continuous kinetics: GapMind achieved AUC = 0.646, matched-condition phenotype transfer achieved AUC = 0.800, and the initial genome-and-condition model achieved AUC = 0.633 under leave-one-strain-out validation. [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma] The [[summaries/enigma_carbon_census_1__REPORT]] quantified a complementary knowledge gap, with 74 of 83 enrichment compounds classified as organism-dark and only 9 initially callable under the project definition. [src: enigma_carbon_census_1] Environmental projects repeatedly distinguish measured associations from hypotheses: the [[summaries/enigma_sso_asv_ecology__REPORT]] detected hydrogeological-zone effects explaining 27.5% of sediment community variance but treated the proposed northeast-to-southwest plume as unconfirmed without direct geochemistry, while the [[summaries/phb_granule_ecology__REPORT]] found PHB prevalence enriched in high-variability environments even after genome-size stratification, including 10.7% versus 2.5% in the smallest-genome quartile. [src: enigma_sso_asv_ecology] [src: phb_granule_ecology]

Taken together, these projects center on microbial systems biology that links genotype to phenotype across scales, identifies ecological and evolutionary determinants of gene or pathway distribution, and converts incomplete evidence into prioritized experiments or interventions. [src: berdl_data_atlas] [src: functional_dark_matter] [src: gene_function_ecological_agora] [src: genotype_to_phenotype_enigma] The same orientation appears in applied designs: the [[summaries/cf_formulation_design__REPORT]] used inhibition, substrate use, growth kinetics, patient metagenomics, and pangenomes to recommend a two-species candidate formulation for suppressing *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*, while the [[summaries/ibd_phage_targeting__REPORT]] combined ecotypes, metabolites, pathways, and phage evidence to prioritize six Tier-A IBD pathobiont targets. [src: cf_formulation_design] [src: ibd_phage_targeting] The corpus therefore suggests research interests in data-integration infrastructure, experimentally grounded gene-function discovery, microbial community ecology, horizontal gene transfer and mobile elements, and rigorous translation of multi-omic evidence into testable biological or therapeutic strategies. [src: berdl_data_atlas] [src: truly_dark_genes] [src: prophage_ecology] [src: plant_microbiome_ecotypes] [src: caulobacter_fur_lipida_loss] [src: cf_formulation_design] [src: ibd_phage_targeting]
